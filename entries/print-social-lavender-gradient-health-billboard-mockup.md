# Lavender Health Billboard on Concrete

![](../images/print-social-lavender-gradient-health-billboard-mockup.png)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Out-of-home poster set
**Tags:** billboard, health, lavender, gradient, photography, outdoor, mockup

## What it is
A large billboard on a concrete wall with a blue-to-lilac gradient, a smiling doctor holding a clipboard, a two-line headline 'Your Health, Smarter Every Day', a short line, logo and URL.

## What it means
Soft pastel gradient and a friendly professional make healthcare feel trustworthy and modern.

## Techniques
- Cut-out portrait bleeding to the bottom edge of the board
- Large sentence-case headline with short subhead
- Pastel gradient for a calm medical tone
- Logo and URL along the base
- Billboard placed in an architectural scene for scale

## Best for
- Healthcare and health-tech campaigns
- Out-of-home advertising concepts
- Trust-led awareness posters

## Vocabulary
- **Cut-out portrait** — A person masked from their background and placed on a design.
- **Billboard mockup** — A design shown on an outdoor sign in a scene.
- **Pastel gradient** — A soft blend of light desaturated colours.
