# 'Series #2 Gradient' five-poster set with radial-blur pink-blue-yellow fields and minimal numbered labels

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Typographic posters · **Design style:** Gradient & Aurora
**Tags:** poster-series, gradient, radial-blur, pink, blue, minimal

## What it is
Five dark-bordered portrait posters numbered 001–005, each filled edge-to-edge with a soft radial-blur gradient (magenta-to-blue, teal-to-navy with concentric rings, pink-to-gold, blue-purple with rotated 'VER O U DE SIGN' type), labelled 'SERIES #2 GRADIENT' with small corner captions.

## What it means
Meditative and premium; treating pure colour blur as the entire image lets a poster series feel abstract and gallery-like.

## Techniques
- Use only a radial gradient blur as the entire visual, no other imagery.
- Number posters as a series with consistent corner labels.
- Occasionally break the pure-gradient rule with thin ring lines or rotated type for variety.

## Best for
- Abstract poster series
- Music or art event backgrounds
- Gradient brand exploration

## Vocabulary
- **Radial blur** — A gradient that blurs outward from a central point.
- **Series numbering** — Labelling each piece in a set with a sequence number.
