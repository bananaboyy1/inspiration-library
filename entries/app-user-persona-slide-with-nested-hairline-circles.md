# User persona slide with a portrait rising from concentric hairline circles

![](../images/app-user-persona-slide-with-nested-hairline-circles.jpg)

**Discipline:** app · **Design type:** UX documentation
**Tags:** persona, research, white, hairline-circles, italic-serif, quote, case-study, navy

## What it is
A near-white slide with a headline mixing light sans and bold italic serif. A cut-out portrait emerges from a frosted name badge on top of two nested thin circles; the inner circle holds a quote, the outer holds goals. Pain and Gain notes sit at the left and right, and a small icon dock sits below.

## What it means
Human and elegant; circles centre the person while research findings orbit around them.

## Techniques
- Nest hairline circles around a portrait to organise persona facts.
- Let the head break above the circle for depth.
- Place opposing notes (Pain, Gain) at both sides.
- Use tiny avatar thumbnails to switch personas.

## Best for
- Persona and research slides
- Fintech case studies
- Elegant light-mode decks

## Vocabulary
- **Persona** — A fictional profile representing a user group.
- **Nested circles** — Concentric outlines that group information.
- **Pain / Gain** — A pair of notes contrasting frustrations and benefits.
- **Icon dock** — A small pill of icons used to switch views.
