# 'Media Infographics' with a radial segmented donut chart around a central megaphone icon

![](../images/print-social-media-infographics-radial-donut-chart-teal-cream.avif)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Product infographic set · **Design style:** Corporate & Clean-Tech
**Tags:** infographic, media, teal, cream, donut-chart, radial

## What it is
A teal-and-cream infographic with a large radial donut chart divided into labelled percentage segments (SMS 25%, internet 21%, TV 18%, radio 15%, news 16%, mail 5%) around a central megaphone icon, surrounded by six labelled text blocks with dotted leader lines to small device icons and a bottom icon strip.

## What it means
Centralised and clear; radiating labelled segments from one central icon makes many data points feel like one connected system.

## Techniques
- Build a radial donut chart with a meaningful central icon.
- Connect each segment to a text block with a dotted leader line.
- Close with a compact icon strip summarising all categories.

## Best for
- Media consumption reports
- Radial data visualisation
- Corporate infographic decks

## Vocabulary
- **Donut chart** — A ring-shaped chart showing proportional values.
- **Leader line** — A line connecting a label to its corresponding data point.
