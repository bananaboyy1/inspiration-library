# Cream SWOT diagram with bold geometric shape accents

![](../images/web-swot-diagram-cream-bold-geometric-shapes.avif)

**Discipline:** web · **Design type:** Data visualization · **Design style:** Geometric & Bauhaus
**Tags:** swot, diagram, cream, bauhaus, geometric shapes, quadrant

## What it is
A cream-background SWOT quadrant diagram labeled with rounded dark letter badges, decorated with abstract bold geometric shape clusters (starbursts, arches, dots) instead of literal icons.

## What it means
Replacing typical corporate icon sets with purely abstract shape clusters gives a standard business-analysis template a distinct, non-generic art-directed identity.

## Techniques
- label a quadrant framework with rounded letter badges instead of full word headers for a cleaner grid
- decorate with abstract geometric shape clusters rather than literal topic icons
- keep decorative shapes clustered at diagram corners so they frame rather than compete with the content

## Best for
- strategy workshop templates
- pitch deck analysis slides
- brand-forward business documentation

## Vocabulary
- **SWOT analysis** — a strategic planning framework evaluating Strengths, Weaknesses, Opportunities and Threats
