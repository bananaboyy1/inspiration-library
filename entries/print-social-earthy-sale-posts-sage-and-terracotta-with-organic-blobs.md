# Earthy sale pack in sage, terracotta and cream with organic blobs and chevron columns

![](../images/print-social-earthy-sale-posts-sage-and-terracotta-with-organic-blobs.avif)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Social template pack · **Design style:** Warm & Organic
**Tags:** sale, black-friday, sage, terracotta, blobs, typography

## What it is
Six square posts in cream, sage green and terracotta with soft organic blob shapes, bold sans headlines ('Diskon 50%', 'Black friday Sale', 'New Items', 'Give. away'), small chevron columns and dot rows.

## What it means
Calm and earthy; muted tones make sale messages feel tasteful rather than loud.

## Techniques
- Use muted earthy tones for a sale.
- Let organic blobs frame big type.
- Add small chevron columns as rhythm.
- Keep each post purely typographic.

## Best for
- Lifestyle shops
- Eco brands
- Tasteful sales

## Vocabulary
- **Earth tones** — Muted browns, greens and creams.
- **Blob** — An irregular rounded shape.
