# 'You Can Relax As We Grow Your Brand' poster with a yellow blob, flat illustration and underlined red 'BRAND'

![](../images/print-social-creative-ninja-relax-grow-brand-poster-navy-yellow.avif)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Poster · **Design style:** Playful & Illustrated
**Tags:** agency, navy, yellow, flat-illustration, poster, typography

## What it is
A navy-bordered white poster for 'Creative Ninja' with a big yellow organic blob behind a three-line headline in mixed weights ('You can RELAX as we GROW your' then huge red underlined 'BRAND'), a flat illustration of a person waving beside a laptop and a coin-sprouting plant, a lightbulb icon and a services list in black label bars.

## What it means
Confident and reassuring; the relaxed illustration and playful blob take the pressure out of a business pitch.

## Techniques
- Vary weight and colour within one multi-line headline for emphasis.
- Place a flat illustration on a simple organic blob backdrop.
- List services as solid black label bars for quick scanning.

## Best for
- Agency and consultancy posters
- Business growth marketing
- Friendly corporate branding

## Vocabulary
- **Organic blob backdrop** — An irregular rounded shape used behind a subject.
- **Label bar** — A solid block holding a short line of text.
