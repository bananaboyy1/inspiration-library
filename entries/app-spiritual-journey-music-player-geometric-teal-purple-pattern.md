# Music player app with a geometric teal-purple triangle pattern behind the album art and track list

![](../images/app-spiritual-journey-music-player-geometric-teal-purple-pattern.avif)

**Discipline:** app · **Design type:** UI components · **Design style:** Geometric & Bauhaus
**Tags:** music-player, geometric, teal, purple, pattern, app-ui

## What it is
Three phone screens for a music app: a 'Now Playing' screen with a circular album art disc over a triangle-and-circle geometric pattern, a track list ('Anchor', 'State Lines'...) with heart icons on the same pattern, and an 'Other Albums' grid on a plain white background.

## What it means
Rhythmic and stylish; the geometric pattern behind the player controls gives the interface a strong, memorable visual identity beyond typical album art.

## Techniques
- Use a bold geometric pattern as a persistent UI background.
- Keep the pattern only on player screens, switching to plain white for browsing.
- Render the album art as a circular disc echoing a vinyl record.

## Best for
- Music streaming apps
- Geometric UI branding
- Audio product design

## Vocabulary
- **Persistent background** — A pattern or colour kept consistent across a set of screens.
- **Disc motif** — A circular shape referencing a vinyl or CD.
