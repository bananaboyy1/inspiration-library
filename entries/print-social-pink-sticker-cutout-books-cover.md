# Pink-outlined cutout with open book, giant black caps and pink caption block

![](../images/print-social-pink-sticker-cutout-books-cover.png)

**Discipline:** print-social ·  · **Design style:** Photographic & Cinematic
**Tags:** cover, pink, cutout, sticker-outline, heavy-caps, script, ghost-pattern, cyrillic

## What it is
A white 4:5 post with a faint repeated account-name pattern. A cut-out woman with a face mask holds an open book, all outlined in thick pink. Above is a pink script number and giant black extended caps 'КНИЖКИ', with a pink rounded caption and a black arrow and handle pill.

## What it means
Loud, personable and feminine; the pink sticker outline turns a photo into a graphic.

## Techniques
- Give the cutout a thick pink border like a sticker.
- Overlap a pink caption box on the bottom of giant black type.
- Repeat the account name as a faint ghost pattern.
- Pair pink script with black heavy caps.

## Best for
- Lists and recommendation posts
- Female-oriented education and lifestyle accounts
- Carousel covers

## Vocabulary
- **Sticker outline** — A thick border around a cutout.
- **Ghost pattern** — A faint repeated word in the background.
- **Script number** — A number written in flourished script.
- **Overlapping caption** — A text box that overlaps a headline.
