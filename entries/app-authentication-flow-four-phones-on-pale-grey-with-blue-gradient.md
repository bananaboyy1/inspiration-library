# Authentication Flow in Four Phones

![](../images/app-authentication-flow-four-phones-on-pale-grey-with-blue-gradient.jpg)

**Discipline:** app ·  · **Design style:** Editorial
**Tags:** authentication, sign-in, otp, fintech, blue-grey, serif-italic, flow

## What it is
A white page with an 'Authentication' pill, a headline with an italic serif 'Financial Journey', a paragraph, four link pills (Create, Login, Verify, Home) and four phones in a cross layout showing create account, welcome back, OTP verify and home, each fading from white to blue-grey at the base.

## What it means
A gradient rising in each screen makes a serious flow feel soft, and the cross layout shows the sequence at a glance.

## Techniques
- Phones in a cross or diamond arrangement
- Blue-grey gradient rising from the bottom of each screen
- Rounded white inputs on soft backgrounds
- Keypad with translucent keys
- Small pill navigation for the flow steps

## Best for
- Sign-in and verification flows
- Fintech onboarding presentations
- Four-screen storytelling

## Vocabulary
- **OTP screen** — A screen for entering a one-time code.
- **Flow pills** — Small labels naming the steps in a flow.
- **Gradient rise** — A colour gradient that strengthens toward the bottom.
