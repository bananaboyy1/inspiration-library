# 'Xvect' white wordmark with a curling swoosh on the X, on a cyan-blue gradient tile

![](../images/branding-xvect-wordmark-with-swoosh-x-on-cyan-gradient.jpg)

**Discipline:** branding · **Design type:** Wordmarks · **Design style:** Minimalist
**Tags:** wordmark, logo, cyan, gradient, app-icon, swoosh

## What it is
A square tile with a cyan-to-blue gradient and a white lowercase 'Xvect' wordmark whose oversized X extends into a curved swoosh with a small hook, like a vector or arrow path.

## What it means
Light and techy; the swoosh hints at motion and vectors while staying simple.

## Techniques
- Enlarge the first letter and give it a single signature stroke.
- Set a white mark on a two-tone gradient tile.
- Keep the rest of the word plain so the flourish stands out.

## Best for
- Tech startup logos
- Design tool brands
- App icons

## Vocabulary
- **Swash** — A decorative extended stroke on a letter.
- **Wordmark** — A logo made purely of the brand name.
