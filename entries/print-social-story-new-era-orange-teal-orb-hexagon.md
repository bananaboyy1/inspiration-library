# 'New Era' story with orange/teal orb and hexagon accents

![](../images/print-social-story-new-era-orange-teal-orb-hexagon.avif)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Story set · **Design style:** Gradient & Aurora
**Tags:** instagram story, gradient, new era, orange teal, orb, hexagon

## What it is
A companion story template from the same futuristic set, featuring a large split orange/teal circular orb, thin orbital ring lines, and a smaller hexagon shape below the 'NEW ERA' headline.

## What it means
A split-color orb divided cleanly down the middle visually suggests duality or transformation, fitting the 'new era' messaging without any literal imagery.

## Techniques
- split a single circular shape into two contrasting gradient halves to suggest transformation
- add thin orbital ring lines around a central shape to imply motion/orbit
- pair one large focal shape with a smaller secondary shape of a different geometry for variety

## Best for
- futuristic/tech brand story templates
- transformation or rebrand announcement stories
- gradient-heavy social template packs

## Vocabulary
- **orbital ring** — a thin circular line drawn around a shape to suggest rotation, orbit or energy
