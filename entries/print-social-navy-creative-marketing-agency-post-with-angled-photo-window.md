## What it is
A square white post with a navy gradient wedge on the left holding a thin-and-bold 'Creative MARKETING AGENCY' headline and contact rows with icons, a photo of a smiling man at a desk in an angled rounded window, a thin white outline echoing the window, and small triangle outlines.

## What it means
Dynamic and sharp; angled shapes add motion to a standard corporate layout.

## Techniques
- Crop the photo in an angled rounded window.
- Echo the window with an offset outline.
- Put contact rows with icons in the text panel.

## Best for
- Agency ads
- Corporate promos
- Service posts

## Vocabulary
- **Offset outline** — A thin stroke shifted from a shape's edge.
- **Wedge** — A tapered angular shape.
