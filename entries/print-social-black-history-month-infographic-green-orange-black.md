# 'Black History Month' infographic with green and orange fact blocks, a fist-in-Africa icon and a closing quote

![](../images/print-social-black-history-month-infographic-green-orange-black.avif)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Product infographic set · **Design style:** Editorial
**Tags:** black-history-month, infographic, green, orange, serif, educational

## What it is
A black-bordered square infographic with a serif 'BLACK HISTORY MONTH' title, an intro line, and stacked green/orange/yellow blocks covering Movements and Activism (with a raised-fist-shaped Africa icon), a '90% Labor' stat, an Agriculture vs Other Industries Venn diagram, an 1808 date callout, and a closing quote from A. Philip Randolph.

## What it means
Respectful and informative; mixing icon, stat and quote formats keeps a history lesson visually varied within one strict palette.

## Techniques
- Combine icon, stat, diagram and quote blocks in one infographic.
- Use a symbolic icon (fist-shaped continent) to open the topic.
- Close with a sourced historical quote for weight.

## Best for
- Educational and awareness campaigns
- History and culture content
- Instagram infographic posts

## Vocabulary
- **Symbolic icon** — An icon whose shape itself carries meaning.
- **Venn diagram** — Overlapping circles showing proportion or relationship.
