# Roadside billboard of three pearl panels: a '85%' bars message, refractive cards and a download-app phone

![](../images/web-billboard-with-three-frosted-panels-payments-cards-and-app.jpg)

**Discipline:** web · **Design type:** Out-of-home poster set
**Tags:** billboard, ooh, pearl, orange-keyword, refractive-cards, fintech, street, panels

## What it is
A billboard on a concrete-slat frame beside trees showing three light frosted panels: a black logo tile with 'Smarter Payments for Everyday Life.' with an orange keyword and a big 85% over white bars, a panel of iridescent translucent payment cards with 'Experience the Future of Finance.', and a panel with 'Download the App', store badges and a phone.

## What it means
Airy and modern; pearly panels look premium against a rough street setting.

## Techniques
- Split a billboard into three panels with one message each.
- Use refractive cards as imagery.
- Colour one keyword orange.
- Contrast pearl gradients with concrete.

## Best for
- Fintech OOH campaigns
- Triptych billboard designs
- Pearl gradient brands

## Vocabulary
- **Triptych billboard** — A billboard divided into three panels.
- **Refractive card** — A translucent card with rainbow highlights.
- **Keyword accent** — One word coloured differently in a headline.
- **Concrete frame** — A slatted concrete surround.
