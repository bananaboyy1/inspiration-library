# Grey Gradient Quote and Project Info Slide

![](../images/app-grey-gradient-project-quote-and-info-slide.jpg)

**Discipline:** app ·  · **Design style:** Minimalist
**Tags:** quote, case-study, grey, minimal, project-info, sans, fintech

## What it is
A soft grey gradient slide with a big two-tone quote (black then grey), a small avatar and name credit, and two bottom columns: project metadata (industry, category, location, year) and an About paragraph.

## What it means
Quiet neutrals and generous white space give an authoritative, editorial calm. The two-tone sentence guides the eye from the key claim to the detail.

## Techniques
- Quote where the first half is dark and the rest fades to grey
- Metadata list with grey labels and black values
- Slash-separated labels for a compact key/value style
- Large empty middle band to let the quote breathe
- Two-column footer that balances metadata against prose

## Best for
- Case-study opening or summary slides
- Testimonial-led introductions
- Fintech and enterprise portfolios

## Vocabulary
- **Two-tone quote** — A quotation whose first phrase is high contrast and remainder lower contrast.
- **Project metadata** — Short labeled facts like industry, location and year.
- **Key/value list** — Label and value pairs in a compact list.
