# 'Coffee Friends' pack with smiling cup and donut characters in red, pink and blue

![](../images/print-social-coffee-friends-posts-with-cute-cup-and-donut-characters.avif)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Social template pack · **Design style:** Playful & Illustrated
**Tags:** coffee, cafe, illustration, kawaii, red, pink

## What it is
Five flat posts with cute illustrated characters (a smiling cup with a donut hat, a croissant, a pour-over), wheat sprigs and coffee beans in red-orange, pink, cream and cobalt, plus mixed outline and solid headlines ('Welcome to Coffee Friends', 'What's your coffee type?').

## What it means
Cheerful and charming; friendly characters make a café feel like a place to hang out.

## Techniques
- Give products faces for charm.
- Mix outline and solid versions of one typeface.
- Use a limited flat palette across posts.
- Ask a question post to invite comments.

## Best for
- Cafés and bakeries
- Kids' food brands
- Playful social feeds

## Vocabulary
- **Kawaii** — A cute style with simple faces on objects.
- **Flat illustration** — Drawings with solid colours and no shading.
