# 'Trusted By The Best People' with three rows of avatar-and-name capsules staggered and fading at the edges over a faint diagonal texture

![](../images/web-marquee-of-avatar-name-pills-in-three-offset-rows-with-fade-edges.webp)

**Discipline:** web · **Design type:** Landing page sections
**Tags:** social-proof, marquee, pills, avatars, white, fade-edges, staggered, testimonial

## What it is
A white page with a bold centred heading and three horizontal rows of white capsules, each holding a round photo avatar with a bold name and grey role, the rows offset from each other and fading out at both sides, over a pale hatch texture.

## What it means
Soft and populous; staggered capsules suggest a crowd of real people.

## Techniques
- Stagger rows of capsules.
- Fade the row edges.
- Use a faint texture behind.
- Keep names bold and roles grey.

## Best for
- Social-proof marquees
- Customer and community sections
- Avatar pill rows

## Vocabulary
- **Avatar capsule** — A pill with a photo, name and role.
- **Staggered rows** — Rows offset horizontally.
- **Edge fade** — Content dissolving at the sides.
- **Marquee** — A continuously scrolling band.
