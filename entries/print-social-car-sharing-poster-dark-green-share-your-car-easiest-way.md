# 'Share your car the easiest way with our APP' poster with an overlapping route-list card and scribble loops

![](../images/print-social-car-sharing-poster-dark-green-share-your-car-easiest-way.avif)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Event poster · **Design style:** Playful & Illustrated
**Tags:** poster, app-promo, dark-green, car-sharing, scribble, phone-mockup

## What it is
A dark green A4 poster with mint scribble loops in the corners, a bold headline mixing solid and script type ('Share your car the easiest way with our APP'), a 'Download it for free and enjoy now!' line, and two overlapping phone-screen cards showing a route search list with avatars and green add buttons.

## What it means
Direct and inviting; overlapping app-screen cards suggest depth and real functionality behind a simple poster message.

## Techniques
- Overlap two UI screenshots for a sense of layered depth.
- Mix solid and script type within one headline for emphasis.
- Repeat the same scribble-loop motif across a campaign's assets.

## Best for
- App download campaigns
- Mobility service posters
- Consistent campaign asset sets

## Vocabulary
- **UI screenshot card** — A cropped image of an app screen used as a design element.
- **Campaign motif** — A recurring visual device tying together multiple assets.
