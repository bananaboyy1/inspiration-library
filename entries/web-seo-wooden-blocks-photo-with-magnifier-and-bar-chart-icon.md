## What it is
A photo of two stacked wooden blocks spelling 'SEO' on a plain grey background, with a black magnifying-glass icon printed across the top blocks framing a small red rising bar chart, set with generous empty copy space to the left.

## What it means
Simple and versatile; the ample negative space and literal magnifier-on-blocks concept make this an easy plug-in header image.

## Techniques
- Print an icon directly onto a physical object in a concept photo.
- Leave large empty copy space for headline overlay.
- Use a magnifying glass as a universal 'search/analysis' symbol.

- Concept stock photography
