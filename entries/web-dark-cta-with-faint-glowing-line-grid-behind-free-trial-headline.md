# Black call-to-action 'Start your free trial' with a small Get Started button over a faint glowing grid

![](../images/web-dark-cta-with-faint-glowing-line-grid-behind-free-trial-headline.webp)

**Discipline:** web ·  · **Design style:** Gradient & Aurora
**Tags:** cta, dark, grid, glow, minimal, white, button, saas

## What it is
A near-black canvas with a barely visible glowing line grid at the centre, a bold white headline 'Start your free trial', a grey line about users and a small white Get Started button.

## What it means
Focused and quiet; a faint grid adds structure without distracting from the ask.

## Techniques
- Use a very faint glowing grid behind text.
- Keep one small button.
- Centre a bold headline.
- Stay black and white.

## Best for
- Closing CTA sections
- Dark minimal SaaS pages
- Grid-glow backgrounds

## Vocabulary
- **Glow grid** — A faint luminous line grid.
- **Closing CTA** — A final call-to-action section.
- **Centred stack** — Headline, line and button centred vertically.
- **Ambient texture** — A subtle pattern adding depth.
