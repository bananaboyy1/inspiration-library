# Business woman riding a red toy rocket concept photo

![](../images/print-social-business-woman-rocket-concept-photo.jpg)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Brand campaign visual · **Design style:** Photographic & Cinematic
**Tags:** concept photo, rocket, business growth, surreal, composite, red

## What it is
A composited studio photo of a businesswoman in a red suit riding a small red rocket, isolated on white with a soft shadow, used as a growth/launch metaphor.

## What it means
A literal visual pun (person + rocket = 'launch') gives an abstract business idea an instantly readable, slightly humorous image that works across many contexts.

## Techniques
- build a literal visual metaphor by compositing a person with a symbolic prop
- isolate the composite on pure white so it drops into any layout with negative space to spare
- color-match the subject's outfit to the prop for a designed, unified look

## Best for
- startup/growth marketing decks
- business blog hero images
- 'launch' or 'success' themed campaigns

## Vocabulary
- **visual metaphor** — an image that represents an abstract idea through a literal or symbolic visual comparison
