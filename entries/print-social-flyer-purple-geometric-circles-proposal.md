# Purple flyer with interlocking Bauhaus circles

![](../images/print-social-flyer-purple-geometric-circles-proposal.avif)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Event flyer · **Design style:** Geometric & Bauhaus
**Tags:** flyer, proposal, geometric, circles, bauhaus, purple, bold

## What it is
An A5 flyer for a design proposal service, with a band of overlapping colored circles cut by thin black bars sitting between bold black headline text.

## What it means
The playful geometric motif signals 'creative but structured,' letting the flyer promise design expertise through its own layout rather than through imagery.

## Techniques
- use a row of overlapping flat circles as a decorative divider instead of a photo
- cut shapes with thin black strokes to add a hand-drawn, collaged feel
- keep typography in pure black/white so the color budget goes entirely to the graphic band

## Best for
- design agency self-promotion
- creative service proposals
- workshops or portfolio one-pagers

## Vocabulary
- **Bauhaus** — an early-20th-century design movement favoring primary shapes, colors and geometric abstraction
