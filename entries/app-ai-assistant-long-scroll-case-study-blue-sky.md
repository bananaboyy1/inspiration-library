# AI Assistant Long-Scroll Case Study

![](../images/app-ai-assistant-long-scroll-case-study-blue-sky.jpg)

**Discipline:** app ·  · **Design style:** Geometric & Bauhaus
**Tags:** ai, case-study, blue, dandelion, long-scroll, voice, assistant

## What it is
A very tall case study strip: dark colour palette header, then alternating photo-backed blocks with dandelion and sky imagery, phone screens, chart close-ups and tablet views for a voice-first AI assistant.

## What it means
Airy nature photography softens the AI theme, with glassy blue UI floating over it for a calm, breathable brand.

## Techniques
- Nature photo backgrounds behind glassy phone UI
- Alternating dark text tiles with image tiles
- Consistent blue palette introduced in the first block
- Close-up hand and screen for scale
- Vertical rhythm through varied tile heights

## Best for
- AI and voice assistant case studies
- Long-form portfolio pages
- Products wanting a calm, natural tone

## Vocabulary
- **Long scroll** — A single tall image or page presenting a full case study.
- **Photo-backed UI** — Interface shown on top of a photographic background.
- **Glass UI** — Translucent blurred surfaces in an interface.
