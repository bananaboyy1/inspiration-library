# Red square 'We Are Hiring!' post with a megaphone on a red chair and translucent role list

![](../images/print-social-red-hiring-square-post-with-megaphone-on-chair.avif)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Recruitment post · **Design style:** Corporate & Clean-Tech
**Tags:** hiring, recruitment, red, megaphone, chair, instagram

## What it is
A square red version of the chair-and-megaphone hiring layout: boxed 'WE ARE' and 'PROFESSIONAL' words in a big white headline, a black megaphone on a red chair, a translucent panel of open positions, CV email, WhatsApp and a white curved footer with perks.

## What it means
Clear and eye-catching; tone-on-tone red keeps focus on the message and the witty prop.

## Techniques
- Keep background, chair and panel in one red family.
- Box selected words in the headline.
- Adapt the same layout across story and feed sizes.

## Best for
- Instagram hiring posts
- Creative studios
- Multi-format campaigns

## Vocabulary
- **Tone-on-tone** — Shades of one colour used together.
- **Format adaptation** — Reworking one design for different sizes.
