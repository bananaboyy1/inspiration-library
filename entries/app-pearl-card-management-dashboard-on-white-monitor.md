# Frosted grey card-management dashboard with a large platinum card, dot-matrix charts and serif figures

![](../images/app-pearl-card-management-dashboard-on-white-monitor.jpg)

**Discipline:** app · **Design type:** Dashboard
**Tags:** dashboard, fintech, pearl, grey, serif-numerals, dot-matrix, card, monitor

## What it is
A white monitor showing a translucent grey dashboard: tile sidebar with a black active tile, a Platinum card carousel with a large pearly card, stat strips with sparklines, dot-matrix and hairline bar charts, spending categories and a Pro upsell card.

## What it means
Calm and premium; monochrome pearl and serif numbers feel like a private bank.

## Techniques
- Centre a large pearl card as the hero.
- Draw charts as dot matrices and hairlines.
- Use serif for numbers and sans for labels.
- Highlight the active nav tile in black.

## Best for
- Card and wallet dashboards
- Monochrome premium UIs
- Glass-style web apps

## Vocabulary
- **Dot-matrix chart** — A chart drawn with a grid of dots.
- **Pearl card** — A payment card with an iridescent pale finish.
- **Active tile** — A black nav tile marking the current page.
- **Upsell card** — A promo tile for a paid plan.
