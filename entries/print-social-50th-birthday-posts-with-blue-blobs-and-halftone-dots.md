# 50th birthday pack with a silver-haired model, blue blobs, sparkles and halftone dots

![](../images/print-social-50th-birthday-posts-with-blue-blobs-and-halftone-dots.avif)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Social template pack · **Design style:** Playful & Illustrated
**Tags:** birthday, celebration, blue, coral, halftone, sparkles, serif, instagram

## What it is
Nine white square posts for a 50th birthday: a silver-haired woman in pale blue, blue gradient blobs behind her, coral starburst badges with dates, black sparkle stars, halftone dot fields and serif headlines ('Fifty & Fabulous', 'Vintage Celebrate').

## What it means
Soft serifs and sparkles feel celebratory yet elegant, pitching a milestone birthday as glamorous rather than old.

## Techniques
- Put a soft colour blob behind the cut-out subject.
- Stamp key details on a starburst badge.
- Use halftone dots as a light retro texture.
- Scatter four-point sparkles as festive punctuation.

## Best for
- Milestone birthday invites
- Celebration events
- Mature-audience lifestyle brands

## Vocabulary
- **Starburst badge** — A spiky seal-shaped label for dates or offers.
- **Halftone** — A pattern of dots that simulates tone.
