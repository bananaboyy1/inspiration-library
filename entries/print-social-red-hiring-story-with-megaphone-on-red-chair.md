# Red 'We Are Hiring!' story with a megaphone resting on a red chair and a translucent role panel

![](../images/print-social-red-hiring-story-with-megaphone-on-red-chair.avif)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Recruitment post · **Design style:** Corporate & Clean-Tech
**Tags:** hiring, recruitment, red, megaphone, chair, story

## What it is
A vertical red story with a white headline where 'WE ARE' and 'PROFESSIONAL' sit in white boxes, a black megaphone lying on a red modern chair, a translucent red panel of open positions, CV and WhatsApp details, and a white curved footer with perks and handle.

## What it means
Striking and witty; the megaphone on an empty chair says 'this seat is calling you'.

## Techniques
- Combine two metaphors: chair and megaphone.
- Reverse words out of white boxes in the headline.
- Close with a curved white footer.

## Best for
- Instagram story hiring
- Creative agencies
- Bold recruitment

## Vocabulary
- **Reversed type** — Coloured text knocked out of a solid box.
- **Curved footer** — A bottom area with a wavy edge.
