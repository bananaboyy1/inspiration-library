# Black and yellow creativity poster with collage portrait and spray texture

![](../images/print-social-poster-collage-creativity-black-yellow-spray.avif)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Event poster · **Design style:** Editorial
**Tags:** poster, collage, creativity, spray paint, black yellow, editorial

## What it is
A black-bordered poster with a cream interior, featuring a surreal collaged portrait (planet and fabric replacing the head) over airbrushed color-block shapes and a bold serif 'Unleash your Creativity' headline.

## What it means
Replacing a normal portrait's head with an abstract collaged element visually embodies 'unleashed creativity' more directly than any literal illustration of the concept could.

## Techniques
- collage unexpected elements (a planet, fabric) directly onto a photographic figure for surrealism
- apply soft airbrush/spray texture behind color-block shapes for an artistic, unfinished edge
- frame the whole poster in a solid black border to give it a gallery-print quality

## Best for
- art school/creative workshop posters
- surreal collage-style campaigns
- gallery or exhibition marketing

## Vocabulary
- **photomontage** — a composite image created by combining multiple photographs or photo elements into one surreal or symbolic scene
