# 'Digital Marketing Agency' banner template with an orange field, organic blob photo frame and white base

![](../images/print-social-digital-marketing-agency-banner-orange-organic-blob-frame.jpg)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Social banner · **Design style:** Minimalist
**Tags:** agency, banner, orange, organic-frame, template, minimal

## What it is
A wide banner split between a solid orange left field with 'AGENCY' label, bold 'DIGITAL MARKETING AGENCY' title, body copy and contact icons, and a white right field with a large pink triangular-organic blob labelled 'Product picture or your logo'.

## What it means
Simple and adaptable; the organic blob photo frame gives an otherwise plain corporate banner a friendly, modern touch.

## Techniques
- Split a banner into a solid colour field and a white field.
- Use an organic blob shape as a placeholder photo frame.
- Keep contact details as a simple icon-plus-text row.

## Best for
- Corporate web banners
- Agency business cards
- Placeholder marketing templates

## Vocabulary
- **Organic blob frame** — An irregular rounded shape used to crop or hold a photo.
- **Placeholder banner** — A template banner with generic labels meant to be replaced.
