# Blush-pink conversational onboarding screen with a rainbow gradient arc on a tufted grey sofa

**Discipline:** app · **Design type:** Device mockup scene
**Tags:** onboarding, conversational, blush, gradient-arc, sofa, top-down, orange, pills

## What it is
A phone lies on a buttoned taupe leather sofa in raking light, its blush-pink screen showing a step tracker, a thanks line, a larger question in rust-brown text, a rainbow gradient arc and two pills: a white Write and an orange Let's talk, with a Skip link.

## What it means
Warm and soft; a pink screen and rainbow arc feel gentle on tufted leather.

## Techniques
- Lay the phone on a tufted surface in raking light.
- Use a rainbow arc as the assistant's presence.
- Set the question in a single warm brown.
- Offer dual pills for input.

## Best for
- Conversational and wellbeing onboarding
- Soft lifestyle mockups
- Warm gradient UIs

## Vocabulary
- **Tufted texture** — A buttoned quilted upholstery surface.
- **Rainbow arc** — A multi-colour curved gradient.
- **Raking light** — Low-angle light that shows texture.
- **Dual-input pills** — Two buttons for typing or speaking.
