## What it is
Four portrait posters: black with stacked orange glowing spheres and 'Payments wait. Digital money moves', white 'Market Notes from 2030' with only hairline grid lines, white 'Digital Payments Simplified' with an orange-pink-violet gradient rising from the bottom, and black with two huge violet spheres behind 'Digital Payments?'.

## What it means
Sleek and future-facing; soft glowing orbs make money feel fluid and light.

## Techniques
- Use glowing spheres as abstract hero forms.
- Show a faint hairline grid as structure.
- Fade a sunset gradient up from the bottom edge.
- Alternate black and white posters in a set.

## Best for
- Fintech campaigns
- Payment app launches
- Minimal tech posters

## Vocabulary
- **Hairline grid** — Very thin structural lines left visible.
- **Sphere render** — A 3D-shaded ball with glow.
