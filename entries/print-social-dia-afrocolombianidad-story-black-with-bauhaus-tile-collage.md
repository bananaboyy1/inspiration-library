# 'Día de la Afrocolombianidad' story with a black portrait and a colourful Bauhaus icon-tile collage

![](../images/print-social-dia-afrocolombianidad-story-black-with-bauhaus-tile-collage.avif)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Social ad · **Design style:** Geometric & Bauhaus
**Tags:** cultural-heritage, spanish, black, bauhaus, tiles, story

## What it is
A black vertical story with orange, red and blue condensed type ('DÍA DE LA — AFRO — COLOMBIANIDAD'), a date and time line, a black-and-white portrait of a man with his hand to his face, overlaid by a grid of small colourful geometric icon tiles (leaves, arrows, triangles, a maze pattern) in orange, red and blue.

## What it means
Respectful and vibrant; overlaying cultural pattern tiles onto a portrait ties heritage motifs directly to a real person.

## Techniques
- Overlay a grid of small pattern tiles onto a portrait photo.
- Use a shared colour palette across dozens of small icon tiles.
- Keep the headline in bold condensed caps with colour-split lines.

## Best for
- Cultural heritage campaigns
- Community awareness events
- Spanish-language social content

## Vocabulary
- **Icon tile grid** — A grid of small repeating pattern or icon squares.
- **Cultural motif** — A visual pattern carrying specific cultural meaning.
