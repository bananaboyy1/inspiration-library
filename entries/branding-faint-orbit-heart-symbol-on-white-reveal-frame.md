# Pale interlocking orbit-heart symbol on white, an early frame of a logo reveal

![](../images/branding-faint-orbit-heart-symbol-on-white-reveal-frame.gif)

**Discipline:** branding ·  · **Design style:** Minimalist
**Tags:** logo, symbol, heart, orbit, pale, minimal, animation-frame, white

## What it is
A white field with a small pale cream-grey symbol of two crossing loops forming a heart and an orbit.

## What it means
Delicate and gentle; the faint mark suggests a fade-in of care.

## Techniques
- Start an animation with a ghost-pale mark.
- Use two crossing loops to suggest heart and orbit.
- Keep to a single tone on white.

## Best for
- Health and care brands
- Logo reveal animations
- Soft minimal symbols

## Vocabulary
- **Ghost mark** — A logo shown at very low contrast.
- **Crossing loops** — Two loops that intersect to form a symbol.
- **Reveal frame** — A frame from an animated logo intro.
- **Orbit motif** — An elliptical line suggesting movement.
