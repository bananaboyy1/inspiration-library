# Vintage sepia phone-and-map statistics infographic

![](../images/print-social-infographic-vintage-sepia-phone-stats.avif)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Product infographic set · **Design style:** Retro & Pixel
**Tags:** infographic, vintage, sepia, retro, map, statistics

## What it is
A muted sepia-green infographic centered on three antique-style phone mockups with speech-bubble stat callouts, a world map with location pins, and rows of bar/line/pie charts below.

## What it means
Aging the palette and phone illustrations to look vintage gives a modern mobile-usage report a nostalgic, storybook credibility rather than a sterile corporate feel.

## Techniques
- render modern devices in an antique illustration style to shift the whole piece's tone
- use a restricted sepia/muted palette across every chart type for cohesion
- mix multiple chart types (map pins, bars, line, pie) in one infographic organized by section

## Best for
- retro-themed marketing reports
- vintage brand storytelling
- editorial infographics needing a nostalgic tone

## Vocabulary
- **sepia palette** — a warm brown-toned monochrome color scheme associated with aged or vintage photography
