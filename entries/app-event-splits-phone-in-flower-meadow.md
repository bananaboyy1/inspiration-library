# Event App in a Flower Meadow

![](../images/app-event-splits-phone-in-flower-meadow.jpg)

**Discipline:** app · **Design type:** Device mockup scene
**Tags:** meadow, sky, events, split-bills, surreal, phone, white-ui

## What it is
A large white-UI phone standing in a photographic flower meadow under a bright blue sky, showing an event feed with a picnic photo and split-payment cards, beside a curved chrome object.

## What it means
The sunny outdoor setting matches the social events theme, making the app feel joyful and open.

## Techniques
- Oversized phone integrated into a natural landscape
- Photo card inside the UI echoing the surrounding picnic mood
- Chrome curved prop as a surreal accent
- Clean white UI to contrast with vivid greens and blues
- Dark floating tab bar with a red-orange plus button

## Best for
- Social and event apps
- Surreal hero images
- Lighthearted consumer brands

## Vocabulary
- **Surreal scale** — Objects shown at impossible sizes within a scene.
- **Split payment** — A feature dividing a cost among people.
- **Floating tab bar** — A dark rounded navigation bar hovering over content.
