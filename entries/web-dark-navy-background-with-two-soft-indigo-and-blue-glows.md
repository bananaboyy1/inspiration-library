# Near-black navy field with a violet glow at the upper left and a blue glow at the lower right

![](../images/web-dark-navy-background-with-two-soft-indigo-and-blue-glows.webp)

**Discipline:** web · **Design type:** Background pattern
**Tags:** background, gradient, navy, violet, blue, glow, dark, mesh

## What it is
A dark blue-black canvas with two large soft radial glows: an indigo-violet one at the upper left and a cobalt blue one at the lower right, fading smoothly to black between them.

## What it means
Calm and deep; two cool lights give a dark page a night-sky depth.

## Techniques
- Place two large radial glows on a diagonal.
- Use adjacent cool hues.
- Keep the base near black.
- Use as a hero or section backdrop.

## Best for
- Dark SaaS heroes
- Ambient backgrounds
- Blue and violet brands

## Vocabulary
- **Radial glow** — A soft circular light fading outward.
- **Diagonal balance** — Two elements placed on opposite corners.
- **Cool palette** — Blues and violets.
- **Ambient backdrop** — A background that adds mood, not content.
