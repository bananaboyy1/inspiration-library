# 'Are You Looking for Finest Idea?' post with a charcoal hexagon panel and yellow-framed photo

![](../images/print-social-yellow-hexagon-business-idea-post-with-charcoal-panel.avif)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Social ad · **Design style:** Corporate & Clean-Tech
**Tags:** business, yellow, charcoal, hexagon, ad, corporate

## What it is
A square post with a charcoal pointed panel holding white and yellow uppercase text, overlapping a photo of a smiling man with earphones at a laptop framed by a yellow hexagonal outline, with a pale grey base and a phone contact.

## What it means
Structured and professional; hexagonal edges feel techy and forward-pointing.

## Techniques
- Point a text panel into the photo like an arrow.
- Frame the photo with a thick yellow angular stroke.
- Break the headline into white and yellow lines.

## Best for
- Corporate service ads
- Consulting promos
- Webinar posts

## Vocabulary
- **Pointed panel** — A text block with an arrow-like edge.
- **Angular frame** — A stroke with cut corners around an image.
