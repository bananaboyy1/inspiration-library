# 'Creative Design For A Bright Future' flyer with a duotone portrait and scattered Matisse-style cut-out shapes

![](../images/print-social-be-a-graphic-designer-flyer-navy-with-matisse-cutout-shapes.avif)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Social ad · **Design style:** Neo-Brutalist
**Tags:** design-career, navy, cutout-shapes, duotone, bold-type, flyer

## What it is
A square flyer split between a black-and-white photo of a woman sketching with headphones on, and a navy field scattered with bold flat shapes (a green scalloped blob, an orange starburst, a purple star, a scribbled orange loop) around bold white 'Creative Design For A Bright Future' and pink 'Be A Graphic Designer' headlines.

## What it means
Bold and youthful; scattered abstract shapes over a serious portrait make a career-recruitment flyer feel current and creative.

## Techniques
- Split the frame between a real photo and a flat colour field.
- Scatter unrelated abstract shapes for energetic texture.
- Keep the photo greyscale so the flat shapes carry all colour.

## Best for
- Design school recruitment
- Creative career campaigns
- Bold youth-oriented flyers

## Vocabulary
- **Matisse-style shapes** — Simple, organic cut-out forms inspired by paper-cut art.
- **Split-field layout** — A frame divided between photographic and flat graphic areas.
