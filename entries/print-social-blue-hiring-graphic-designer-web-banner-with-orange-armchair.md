# Blue 'We're Hiring Graphic Designer' web banner with a man on an orange armchair

![](../images/print-social-blue-hiring-graphic-designer-web-banner-with-orange-armchair.avif)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Recruitment post · **Design style:** Corporate & Clean-Tech
**Tags:** hiring, recruitment, blue, cyan, orange, web-banner

## What it is
A wide blue web banner with cyan and white 'WE'RE HIRING GRAPHIC DESIGNER', a 'Responsibilities' list with chevrons on gradient bars, a smiling man with headphones on a laptop in an orange armchair at centre, and CV email, WhatsApp, a cyan 'Apply now' button and tagline on the right.

## What it means
Clear and balanced; a three-column banner gives role, person and action equal weight.

## Techniques
- Arrange a banner in three columns: role, person, action.
- Use an orange prop to pop against blue.
- Put list items on fading gradient bars.

## Best for
- Web hero banners
- Job ads
- LinkedIn covers

## Vocabulary
- **Three-column banner** — A wide layout split into left, centre and right.
- **Gradient bar** — A strip fading from colour to transparent.
