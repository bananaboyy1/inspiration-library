# Navy 'We Are Hiring! Graphic Designer' cover banner with a 3D blue megaphone and glass role list

![](../images/print-social-navy-hiring-graphic-designer-cover-with-3d-megaphone.avif)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Recruitment post · **Design style:** Glassmorphism
**Tags:** hiring, recruitment, navy, blue, megaphone, facebook-cover

## What it is
A wide navy Facebook cover with a frosted glass panel holding 'We Are HIRING! GRAPHIC Designer' in mixed weights and blue, a blue pill tagline, perks line, a black and blue 3D megaphone, and a second glass panel listing open positions in outlined bars, plus CV email, WhatsApp number and an 'Apply now' button.

## What it means
Loud but polished; the megaphone literally announces the roles.

## Techniques
- Use a 3D megaphone as the announcement symbol.
- Split content into two glass panels: pitch and roles.
- List roles in stacked outlined bars.
- Mix weights and colours in the headline.

## Best for
- Job postings
- Facebook covers
- Creative team hiring

## Vocabulary
- **Cover banner** — A wide header image for a social profile.
- **Glass panel** — A translucent frosted card.
