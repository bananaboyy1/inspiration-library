# Insight cards on black with blurred gradient orbs and a pale donut

![](../images/app-research-insight-cards-with-gradient-orbs.gif)

**Discipline:** app ·  · **Design style:** Glassmorphism
**Tags:** research, cards, dark-background, gradient-blur, donut, blue, orange, carousel

## What it is
A black slide with three large rounded cards in a horizontal carousel: a white card with a pale blue donut and a highlighted blue phrase, a cream card whose lower half glows orange, and a blue-gradient card peeking at the left. Small caps text says 'a collection of insights gathered from: User interviews'.

## What it means
Soft and reflective; blurred gradient orbs make qualitative research feel emotional.

## Techniques
- Show insights as a carousel of cards that bleed off the edges.
- Use a blurred coloured orb at the bottom of each card as a mood cue.
- Colour one phrase in each headline with the card's accent.
- Set cards on black so their colours are vivid.

## Best for
- User research summaries
- Insight and theme presentations
- Case-study interludes

## Vocabulary
- **Insight card** — A card stating one research finding.
- **Gradient orb** — A blurred blob of colour used as decoration.
- **Bleed-off carousel** — Cards cropped by the frame to imply more.
- **Accent phrase** — A highlighted phrase in a headline.
