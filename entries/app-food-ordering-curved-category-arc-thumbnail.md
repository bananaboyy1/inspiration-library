# Food Ordering App Thumbnail

![](../images/app-food-ordering-curved-category-arc-thumbnail.jpg)

**Discipline:** app · **Design type:** Device mockup scene
**Tags:** food, hands, thumbnail, arc, peach, cover, touch

## What it is
A small square cover of the same food ordering app in dark hands with a pizza slice card and category arc under a peach gradient.

## What it means
Food photography and a finger pointing to it read instantly even at small size.

## Techniques
- Tight crop on hands and screen
- Bright food photos as the colour pop
- Soft peach-to-grey background
- Arc of category thumbnails as a recognisable motif
- No text overlay

## Best for
- Portfolio grid thumbnails
- Food app teasers
- Quick-glance covers

## Vocabulary
- **Cover crop** — A crop chosen to fit a square gallery thumbnail.
- **Colour pop** — A saturated element against neutral surroundings.
- **Recognisable motif** — A repeated shape that identifies a product.
