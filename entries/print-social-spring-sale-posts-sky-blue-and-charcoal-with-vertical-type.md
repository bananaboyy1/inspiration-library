# Spring sale pack in sky blue and charcoal with vertical condensed titles and leaf sprigs

![](../images/print-social-spring-sale-posts-sky-blue-and-charcoal-with-vertical-type.avif)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Social template pack · **Design style:** Photographic & Cinematic
**Tags:** spring, sale, fashion, sky-blue, charcoal, condensed-type

## What it is
Four square posts with photos of a couple in denim against a sky-blue backdrop, white and charcoal panels with sky-blue condensed titles ('SPRING SALE', 'BEST DEALS') set vertically or horizontally, italic '/up to 50% off' notes and thin leaf-branch illustrations in corners.

## What it means
Cool and stylish; matching the type colour to the studio backdrop unifies photo and graphics.

## Techniques
- Match type colour to the photo backdrop.
- Rotate condensed titles to run vertically.
- Alternate light and dark panels across a set.
- Tuck fine leaf sprigs into corners.

## Best for
- Fashion retail
- Seasonal sales
- Denim and streetwear

## Vocabulary
- **Vertical type** — Text rotated to read bottom to top.
- **Colour echo** — Repeating a photo's colour in graphics.
