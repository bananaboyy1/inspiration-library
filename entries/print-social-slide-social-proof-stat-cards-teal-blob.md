# Minimal slide with social platform stat cards over a soft blob

![](../images/print-social-slide-social-proof-stat-cards-teal-blob.avif)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Presentation slides · **Design style:** Minimalist
**Tags:** presentation, slide, stat card, teal gradient, blob shape, minimal

## What it is
A clean presentation slide pairing a short headline and body copy on the left with three floating white stat cards (Facebook, YouTube, Twitter follower counts) laid over a soft teal organic blob shape.

## What it means
A single soft gradient blob gives an otherwise flat white slide a focal point and depth, letting small stat cards feel like they're 'floating' with purpose rather than just placed.

## Techniques
- use one large soft organic blob as a background anchor instead of a busy image
- float small white data cards partially over the blob edge for layered depth
- keep typography strictly left-aligned and minimal so the blob/cards carry the visual interest

## Best for
- pitch deck social proof slides
- minimal SaaS presentation templates
- marketing report title slides

## Vocabulary
- **blob shape** — an organic, irregularly rounded background shape used to add softness and focal interest
