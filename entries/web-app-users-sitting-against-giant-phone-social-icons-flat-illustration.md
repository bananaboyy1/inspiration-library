# Two flat-illustrated people sitting against a giant phone surrounded by colourful floating social and app icons

![](../images/web-app-users-sitting-against-giant-phone-social-icons-flat-illustration.jpg)

**Discipline:** web · **Design type:** Feature illustration · **Design style:** Playful & Illustrated
**Tags:** app, flat-illustration, social-icons, transparent, colourful, people

## What it is
A flat vector illustration of a man and woman sitting back-to-back against a giant smartphone showing an app grid, surrounded by a scattered cloud of colourful icon tiles (chat, camera, heart, gear, mail, share) on a transparent chequered background.

## What it means
Busy and energetic; a dense cloud of icons around a calm seated pose suggests an app that connects to everything at once.

## Techniques
- Surround a calm central scene with a dense cloud of small icons.
- Scale a device to giant size so figures can lean against it.
- Vary icon colours widely for a lively, connected feel.

## Best for
- App ecosystem marketing
- Social platform illustrations
- SaaS feature graphics

## Vocabulary
- **Icon cloud** — A loose scattered cluster of many small icons.
- **Transparent background asset** — An illustration with no background, ready to overlay anywhere.
