# Green gradient background with layered wavy ribbon shapes on the left edge

![](../images/web-green-wavy-layered-ribbon-background.avif)

**Discipline:** web · **Design type:** Background pattern · **Design style:** Gradient & Aurora
**Tags:** background, green, gradient, wave, ribbon, minimal

## What it is
A white background with three overlapping vertical wavy ribbon shapes hugging the left edge, shaded from dark to bright lime green, leaving the right two-thirds empty.

## What it means
Fresh and organic; layered waves in one hue feel natural and energetic while keeping most of the canvas free for content.

## Techniques
- Layer several wavy shapes in one colour family for depth.
- Anchor decorative shapes to one edge, leaving space free elsewhere.
- Vary shade lightness across layers to imply depth.

## Best for
- Presentation backgrounds
- Eco and wellness brand banners
- Slide templates

## Vocabulary
- **Wave shape** — A smooth undulating form used decoratively.
- **Colour family** — Several shades and tints of one base hue.
