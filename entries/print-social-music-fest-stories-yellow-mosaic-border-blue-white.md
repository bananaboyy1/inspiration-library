# 'Music Fest' story set with a colourful mosaic tile border framing blue-and-white event details

![](../images/print-social-music-fest-stories-yellow-mosaic-border-blue-white.avif)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Story set · **Design style:** Playful & Illustrated
**Tags:** music-festival, mosaic, yellow, blue, border, story

## What it is
Four vertical yellow story cards each framed by a colourful mosaic tile border (musical notes, vinyl records, abstract shapes in pink, blue, orange), with a blue diamond 'MUSIC FEST' badge, date, DJ photos and 'Book Now Your Tickets' details.

## What it means
Festive and rhythmic; the mosaic border itself feels musical, like tiles arranged to a beat.

## Techniques
- Frame content with a dense mosaic tile pattern border.
- Rotate a diamond badge as the central event mark.
- Keep the centre content area simple against a busy border.

## Best for
- Music festival promotion
- Event ticket marketing
- Colourful border-framed content

## Vocabulary
- **Mosaic border** — A frame made of many small repeating pattern tiles.
- **Diamond badge** — A rotated square used as a label shape.
