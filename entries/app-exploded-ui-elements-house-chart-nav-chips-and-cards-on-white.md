# Exploded Assistant UI on White

![](../images/app-exploded-ui-elements-house-chart-nav-chips-and-cards-on-white.png)

**Discipline:** app · **Design type:** UI components
**Tags:** exploded-ui, assistant, house-chart, chips, nav, cards, white, gradient

## What it is
A white board with a central assistant card ('How can I help you today?' with chips and a gradient arc) surrounded by floating parts: a house-shaped chart of coloured blocks with percentages, a pill nav bar with a blue Home tab, category cards with letter tiles, toggle pills, insight rows with round coloured icons and letter circles.

## What it means
Pulling components apart and floating them shows the design system's pieces and its playful colour palette.

## Techniques
- House-shaped block chart as a signature graphic
- Components floated around a central card
- Letter circles as avatars
- Round coloured icons in insight rows
- Warm palette of coral, yellow, blue and navy

## Best for
- Design-system component showcases
- Family and home apps
- Exploded UI presentations

## Vocabulary
- **Exploded UI** — Interface parts separated and arranged around a hero.
- **Block chart** — A chart made of blocks whose areas show shares.
- **Toggle pill** — A coloured pill with a close or switch control.
