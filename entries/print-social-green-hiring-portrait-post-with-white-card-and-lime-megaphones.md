# Green 'We Are Hiring!' portrait post with a white rounded card and two lime megaphones

![](../images/print-social-green-hiring-portrait-post-with-white-card-and-lime-megaphones.avif)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Recruitment post · **Design style:** Corporate & Clean-Tech
**Tags:** hiring, recruitment, green, lime, megaphone, portrait

## What it is
A portrait green post with a white rounded card holding a dark green headline panel ('We Are Hiring! Join Our CREATIVE & Professional Team!' with lime 'CREATIVE'), a lime 'Open Positions' speech tab over green role bars, CV email, and two black-and-lime megaphones poking out of the card edges, with 'Apply now' and WhatsApp in the footer.

## What it means
Fresh and upbeat; megaphones bursting out of the card add energy.

## Techniques
- Let 3D props break out of the card edges.
- Nest a dark headline panel inside a white card.
- Keep one green family with a lime accent.

## Best for
- Instagram hiring posts
- Creative agency careers
- Job fairs

## Vocabulary
- **Break-out prop** — An object overlapping a container's edge.
- **Nested panel** — A panel placed inside another.
