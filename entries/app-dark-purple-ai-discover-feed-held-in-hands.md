# Dark Purple AI Discover Feed in Hands

![](../images/app-dark-purple-ai-discover-feed-held-in-hands.png)

**Discipline:** app ·  · **Design style:** Gradient & Aurora
**Tags:** dark, purple, ai, discover, hands, glow, cover

## What it is
A deep purple gradient cover with two hands holding a phone showing a dark Discover feed with category chips and a glowing ChatGPT card, an App Design pill top left and a logo top right.

## What it means
Purple glow on dark feels futuristic, and hands make it human.

## Techniques
- Purple gradient with subtle diagonal lines
- Glowing card inside a dark UI
- Hands from both sides holding the phone
- Frosted pill labels in the corners
- Category chips at the top of the feed

## Best for
- AI news and discovery apps
- Dark purple covers
- Feed UI presentations

## Vocabulary
- **Discover feed** — A browsing screen of recommended content.
- **Glow card** — A card with a luminous effect around its artwork.
- **Frosted pill** — A blurred translucent rounded label.
