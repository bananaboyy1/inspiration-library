# 'Our Creativity Has No Limits' poster with looping organic shapes and a numbered blue-pink service list

![](../images/print-social-creative-design-no-limits-poster-blue-pink-organic-shapes.avif)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Poster · **Design style:** Playful & Illustrated
**Tags:** agency, blue, pink, organic-shapes, numbered-list, poster

## What it is
A white A4 poster with looping blue-and-pink organic ribbon shapes in the corners, a circular 'Design and Digital Creativity' badge, bold blue-and-navy headline 'Creative Design — Our Creativity Has No Limits', body copy, social handles, three numbered service bars (Big Ideas, Projects, Execution) in orange, blue and pink, and a photo of a woman in a sun hat.

## What it means
Friendly and organised; looping ribbon shapes soften a structured numbered list into something more playful.

## Techniques
- Frame corners with looping organic ribbon shapes.
- Colour-code a numbered service list for quick scanning.
- Add a circular rotating-text badge as a logo device.

## Best for
- Creative agency posters
- Service overview flyers
- Playful brand systems

## Vocabulary
- **Organic ribbon shape** — A smooth looping form without hard edges.
- **Rotating-text badge** — A circular logo with text following the curve.
