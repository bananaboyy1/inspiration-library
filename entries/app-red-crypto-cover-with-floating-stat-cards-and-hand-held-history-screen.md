# Red Crypto Cover with Floating Stat Cards

![](../images/app-red-crypto-cover-with-floating-stat-cards-and-hand-held-history-screen.png)

**Discipline:** app · **Design type:** Device mockup scene
**Tags:** crypto, red, black, hand, stat-cards, striped, cover

## What it is
A red-to-black vignette cover with vertical stripe chart shapes, a hand holding a tilted phone with a transactions history screen, two floating stat cards (Realized PL in white, Projected Growth in red), a two-logo lockup, a project pill and a large headline 'A Smarter Way to Trade & Swap Digital Assets'.

## What it means
Floating stat cards break the screen's boundary to highlight key numbers, and red on black is intense and confident.

## Techniques
- Floating stat cards outside the phone
- Striped chart shapes as background
- Headline with alternating bright and dim words
- Two-logo lockup in white tiles
- Hand silhouetted in red light

## Best for
- Crypto and trading app covers
- Feature-highlight hero images
- Dark red brand systems

## Vocabulary
- **Floating stat card** — A metric card placed outside the device to emphasise a number.
- **Stripe chart** — A chart made of vertical thin lines.
- **Lockup tile** — Logos placed in rounded white squares.
