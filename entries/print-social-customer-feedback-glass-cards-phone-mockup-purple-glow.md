# Customer feedback shown as frosted-glass testimonial cards inside a phone mockup on a purple glow

![](../images/print-social-customer-feedback-glass-cards-phone-mockup-purple-glow.avif)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Social promo post · **Design style:** Glassmorphism
**Tags:** testimonial, feedback, purple-glow, glassmorphism, phone-mockup, instagram

## What it is
A phone outline mockup on a dark purple radial glow, with two overlapping frosted-glass testimonial cards each showing a circular customer photo, name, five-star rating and a short quote, plus standard Instagram action icons below.

## What it means
Trustworthy and modern; presenting reviews as glass cards inside a phone frame makes social proof feel like a live, native app experience.

## Techniques
- Overlap two testimonial cards for a layered, dynamic feel.
- Frame testimonials inside a phone outline for an in-app feel.
- Glow the background radially to draw focus to the centre cards.

## Best for
- Customer testimonial posts
- Review and social proof marketing
- Glassmorphism UI branding

## Vocabulary
- **Testimonial card** — A card featuring a customer's photo, rating and quote.
- **Radial glow** — Light fading outward from a central point.
