# Dark analytics dashboard on a monitor with orange highlights, arc bars and donut charts

![](../images/app-dark-analytics-dashboard-on-monitor-with-orange-accents.jpg)

**Discipline:** app · **Design type:** Dashboard
**Tags:** dashboard, dark, orange, monitor-mockup, charts, tiles, analytics, red-glow

## What it is
A monitor showing a black dashboard with a left menu, greeting, a big orange tile, stat cards, arch-shaped bar charts, donut rings and a ranked product list with orange and grey accents, against a red-black gradient.

## What it means
Bold and technical; orange on black feels like a performance console.

## Techniques
- Use one orange for key tiles.
- Draw bars as arches.
- Keep secondary charts grey and white.
- Stage the monitor on a red glow.

## Best for
- Analytics and admin dashboards
- Dark SaaS panels
- Orange-accent brands

## Vocabulary
- **Arch bars** — Bars with rounded tops shaped like arches.
- **Accent tile** — One tile in the accent colour.
- **Donut ring** — A ring chart.
- **Monitor mockup** — A desktop screen in a scene.
