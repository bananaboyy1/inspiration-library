# Blue, Orange and Yellow Brand Kit Tiles

![](../images/branding-blue-orange-yellow-brand-kit-tiles-with-atom-heart-mark.png)

**Discipline:** branding ·  · **Design style:** Geometric & Bauhaus
**Tags:** brand-kit, blue, orange, yellow, palette, typeface, logo, bento

## What it is
A bento board of tiles: blue stripe ramp, warm gradient, a button, a typeface card 'Roobert', a navy tile with big AaBbCc glyphs, a warm-gradient tile with a white orbit-heart symbol and six vertical swatches with rotated hex codes.

## What it means
Blue against orange and yellow feels human and optimistic, and the bento shows the whole system at a glance.

## Techniques
- Bento tiles of different sizes
- Vertical swatch strip with rotated hex labels
- Blue stripe ramp as a tonal scale
- Warm mesh gradient behind the mark
- One large glyph tile to show the type

## Best for
- Brand kit overviews
- Friendly tech identities
- Colour, type and logo in one board

## Vocabulary
- **Tonal ramp** — Stripes of one hue from dark to light.
- **Glyph tile** — A tile showing letters, numerals and symbols.
- **Rotated label** — Text set vertically along a swatch.
