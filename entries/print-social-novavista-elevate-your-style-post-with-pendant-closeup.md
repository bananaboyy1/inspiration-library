# Cream 'Elevate your style, simplify your life.' post with a green pendant close-up in a ruled grid

![](../images/print-social-novavista-elevate-your-style-post-with-pendant-closeup.avif)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Social promo post · **Design style:** Minimalist
**Tags:** fashion, jewellery, cream, grid, minimal, shop-now

## What it is
A square cream post with a top row of ruled cells (arrow icon, 'Shop now', URL), a centred black uppercase headline, a wide close-up photo of a green drop pendant on a cream turtleneck, and a footer with a line of copy and a bold 'N' logo.

## What it means
Calm and luxurious; one detail shot and lots of cream space feel curated.

## Techniques
- Build header and footer as ruled table rows.
- Let one detail photo carry the post.
- Centre a two-line uppercase headline.

## Best for
- Jewellery promos
- Minimal fashion ads
- Boutique brands

## Vocabulary
- **Detail shot** — A close-up of one product feature.
- **Table layout** — A design structured by cells and lines.
