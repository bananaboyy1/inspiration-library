# Blue and yellow B2B ad carousel with flowchart diagrams, floating pills and white cards

![](../images/print-social-b2b-ad-cards-with-flowchart-and-yellow-pills.webp)

**Discipline:** print-social ·  · **Design style:** Corporate & Clean-Tech
**Tags:** b2b, ads, blue, yellow, flowchart, white-cards, carousel, enterprise

## What it is
A blue-and-yellow presentation of square ads: a mock social post beside a large light-blue card with bold headline 'competitive advantage', and five white cards each with a small tag pill, a bold-keyword headline and a diagram (node flowchart, impact map, prompt box, alert) with a yellow CTA pill and a black results card '10x 7x'.

## What it means
Clear and helpful; diagrams show the product rather than tell.

## Techniques
- Show product diagrams inside ad cards.
- Use tag pills to label each step.
- Bold keywords in headlines.
- End with a dark stat card.

## Best for
- B2B and SaaS ad carousels
- Explaining complex products
- Blue-yellow ad systems

## Vocabulary
- **Diagram card** — An ad card built around a product diagram.
- **Tag pill** — A small label naming a step.
- **Stat card** — A dark card with big numbers.
- **Swipe CTA** — A pill inviting the viewer to swipe.
