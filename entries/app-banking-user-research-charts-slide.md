# Banking User Research Charts

![](../images/app-banking-user-research-charts-slide.jpg)

**Discipline:** app · **Design type:** Research insight slide
**Tags:** research, charts, survey, grey, gauge, bars, red-green

## What it is
A tall grey slide with three survey questions each above a white card chart: a set of pink bars with percentages, a semicircular gauge in green, red and grey, and a rising staircase distribution, closed by a quote.

## What it means
Rounded white cards with muted red and green keep research light and readable. Each question is paired with the chart form that suits it.

## Techniques
- Vary chart type per question (bars, gauge, stair steps)
- Pale gradient bars instead of harsh fills
- Question sits above its chart in lighter type
- Cards staggered left and right for rhythm
- Pull-quote at the end humanising the data

## Best for
- UX research summary slides
- Survey results in case studies
- Fintech product storytelling

## Vocabulary
- **Gauge chart** — A semicircular chart showing a value in a range.
- **Stair chart** — Bars arranged as a descending or ascending staircase.
- **Pull-quote** — A highlighted user statement.
