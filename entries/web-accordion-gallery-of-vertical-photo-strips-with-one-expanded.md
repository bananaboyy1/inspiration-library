# Photo accordion: thin rounded vertical strips of nature photos with one wide expanded mountain photo in the middle

![](../images/web-accordion-gallery-of-vertical-photo-strips-with-one-expanded.webp)

**Discipline:** web · **Design type:** UI components
**Tags:** component, gallery, accordion, photo-strips, rounded, white, expanding, nature

## What it is
A white frame with a horizontal row of narrow pill-shaped photo strips (sky and tree, red sail, waterfall, blue flower, building, yellow leaves, sunset) and one wide rounded photo of green mountains and cloudy sky expanded near the left.

## What it means
Playful and immersive; strips reveal to a full photo on hover.

## Techniques
- Show photos as narrow pills.
- Expand the active one horizontally.
- Keep spacing even.
- Use a white background.

## Best for
- Image galleries
- Hover-expanding accordions
- Photography showcases

## Vocabulary
- **Expanding strips** — Narrow items that widen when active.
- **Pill photo** — A photo cropped to a tall capsule.
- **Focus item** — The single item shown at full size.
- **Horizontal accordion** — An accordion that expands sideways.
