# 'Mass Media Infographic' with an orange city-skyline header and a history-of-media bar chart

**Tags:** infographic, mass-media, orange, skyline, bar-chart, flat-icons

## What it is
A white infographic with a bold orange skyline header reading 'Mass Media Infographic', percentage stats (19%, 22%, 27%, 32%) beside flat icons (radio, TV, phone, globe), a bar chart titled 'History of Mass Media' comparing paper, TV/radio and internet from 1900-2015, and icon rows for SMS, News, Cinema, Social Media with two hands holding phones.

## What it means
Historical and structured; combining a timeline chart with modern icons shows media evolution in one connected story.

## Techniques
- Use a skyline silhouette as a thematic header graphic.
- Pair a historical bar chart with modern channel icons.
- Show hands holding devices to ground abstract stats in real use.

## Best for
- Media industry reports
- History and technology infographics
- Educational presentation graphics
