# Catering Instagram pack with a warm taupe icon-grid header bar and rounded photo cards

![](../images/print-social-catering-posts-warm-taupe-icon-grid-header-bar.avif)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Social template pack · **Design style:** Warm & Organic
**Tags:** catering, food, taupe, icon-grid, header-bar, instagram

## What it is
Nine warm taupe square posts each topped by a four-icon coloured header bar (cloche, cutlery, coffee cup, wine glass in red, cream, green and brown), with rounded food photos, serif headlines ('Tasty treats for the cozy season', 'We do, deliciously'), small logo badges and 'Discount 30% off' tags.

## What it means
Warm and appetising; the repeating icon header bar acts like a menu category strip, tying every post to the catering theme.

## Techniques
- Open every post with the same coloured icon-grid header.
- Round photo corners softly for a warm, inviting feel.
- Rotate a small side tag for visual variety across a set.

## Best for
- Catering and event food services
- Restaurant social content
- Warm, cosy food branding

## Vocabulary
- **Icon-grid header** — A row of small coloured icon tiles used as a consistent top banner.
- **Rounded photo card** — An image with softened corners set inside a card.
