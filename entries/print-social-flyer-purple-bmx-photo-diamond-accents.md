# Purple BMX flyer with gradient diamond accents

![](../images/print-social-flyer-purple-bmx-photo-diamond-accents.avif)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Social ad · **Design style:** Dark & Neon
**Tags:** flyer, bmx, sport, purple, gradient, diamond shape

## What it is
A square flyer for a BMX club on a purple background, combining a gradient-bordered action photo with pink-purple gradient diamond and dotted-line decorative shapes.

## What it means
A gradient-outlined photo frame and matching gradient decorative shapes tie a real action photo into an otherwise flat vector-graphic layout.

## Techniques
- outline a photo with a gradient border to bridge photographic and flat vector elements
- scatter gradient-filled geometric shapes (diamonds) as texture rather than plain flat color
- angle the photo frame slightly for energy appropriate to an action-sport subject

## Best for
- sports club/event promotion
- youth/extreme-sport marketing
- dark vibrant social ads

## Vocabulary
- **gradient border** — a frame or outline stroke filled with a color gradient rather than a single flat color
