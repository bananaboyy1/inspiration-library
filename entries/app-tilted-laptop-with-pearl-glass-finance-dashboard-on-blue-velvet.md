# Pearl frosted banking dashboard on a laptop tilted low over a blue ribbed velvet surface

**Discipline:** app · **Design type:** Device mockup scene
**Tags:** dashboard, fintech, pearl, glass, laptop, blue-velvet, light-grey, card

## What it is
A dark laptop in perspective against a pale blue-grey wall showing a light frosted dashboard with an available balance, quick actions, revenue and expense tiles, an activity chart, transactions and upcoming payments lists, a send-money panel with a Continue button and a gradient credit card, resting on ribbed blue velvet.

## What it means
Calm and premium; soft glass and a tactile velvet make finance feel gentle.

## Techniques
- Tilt the laptop low in perspective.
- Use frosted glass tiles on a pale blue base.
- Rest the device on a tactile fabric.
- Set numerals in a light sans.

- Fintech dashboard showcases

- Fabric-stage mockups
