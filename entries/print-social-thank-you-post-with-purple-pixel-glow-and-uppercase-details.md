# Design resource post with big Thank You headline over a glowing violet pixel gradient

![](../images/print-social-thank-you-post-with-purple-pixel-glow-and-uppercase-details.jpg)

**Discipline:** print-social ·  · **Design style:** Retro & Pixel
**Tags:** thank-you, pixel-gradient, violet, white, sans-serif, resource-post, grid, save-for-later

## What it is
A pale grid-lined portrait with 'Thank You!' in heavy black and violet, a small follow prompt, and a soft violet-to-white pixelated gradient across the lower half with a small button, resource title lines and tiny corner text ending with a save icon.

## What it means
Clean and modern; the pixel glow gives a light post digital depth.

## Techniques
- Colour 'You!' in violet to create a two-tone title.
- Use a pixelated gradient block as the visual.
- Keep tiny caps details in the corners.
- Add save-for-later cue at the bottom.

## Best for
- Design resource and newsletter posts
- Closing slides of carousels
- Minimal light social sets

## Vocabulary
- **Pixel gradient** — A colour fade made from square tiles.
- **Save cue** — A prompt with a bookmark icon.
- **Two-tone headline** — A heading in two colours.
- **Corner details** — Small text set in the four corners.
