# White 'New Arrival Fashion Sale 50% Off' post with an orange blob-cropped portrait

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Social ad · **Design style:** Minimalist
**Tags:** fashion, sale, orange, white, blob, ad

## What it is
A square white post with an orange script 'New Arrival', grey 'Fashion' and orange 'Sale' with '50% OFF', an orange 'Shop now' button, dot rows, contact icons, and a moody orange-lit portrait of a woman cropped into an organic blob.

## What it means
Clean and bold; the blob crop and orange lighting feel editorial yet simple.

## Techniques
- Crop a portrait into an organic blob.
- Match the photo's lighting to the accent colour.
- Separate sections with dot rows.

## Best for
- Fashion sales
- Beauty promos
- Minimal retail ads

## Vocabulary
- **Organic shape** — An irregular rounded form.
- **Script accent** — A handwritten-style line above a headline.
