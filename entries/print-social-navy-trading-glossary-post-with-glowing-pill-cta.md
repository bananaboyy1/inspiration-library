# Navy '6 Words Every Trader Should Know' post with cyan highlight and glowing tilted pill

![](../images/print-social-navy-trading-glossary-post-with-glowing-pill-cta.avif)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Social educational post · **Design style:** Dark & Neon
**Tags:** trading, finance, navy, cyan, glow, typography, educational

## What it is
A dark navy portrait post with a huge bold uppercase headline ('6 WORDS EVERY TRADER SHOULD KNOW.') with the number and 'TRADER' in cyan, a slightly tilted pill 'Here's what they mean' with a glowing cyan orb, a 'Check full story' button and a contact footer bar.

## What it means
Big type and a single glow feel confident and click-worthy, framing finance education as a hook.

## Techniques
- Colour the number and keyword in one accent within a white headline.
- Tilt a pill slightly to add motion.
- Add a single glowing orb as the light source.
- Anchor contacts in a solid footer bar.

## Best for
- Finance education carousels
- Trading brand content
- Listicle hook posts

## Vocabulary
- **Hook headline** — A headline built to stop the scroll with a number or promise.
- **Glow orb** — A blurred light spot used as an accent.
