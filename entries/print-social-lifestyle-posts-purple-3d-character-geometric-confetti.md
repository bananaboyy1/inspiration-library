# 'Lifestyle — A New Way of Living' pack in purple with a 3D cartoon character and geometric confetti bursts

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Social template pack · **Design style:** 3D & Surreal
**Tags:** lifestyle, purple, 3d-character, geometric-confetti, instagram

## What it is
Nine deep-purple square posts with a recurring glossy 3D cartoon woman in casual clothes shown relaxing, exercising and using devices, surrounded by colourful geometric confetti bursts (triangles, circles, bars), and headlines ('Time to Relax', 'Just Chilling, Enjoy Every Moment', 'Ascend, Fly Like a Rocket').

## What it means
Playful and premium; a consistent 3D character across many scenarios builds a relatable, ownable brand mascot for a lifestyle brand.

## Techniques
- Reuse one 3D character in different poses across a whole post set.
- Surround the character with geometric confetti bursts for energy.
- Keep a single saturated background colour for series cohesion.

## Best for
- Lifestyle and wellness brands
- 3D character mascots
- Cohesive Instagram grids

## Vocabulary
- **3D character mascot** — A consistent rendered character used across branded content.
- **Confetti burst** — A cluster of small shapes suggesting celebration or energy.
