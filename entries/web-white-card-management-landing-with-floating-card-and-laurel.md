# Card Management Hero with Floating Card

![](../images/web-white-card-management-landing-with-floating-card-and-laurel.jpg)

**Discipline:** web · **Design type:** Landing page hero
**Tags:** card, hero, white, navy, serif-italic, laurel, nav-rail, minimal

## What it is
A white hero with a two-tone headline where 'Card Management.' is in bold italic serif, a subline, a vertical icon rail with a Home pill on the left, a tilted gradient Visa card floating with a soft shadow, a floating caption, a #1 laurel and project metadata at the bottom.

## What it means
A single floating card on white feels elegant, and small labels and rail add a sense of a real product.

## Techniques
- Italic serif emphasis inside a light sans headline
- Single tilted 3D object as the hero
- Vertical icon rail with a highlighted item
- Laurel with rating for trust
- Metadata row of project details at the base

## Best for
- Card and payment product pages
- Minimal fintech portfolio heroes
- Object-led hero sections

## Vocabulary
- **Hero object** — One object rendered large as the focus of a hero.
- **Icon rail** — A vertical strip of navigation icons.
- **Project metadata** — Labels listing name, category, designer and date.
