# AI Support App Extra-Long Case Study

![](../images/app-harbor-ai-support-app-extra-long-case-study.jpg)

**Discipline:** app ·  · **Design style:** Minimalist
**Tags:** long-scroll, case-study, white, ghost-type, wireframes, flowchart, app-icon

## What it is
An extremely tall white case-study page for a support-agent app: ghost 'Harbor' lettering above a phone lock screen, project overview, app-icon closeups, a challenge section with red problem cards, wireframes, solution phones, a happy-path flow, a strategy plan and categorised work queues.

## What it means
A long, calm sequence of alternating text and device images tells a full design story at an editorial pace.

## Techniques
- Giant ghost brand name behind the hero device
- Section labels in tiny caps with large statements beside
- Red-outlined problem cards connected by dotted lines
- Wireframes shown before final UI to show process
- Dark gloved hand holding the phone for contrast

## Best for
- Full-process app case studies
- AI and enterprise tools
- Behance-style long pages

## Vocabulary
- **Problem card** — A card stating a specific issue the design solves.
- **Happy path** — The ideal sequence a user follows to complete a task.
- **Section label** — A tiny caption naming the stage of a case study.
