# Orange and blue 'Grow Your Business!' ad with a cross-legged man on a laptop

![](../images/print-social-orange-and-blue-grow-your-business-ad-with-seated-man.avif)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Social ad · **Design style:** Corporate & Clean-Tech
**Tags:** agency, orange, blue, cutout, wave-edge, ad

## What it is
A square ad split diagonally into orange and blue, a man in shirt and tie sitting cross-legged with a laptop, a white 'Grow Your' and blue outlined 'BUSINESS!' headline, a tilted 'Have an idea or project?' tag, Memphis dot patterns and a white wavy bottom edge.

## What it means
Bright and approachable; complementary colours and a relaxed pose feel upbeat.

## Techniques
- Split the background diagonally in complementary colours.
- Give the headline a stroked 3D effect.
- Add a wavy edge to seat the cut-out.
- Scatter dot grids for texture.

## Best for
- Small business promos
- Agency ads
- Bright service flyers

## Vocabulary
- **Complementary colours** — Hues opposite each other on the colour wheel.
- **Memphis pattern** — Playful dots, zigzags and shapes from 1980s design.
