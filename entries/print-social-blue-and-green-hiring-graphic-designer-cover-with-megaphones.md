# 'We Are HIRING! GRAPHIC Designer' cover with blue and green type, two megaphones and a two-column role panel

![](../images/print-social-blue-and-green-hiring-graphic-designer-cover-with-megaphones.avif)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Recruitment post · **Design style:** Corporate & Clean-Tech
**Tags:** hiring, recruitment, blue, green, megaphone, facebook-cover

## What it is
A wide light-grey Facebook cover with a blurred office, a headline with navy 'HIRING!' and green 'GRAPHIC', two black-and-blue megaphones, and a deep blue rounded panel with a green 'Open Positions' speech tab over a two-column grid of white role pills, plus CV email, WhatsApp and address.

## What it means
Busy but organised; the two-column grid fits many roles in a banner.

## Techniques
- Arrange many roles in a two-column pill grid.
- Use a speech-tab label on the panel.
- Pair two accent colours in the headline.

## Best for
- Multi-role hiring
- Agency careers
- Facebook covers

## Vocabulary
- **Speech tab** — A label with a small pointer like a speech bubble.
- **Pill grid** — Rounded labels arranged in columns.
