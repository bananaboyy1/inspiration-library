# 'Digital Leaders' conference schedule with cyan and white agenda rows and teal glass spheres

![](../images/print-social-digital-leaders-schedule-with-cyan-and-white-agenda-rows.avif)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Event poster · **Design style:** Dark & Neon
**Tags:** conference, schedule, dark, cyan, agenda, spheres

## What it is
A portrait dark teal poster with 'DIGITAL' white and 'LEADERS' cyan, a rounded glass panel titled 'Schedule' with time pills and session rows — cyan for breaks, white for keynotes with speaker and talk — a host credit, sponsor boxes and glowing teal spheres and rings at the corners.

## What it means
Professional and techy; colour-coded rows make a busy agenda easy to scan.

## Techniques
- Colour-code rows by type (break vs talk).
- Pair each row with a separate time pill.
- Frame the agenda in a glass panel.
- Place glowing spheres at corners for depth.

## Best for
- Tech conferences
- Leadership summits
- Event agendas

## Vocabulary
- **Agenda row** — A line in a schedule with time and session.
- **Colour coding** — Using colour to categorise.
