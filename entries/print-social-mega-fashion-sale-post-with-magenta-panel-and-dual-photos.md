# 'Mega Fashion Sale' post with a magenta-to-purple panel and two rounded photos in yellow

![](../images/print-social-mega-fashion-sale-post-with-magenta-panel-and-dual-photos.avif)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Social ad · **Design style:** Gradient & Aurora
**Tags:** fashion, sale, magenta, yellow, gradient, ad

## What it is
A square white post with a magenta-to-purple gradient panel holding a script 'Mega', 'FASHION' and yellow 'SALE', a yellow 'Shop now' button, and two overlapping rounded photos of a man in a yellow jacket framed by a tilted yellow shape.

## What it means
Vibrant and energetic; the yellow jacket and frame tie product to brand colour.

## Techniques
- Pair a small and large photo of the same model.
- Frame photos with a tilted accent shape.
- Use a gradient panel for text.

## Best for
- Fashion sales
- Streetwear promos
- Retail ads

## Vocabulary
- **Photo pair** — Two photos of one subject at different scales.
- **Accent frame** — A coloured shape behind a photo.
