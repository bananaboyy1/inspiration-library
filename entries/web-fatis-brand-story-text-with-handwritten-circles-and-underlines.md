# Fati's brand story line with handwritten words, a hand-drawn circle and underline in pink

![](../images/web-fatis-brand-story-text-with-handwritten-circles-and-underlines.gif)

**Discipline:** web · **Design type:** Page section · **Design style:** Playful & Illustrated
**Tags:** copywriting, handwritten, pink, annotation, brand-voice, animation

## What it is
A centred animated text block on off-white: 'we are a Happy Crew of Flavors Creators, sweet thinkers and donut dreamers not to brag' mixing pink bold sans with pink handwriting, with 'Happy Crew' circled and 'donut dreamers' underlined by hand.

## What it means
Warm and cheeky; the hand annotations make the copy sound spoken rather than written.

## Techniques
- Mix bold sans and handwriting in one sentence.
- Circle and underline key words by hand.
- Centre a short manifesto with lots of space.

## Best for
- About pages
- Brand voice sections
- Playful copy

## Vocabulary
- **Annotation** — Hand-drawn marks emphasising words.
- **Manifesto** — A short statement of a brand's attitude.
