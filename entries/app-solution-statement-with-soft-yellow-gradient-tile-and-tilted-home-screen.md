# Solution statement card with a soft yellow gradient tile and a tilted dark home screen

![](../images/app-solution-statement-with-soft-yellow-gradient-tile-and-tilted-home-screen.png)

**Discipline:** app · **Design type:** Case study slide
**Tags:** case-study, solution, warm-grey, yellow, gradient, app-icon, tilted-phone, rounded

## What it is
A warm grey rounded panel with a small caps label and a large sans statement about an assistant balancing chores and rest, a small side paragraph, and two tiles below: an orange-yellow soft gradient and a black phone corner showing the app icon among Photos, Mail and Notes.

## What it means
Warm and trustworthy; the sunny gradient stands for calm while the icon proves it exists.

## Techniques
- Pair a big statement with a small right-aligned explainer.
- Show a pure gradient tile as mood.
- Crop the phone home screen to show the icon in context.
- Use warm grey panels.

## Best for
- Solution introduction slides
- App identity in context
- Soft, warm presentations

## Vocabulary
- **Mood tile** — A gradient tile conveying feeling.
- **Icon in context** — An app icon among real icons.
- **Side explainer** — A small paragraph at the right of a big statement.
- **Warm grey** — A greige neutral.
