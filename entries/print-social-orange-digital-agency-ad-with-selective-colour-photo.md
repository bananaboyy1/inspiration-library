# White and orange agency ad with a greyscale man in an orange shirt and glossy 3D icons

![](../images/print-social-orange-digital-agency-ad-with-selective-colour-photo.avif)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Social ad · **Design style:** Corporate & Clean-Tech
**Tags:** agency, orange, selective-colour, 3d-icons, outline-type, ad

## What it is
A square ad with a grinning man in glasses whose photo is greyscale except his orange shirt, orange 3D thumbs-up and like spheres, a headline mixing solid orange 'DIGITAL', outlined 'MARKETING' and black 'AGENCY', a services list and a slanted orange footer.

## What it means
Loud and cheeky; selective colour ties the person directly to the brand hue.

## Techniques
- Keep only brand-coloured items in colour.
- Mix solid and outline type in one headline.
- Float glossy 3D icons as social signals.
- Slant the footer band for motion.

## Best for
- Agency ads
- Social media marketing services
- High-contrast promos

## Vocabulary
- **Selective colour** — A mostly greyscale photo with one colour kept.
- **Outline type** — Letters drawn as strokes only.
