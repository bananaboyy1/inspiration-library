# 'Creative Business Agency' story mockup: a black sticker-collage flyer laid on wrinkled yellow fabric

![](../images/print-social-creative-business-agency-story-sticker-collage-yellow-fabric.avif)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Social ad · **Design style:** Retro & Pixel
**Tags:** agency, sticker-collage, black, yellow, mockup, typography

## What it is
A vertical black flyer with a sticker-collage of mismatched fonts and shapes ('CREATIVE', 'BUSINES', 'AGENCY' in a blue box), an eye icon, a numbered services list, sticky-note style tags ('Our Services', 'Your Place'), and 'BOOK NOW', all photographed lying on crumpled yellow fabric.

## What it means
Playful and tactile; deliberately clashing type and a real fabric photo mockup give a corporate flyer street-poster energy.

## Techniques
- Mix multiple unrelated typefaces and colours as a deliberate sticker style.
- Photograph the final design on a textured real surface for presentation.
- Tag key info as if stuck on with individual sticky notes.

## Best for
- Creative agency promotion
- Sticker-style branding
- Textured flyer mockups

## Vocabulary
- **Sticker collage** — A layout mimicking overlapping stuck-on stickers of mixed styles.
- **Fabric mockup** — A design photographed on real cloth for a tactile presentation.
