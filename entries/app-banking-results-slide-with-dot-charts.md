# Banking Results Slide with Dot Charts

![](../images/app-banking-results-slide-with-dot-charts.jpg)

**Discipline:** app · **Design type:** Research insight slide
**Tags:** results, metrics, grey, dot-chart, gauge, testimonials, minimal

## What it is
A light grey results slide with a large title, three pill-shaped feedback statements with avatars, and three small cards: a dotted bar chart, a semicircle gauge (86%) and a line-bar chart (98%).

## What it means
Tiny dot and line chart textures make numbers feel crafted, and pill quotes keep human voices present.

## Techniques
- Dotted-matrix bars for a tactile data texture
- Pill-shaped feedback lines with avatar
- Dark pill labels floating above chart peaks
- Cards placed at staggered heights bottom right
- Large empty space around a small cluster of content

## Best for
- Case study results slides
- Usability metric summaries
- Fintech and product portfolios

## Vocabulary
- **Dot-matrix chart** — A chart built from small dots instead of solid bars.
- **Feedback pill** — A rounded strip holding a user statement and avatar.
- **Value tag** — A small label pinned to the peak of a chart.
