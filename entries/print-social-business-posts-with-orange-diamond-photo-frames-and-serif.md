# Pale business pack with circle portraits inside orange diamonds, stair motifs and condensed serif

![](../images/print-social-business-posts-with-orange-diamond-photo-frames-and-serif.avif)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Social template pack · **Design style:** Geometric & Bauhaus
**Tags:** business, orange, teal, diamond, geometric, serif, instagram

## What it is
Five pale grey-green square posts: circular black-and-white portraits set inside orange rotated squares, small mustard staircase shapes, half-circles and dot rows, and tall condensed serif headlines in teal ('See The World With Business.', 'Grow Your Business').

## What it means
Refined and optimistic; geometry and the stair motif signal growth without clichéd charts.

## Techniques
- Nest a circle photo in a rotated square.
- Use a small staircase shape as a growth symbol.
- Pair a condensed serif with sparse geometric confetti.
- Keep photos greyscale so one accent colour dominates.

## Best for
- Consultancy social posts
- Business coaching brands
- Professional services

## Vocabulary
- **Rotated square** — A square turned 45° into a diamond.
- **Condensed serif** — A narrow serif typeface for tall headlines.
