# Yellow 'Professional Solution for Your Business' ad with a thinking man cut-out and doodle bulb

![](../images/print-social-yellow-business-solution-ad-with-thinking-man-cutout.avif)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Social ad · **Design style:** Corporate & Clean-Tech
**Tags:** business, yellow, ad, cutout, condensed-type, doodle, agency

## What it is
A square yellow ad with a cut-out man in shirt and tie, hand on chin, a hand-drawn lightbulb above him, condensed white uppercase 'SOLUTION BUSINESS' with dark label boxes, chevrons, a hand-drawn arrow, phone number and 'Contact us' button.

## What it means
Energetic and direct; the thinking pose and doodle bulb sell ideas with a human face.

## Techniques
- Cut out a posed person and let them overlap a curved shape.
- Add a hand-drawn doodle that literalises the idea.
- Mix condensed display type with dark label boxes.
- Use chevron rows to point towards the CTA.

## Best for
- Agency and consulting ads
- Business service promos
- Lead-generation posts

## Vocabulary
- **Cut-out** — A photo subject with the background removed.
- **Label box** — A solid block behind a small word.
