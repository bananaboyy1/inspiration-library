# Fintech Hero on Tablet in Clouds

![](../images/web-cloud-sky-fintech-hero-on-tablet-with-navigation-tags.jpg)

**Discipline:** web · **Design type:** Device mockup scene
**Tags:** tablet, clouds, sky, fintech, light, tags, hand-phone

## What it is
A tablet floating among clouds on a light blue sky showing a fintech hero with a serif headline, a hand-held phone and floating stat cards, with pill tags across the top listing discipline and format.

## What it means
Sky and cloud imagery inside and outside the device feel open and calm, and top tags label the project.

## Techniques
- Clouds inside the UI and around the device
- Tablet mounted on a tall stand fading into clouds
- Pill tags across the top as metadata
- Hand-held phone inside the hero
- Floating white stat cards

## Best for
- Fintech website case studies
- Cloud and sky themed brands
- Project covers with metadata tags

## Vocabulary
- **Metadata tags** — Pills at the top of a cover naming discipline and format.
- **Atmospheric UI** — An interface using sky or cloud imagery as its background.
- **Floating stand** — A device on a support that fades out of view.
