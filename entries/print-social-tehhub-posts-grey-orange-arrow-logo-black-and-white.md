# 'TehHub' pack in grey and orange with a flag-arrow logo shape and black post-preview cards

![](../images/print-social-tehhub-posts-grey-orange-arrow-logo-black-and-white.avif)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Social template pack · **Design style:** Corporate & Clean-Tech
**Tags:** agency, grey, orange, arrow-logo, black-white, instagram

## What it is
Six square posts alternating grey, orange and black: a grey card with a giant orange arrow-flag holding 'Tech', black cards with 'Title of your upcoming post' over a phone photo and a stepped funnel shape, a building photo with an orange rotated square frame, an orange card with the flag shape holding 'Hub', and an office photo with a highlighted headline word.

## What it means
Clean and modular; the flag-arrow shape doubles as logo, frame and headline container across a whole post set.

## Techniques
- Reuse one signature shape (a flag-arrow) as logo, frame and container.
- Alternate three tones (grey, orange, black) for rhythm.
- Rotate a frame shape over a photo for a dynamic crop.

## Best for
- Tech agency branding
- Corporate Instagram packs
- Modular shape-based systems

## Vocabulary
- **Signature shape** — A distinctive form reused across a brand's materials.
- **Stepped funnel shape** — A shape narrowing in tiers, suggesting hierarchy or focus.
