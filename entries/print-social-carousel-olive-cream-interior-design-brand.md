# Olive and cream interior design brand carousel

![](../images/print-social-carousel-olive-cream-interior-design-brand.avif)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Social carousel · **Design style:** Corporate & Clean-Tech
**Tags:** carousel, interior design, olive green, cream, corporate, testimonial

## What it is
A six-tile carousel for an interior design company in olive green and cream, mixing a photo tile, service icons, a circular process diagram, stat callouts, team photo and a client testimonial.

## What it means
Cycling through photo, process, stats, team and testimonial tiles in one restrained two-color palette gives a services business a complete 'about us' story in a single swipe sequence.

## Techniques
- sequence a carousel to tell a story arc: intro, services, process, proof, team, testimonial
- use a circular/radial diagram for a repeating process rather than a linear list
- hold a two-color earthy palette throughout to feel calm and premium

## Best for
- interior design or architecture firm marketing
- professional services carousels
- earthy/organic brand social kits

## Vocabulary
- **process diagram** — a visual representation of sequential steps, often arranged circularly to imply an ongoing cycle
