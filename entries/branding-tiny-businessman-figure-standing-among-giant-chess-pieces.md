# A tiny 3D businessman figure in glasses standing confidently among giant black chess pieces

![](../images/branding-tiny-businessman-figure-standing-among-giant-chess-pieces.jpg)

**Discipline:** branding · **Design type:** Brand application mockups · **Design style:** 3D & Surreal
**Tags:** 3d-character, chess, strategy, business, toy-figure, photography

## What it is
A macro photo of a small painted 3D toy figure of a bespectacled man in a suit, hands on hips, standing on a chessboard surrounded by blurred oversized black chess pieces (king, knight, pawns) with a blurred person in the background.

## What it means
Playful and strategic; miniature scale against giant pieces visualises facing big competitive challenges with confidence.

## Techniques
- Scale a tiny character against oversized familiar objects.
- Blur the background pieces to keep focus on the figure.
- Use a chessboard as a ready-made strategy metaphor.

## Best for
- Business strategy content
- Consulting brand imagery
- Blog and article headers

## Vocabulary
- **Scale contrast** — Placing a small subject against much larger objects for effect.
- **Macro photography** — Close-up photography of small subjects.
