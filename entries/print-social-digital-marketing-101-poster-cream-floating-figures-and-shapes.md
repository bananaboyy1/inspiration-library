# 'Digital Marketing 101' poster with cream floating flat-illustrated figures and abstract geometric shapes

![](../images/print-social-digital-marketing-101-poster-cream-floating-figures-and-shapes.avif)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Event poster · **Design style:** Playful & Illustrated
**Tags:** webinar, education, cream, navy, flat-illustration, geometric

## What it is
A navy-bordered cream A4 poster with two flat-illustrated people floating weightlessly around a cluster of abstract shapes (a red arch, a navy sphere with a dark diamond, sparkle stars, dot rows), a bold headline 'Digital Marketing 101: Everything You Need to Know About Marketing', event date and a bit.ly link.

## What it means
Light and welcoming; floating figures around abstract shapes make a marketing webinar feel exploratory rather than dry.

## Techniques
- Have illustrated figures float weightlessly around a shape cluster.
- Mix simple geometric shapes (sphere, arch, diamond) as a decorative centrepiece.
- Keep type minimal so the illustration leads.

## Best for
- Webinar and course posters
- Educational event marketing
- Flat illustration branding

## Vocabulary
- **Floating figure** — An illustrated person shown without ground contact, implying weightlessness.
- **Shape cluster** — Several abstract geometric forms grouped as one visual unit.
