# 'Big Ideas Great Design' poster with orange and blue colour-block photos and scribble doodles

![](../images/print-social-big-ideas-great-design-poster-white-orange-blue-blocks.avif)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Poster · **Design style:** Neo-Brutalist
**Tags:** design-agency, orange, blue, colour-blocks, scribble, poster

## What it is
A white A4 poster with a paintbrush and pen-tool doodle in the corners, a bold orange 'BIG IDEAS GREAT DESIGN' headline, an '@GSCOMUNIC' handle, a blue text block and an orange text block flanking two black-and-white photos of a designer at work, small confetti diamonds and a tablet illustration.

## What it means
Confident and hands-on; real work photos paired with bold colour blocks make design services feel personal and demonstrably skilled.

## Techniques
- Alternate colour-block text panels beside greyscale work photos.
- Scatter hand-tool doodles (brush, pen tool) as thematic decoration.
- Mark corners with small confetti diamond shapes.

## Best for
- Design agency posters
- Personal portfolio marketing
- Bold two-colour branding

## Vocabulary
- **Colour-block panel** — A solid rectangle of colour holding text.
- **Confetti diamond** — A small scattered diamond shape used as decoration.
