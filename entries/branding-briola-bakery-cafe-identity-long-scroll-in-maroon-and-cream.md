# Briola bakery-café identity long scroll: maroon and cream, coffee-bean 'B' monogram, packaging and signage

![](../images/branding-briola-bakery-cafe-identity-long-scroll-in-maroon-and-cream.webp)

**Discipline:** branding · **Design type:** Brand identity presentation · **Design style:** Warm & Organic
**Tags:** bakery, cafe, maroon, cream, monogram, packaging

## What it is
A tall presentation in maroon and cream: logo explanation (coffee bean + B = monogram), business cards, a pastry shop window, coffee cups, a donut box, storefront signage, menus, a loyalty card, social posts, bags and a website, ending with a script 'Thank you'.

## What it means
Warm and classic; one deep colour and a soft serif carry the brand across every touchpoint.

## Techniques
- Explain the logo as an equation of ideas.
- Apply the brand to many real touchpoints in sequence.
- Keep a strict two-colour palette throughout.

## Best for
- Café and bakery branding
- Hospitality identities
- Behance brand presentations

## Vocabulary
- **Touchpoint** — Any place a customer meets the brand.
- **Logo rationale** — An explanation of how a logo was built.
