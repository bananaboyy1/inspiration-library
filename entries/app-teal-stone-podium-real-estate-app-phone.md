# Real Estate App on Teal Stone

![](../images/app-teal-stone-podium-real-estate-app-phone.png)

**Discipline:** app ·  · **Design style:** 3D & Surreal
**Tags:** teal, yellow, phone, stones, real-estate, ribbed-glass, 3d

## What it is
A tilted phone with a green and yellow property search UI leaning on dark teal rocks, next to a ribbed-glass panel with the brand name and a leaf-house icon.

## What it means
Earthy teal and sunny yellow suggest home and nature. Ribbed glass and stone add tactile richness.

## Techniques
- Ribbed glass panel with rotated brand text
- Rock forms as a natural pedestal
- Yellow CTA against dark green UI
- App icon floating as a 3D tile
- Palette matched between screen and scene

## Best for
- Property and lifestyle apps
- Case-study hero mockups
- Eco-friendly brands

## Vocabulary
- **Ribbed glass** — A glass texture of vertical flutes that distorts what's behind it.
- **Natural pedestal** — Rocks or organic objects used to support a product.
- **Floating icon** — An app icon rendered as a 3D object beside the device.
