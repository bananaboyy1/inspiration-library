A square post with a navy top section holding the green logo, a white headline, body copy and a green 'Get in touch now!' button, an indigo side block with a ghost 'N', and a bottom photo of people working at monitors tinted violet.

Inviting and on-brand; tinting the office photo keeps the palette unified.

- Stack text block over a photo strip.
- Repeat the ghost N for recognition.
