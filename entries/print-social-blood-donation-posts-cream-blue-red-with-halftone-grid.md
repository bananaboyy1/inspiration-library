# Blood donation pack in cream, blue and red with a halftone grid, boxed headlines and outlined figures

![](../images/print-social-blood-donation-posts-cream-blue-red-with-halftone-grid.avif)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Social template pack · **Design style:** Retro & Pixel
**Tags:** blood-donation, healthcare, blue, red, halftone, retro

## What it is
Nine square posts in cream, cobalt blue and coral red with a faint halftone dot grid, bold boxed headlines ('BLOOD DONATION', 'Who Can Donate?', 'Save a Life.'), outlined illustrations of a blood bag, a donor and clasped hands, a large '2021' watermark, and a 'Register and become a donor!' CTA with contact details.

## What it means
Trustworthy and organised; the halftone grid and boxed type give a health campaign a retro-poster credibility.

## Techniques
- Split each post into solid colour-block halves.
- Use a faint halftone dot grid as texture across a set.
- Add a large watermark year for a dated campaign feel.
- Keep illustration style to simple outlines for medical clarity.

## Best for
- Healthcare and charity campaigns
- Blood donation drives
- NGO social content

## Vocabulary
- **Halftone grid** — A pattern of dots that simulates tone or texture.
- **Watermark year** — A large background numeral dating a campaign.
