# Lilac Online Course Dashboard

![](../images/web-lilac-online-course-dashboard-with-hero-banner-and-mentor-list.webp)

**Discipline:** web ·  · **Design style:** Playful & Illustrated
**Tags:** dashboard, education, lilac, violet, cards, mentors, memoji, white

## What it is
A white app window on grey with a sidebar, a search and profile header, a violet hero banner with sparkles and a Join Now button, three course progress cards, Continue Watching photo cards with tags and progress bars, a lesson table, and a right column with a statistic ring around a memoji avatar, a bar chart and a mentor list with Follow buttons.

## What it means
A violet hero and pastel tags make a learning dashboard friendly, and memoji avatars add personality.

## Techniques
- Violet hero banner with sparkle shapes
- Pastel category tags on course cards
- Avatar in a progress ring
- Bar chart with one dark bar
- Follow buttons with tiny icons

## Best for
- E-learning dashboards
- Student and course platforms
- Violet and white UI

## Vocabulary
- **Hero banner** — A large promotional card at the top of a dashboard.
- **Continue watching** — A row of in-progress content cards.
- **Memoji avatar** — A cartoon-style portrait used as a profile picture.
