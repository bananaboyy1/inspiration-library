# 'Digital Marketing Agency' post with a portrait fading into an orange-to-crimson gradient

![](../images/print-social-digital-marketing-agency-post-with-orange-to-crimson-fade.avif)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Social ad · **Design style:** Gradient & Aurora
**Tags:** agency, gradient, orange, crimson, portrait, ad

## What it is
A square post with a faded laptop scene behind a smiling woman in a patterned headwrap, the lower half washed by an orange-to-crimson gradient carrying a white headline, a bracketed yellow-bordered tagline, phone numbers and a yellow 'Join us' pill.

## What it means
Warm and vibrant; the fade blends person and message into one glow.

## Techniques
- Fade a warm gradient up from the bottom to hold text.
- Frame the tagline in a thin outlined box.
- Ghost a background scene for context.

## Best for
- Agency promos
- Warm-palette ads
- Service announcements

## Vocabulary
- **Bottom fade** — A gradient rising from the base to create a text area.
- **Ghosted image** — A faint, low-opacity background photo.
