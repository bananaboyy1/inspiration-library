# Phone on Grey Floor beside a Chair

![](../images/app-lock-screen-style-phone-on-grey-with-wooden-chair.png)

**Discipline:** app · **Design type:** Device mockup scene
**Tags:** top-down, grey, yellow, chair, lock-screen, gradient-orb, thumbnail

## What it is
A top-down shot of a phone lying on a grey surface next to a wooden chair leg and yellow furniture, showing a bold black-and-white notification screen with an orange and blue orb clock.

## What it means
Everyday objects make the scene feel like a real moment, and the bold black type on the screen stays legible from above.

## Techniques
- Overhead angle for a natural resting-phone view
- Neutral grey surface with a single yellow accent
- Blurred gradient orb as the screen's visual centre
- Heavy black headline in the UI
- Sharp, casual crop with props at the edges

## Best for
- Lifestyle app presentations
- Wellness and sleep apps
- Cover thumbnails with a documentary feel

## Vocabulary
- **Top-down mockup** — A device shot from directly above.
- **Gradient orb** — A blurred multi-colour circle used as a visual centre.
- **Casual crop** — A composition that looks captured rather than staged.
