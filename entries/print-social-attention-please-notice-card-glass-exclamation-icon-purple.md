# 'Attention Please' notice post with a frosted glass card and a glossy 3D glass exclamation-mark icon

![](../images/print-social-attention-please-notice-card-glass-exclamation-icon-purple.avif)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Social promo post · **Design style:** Glassmorphism
**Tags:** notice, announcement, purple, glassmorphism, 3d-icon, instagram

## What it is
A dark purple striped post with a frosted-glass rounded card holding a bold 'Attention Please' headline and an office-closure announcement, flanked by two glossy translucent 3D triangle icons with exclamation marks, and Instagram action icons along the bottom.

## What it means
Clear and polite; the glass card format gives even an inconvenient announcement (a closure notice) a premium, considerate presentation.

## Techniques
- Present an announcement inside a frosted glass card for gravity.
- Use glossy 3D warning icons instead of flat ones for a premium feel.
- Keep text concise and apologetic in tone for closure notices.

## Best for
- Business announcements
- Office and service notices
- Glassmorphism social branding

## Vocabulary
- **Notice card** — A card format used to deliver an official announcement.
- **3D warning icon** — A glossy rendered exclamation or alert symbol.
