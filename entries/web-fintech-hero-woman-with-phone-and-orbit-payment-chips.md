# White studio shot of a woman with a phone, a navy arc linking payment chips and italic-serif 'Pay Send Track'

![](../images/web-fintech-hero-woman-with-phone-and-orbit-payment-chips.jpg)

**Discipline:** web · **Design type:** Landing page hero
**Tags:** hero, fintech, white, motion-blur, arc, chips, italic-serif, navy

## What it is
A white cyclorama with a woman in navy top and light jeans looking at her phone, blurred pedestrians walking behind, a navy arc connecting two floating payment chips, and stacked words 'Pay' (italic serif), 'Send', 'Track' in navy at right.

## What it means
Calm amid motion; the still figure with a blurred crowd says control.

## Techniques
- Blur passers-by against a still subject.
- Draw a single arc linking UI chips around her.
- Stack action verbs with one in italic serif.
- Keep the palette navy on white.

## Best for
- Fintech hero visuals
- Payment brand films and key art
- Minimal white campaigns

## Vocabulary
- **Still-in-motion** — A sharp subject among blurred movement.
- **Payment chip** — A small floating card showing an amount.
- **Verb stack** — Action words stacked as a headline.
- **Cyclorama** — A seamless white studio backdrop.
