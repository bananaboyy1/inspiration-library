# Grow Your Profit 92% Glass Card Slide

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Social carousel
**Tags:** carousel, black, green, glass, stat, progress-bar, learn-more, vector

## What it is
A black carousel slide with a centred logo above a large rounded glass card with a green glow at the right edge, a segmented progress bar, the heading 'Grow Your Profit With Us', a giant white 92% with a green arrow, a paragraph and a Learn more pill; above it the base of a blue slide with an email and Swipe >>, and below a lime notebook-tab card.

## What it means
One huge number and a glowing card make a claim feel confident, and the story-style progress bar shows the swipe.

## Techniques
- Huge stat with a small growth arrow
- Green glow bleeding into a glass card
- Segmented progress bar at the card top
- Small paragraph and outlined pill at the base
- Black canvas around a single card

## Best for
- Stat slides in carousels
- Finance and growth marketing
- Glass card templates

## Vocabulary
- **Stat slide** — A slide built around one big figure.
- **Growth arrow** — A small upward arrow beside a percentage.
- **Segment progress** — A row of dashes showing position in a sequence.
