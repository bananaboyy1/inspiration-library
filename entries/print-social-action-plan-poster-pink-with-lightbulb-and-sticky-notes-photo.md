# 'Action Plan' creative agency poster with a real photo of a lightbulb on scattered pink sticky notes

![](../images/print-social-action-plan-poster-pink-with-lightbulb-and-sticky-notes-photo.avif)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Poster · **Design style:** Minimalist
**Tags:** agency, action-plan, pink, photography, sticky-notes, poster

## What it is
A hot pink poster with a hexagon logo, bold 'ACTION PLAN' and 'Creative Marketing Agency' subtitle, body copy, a 'Digital Positioning' label with social icons, geometric pink colour blocks in the corner, and a real photograph of a lightbulb resting on fanned pink and lilac sticky notes with crumpled paper balls.

## What it means
Tactile and idea-driven; a real photographed lightbulb-on-notes scene grounds an abstract 'action plan' in something physical.

## Techniques
- Photograph a symbolic object (a lightbulb) on real sticky notes.
- Mix flat geometric blocks with real photography in one layout.
- Use crumpled paper as a texture suggesting brainstorming.

## Best for
- Marketing agency posters
- Strategy and planning documents
- Idea-generation branding

## Vocabulary
- **Symbolic photography** — A staged photo where an object represents an abstract idea.
- **Colour block corner** — A geometric shape anchoring one corner of a layout.
