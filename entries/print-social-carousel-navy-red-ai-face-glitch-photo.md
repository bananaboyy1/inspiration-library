# Navy/red AI carousel with glitched face-splash photography

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Social template pack · **Design style:** Dark & Neon
**Tags:** carousel, ai, glitch photo, navy red, instagram, technology

## What it is
An eight-tile Instagram grid on a deep navy background, repeating a heavily glitch/color-splashed portrait photo (red-blue channel split) alongside bold '80% We are the future' statistic headlines.

## What it means
A single dramatically distorted portrait reused across every tile, rather than eight different photos, keeps production simple while still filling a whole grid with striking, on-theme imagery.

## Techniques
- reuse one heavily stylized hero photo across every tile of a grid instead of sourcing new imagery each time
- apply a strong red/blue channel-split glitch treatment to signal 'AI/digital' distortion
- pair a single large percentage stat with a short bold claim for an easily quotable headline tile

## Best for
- AI/tech brand social campaigns
- futuristic content marketing
- glitch-aesthetic template packs

## Vocabulary
- **channel split** — offsetting an image's red, green and blue color channels from each other to create a distorted, digital look
