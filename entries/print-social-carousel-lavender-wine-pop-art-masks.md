# Lavender wine-tasting carousel with pop-art mask illustrations

![](../images/print-social-carousel-lavender-wine-pop-art-masks.avif)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Social carousel · **Design style:** Playful & Illustrated
**Tags:** carousel, wine tasting, lavender, pop art, mask illustration, 3d render

## What it is
A five-tile Instagram grid for a wine-tasting event on a soft lavender background, combining a glossy 3D wine-bottle render, playful pop-art masked-face illustrations, and scattered starburst accents.

## What it means
Pairing a photoreal 3D product render with flat pop-art illustrated characters gives an event promo both product desirability and a fun, social event atmosphere in one cohesive palette.

## Techniques
- combine a glossy photoreal 3D render with flat pop-art character illustration in one grid
- use a soft pastel background to let saturated purple/yellow accent elements stand out
- scatter small starburst/sparkle shapes throughout for a celebratory, event-ready feel

## Best for
- wine tasting/food & drink event promotion
- lifestyle event Instagram carousels
- pastel-and-pop-art brand kits

## Vocabulary
- **pop art** — an art style using bold flat colors, hard outlines and mass-media-inspired imagery
