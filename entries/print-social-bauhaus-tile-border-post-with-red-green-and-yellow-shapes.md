# Post with a Bauhaus tile border of red, green, yellow and grey quarter-circles and triangles

![](../images/print-social-bauhaus-tile-border-post-with-red-green-and-yellow-shapes.avif)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Social promo post · **Design style:** Geometric & Bauhaus
**Tags:** geometric, bauhaus, red, green, yellow, border

## What it is
A square white post with a border of geometric tiles (quarter-circles, triangles, squares) in red, green, yellow, grey and photo-filled blocks, a photo of a smiling woman in multicoloured sunglasses and a yellow sweater, a black and red headline 'Your Headline Goes Here.' and a pill URL.

## What it means
Vibrant and modern; the tile grid adds playful structure and picks up the sunglass colours.

## Techniques
- Frame content with a border of geometric tiles.
- Pull the palette from a detail in the photo.
- Split the headline into black and red lines.

## Best for
- Fashion and eyewear
- Creative studios
- Colourful brand posts

## Vocabulary
- **Bauhaus tiles** — Simple circles, triangles and squares in primary colours.
- **Modular border** — A frame made from repeating units.
