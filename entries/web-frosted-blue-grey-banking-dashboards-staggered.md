# Frosted Blue-Grey Banking Dashboards

![](../images/web-frosted-blue-grey-banking-dashboards-staggered.jpg)

**Discipline:** web · **Design type:** Dashboard
**Tags:** dashboard, banking, frosted, blue-grey, cards, transactions, staggered

## What it is
Three desktop banking dashboards staggered across a white canvas, pale frosted panels with blue-grey blur gradients, sidebars, a big balance, a card, transaction tables and bar charts.

## What it means
Frosted layers and a cool blue palette feel calm and secure. The stagger shows multiple screens without needing a grid.

## Techniques
- Blurred blue smudge across the header of each window
- Nested translucent cards inside an app shell
- Hatched highlighted bar in the chart
- Screens offset and cropped at the right edge
- White background so the glass reads as light

## Best for
- Banking and finance dashboards
- Portfolio presentations of desktop UI
- Calm data-heavy product concepts

## Vocabulary
- **App shell** — The frame of sidebar and header around content.
- **Staggered screens** — Multiple screens offset to imply a flow.
- **Glass panel** — A translucent card that shows a blurred backdrop.
