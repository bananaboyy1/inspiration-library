# Magenta 'World of Possibilities' carousel with dominant cover tile

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Social template pack · **Design style:** Dark & Neon
**Tags:** carousel, tech, magenta, 3d spheres, dark, instagram

## What it is
A third framing of the same magenta carousel system, with the cover tile enlarged relative to the grid to foreground the product render and headline stack more prominently.

## What it means
Presenting the cover tile at a larger relative scale in marketing shots for the template pack itself helps a potential buyer judge the hero tile's impact before purchasing.

## Techniques
- enlarge the cover/hero tile relative to supporting tiles when presenting a template pack for sale
- stack multiple short headline phrases vertically on a cover tile to build a rhythm before the CTA

## Best for
- template pack sales/marketing imagery
- tech innovation campaign carousels

## Vocabulary
- **hero tile** — the dominant, usually largest tile in a grid or carousel meant to capture initial attention
