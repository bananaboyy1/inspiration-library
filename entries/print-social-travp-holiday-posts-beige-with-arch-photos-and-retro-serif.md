# 'travp' holiday pack in beige and terracotta with arch photos, organic blobs and retro serif

![](../images/print-social-travp-holiday-posts-beige-with-arch-photos-and-retro-serif.avif)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Social template pack · **Design style:** Warm & Organic
**Tags:** travel, holiday, beige, terracotta, serif, organic

## What it is
Five square posts in beige with terracotta and mustard blobs, arch and pill-cropped photos of masked travellers, heavy retro serif headlines ('May your occasions be cheerful and brilliant!') mixing maroon and mustard, a 'Live podcast' pill and leaf outlines.

## What it means
Warm and nostalgic; the soft serif and earthy tones feel gentle and hopeful.

## Techniques
- Use a heavy retro serif in two warm colours.
- Crop photos into arches and pills.
- Layer organic blobs behind content.
- Add a vertical rule beside body text.

## Best for
- Travel brands
- Holiday greetings
- Podcast promos

## Vocabulary
- **Retro serif** — A soft, heavy serif reminiscent of the 1970s.
- **Organic blob** — An irregular rounded shape.
