# Rounded portrait card with a white scribble loop and floating receipt beside a pale quote card with a big quotation mark

**Discipline:** web ·  · **Design style:** Photographic & Cinematic
**Tags:** testimonial, fintech, quote, portrait, navy, grey, rounded-cards, receipt

## What it is
Two rounded cards: left a blue-grey gradient with a woman with braids smiling at her phone over a huge white looped scribble and a white transaction card 'Starbucks $1.99'; right a pale grey card with a 'Customer Request' pill, a big grey quotation mark, a large navy quote about a smarter unified wallet and an avatar with name and role.

## What it means
Human and clear; a real person plus a UI receipt makes the quote feel lived.

## Techniques
- Pair a portrait card with a quote card.
- Overlay a UI receipt on the photo.
- Use a giant faint quotation mark.
- Add a huge white loop behind the person.

## Best for
- Testimonial sections
- Fintech user-voice blocks
- Paired card layouts

## Vocabulary
- **Quote card** — A card with a big quote and attribution.
- **Receipt overlay** — A transaction card laid on a photo.
- **Scribble loop** — A huge white looped stroke as a backdrop.
- **Customer request tag** — A pill labelling where the quote came from.
