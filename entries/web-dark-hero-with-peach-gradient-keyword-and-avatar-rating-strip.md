**Tags:** hero, dark, peach, gradient-text, badge, buttons, avatars, stars

## What it is
A black page with a dark badge 'Introducing Sparkles' with an arrow, a heavy white headline whose second line is peach, a grey paragraph mentioning React and Vue.js in bold, a white pill button 'Get lifetime access — $59' and an outlined 'Explore components', and below a row of overlapping avatars with five yellow stars and 'Trusted by 400+ users'.

## What it means
Direct and warm; a peach keyword softens a stark black hero.

## Techniques
- Colour the second headline line in a soft gradient.
- Use a badge above the headline.
- Pair a white filled and an outlined button.
- Add an avatar and stars strip for trust.

## Best for
- Developer product heroes
- Dark landing pages
- Social-proof strips
