# Four 'Creative' posters in deep violet with glowing gradient circles, triangle cuts and cropped display type

![](../images/print-social-creative-poster-set-deep-violet-gradient-circles-triangles.avif)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Typographic posters · **Design style:** Gradient & Aurora
**Tags:** poster-set, violet, gradient, geometric, typography, glow

## What it is
Four near-black to violet portrait posters, each with large glowing gradient circles and angular triangle cut-outs, a subtle grain texture, a small dot-grid pattern, and 'Creative' split across two lines in bold white type with a four-point sparkle accent.

## What it means
Bold and premium; glowing gradient orbs on near-black feel high-end and gallery-ready.

## Techniques
- Combine glowing gradient circles with hard-edged triangle cuts.
- Split one word across two lines for a graphic headline.
- Add fine grain texture to keep a dark gradient from looking flat.

## Best for
- Poster series and cover sets
- Creative agency branding
- Event or exhibition posters

## Vocabulary
- **Grain texture** — A fine noise texture added for depth and realism.
- **Glow orb** — A softly blurred circle of light used as a design accent.
