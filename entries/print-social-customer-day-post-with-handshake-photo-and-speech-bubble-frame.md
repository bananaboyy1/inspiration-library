## What it is
A square white post with a grey and orange headline 'NENHUMA HISTÓRIA É CONTADA SOZINHA!', a date line '15 de Setembro | Dia do Cliente', a photo of a smiling woman shaking a client's hand cropped into a rounded shape with speech-bubble tails, orange dot grids and chevrons, and an outlined orange logo box.

## What it means
Warm and grateful; the speech-bubble frame turns the photo into a conversation.

## Techniques
- Crop a photo with speech-bubble tails.
- Colour keywords of the headline in the accent.
- Use dot grids in corners as balance.

## Best for
- Customer appreciation days
- Service businesses
- Relationship messaging
