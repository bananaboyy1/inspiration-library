# Crimson 'We're Hiring!' post with a man at a laptop and yellow label boxes

![](../images/print-social-crimson-hiring-post-with-man-at-laptop-and-yellow-labels.avif)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Recruitment post · **Design style:** Corporate & Clean-Tech
**Tags:** hiring, recruitment, crimson, yellow, cutout, instagram

## What it is
A square crimson post with a yellow 'Hiring!' headline and a yellow tagline box, a cut-out grey-haired man with a sweater over his shoulders working at a laptop with coffee, a yellow 'Open Positions' speech tab over roles with left-rule chevrons, CV and WhatsApp details and a yellow handle bar.

## What it means
Warm and inclusive; an experienced professional signals the team values all ages.

## Techniques
- Show a real person working rather than a prop.
- Use yellow label boxes against crimson.
- Mark roles with a left rule and chevron.

## Best for
- Hiring posts
- Inclusive recruitment
- Agency careers

## Vocabulary
- **Left rule** — A vertical line on the left of an item.
- **Label box** — A solid box behind short text.
