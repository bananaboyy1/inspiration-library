# Red 'We Are Hiring!' post with a white wave top and three floating red megaphones

![](../images/print-social-red-hiring-post-with-wave-split-and-three-megaphones.avif)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Recruitment post · **Design style:** Corporate & Clean-Tech
**Tags:** hiring, recruitment, red, wave, megaphone, instagram

## What it is
A square post with a white top separated from a deep red body by a wave, three black-and-red megaphones floating at different sizes, a white headline ('We Are Hiring! Join Our CREATIVE & Professional Team!'), a translucent panel of open positions, CV email and WhatsApp.

## What it means
Loud and celebratory; multiple megaphones feel like an announcement echoing around.

## Techniques
- Scatter one prop at several sizes for depth.
- Divide the post with a soft wave edge.
- Centre all text in a single column.

## Best for
- Hiring campaigns
- Agency careers
- Announcement posts

## Vocabulary
- **Scale variation** — Repeating an object at different sizes for depth.
- **Wave divider** — A curved line separating sections.
