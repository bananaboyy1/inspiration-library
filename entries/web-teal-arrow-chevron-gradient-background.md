# Teal gradient background with layered translucent arrow-chevron shapes pointing right

![](../images/web-teal-arrow-chevron-gradient-background.avif)

**Discipline:** web · **Design type:** Background pattern · **Design style:** Gradient & Aurora
**Tags:** background, teal, gradient, chevron, arrow, minimal

## What it is
A horizontal teal gradient background with two overlapping translucent chevron/arrow shapes pointing right, darker at the edges and brighter cyan at the tip, leaving open space on the right for text.

## What it means
Directional and calm; the arrow shapes quietly suggest forward motion or progress without any text.

## Techniques
- Layer translucent chevrons for depth without clutter.
- Brighten a shape's tip to draw the eye in one direction.
- Leave open negative space for a headline to sit later.

## Best for
- Presentation backgrounds
- Banner templates
- Corporate slide decks

## Vocabulary
- **Chevron** — A V-shaped arrow form.
- **Negative space** — Empty area left deliberately for content or breathing room.
