# Burgundy Corner Letterhead with Lotus Footer

![](../images/branding-burgundy-corner-and-lotus-footer-letterhead-on-stand.png)

**Discipline:** branding · **Design type:** Packaging and print collateral
**Tags:** letterhead, burgundy, lotus, stand, dark, stationery, corner

## What it is
A letterhead standing on a grey plinth with a burgundy L-shaped corner band holding a bird logo at the top right, a thin vertical burgundy line by the body text and a lotus flourish above the footer contacts, plus a dark-grey sheet with a cut-out logo tab peeking below.

## What it means
A single corner band and one flourish keep the page mostly white and elegant while still branded.

## Techniques
- Corner band that wraps two edges
- Vertical accent rule beside the body copy
- Decorative flourish anchoring the footer
- Paper standing upright on a plinth
- Two contrasting sheets shown in one scroll

## Best for
- Boutique and wellness stationery
- Elegant corporate identities
- Letterhead templates

## Vocabulary
- **L-band** — A colour band that turns a corner along two edges.
- **Flourish** — A decorative shape used as ornament.
- **Plinth mockup** — A paper piece standing on a block for presentation.
