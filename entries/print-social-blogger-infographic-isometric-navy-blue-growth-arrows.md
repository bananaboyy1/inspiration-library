# 'Blogger Infographics' isometric diagram with four platform scenes and growth-percentage arrows

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Product infographic set · **Design style:** Corporate & Clean-Tech
**Tags:** infographic, blogger, isometric, navy, growth-arrows, stats

## What it is
A navy-and-white infographic with a bold 'BLOGGER INFOGRAPHICS' title, four isometric scenes (smartphone blogging, live-stream broadcasting, laptop blogging, street shooting) each with a pie chart and connecting line, and a bottom row of blue arrow shapes showing growth percentages (5%, 15%, 23%, 34%).

## What it means
Structured and credible; isometric scenes paired with percentage arrows make blogging activity feel measurable and professional.

## Techniques
- Illustrate each activity as a small isometric 3D scene.
- Pair each scene with its own small pie chart.
- Close with a row of arrow shapes showing growth by category.

## Best for
- Content creator statistics
- Media industry infographics
- Isometric data presentations

## Vocabulary
- **Isometric scene** — A 3D-style illustration drawn at a fixed angle without perspective distortion.
- **Growth arrow** — An arrow-shaped graphic showing a percentage increase.
