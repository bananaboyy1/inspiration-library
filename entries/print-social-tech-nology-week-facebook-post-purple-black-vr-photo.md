# 'Tech Nology Week' Facebook post with a purple-black grid pattern and a VR headset photo shattering into pixels

![](../images/print-social-tech-nology-week-facebook-post-purple-black-vr-photo.avif)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Social ad · **Design style:** Retro & Pixel
**Tags:** tech-event, purple, black, vr, grid-pattern, facebook-ad

## What it is
A wide purple-bordered post split into a black-and-purple diagonal grid pattern with the word 'TECH' broken across a line, a mustard 'HITECH' logo tag, and a photo of a man in a VR headset with a particle-shatter effect breaking off his hand.

## What it means
Energetic and eye-catching; the shattering pixel effect on the VR photo makes a generic tech event feel cutting-edge.

## Techniques
- Split a headline word awkwardly across a line break for graphic effect.
- Add a particle-shatter effect to a photo for a tech, digital feel.
- Use a diagonal grid pattern with hatching as background texture.

## Best for
- Tech event promotion
- VR and gadget marketing
- Bold Facebook ad templates

## Vocabulary
- **Particle shatter effect** — A photo edge breaking into small floating fragments.
- **Diagonal grid pattern** — A background texture of angled lines and hatching.
