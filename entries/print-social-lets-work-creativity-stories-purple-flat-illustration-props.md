# 'Let's Work / Be Creative' story set on white with flat-illustrated design tool clusters and dashed lines

![](../images/print-social-lets-work-creativity-stories-purple-flat-illustration-props.avif)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Story set · **Design style:** Playful & Illustrated
**Tags:** creativity, design-tools, white, flat-illustration, purple, story

## What it is
Four vertical white story cards on a lilac background, each with bold headlines ('Let's Work', 'Be Creative', 'Create with Us', 'Build Your Creativity') above a cluster of flat-illustrated design props (pencils, a laptop, a folder, a target icon, a lightbulb) in coral, purple and cream, with dashed decorative lines.

## What it means
Friendly and inviting; clustering recognisable design tools makes an abstract 'creativity' pitch feel grounded in real work.

## Techniques
- Cluster several related props into one illustrated group per card.
- Keep a consistent white base across a story set for a clean feel.
- Use dashed lines as a light decorative accent.

## Best for
- Design agency stories
- Creative service promotion
- Prop-cluster illustration

## Vocabulary
- **Prop cluster** — Several related illustrated objects grouped together.
- **Dashed line accent** — A broken line used as subtle decoration.
