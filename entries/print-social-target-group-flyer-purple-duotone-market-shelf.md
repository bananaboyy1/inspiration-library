# 'Creative solutions to digital challenges' flyer with a purple duotone market shelf photo and chevron list

![](../images/print-social-target-group-flyer-purple-duotone-market-shelf.avif)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Social ad · **Design style:** Minimalist
**Tags:** marketing, purple, duotone, chevron, flyer, corporate

## What it is
A purple square flyer with an angled duotone photo of a striped-awning market shelf stocked with fruit and bottles, a thumbs-up icon, a bold three-colour headline ('Creative solutions to digital challenges'), a chevron-marked 'Marketing Professionals' line, and an @-symbol logo with social icons.

## What it means
Clean and confident; duotone photography lets a literal product shelf double as an abstract marketing metaphor.

## Techniques
- Duotone a real photo in the brand's single accent colour.
- Split a headline across three text weights and colours.
- Mark a supporting line with chevron arrows for direction.

## Best for
- Marketing agency flyers
- B2B service promotion
- Single-colour brand systems

## Vocabulary
- **Duotone photography** — A photo rendered using only two colours.
- **Chevron marker** — A small arrow symbol used to lead into text.
