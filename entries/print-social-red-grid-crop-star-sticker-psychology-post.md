# Red post with cropped face tiles and outline stars behind a bold statement

![](../images/print-social-red-grid-crop-star-sticker-psychology-post.jpg)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Social educational post
**Tags:** psychology, red, grid-crop, stars, white-type, photo-tiles, editorial, portuguese

## What it is
A deep red portrait post where a close crop of a face is split into grid tiles with thin white lines, overlaid with hand-drawn white starbursts and a big white sentence that flows around the image, framed by tiny corner captions.

## What it means
Intense, intimate and emotional; the cropped skin and red tone feel raw yet stylish.

## Techniques
- Crop tightly on facial features and split into a tile grid.
- Tint the whole photo red.
- Scatter outline star doodles across the image.
- Set a large bold sentence in white lowercase around the visual.

## Best for
- Psychology and mental-health content
- Statement posts with strong imagery
- Editorial social covers

## Vocabulary
- **Tile crop** — An image divided into a grid of cells.
- **Outline star** — A star drawn as a line-only shape.
- **Monochrome tint** — A single colour applied over a photo.
- **Wrapped headline** — Text arranged around parts of an image.
