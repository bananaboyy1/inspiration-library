# Red-Lit Portrait with Floating Finance Cards

![](../images/branding-red-lit-woman-laptop-with-floating-finance-cards.png)

**Discipline:** branding · **Design type:** Campaign photography
**Tags:** red, portrait, laptop, cards, lines, night, fintech

## What it is
A photo of a smiling woman at a laptop lit in deep red at night, overlaid with a red card and a white card showing profit numbers, and white and orange line graphs weaving through the frame, with a caption 'A smooth experience that builds confidence and trust from day one'.

## What it means
Red lighting keeps the photography on-brand, and floating numbers connect a human moment to product value.

## Techniques
- Photo graded entirely in the brand red
- UI stat cards overlaid on the person
- Line graph running through the whole frame
- Caption with alternating bright and dim words
- Rounded frame edge matching other slides

## Best for
- Fintech campaign imagery
- Brand storytelling slides
- Photography plus UI compositions

## Vocabulary
- **Brand grade** — Colour grading photography to the brand palette.
- **Data line overlay** — Chart lines drawn over a photo as graphic texture.
- **Stat card overlay** — A card with a number placed on an image.
