# Care-app hero on a tilted monitor floating in lilac mist with a dashed route and credits

![](../images/web-care-app-website-on-floating-tilted-monitor-in-lilac.jpg)

**Discipline:** web ·  · **Design style:** Editorial
**Tags:** monitor, lilac, tilted, dashed-route, serif, yellow, senior-care, credits

## What it is
A large monitor tilted and floating in a lilac haze with a dashed white loop behind it, showing an indigo hero with 'Care, from anywhere.' in serif with yellow italic, a smiling older woman, store buttons and an award laurel, with small captions at the lower corners.

## What it means
Soothing and dreamy; the floating tilted screen feels light.

## Techniques
- Tilt and float the monitor in a monochrome haze.
- Trace a dashed route behind it.
- Use serif with a yellow italic word.
- Fade the stand into mist.

## Best for
- Healthcare app site presentations
- Lilac tech brands
- Floating mockups

## Vocabulary
- **Floating mockup** — A device suspended without a surface.
- **Mist backdrop** — A soft colour haze behind an object.
- **Italic accent word** — One word in yellow italic.
- **Corner captions** — Small text in the lower corners.
