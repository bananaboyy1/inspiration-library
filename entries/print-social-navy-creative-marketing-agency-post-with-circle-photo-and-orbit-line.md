# Navy 'Creative Marketing Agency' post with a large navy circle, circle photo and orbit line

![](../images/print-social-navy-creative-marketing-agency-post-with-circle-photo-and-orbit-line.avif)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Social ad · **Design style:** Corporate & Clean-Tech
**Tags:** agency, navy, yellow, circle, orbit, ad

## What it is
A square white post with a large navy circle holding 'Creative' in thin white, 'MARKETING' in bold yellow and 'AGENCY' in white, a contact strip inside the circle, a circle-cropped photo of a smiling man in a light suit at a desk, thin navy orbit lines with dot ends and triangle outlines.

## What it means
Clean and professional; interlocking circles tie message and person together.

## Techniques
- Overlap a text circle with a photo circle.
- Trace an orbit line with dot ends around both.
- Use weight contrast across three headline words.

## Best for
- Agency ads
- Business services
- Corporate social posts

## Vocabulary
- **Interlocking circles** — Two circles overlapping to link content.
- **Orbit line** — A curved stroke circling a shape.
