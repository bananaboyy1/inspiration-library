# Concentric Violet Pills Growing Businesses Post

![](../images/print-social-navy-and-violet-concentric-pill-stack-growing-businesses-post.png)

**Discipline:** print-social ·  · **Design style:** Geometric & Bauhaus
**Tags:** navy, violet, concentric-pills, gradient, startup, cta, tall-post

## What it is
A dark navy tall post with a light heading 'The new platform for growing businesses', a small subline and a logo top right, and a stack of nested violet rounded-rectangle shapes glowing outward from a lavender Explore solutions pill with an arrow.

## What it means
Nested capsules radiate from the button like ripples, pulling the eye to the call to action.

## Techniques
- Concentric capsule shapes around the button
- Violet gradient rising from the base of a navy field
- Large light headline in the upper left
- Lavender pill as the brightest element
- Small logo mark in the corner

## Best for
- SaaS and startup social posts
- CTA-focused templates
- Dark navy and violet brand systems

## Vocabulary
- **Ripple shapes** — Nested outlines radiating from a focal element.
- **Glow gradient** — A luminous colour blend rising from an edge.
- **Call-to-action pill** — A capsule button carrying the main action.
