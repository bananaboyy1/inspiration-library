# 'Marketing Professionals At Your Service' poster in pure black and white with line-art figures

![](../images/print-social-marketing-professionals-poster-black-white-line-illustration.avif)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Poster · **Design style:** Minimalist
**Tags:** marketing, black-white, line-art, minimal, poster, typography

## What it is
A black A4 poster with a rotated side label 'Poster A4 Flyer A5 Template Marketing', a bold white circled 'MARKETING' with 'PROFESSIONALS AT YOUR SERVICE', two-column body copy, a logo line, social icons, and white line-art illustrations of two people reviewing a presentation chart.

## What it means
Bold and sophisticated; pure black-and-white line art feels editorial and confident without needing any colour.

## Techniques
- Restrict a poster entirely to black and white for editorial impact.
- Circle the key word of a headline for emphasis.
- Rotate a category label along the poster's edge.

## Best for
- Marketing agency posters
- Monochrome brand systems
- Editorial-style B2B marketing

## Vocabulary
- **Monochrome poster** — A design using only black, white and greys.
- **Line-art figure** — A person illustrated using only outline strokes.
