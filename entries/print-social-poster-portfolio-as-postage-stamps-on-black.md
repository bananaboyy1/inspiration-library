# Poster portfolio shown as perforated postage stamps on black with line self-portraits

![](../images/print-social-poster-portfolio-as-postage-stamps-on-black.png)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Typographic posters · **Design style:** Retro & Pixel
**Tags:** posters, stamps, portfolio, collage, black, illustration

## What it is
A black board covered with posters framed as perforated postage stamps with denominations (₹5, 4) — Bollywood, music and type posters in red, yellow and teal — plus white line-drawn portraits of two designers.

## What it means
Collectible and personal; the stamp frame turns a body of work into a curated collection.

## Techniques
- Frame each piece as a perforated stamp.
- Add tiny denominations for authenticity.
- Sign the board with line-drawn self-portraits.

## Best for
- Poster portfolios
- Music and film art
- Collections of small works

## Vocabulary
- **Perforation** — The wavy punched edge of a stamp.
- **Denomination** — The printed value on a stamp.
