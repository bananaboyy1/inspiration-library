# 'Today Release' press kit landing page in red with a flat-illustrated coffee cup and phone mockup

![](../images/web-today-release-press-kit-landing-page-red-coffee-illustration.avif)

**Discipline:** web · **Design type:** Landing page · **Design style:** Playful & Illustrated
**Tags:** press-kit, landing-page, red, coffee, flat-illustration, announcement

## What it is
A red landing page with a left nav (Home, News, Categories, About Us), a boxed 'TODAY RELEASE' headline, 'Coffee Shops Everywhere' subtitle, a phone mockup showing an order app, a flat-illustrated coffee cup with splashing coffee, body copy, 'Read more' and 'Register here' buttons, and a flat-illustrated woman with a quote.

## What it means
Warm and newsy; framing a product launch as a 'press release' landing page gives it instant credibility and urgency.

## Techniques
- Frame an announcement page like a news release.
- Pair a product screen mockup with a matching flat illustration.
- Include a customer quote for social proof directly on the landing page.

## Best for
- Product launch landing pages
- Press kit and announcement sites
- Food and beverage brand launches

## Vocabulary
- **Press kit page** — A landing page framed as an official announcement or news release.
- **Customer quote** — A short testimonial used as social proof.
