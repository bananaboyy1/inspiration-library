# Navy frame card and four rounded tiles pairing short slogans with smiling lifestyle photos

![](../images/print-social-navy-brand-card-collage-with-lifestyle-photos.jpg)

**Discipline:** print-social ·  · **Design style:** Geometric & Bauhaus
**Tags:** fintech, navy, tiles, lifestyle, slogans, rounded, logo-pill, bento

## What it is
A white board with a large navy panel holding a white portrait card of a woman with phone and card and a CTA, plus four square tiles: two solid navy with slogans ('Spend money with control.', 'Move money when you need it.') and two photos (car passenger, man laughing) with text and a logo pill.

## What it means
Warm and cohesive; alternating solid and photo tiles sets the brand voice.

## Techniques
- Alternate solid colour and photo tiles.
- Put the logo in a white pill on every tile.
- Keep slogans short and left-aligned.
- Frame in a bento of rounded squares.

## Best for
- Fintech social systems
- Ad tile sets
- Brand voice boards

## Vocabulary
- **Tile alternation** — Switching between colour and photo squares.
- **Logo pill** — A white capsule with the logo.
- **Brand voice tile** — A tile whose main job is a slogan.
- **Bento board** — A layout of unequal rounded tiles.
