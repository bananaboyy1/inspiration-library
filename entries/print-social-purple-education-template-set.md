# Purple gradient education post templates with pill labels

![](../images/print-social-purple-education-template-set.jpg)

**Discipline:** print-social ·  · **Design style:** Corporate & Clean-Tech
**Tags:** education, templates, purple, gradient, pills, photo, editable

## What it is
A set of six post templates for educators in a purple gradient palette: photos cropped in rounded frames, bold sans titles, capsule labels for dates and times, and quote bubbles. A banner notes 'Easy to edit'.

## What it means
Friendly and structured; gradients and pills make information cards feel digital and approachable.

## Techniques
- Use a purple gradient across all posts as the system colour.
- Turn date and time into stacked white capsules.
- Crop photos into rounded frames or arches.
- Place a quote bubble as a graphic accent.

## Best for
- Education, events and webinars
- Template marketplaces
- School or academy accounts

## Vocabulary
- **Capsule label** — A rounded pill containing brief info.
- **Gradient system** — One gradient reused across a set.
- **Rounded frame** — A photo crop with soft corners.
- **Editable template** — A layout built to be changed with new content.
