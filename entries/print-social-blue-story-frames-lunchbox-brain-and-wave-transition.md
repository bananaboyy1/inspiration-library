# Four blue vertical story frames: yellow lunchbox, mint wave transition, glowing brain and a tub with benefit chips

![](../images/print-social-blue-story-frames-lunchbox-brain-and-wave-transition.gif)

**Discipline:** print-social ·  · **Design style:** 3D & Surreal
**Tags:** stories, animated, royal-blue, mint, 3d-objects, wave, benefit-chips, supplements

## What it is
A strip of four tall royal-blue frames on crumpled paper: a serif 'Lunchbox coming back full?' with a yellow 3D lunchbox, a blank frame with a mint wave rising, a glowing pink brain with a light streak and a product tub ringed by white benefit chips with emoji.

## What it means
Playful and rhythmic; the mint wave is a transition that ties frames together.

## Techniques
- Use a rising wave as a transition.
- Show one 3D object per frame.
- Ring a product with benefit chips.
- Keep serif headlines with a green keyword.

## Best for
- Animated story ads
- Storyboard presentations
- Child-nutrition promos

## Vocabulary
- **Wave transition** — A wave shape moving up to change scenes.
- **Storyboard strip** — Frames of an animation shown in sequence.
- **Benefit chip** — A white label with an emoji and benefit.
- **Glow object** — A 3D object with a radiating light.
