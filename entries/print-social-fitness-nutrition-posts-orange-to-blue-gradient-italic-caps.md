# Fitness nutrition pack on orange-to-blue gradients with italic caps and blue label bars

![](../images/print-social-fitness-nutrition-posts-orange-to-blue-gradient-italic-caps.avif)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Social template pack · **Design style:** Gradient & Aurora
**Tags:** fitness, nutrition, gradient, orange, blue, italic

## What it is
Nine square posts on peach-orange to blue gradients with heavy italic uppercase headlines ('START TODAY', 'NEVER GIVE UP', 'TRAINING IS WELLNESS'), blue and orange label bars, X marks, dot grids and occasional photos of a woman training.

## What it means
Energetic and sporty; italic type leans forward like motion.

## Techniques
- Use heavy italic caps to imply speed.
- Put subheads in solid colour bars.
- Blend a warm-to-cool gradient behind type.
- Use X marks and dots as sporty ornaments.

## Best for
- Gym motivation posts
- Nutrition coaching
- Sports brands

## Vocabulary
- **Italic caps** — Slanted uppercase letters.
- **Label bar** — A solid bar behind a short line.
