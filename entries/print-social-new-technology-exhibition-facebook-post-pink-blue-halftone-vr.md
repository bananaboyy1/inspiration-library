# 'New Technology Exhibition' Facebook post with a pink-blue neon VR photo and radial halftone dots

![](../images/print-social-new-technology-exhibition-facebook-post-pink-blue-halftone-vr.avif)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Social ad · **Design style:** Dark & Neon
**Tags:** tech-exhibition, vr, pink, blue, halftone, facebook-ad

## What it is
A pink-bordered dark post with a neon pink-and-blue lit photo of a man wearing a VR headset, a 'Limited Availability' pill tag, bold 'NEW TECHNOLOGY EXHIBITION' headline in white and pink, body copy, and a radial halftone dot pattern fading across the right side.

## What it means
Exciting and futuristic; neon gel lighting on the VR photo makes a tech exhibition feel like a nightlife event.

## Techniques
- Light a product photo with dual neon gel colours.
- Fade a radial halftone dot pattern as background texture.
- Mark urgency with a small pill tag near the headline.

## Best for
- Tech exhibitions and expos
- VR and gadget marketing
- Neon-lit event promotion

## Vocabulary
- **Neon gel lighting** — Coloured lighting used to tint a photo dramatically.
- **Radial halftone** — A dot pattern fading outward or in one direction.
