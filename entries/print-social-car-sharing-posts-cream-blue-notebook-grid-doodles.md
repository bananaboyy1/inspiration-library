# Car sharing pack on a notebook-grid cream background with cobalt blue swoosh shapes and hand-drawn cars

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Social template pack · **Design style:** Playful & Illustrated
**Tags:** car-sharing, cream, blue, notebook-grid, doodle, instagram

## What it is
Nine square posts on a faint graph-paper grid, with a big blue swoosh ribbon shape, hand-drawn line-art cars, gear icons, a vertical 'Car Sharing' rotated tag with a car icon, bold headlines ('Car Sharing To Cut Gas Emissions In Half!', 'Top 5 Benefits Of Car Sharing') and a 'Know More' arrow link.

## What it means
Casual and approachable; the notebook grid and doodle cars make an eco-transport pitch feel like a friendly sketch rather than a corporate pitch.

## Techniques
- Use a faint graph-paper grid as a background texture.
- Rotate a small tag label along one edge.
- Draw the product (a car) as a simple line-art doodle.

## Best for
- Car sharing and mobility apps
- Eco-transport campaigns
- Sketchbook-style social content

## Vocabulary
- **Graph-paper grid** — A faint grid pattern mimicking notebook paper.
- **Swoosh ribbon** — A curved flowing band shape used for movement or connection.
