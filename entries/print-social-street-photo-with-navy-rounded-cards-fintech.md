# Low-angle street portrait with a navy tile and a frosted glass tile carrying fintech slogans

![](../images/print-social-street-photo-with-navy-rounded-cards-fintech.jpg)

**Discipline:** print-social ·  · **Design style:** Glassmorphism
**Tags:** campaign, photography, navy, glass-tile, fintech, italic-serif, dollar-orb, rounded-square

## What it is
A candid low-angle photo of a woman in a blue cardigan looking at her phone in a station. A solid navy rounded square with 'Spend with more control.' (with italic first word) overlaps the left, and a frosted glass tile with a glossy dollar orb and 'One wallet. More Control.' sits at the right.

## What it means
Warm and reassuring; solid and glass tiles show two moods of the same brand.

## Techniques
- Overlay a solid colour tile and a glass tile on one photo.
- Italicise one word in a headline.
- Colour-match the photo's clothing to the brand navy.
- Place a glass orb icon as a small graphic.

## Best for
- Fintech ad campaigns
- Photo-led brand visuals
- Two-tile social layouts

## Vocabulary
- **Glass tile** — A translucent rounded square over a photo.
- **Solid tile** — An opaque colour panel carrying text.
- **Italic first word** — Setting the opening word in italic for contrast.
- **Colour-matched wardrobe** — Clothing chosen to echo brand colours.
