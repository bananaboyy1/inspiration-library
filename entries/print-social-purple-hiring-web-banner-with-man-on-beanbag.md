# Purple 'We Are Hiring!' web banner with a man pointing from a purple beanbag

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Recruitment post · **Design style:** Corporate & Clean-Tech
**Tags:** hiring, recruitment, purple, orange, beanbag, web-banner

## What it is
A wide purple web banner with an orange and white headline ('WE ARE HIRING! Join Our CREATIVE & Professional Team!'), orange icon contact rows, a translucent 'Open Positions' panel, and a smiling man with headphones on a purple beanbag pointing at the list with a laptop on his lap.

## What it means
Casual and fun; the beanbag and pointing gesture promise a relaxed creative culture.

## Techniques
- Have the subject point toward the key content.
- Match furniture to the background colour.
- Pair orange accents with a purple base.

## Best for
- Careers page heroes
- Creative agency hiring
- Web banners

- **Directional gesture** — A pose pointing the eye toward content.
