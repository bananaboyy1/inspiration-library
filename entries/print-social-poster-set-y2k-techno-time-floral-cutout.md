# Magenta/navy Y2K 'Techno Time' poster set with floral photo cutouts

![](../images/print-social-poster-set-y2k-techno-time-floral-cutout.avif)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Typographic posters · **Design style:** Retro & Pixel
**Tags:** poster set, y2k, magenta navy, floral photo cutout, vertical bold type

## What it is
Four bold magenta-bordered vertical posters mixing navy grid panels, orange/purple photo-cutout flowers, and a large vertical 'TECHNO TIME' sidebar headline repeated across the set.

## What it means
Cutting real flower photography against flat graphic panels bridges organic and digital aesthetics, embodying the 'techno' theme through contrast rather than literal tech imagery.

## Techniques
- run a bold headline vertically along one edge as a repeating structural sidebar across a poster series
- juxtapose real photographic cutouts against flat grid/color panels for organic-vs-digital contrast
- use a thick solid-color border to unify visually varied interior panels within one series

## Best for
- Y2K/rave or techno event posters
- retro-futuristic brand campaigns
- bold vertical poster series templates

## Vocabulary
- **photo cutout** — a photographic element isolated from its background and placed directly into a flat graphic composition
