# Dark Social Media Slide with Post and Story

![](../images/app-dark-social-media-slide-with-post-story-and-lifestyle-photo.jpg)

**Discipline:** app ·  · **Design style:** Photographic & Cinematic
**Tags:** social-media, dark, story, post, photography, progress-bar, travel

## What it is
A dark slate slide titled Social Media with a plane progress bar, a paragraph, an Instagram-style post with a hiker and script 'Explore More', a blue story frame with a plane and sunrise and a Swipe Up button, and a wide lifestyle photo of a man on a sofa with a laptop.

## What it means
Showing post, story and lifestyle photography together proves the brand voice across social, and the dark slide makes the bright images pop.

## Techniques
- Instagram post and story frames as mockups
- Script type over travel photography
- Story overlapping the lifestyle photo
- Blue brand overlay on the story
- Dark slate ground to lift the colour

## Best for
- Social media sections in brand case studies
- Travel brand social content
- Post and story mockup boards

## Vocabulary
- **Story frame** — A vertical panel with story-style chrome.
- **Script overlay** — Handwriting-style lettering laid over a photo.
- **Lifestyle photo** — A photo of people in a natural setting using or evoking the product.
