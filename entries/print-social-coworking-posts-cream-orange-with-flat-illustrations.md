# Cream coworking pack with orange bars, squiggle lines and flat people illustrations

![](../images/print-social-coworking-posts-cream-orange-with-flat-illustrations.avif)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Social template pack · **Design style:** Playful & Illustrated
**Tags:** coworking, cream, orange, squiggle, flat-illustration, pricing, instagram

## What it is
Nine cream square posts for a coworking brand: big orange headlines ('We Are Here To Help You'), a red squiggly line, flat illustrations of people on laptops, pricing boxes (Daily, Monthly, Annually), stacked orange/yellow/black bars and orange footer strips.

## What it means
Warm, casual and friendly; the scribble and flat figures make office space feel relaxed.

## Techniques
- Draw one loose squiggle as a signature accent.
- Use outlined boxes for plan options.
- Pair flat figure illustrations with lots of cream space.
- Finish every post with the same coloured footer strip.

## Best for
- Coworking spaces
- Freelancer services
- Friendly B2B offers

## Vocabulary
- **Squiggle** — A loose hand-drawn wavy line.
- **Footer strip** — A coloured band along a post's bottom edge.
