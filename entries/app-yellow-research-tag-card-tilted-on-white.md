# Tilted Yellow Research Card

![](../images/app-yellow-research-tag-card-tilted-on-white.gif)

**Discipline:** app ·  · **Design style:** Warm & Organic
**Tags:** process, yellow, tilted-card, research, white, tags

## What it is
A mostly white wide frame with a tilted rounded yellow card at the left listing Research activities such as data analysis, user interviews, problem statement, persona and journey in white text.

## What it means
A single coloured card on white is an eye-catching label, and the tilt adds motion, as if mid-animation.

## Techniques
- Tilted coloured card as a title element
- Small white text in scattered columns on the card
- Large blank space to allow an animation or content to arrive
- One warm yellow against white

## Best for
- Process stage cards
- Animated case-study intros
- Minimal step labels

## Vocabulary
- **Tilted card** — A card rotated slightly for energy.
- **Stage card** — A card naming one phase of a process.
- **Negative space** — Empty area around content.
