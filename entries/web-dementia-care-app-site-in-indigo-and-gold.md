# Care-app landing page with indigo-to-white fade, gold cards and a large warm portrait

![](../images/web-dementia-care-app-site-in-indigo-and-gold.jpg)

**Discipline:** web ·  · **Design style:** Editorial
**Tags:** healthcare, indigo, gold, serif, portrait, stat-cards, tilted-cards, testimonials

## What it is
A tall board with a long page and a second column. The hero has an indigo night gradient with a smiling older woman, italic serif 'Care, from anywhere.', store badges and a laurel. Below are a phone with orbit icons, tilted gold and indigo stat cards (94%, 82%, +90%), numbered step cards, tinted testimonial photos and a knowledge carousel.

## What it means
Warm, dignified and reassuring; gold on indigo feels caring rather than clinical.

## Techniques
- Fade an indigo hero into white.
- Tint testimonial photos in the two brand colours.
- Tilt stat cards in gold and indigo.
- Use serif type for headings.

## Best for
- Healthcare and elder-care apps
- Emotionally sensitive products
- Two-colour tinted photography

## Vocabulary
- **Tinted testimonial** — A quote photo coloured in brand hues.
- **Tilted stat card** — A rotated card with a big percentage.
- **Night-to-day fade** — A dark hero blending to white.
- **Orbit icons** — Small icons circling a device.
