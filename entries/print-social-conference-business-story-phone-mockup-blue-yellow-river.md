# 'Conference. Business' story shown in a phone mockup with a flowing blue-and-yellow river shape

![](../images/print-social-conference-business-story-phone-mockup-blue-yellow-river.avif)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Social ad · **Design style:** Geometric & Bauhaus
**Tags:** conference, story, blue, yellow, river-shape, phone-mockup

## What it is
An Instagram story shown inside a phone outline: a flowing indigo river shape winding down a mustard-yellow field, 'Conference. Business' in black and white, a 'Never Stop Learning' quote in giant quotation marks, and a 'June 2021' date tag, with the same river motif echoed outside the phone frame.

## What it means
Fluid and confident; one winding shape carries the whole story from top to bottom like a single brushstroke.

## Techniques
- Let one flowing shape wind through the entire vertical frame.
- Echo the story's motif outside the phone mockup for context.
- Pair a large quotation mark graphic with a short quote.

## Best for
- Conference and event promotion
- Instagram story templates
- Two-colour brand systems

## Vocabulary
- **River shape** — A winding organic form flowing through a layout.
- **Phone mockup frame** — An outline showing content as it would appear on a device.
