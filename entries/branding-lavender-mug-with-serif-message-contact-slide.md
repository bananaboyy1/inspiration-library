# Lavender Mug Contact Slide

![](../images/branding-lavender-mug-with-serif-message-contact-slide.jpg)

**Discipline:** branding · **Design type:** Merch and packaging
**Tags:** mug, merch, lavender, serif, contact, dashed-line, closing

## What it is
A lavender gradient slide with a white mug printed in navy serif 'A gentle reminder to enjoy your day!' floating at right, dashed curves and dots around it, and a white 'Get in touch!' call to action bottom left.

## What it means
A branded mug turns a contact slide into a warm, tactile moment, and the gentle serif keeps the tone kind.

## Techniques
- Product object as a sign-off hero
- Navy serif text on white ceramic
- Dashed curved lines threading the scene
- Italic serif call to action
- Lavender gradient deepening at the base

## Best for
- Contact and closing slides
- Merch showcases
- Gentle wellness brands

## Vocabulary
- **Merch mockup** — A branded everyday object shown as a product.
- **Dashed thread** — A dashed line that guides the eye across a slide.
- **Italic serif CTA** — A call to action set in italic serif type.
