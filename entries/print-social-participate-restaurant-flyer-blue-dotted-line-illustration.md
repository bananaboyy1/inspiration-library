# 'Participate! Share our food dishes and win' flyer with blue dotted-line doodles over a real dining photo

![](../images/print-social-participate-restaurant-flyer-blue-dotted-line-illustration.avif)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Social ad · **Design style:** Playful & Illustrated
**Tags:** restaurant, contest, blue, dotted-line, doodle, flyer

## What it is
A blue-bordered white flyer with a bold coral 'Participate!' headline, a photo of two people sharing sushi and taking phone photos of their food, overlaid with blue dotted-line doodles: a grid, a jellyfish, a bowl outline and a starburst pointing at the plate.

## What it means
Playful and social; simple dotted doodles turn an ordinary dining photo into an active call to share and participate.

## Techniques
- Overlay simple dotted-line doodles onto a real photo.
- Point a starburst doodle at the exact detail to notice.
- Keep the doodle colour separate from the photo for clarity.

## Best for
- Restaurant social contests
- User-generated content campaigns
- Doodle-accented photo marketing

## Vocabulary
- **Dotted-line doodle** — A simple broken-line drawing overlaid on other content.
- **User-generated content** — Content created and shared by customers rather than the brand.
