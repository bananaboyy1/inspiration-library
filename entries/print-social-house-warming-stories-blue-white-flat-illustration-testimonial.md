# 'Warm Welcome Guaranteed' house warming story set in blue and white with flat illustration and a client photo testimonial

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Story set · **Design style:** Playful & Illustrated
**Tags:** house-warming, event-planning, blue, white, flat-illustration, testimonial

## What it is
Five vertical story cards alternating blue and white, with a photo of a cosy living room, flat illustrations of a house and a checklist (decoration, catering, coordination), a photo of a smiling client with a star-rating quote, and headlines ('Your Home, Your Party', 'Embrace Your New Home', 'Start Making Memories').

## What it means
Warm and trustworthy; pairing illustration with a real client testimonial balances friendly branding with credibility.

## Techniques
- Mix flat illustration with real testimonial photography.
- List services as a simple checklist for clarity.
- Alternate blue and white cards for rhythm across a set.

## Best for
- Event planning services
- House warming and moving campaigns
- Testimonial-led marketing

## Vocabulary
- **Testimonial card** — A card featuring a real customer's photo and quote.
- **Service checklist** — A short list outlining what is included in an offering.
