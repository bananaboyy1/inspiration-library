# 'We are Creative Digital Marketing Agency' post with yellow and white rounded panels and an orange strategy checklist

![](../images/print-social-yellow-and-orange-marketing-agency-post-with-strategy-checklist.jpg)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Social ad · **Design style:** Corporate & Clean-Tech
**Tags:** agency, yellow, orange, checklist, script, portrait

## What it is
A square light grey post with a mustard rounded panel carrying a white script 'We are Creative' over condensed black 'DIGITAL MARKETING AGENCY' and a script quote, a white rounded panel behind a smiling woman holding a coffee, an orange 'Our Strategy' checklist box, a yellow logo pill and a dark contact bar.

## What it means
Warm and credible; layered rounded panels and a checklist feel organised and friendly.

## Techniques
- Layer two rounded panels in different colours behind the subject.
- Pair condensed caps with script accents.
- Show services as a ticked checklist box.
- Anchor contacts in a dark bottom bar.

## Best for
- Marketing agencies
- Consultants
- Service ads

## Vocabulary
- **Layered panels** — Overlapping shapes creating depth.
- **Checklist box** — A block listing items with ticks.
