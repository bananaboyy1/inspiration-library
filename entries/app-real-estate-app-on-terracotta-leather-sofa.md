# Cream real-estate app photographed leaning on a terracotta leather cushion

![](../images/app-real-estate-app-on-terracotta-leather-sofa.jpg)

**Discipline:** app ·  · **Design style:** Warm & Organic
**Tags:** real-estate, mockup, warm, terracotta, leather, cream-ui, photographic, gold-phone

## What it is
A gold-framed phone leaning on a quilted terracotta leather sofa in soft light. The screen is cream with a greeting, a row of round property avatars, a search field and two property cards with photos.

## What it means
Cosy, warm and upscale; the material and colour of the setting match the interface palette.

## Techniques
- Pick a real scene whose colour matches the UI (terracotta and cream).
- Use round photo avatars as a horizontal filter.
- Keep the UI light with subtle borders and big photo cards.
- Use natural side lighting and shallow depth.

## Best for
- Real-estate, home and lifestyle apps
- App launch imagery
- Warm neutral palettes

## Vocabulary
- **Scene matching** — Choosing a mockup setting that mirrors the UI palette.
- **Avatar filter row** — A row of circular images acting as category filters.
- **Property card** — A card showing a listing photo and key facts.
- **Warm neutral** — Cream, tan and terracotta tones.
