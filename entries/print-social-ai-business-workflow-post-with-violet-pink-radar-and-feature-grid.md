# 'AI that fits your business workflow' post with a violet-pink radar arc and five-feature grid

![](../images/print-social-ai-business-workflow-post-with-violet-pink-radar-and-feature-grid.avif)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Social educational post · **Design style:** Gradient & Aurora
**Tags:** ai, saas, violet, pink, radar, features

## What it is
A square dark post with a violet-to-magenta glow, concentric arcs and a light wedge above a horizon line with a white org-chart icon circle, a centred white headline, and a thin-ruled grid of five features with small colourful icons (Instant Setup, Seamless Integration, Adapts to Your Process, Flexible Across Teams, Business-Ready Results).

## What it means
Modern and confident; the radar suggests scanning your business, and the grid gives quick benefits.

## Techniques
- Place a semicircle radar above a horizon line.
- Divide features with thin rules instead of cards.
- Use tiny coloured icons on a dark field.
- Glow a violet-pink gradient behind the hero.

## Best for
- AI product ads
- B2B SaaS
- Feature overview posts

## Vocabulary
- **Ruled grid** — Content separated by thin lines.
- **Horizon line** — A horizontal rule anchoring a graphic.
