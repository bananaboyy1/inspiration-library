# Periwinkle to Navy Blurred Gradient

![](../images/web-indigo-to-navy-blurred-gradient-background.jpg)

**Discipline:** web ·  · **Design style:** Glassmorphism
**Tags:** gradient, indigo, navy, blur, background, dashed-line

## What it is
A wide image of a soft blurred gradient from bright periwinkle at the top through deep indigo to near-black navy at the bottom, with a faint dashed arc at the right.

## What it means
A smooth light-to-dark fall feels like dusk and gives depth for text or dividers.

## Techniques
- Blur two hues into a single smooth transition
- Fade from light to very dark toward the bottom
- Keep a faint dashed line as a subtle detail
- Use it as a footer or section transition
- No hard edges anywhere

## Best for
- Section transitions
- Hero backgrounds
- Colour references for dark-mode tech brands

## Vocabulary
- **Blurred gradient** — A smooth colour blend with no visible shapes.
- **Dusk gradient** — A transition from light to dark like fading daylight.
- **Transition band** — A gradient strip linking two sections.
