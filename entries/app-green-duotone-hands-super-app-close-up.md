# Green Duotone Hands with Super-App Home

![](../images/app-green-duotone-hands-super-app-close-up.jpg)

**Discipline:** app · **Design type:** Device mockup scene
**Tags:** green, duotone, hands, super-app, illustration, dark, touch

## What it is
A phone held in two hands, a finger about to tap, all rendered in a green duotone glow on a dark green gradient, showing a super-app home screen with a city illustration, wallet balance and service icons.

## What it means
Tinting the hands and backdrop in one green makes the colorful UI the only full-color element, so the screen glows. The touching finger adds anticipation.

## Techniques
- Duotone treatment for photographic hands so the UI is the sole colour source
- Finger approaching a target for narrative tension
- Dark radial gradient with subtle dot texture
- Illustrated header art inside the app for character
- Grid of glossy 3D service icons

## Best for
- Super-app and mobility products
- Hero shots that focus attention on the interface
- Green or fintech brand launches

## Vocabulary
- **Duotone** — An image rendered in two tones or one hue range.
- **Touch moment** — A frame capturing a finger about to interact.
- **Service grid** — A grid of icons each opening a different service.
