# Month calendar with coloured dash markers and pill tab bar

![](../images/app-monthly-calendar-with-colour-dashes-in-hand.png)

**Discipline:** app · **Design type:** Calendar and planner app
**Tags:** calendar, planner, light-ui, colour-dashes, segmented-control, blue-accent, in-hand, angled

## What it is
A photograph of a hand tilting a phone showing a light-grey monthly calendar. Days show short coloured dashes for events, today is a black circle, a day/week/month segmented control has a blue selected pill, and a bottom bar has a floating blue calendar tab.

## What it means
Airy and clear; small colour marks avoid clutter while showing density.

## Techniques
- Mark events as thin coloured dashes under dates to show load without text.
- Use a segmented control with one blue pill to show the current view.
- Highlight today with a solid black circle.
- Rotate the phone in a hand scene on a blurred warm background for depth.

## Best for
- Calendar, scheduling and planning apps
- Minimal productivity UI
- Realistic phone photos in portfolios

## Vocabulary
- **Event dash** — A short coloured line under a date marking an event.
- **Segmented control** — A compact toggle among a few views.
- **Today marker** — A distinct circle around the current date.
- **Angled mockup** — A device photographed at a tilt.
