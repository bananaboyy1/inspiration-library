# 'Bring Your Brand To Life' poster in pink and blue with a framed desk photo and stacked 3D-shadow type

![](../images/print-social-digimarkt-bring-your-brand-to-life-poster-pink-blue.avif)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Poster · **Design style:** Corporate & Clean-Tech
**Tags:** agency, poster, pink, blue, 3d-type, desk-photo

## What it is
A pink-bordered blue poster with a white-framed desk photo (hands typing beside charts and a compass), a small sign-up URL line, giant white condensed headline 'BRING YOUR BRAND TO LIFE' with a blue drop shadow on each letter, a tagline 'Crafting Digital Journeys', topographic contour-line art and a paper-plane logo.

## What it means
Energetic and confident; the layered shadow type gives a simple poster real punch without extra imagery.

## Techniques
- Give headline type a offset colour shadow for a 3D pop.
- Frame a supporting photo in a clean white border.
- Add topographic contour lines as light background texture.

## Best for
- Marketing agency posters
- Sign-up campaign flyers
- Bold headline-led designs

## Vocabulary
- **Drop shadow type** — Lettering with an offset colour shadow for a layered, 3D look.
- **Contour line art** — Thin curved lines resembling a topographic map.
