## What it is
A rounded phone-shaped card in dark navy with a flat illustration of two people beside a giant megaphone in front of an oversized phone, speech bubbles, scattered confetti shapes, placeholder 'Lorem ipsum' headline and a white 'Register now' pill button.

## What it means
Energetic and generic-friendly; the placeholder text shows how a flat illustration template is meant to be reused across many campaigns.

## Techniques
- Frame a promo illustration inside a rounded phone-card shape.
- Scatter simple confetti shapes for casual energy.
- Keep the CTA button contrast-white against a dark card.

## Best for

- Flat illustration marketing
