# Purple April Fools' story set with a 'TAP to claim your prize' fake-giveaway prank card

![](../images/print-social-april-fools-instagram-stories-purple-tap-to-claim-prank.avif)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Story set · **Design style:** Playful & Illustrated
**Tags:** april-fools, stories, purple, prank, interactive, typography

## What it is
Five purple story cards with a party-horn icon: a giveaway card with a large 'TAP' circle button and 'Gotcha. No prize. But hey, you won our respect.' beneath, and four more cards pranking with a silent audio waveform, a fake 'new video', an unreadable QR-style pattern and a fake new-message notification, each tagged 'April Fool's Day'.

## What it means
Witty and interactive; mimicking real UI patterns (tap buttons, notifications) makes the prank feel like an actual app moment.

## Techniques
- Mimic real app UI (buttons, notifications) for a convincing prank.
- Reveal the joke's payoff only after the fake interaction.
- Use one consistent badge label across all prank variations.

## Best for
- April Fools' social campaigns
- Interactive story content
- UI-mimicking marketing

## Vocabulary
- **Fake interaction** — A mimicked UI element used for comedic or marketing effect.
- **Payoff line** — The punchline text revealed after an interaction.
