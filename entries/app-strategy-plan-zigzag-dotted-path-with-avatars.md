# Strategy Plan on a Zigzag Dotted Path

![](../images/app-strategy-plan-zigzag-dotted-path-with-avatars.jpg)

**Discipline:** app · **Design type:** Process slide
**Tags:** strategy, zigzag, avatars, dotted-line, grey, quotes, tone-shift

## What it is
A grey slide titled Strategy Plan where a dotted line snakes down the page connecting three circular portraits, each above a paragraph with dark and grey phrases, a name and a role.

## What it means
A winding line turns a list of contributors into a story, and the grey phrases emphasise key decisions.

## Techniques
- Dotted path zigzagging between portraits
- Alternating left and right text blocks
- Dark and grey phrase shifts
- Name and role captions beneath
- Large empty space between stops

## Best for
- Strategy and process slides
- Stakeholder input summaries
- Storytelling with people

## Vocabulary
- **Zigzag path** — A line that alternates direction to link items.
- **Stakeholder quote** — A statement attributed to a team member.
- **Portrait node** — A circular photo used as a stop on a path.
