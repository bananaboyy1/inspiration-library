# Public transportation pack in blue and yellow with arch-framed commuter photos and line icons

![](../images/print-social-public-transportation-posts-blue-and-yellow-with-arch-photos.avif)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Social template pack · **Design style:** Corporate & Clean-Tech
**Tags:** transport, travel, blue, yellow, arch, icons

## What it is
Five white posts with royal-blue diagonal bands and yellow-outlined arch and circle photo frames of masked commuters on buses and trains, bold blue headlines ('Public Transportation', 'Avoid Traffic Jams'), blue circle badges ('Book now', '$20 per week'), yellow URL pills and thin line icons of trains, pins and planes.

## What it means
Clear and civic; blue and yellow feel like transit signage.

## Techniques
- Frame photos in arches with a yellow outline.
- Use diagonal blue bands for motion.
- Put prices and CTAs in circle badges.
- Scatter thin line transport icons.

## Best for
- Transit agencies
- Travel booking
- Commuter services

## Vocabulary
- **Wayfinding palette** — Colours borrowed from signage for clarity.
- **Circle badge** — A round label for offers or CTAs.
