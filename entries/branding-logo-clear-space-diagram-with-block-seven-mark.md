# Logo clear-space grid with X, X/2 and X/4 measurements around a block mark and blocky wordmark

![](../images/branding-logo-clear-space-diagram-with-block-seven-mark.jpg)

**Discipline:** branding · **Design type:** Logo guidelines
**Tags:** logo, guidelines, clear-space, grid, black, grey, stencil-type, construction

## What it is
A light grey page with dashed grid lines, a black quarter-circle-and-block symbol with a dot and a custom stencil-like wordmark, surrounded by shaded margins labelled X/2, X/4 and a dimension arrow marked X.

## What it means
Precise and systematic; showing the rules behind the logo signals maturity.

## Techniques
- Show clear space as shaded margins with fractional labels.
- Use a dashed grid over pale grey.
- Design a custom wordmark from stencil-cut rounded blocks.
- Keep the diagram in black and grey.

## Best for
- Brand guidelines
- Logo usage rules
- Tech brands with modular marks

## Vocabulary
- **Clear space** — Minimum empty area around a logo.
- **X unit** — A measure based on part of the logo.
- **Stencil wordmark** — Lettering with breaks like a stencil.
- **Guideline diagram** — A drawing explaining logo rules.
