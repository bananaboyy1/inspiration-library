# Empty black rounded panel with a tiny centred caption about effortless flow

![](../images/app-black-closing-statement-strip-with-tiny-centred-caption.png)

**Discipline:** app ·  · **Design style:** Minimalist
**Tags:** closing, black, minimal, caption, caps, empty-space, case-study

## What it is
A black rounded panel with only a tiny centred uppercase paragraph near the bottom explaining the app was designed to feel effortless.

## What it means
Hushed and confident; emptiness lets a single line land.

## Techniques
- Leave most of the panel empty.
- Set a tiny caps paragraph centred low.
- Use pure black.
- Use as a breather between sections.

## Best for
- Case-study breathers
- Dark minimal decks
- Section endings

## Vocabulary
- **Breather panel** — A near-empty section giving the eye rest.
- **Micro caps** — Very small uppercase text.
- **Bottom-weighted caption** — Text placed in the lower part of a panel.
- **Void** — Large empty area used as design.
