# 'Virtual Reality or Classic Console?' esports flyer with a red-navy diagonal split and duotone controller photo

![](../images/print-social-esports-virtual-reality-classic-console-flyer-red-navy-diagonal.avif)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Social ad · **Design style:** Neo-Brutalist
**Tags:** esports, gaming, red, navy, diagonal-split, duotone

## What it is
A square flyer split diagonally between white and red, with a red-duotone photo of a hand holding a game controller at top, a black-and-white photo of a man using VR controllers below, a bold navy 'Virtual Reality or Classic Console?' headline, a 'Play to win' tag and body copy.

## What it means
Punchy and on-topic; posing VR against classic consoles as a real question makes the flyer feel like part of the gaming conversation, not just an ad.

## Techniques
- Split a layout diagonally between two contrasting fields.
- Duotone one photo while keeping another in natural greyscale for contrast.
- Frame the headline as a genuine question to the audience.

## Best for
- Esports and gaming events
- Console and VR marketing
- Bold diagonal layouts

## Vocabulary
- **Diagonal split** — A layout divided by an angled rather than straight line.
- **Rhetorical headline** — A headline phrased as a question to engage the reader.
