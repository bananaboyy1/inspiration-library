# Centred announcement: violet outlined pill 'What's new?' followed by grey 'Just released v4.3.1 >' on white

![](../images/web-whats-new-badge-with-outlined-violet-pill-and-release-link.webp)

**Discipline:** web ·  · **Design style:** Minimalist
**Tags:** component, badge, announcement, violet, outlined, white, chevron, minimal

## What it is
A white page with a single line at the centre: a thin violet-bordered capsule with violet text 'What's new?' and to its right grey text 'Just released v4.3.1' with a small chevron.

## What it means
Understated and useful; a tiny announcement line signals activity without demanding attention.

## Techniques
- Outline a coloured pill for the label.
- Follow with plain grey text.
- End with a chevron link.
- Centre with lots of white space.

## Best for
- Release announcement badges
- Hero eyebrow components
- Minimal marketing pages

## Vocabulary
- **Announcement line** — A short line announcing a release.
- **Outlined pill** — A capsule with a border only.
- **Version tag** — A label with a release number.
- **Chevron link** — A link ending with a small arrow.
