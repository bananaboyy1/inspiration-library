# Cyan and black VR banner with diagonal stripe field

![](../images/print-social-banner-cyan-black-vr-diagonal-stripes.avif)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Social banner · **Design style:** Dark & Neon
**Tags:** banner, vr, cyan black, diagonal stripes, duotone photo, tech

## What it is
A wide banner split into a black text panel and a duotone-blue VR-headset photo panel, with a field of thick diagonal cyan/black stripes filling the photo side.

## What it means
A bold diagonal stripe field gives the photographic half as much graphic energy as the flat typographic half, so neither side of the split composition feels weaker.

## Techniques
- split a banner into a flat type panel and a graphic-treated photo panel of near-equal visual weight
- duotone a photo into the brand's two core colors to match the flat panel exactly
- fill negative space with a bold diagonal stripe pattern instead of leaving it empty

## Best for
- tech/VR brand web banners
- dark-mode product launch headers
- split-layout ad templates

## Vocabulary
- **split composition** — a layout divided into two or more distinct zones, often typographic vs. photographic
