## What it is
A top-down desk photo of a person in a red patterned jacket and green cap drawing, with a red rounded panel at the bottom with a notch and 'Happy New Week. Clean slate. Strategy starts now.' plus three bullet points, an avatar with a brand name and a green 'Speak to us' pill, and a small logo and hashtag at the top.

## What it means
Warm and motivational; the red panel makes the greeting a promo card.

## Techniques
- Overlay a notched red panel over a bottom of a photo.
- Match the panel to the jacket.
- Use a green pill for the CTA.
- Add a small hashtag at the top.

## Best for
- Weekly greeting posts
- Creative-agency promos
- Photo-plus-panel layouts

## Vocabulary
- **Notched panel** — A panel with a cut-out corner.

- **CTA pill** — A capsule with an action.
