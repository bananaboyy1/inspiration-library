# Problem and solution slide with a white sphere wrapped by red and green petal segments

![](../images/app-problem-and-solution-orbit-diagram-with-red-and-green-petals.jpg)

**Discipline:** app · **Design type:** Process slide
**Tags:** problem-solution, diagram, light-grey, red, green, sphere, stats, fintech

## What it is
A light grey slide with a headline and paragraph with highlighted phrases, a soft white sphere with two red petal segments numbered 01 and 02 and one green segment 03, red connector labels 'Problem 1/2' with 68% and 36% stats and a green dot leading to a 'Solution' heading and paragraph.

## What it means
Clear and memorable; the sphere makes problems and the solution one object.

## Techniques
- Represent problems and solution as segments of a sphere.
- Colour problems red and the solution green.
- Connect with thin elbow lines to labels.
- Show big percentages beside each problem.

## Best for
- Problem and solution slides
- Fintech case studies
- Segmented-object diagrams

## Vocabulary
- **Petal segment** — A curved segment wrapping a sphere.
- **Status colour** — Red for problems, green for solutions.
- **Connector label** — A pill linked by a thin line.
- **Stat callout** — A big percentage with a caption.
