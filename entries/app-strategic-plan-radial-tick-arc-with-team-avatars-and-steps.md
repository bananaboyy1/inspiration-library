# Strategic Plan with Radial Arc and Team Avatars

![](../images/app-strategic-plan-radial-tick-arc-with-team-avatars-and-steps.png)

**Discipline:** app ·  · **Design style:** Gradient & Aurora
**Tags:** strategy, team, avatars, arc, tick-marks, steps, pale-blue

## What it is
A pale grey-blue slide with a numbered Strategic Plan pill, a large fading two-line title, a paragraph, a big arc of tick marks around a cluster of six avatars and three blue-dot nodes linked by lines to three team members with a Step 1 pill, name, role and description.

## What it means
The tick arc and radiating lines make a team feel like a coordinated system, and each role gets a simple card.

## Techniques
- Arc of fine tick marks as a circular frame
- Avatar cluster at the centre
- Thin lines to blue dot nodes at three points
- Step pills above each person
- Title fading from dark to light

## Best for
- Team and role slides
- Strategy documentation
- Radial team diagrams

## Vocabulary
- **Tick arc** — A curved row of tiny marks forming a dial-like arc.
- **Avatar cluster** — A group of small portraits arranged together.
- **Node line** — A line connecting a central group to an outer point.
