# Square-tile gradient from white to deep blue

![](../images/web-pixel-gradient-square-grid-background.webp)

**Discipline:** web · **Design type:** Background pattern
**Tags:** pattern, pixel-grid, gradient, blue, squares, background, minimal

## What it is
A rectangular pattern of small rounded squares arranged in a dense grid whose colour fades from white at the top through light blue to deep navy at the bottom, with tile sizes shrinking near the bottom.

## What it means
Calm and digital; a stepped gradient reads like a pixelated sky or sunset.

## Techniques
- Render a gradient as a grid of small tiles rather than a smooth blend.
- Vary tile size or gap along the gradient to add rhythm.
- Keep one hue family from white to navy.
- Use the pattern as a hero or divider background.

## Best for
- Hero backgrounds and section dividers
- Fintech and tech decks
- Textures for social covers and posters

## Vocabulary
- **Tile gradient** — A gradient built from discrete squares.
- **Pixel grid** — A regular grid of square cells.
- **Tonal ramp** — A smooth progression of one hue from light to dark.
- **Background texture** — A pattern that adds surface without content.
