# 'Webie' digital strategy flyer with a photo of sticky-note wireframes and a yellow '50% off' sticker

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Social ad · **Design style:** Corporate & Clean-Tech
**Tags:** web-agency, black, yellow, sticky-notes, photo, flyer

## What it is
A blue-bordered black flyer with a 'Webie' bowtie-icon logo, a photo of a designer placing coloured sticky notes and wireframe sketches on a whiteboard, a yellow starburst '50% off' sticker, a code-bracket icon, a pointing hand cutout, and a bold yellow 'Digital Strategy That Delivers' headline.

## What it means
Authentic and process-led; a real whiteboard photo proves the agency actually does the strategic work it claims to sell.

## Techniques
- Photograph real work-in-progress (sticky notes, sketches) as proof of process.
- Overlay small icon stickers (code, offer) onto the photo.
- Use a cut-out hand to add a pointing, active gesture.

## Best for
- Web design agencies
- UX/strategy service marketing
- Process-led B2B flyers

## Vocabulary
- **Process photo** — An image showing real work in progress, not a finished product.
- **Sticker overlay** — A small icon or badge layered onto a photo for extra detail.
