# Serif Headline Landing with Painterly Hero

![](../images/web-painterly-photo-cards-ai-agent-landing.png)

**Discipline:** web ·  · **Design style:** Editorial
**Tags:** serif, painterly, light, hero-image, testimonial, cards, ai

## What it is
A pale mint-white landing page in a rounded frame with a large tightly set serif headline, email capture, a painterly illustrated coastal landscape hero with a car and a floating testimonial card, and three feature cards with product UI over landscape imagery.

## What it means
The illustrative scene and serif type bring warmth and human trust to a technical product, while clean UI cards keep it credible.

## Techniques
- Tight-tracked large serif headline as the main visual
- Painterly landscape as hero backdrop with a testimonial card overlaid
- Email-in-hero capture field instead of a button alone
- Product UI cards set over blurred landscape crops
- Tabbed customer story logos underneath the hero

## Best for
- AI/CX platforms seeking warmth
- B2B products aimed at non-technical buyers
- Brands blending editorial feel and product proof

## Vocabulary
- **Hero capture** — An inline email input inside the hero.
- **Overlaid testimonial** — A quote card floated over the hero image.
- **Painterly render** — Illustration that mimics brush-based imagery.
