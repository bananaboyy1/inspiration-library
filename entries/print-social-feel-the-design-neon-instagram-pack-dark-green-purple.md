# '#FeelTheDesign' Instagram pack in black with neon green-to-purple duotone portraits and glitch grid lines

![](../images/print-social-feel-the-design-neon-instagram-pack-dark-green-purple.avif)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Social template pack · **Design style:** Dark & Neon
**Tags:** design-agency, neon, duotone, black, glitch, instagram

## What it is
Nine black square posts with neon green-to-purple duotone photos of designers, glitchy dot-grid and line textures, bold condensed headlines ('MEET THE TEAM', 'IDEAS THAT GROW', 'CREATIVE TRENDS'), a 'Workshop' pill tag, and small logo marks with hashtag branding.

## What it means
Edgy and futuristic; neon duotone and glitch textures make a design studio feel technical and forward-thinking.

## Techniques
- Duotone photos in clashing neon colours for a glitch feel.
- Add fine dot-grid and line textures as tech decoration.
- Use a consistent hashtag as a recurring brand device.

## Best for
- Design studio branding
- Tech-adjacent creative agencies
- Neon-accented social content

## Vocabulary
- **Glitch texture** — Visual noise or distortion mimicking digital error.
- **Hashtag branding** — Using a consistent hashtag as part of visual identity.
