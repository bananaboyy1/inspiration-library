# 'New App New Habits' invitation with a pink-black quadrant background and a blue phone showing a routine ring

![](../images/app-new-habits-goal-tracking-invitation-pink-black-quadrant-blue-phone.avif)

**Discipline:** app · **Design type:** Case study cover · **Design style:** Neo-Brutalist
**Tags:** habit-tracking, invitation, pink, black, quadrant, phone-mockup

## What it is
A pink invitation split into black and cream quadrant blocks, with a bold 'NEW APP NEW HABITS' headline, a small 'S' logo, an electric-blue phone mockup showing a '25 MIN' progress ring and a routine timeline (Wake Up, Running, Yoga), body copy, a download link and a bold '#NewHabits' hashtag footer.

## What it means
Bold and motivating; the high-contrast quadrant background makes a calm habit-tracking app announcement feel energetic and current.

## Techniques
- Split a background into contrasting quadrant blocks.
- Show one clear hero metric (a progress ring) on the app screen.
- Anchor the invitation with a bold branded hashtag footer.

## Best for
- Habit and wellness app launches
- App invitation templates
- Bold quadrant branding

## Vocabulary
- **Quadrant background** — A layout split into four contrasting colour or texture blocks.
- **Routine timeline** — A vertical list of scheduled activities with times.
