# Pixel-step F symbol in violet gradient with white wordmark on dark plum diagonals

![](../images/branding-pixel-f-mark-with-purple-gradient-on-dark-diagonals.jpg)

**Discipline:** branding · **Design type:** Logo with symbol
**Tags:** logo, symbol, pixel, violet, gradient, dark, fintech, diagonal-bands

## What it is
A dark plum background of diagonal soft bands with a violet F built from stepping squares and a rounded top, beside a white bold sans wordmark 'Fintech'.

## What it means
Digital and progressive; pixel steps suggest data and growth.

## Techniques
- Build a letter with square pixel steps.
- Fill with a lilac-to-violet gradient.
- Use diagonal tonal bands as backdrop.
- Pair with a round geometric sans.

## Best for
- Fintech and data brands
- Pixel-inspired marks
- Dark-mode logo displays

## Vocabulary
- **Pixel step** — A stair-like edge made of squares.
- **Gradient symbol** — A symbol filled with a colour blend.
- **Tonal diagonal** — Angled bands in near-identical dark shades.
- **Initial mark** — A logo based on a first letter.
