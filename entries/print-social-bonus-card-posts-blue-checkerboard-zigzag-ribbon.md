# 'Bonus Card' Instagram pack in cobalt blue with a checkerboard-and-zigzag ribbon and red/yellow corner stars

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Social template pack · **Design style:** Retro & Pixel
**Tags:** bonus-card, fintech, blue, checkerboard, zigzag, retro

## What it is
Nine cobalt blue square posts with a black-yellow-pink zigzag ribbon and checkerboard band running diagonally, red and yellow starburst corner tags, black-and-white photos of hands holding a card and phone, and bold headlines ('When Bills Go Smart', 'Buy Smart With Our Card', 'Be Smart, Use the Card').

## What it means
Retro and confident; a bold graphic ribbon across every post gives a fintech product an unexpectedly playful identity.

## Techniques
- Run one diagonal zigzag-and-checkerboard ribbon across an entire post set.
- Use small starburst corner tags as consistent branding.
- Keep photos greyscale so the ribbon carries all the colour.

## Best for
- Fintech and card products
- Retail loyalty cards
- Retro-styled financial branding

## Vocabulary
- **Zigzag ribbon** — A diagonal decorative band with a jagged edge.
- **Starburst tag** — A small spiky badge shape used as a corner accent.
