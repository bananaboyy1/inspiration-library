# Two portrait ads: card-over-eye in navy with #1 laurel badge, and 'Move smarter' with a terminal

![](../images/print-social-navy-and-pale-blue-fintech-ad-pair-with-laurel-badge.jpg)

**Discipline:** print-social ·  · **Design style:** Neo-Brutalist
**Tags:** fintech, ads, navy, pale-blue, laurel, italic-serif, portrait, card-terminal

## What it is
A pair of rounded ads. The left: navy background with a woman in a navy sweater holding a bank card over one eye, the logo, a white laurel wreath with '#1 4.9' and an Explore button. The right: pale blue with a big navy 'Move smarter.' (italic serif second word) beside a woman using a card terminal.

## What it means
Playful yet credible; the card-over-eye pose is memorable and the laurel adds proof.

## Techniques
- Colour the wardrobe like the background.
- Use a laurel badge as social proof.
- Mix sans and italic serif in a headline.
- Crop tightly on props like terminals.

## Best for
- Fintech ad creative
- Portrait ad pairs
- Proof-driven app promotion

## Vocabulary
- **Laurel badge** — A wreath around a rating or rank.
- **Playful pose** — A gesture such as covering an eye with a card.
- **Wardrobe matching** — Clothing coloured like the brand.
- **Tight crop** — Framing a detail closely.
