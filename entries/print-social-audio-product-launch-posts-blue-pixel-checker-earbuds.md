# 'Visit The Product Launch!' audio pack in royal blue with pixel checker corners and earbud product photos

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Social template pack · **Design style:** Corporate & Clean-Tech
**Tags:** audio, product-launch, blue, pixel-checker, earbuds, instagram

## What it is
Five royal blue square posts with white pixel-checkerboard corner patterns, a small asterisk logo, photos of wireless earbuds and headphones, bold headlines ('Visit The Product Launch!', 'The Future of Audio', 'Opening Discount'), dates, venue details and pricing ('$149', '$499').

## What it means
Clean and premium; pixel checker corners give a straightforward product launch a distinct, techy signature.

## Techniques
- Use a small pixel checkerboard pattern as a consistent corner motif.
- Pair product photography with clear pricing on dedicated cards.
- Keep one saturated blue as the entire set's base colour.

## Best for
- Audio and electronics launches
- Product event marketing
- Corporate tech branding

## Vocabulary
- **Pixel checker pattern** — A small grid of alternating filled squares used as texture.
- **Pricing card** — A card pairing a product name with its price.
