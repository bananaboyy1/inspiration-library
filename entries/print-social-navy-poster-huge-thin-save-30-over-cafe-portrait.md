# Navy band with giant thin white 'Save 30%' behind a smiling man with a phone in a cafe

![](../images/print-social-navy-poster-huge-thin-save-30-over-cafe-portrait.jpg)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Social ad
**Tags:** fintech, ad, navy, thin-type, cafe, portrait, behind-subject, promo

## What it is
A wide ad with a navy top band carrying giant thin white 'Save 30%' with the subject's curly hair overlapping the digits, a small 'with payno' line and a photo of a smiling man in a navy shirt with a phone at a cafe table.

## What it means
Warm and direct; the person breaks the type, tying offer and human moment.

## Techniques
- Let the subject overlap giant thin type.
- Match clothing to the navy band.
- Put a small logo line under the number.
- Use a candid cafe photo.

## Best for
- Fintech promo banners
- Wide ads
- Type-behind-subject layouts

## Vocabulary
- **Thin giant type** — Light-weight letters at huge size.
- **Overlap subject** — A person in front of type.
- **Colour band** — A solid coloured strip at the top.
- **Candid scene** — A natural-looking photo.
