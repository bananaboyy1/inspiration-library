# 'Did You Know?' fact post with a grainy mint gradient blob and a numbered fact category

![](../images/print-social-did-you-know-post-mint-gradient-blob-grainy-texture.avif)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Social educational post · **Design style:** Gradient & Aurora
**Tags:** fact-post, mint, gradient, grainy-texture, numbered, educational

## What it is
A square post with pale mint-to-teal gradient background, a huge ghosted 'DID YOU KNOW?' headline overlapping a grainy glossy teal-and-yellow blob shape, a small corner arrow, and a numbered fact line ('04 Fact & Mystery') introducing a short trivia statement about streaming technology.

## What it means
Curious and premium; the grainy gradient blob makes a simple trivia fact feel like a polished, collectible card.

## Techniques
- Add film-grain noise to a gradient blob for texture.
- Let a giant ghosted headline overlap the hero shape.
- Number and categorise facts for a recognisable series.

## Best for
- Educational fact series
- Trivia and 'did you know' content
- Gradient-textured branding

## Vocabulary
- **Grain texture** — A fine noise texture added over a gradient or photo.
- **Ghosted headline** — Oversized, low-contrast text layered behind other content.
