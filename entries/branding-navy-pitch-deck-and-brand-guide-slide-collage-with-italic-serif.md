# Navy Pitch Deck and Guide Slide Collage

![](../images/branding-navy-pitch-deck-and-brand-guide-slide-collage-with-italic-serif.jpg)

**Discipline:** branding · **Design type:** Brand guidelines deck
**Tags:** pitch-deck, brand-guide, navy, italic-serif, slides, palette, typography, browser

## What it is
A pale grey collage of many slides for a finance brand: welcome, contents, why, the opportunity with a silk image, palette (navy and grey swatches), logo, principles, a navy statement slide, brand strategy, an opportunity chart, an Aa specimen with weights and a browser tab mockup, alternating white and navy with italic serif emphasis words.

## What it means
Italic serif accents on light sans headlines add elegance to a plain layout, and alternating navy slides give rhythm.

## Techniques
- Italic serif accent word in each headline
- Alternating white and navy slides
- Consistent header and footer chrome
- Big Aa specimen with a weight list
- Line chart with a dotted projection

## Best for
- Pitch and brand guideline decks
- Fintech identity presentations
- Slide overview boards

## Vocabulary
- **Accent word** — A word in a contrasting style within a headline.
- **Slide chrome** — Repeated header and footer details across slides.
- **Deck overview** — A collage of slides shown together.
