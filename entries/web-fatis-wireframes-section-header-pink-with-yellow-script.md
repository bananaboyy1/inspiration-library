# 'Wireframes — Where Ideas Connect' divider in white condensed type over yellow script on hot pink

![](../images/web-fatis-wireframes-section-header-pink-with-yellow-script.webp)

**Discipline:** web · **Design type:** Case study divider · **Design style:** Playful & Illustrated
**Tags:** divider, pink, yellow, script, condensed, case-study

## What it is
A wide hot pink banner with a white condensed sans 'Wireframes' overlapped by a yellow brush-script 'Where Ideas Connect'.

## What it means
Punchy and consistent; a bold title and script pair signal each new chapter.

## Techniques
- Overlap a script line across the base of a condensed title.
- Use full-colour dividers to pace a long case study.
- Repeat the same pairing for every section.

## Best for
- Case study dividers
- Chapter headers
- Brand-led presentations

## Vocabulary
- **Section divider** — A banner marking a new part of a document.
- **Type pairing** — Two typefaces used together.
