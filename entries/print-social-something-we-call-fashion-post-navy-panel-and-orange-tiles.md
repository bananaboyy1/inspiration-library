# 'Some-Thing We Call Fashion.' post with a navy panel, white-washed photo and orange and navy edge tiles

![](../images/print-social-something-we-call-fashion-post-navy-panel-and-orange-tiles.avif)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Social promo post · **Design style:** Geometric & Bauhaus
**Tags:** fashion, navy, orange, tiles, menswear, shop-now

## What it is
A square post with a photo of a smiling man in a blue blazer, partly washed in white, a tall navy panel with an orange dash and a hyphenated headline 'Some-Thing We Call Fashion.', an orange 'SHOPNOW!' tab, and small orange, navy and white rectangles scattered along the edges like a mosaic.

## What it means
Sharp and structured; edge tiles give the post a modular, architectural rhythm.

## Techniques
- Break a word with a hyphen for a stylised headline.
- Scatter rectangles along the frame edge.
- Wash part of the photo in white.
- Match the panel colour to the outfit.

## Best for
- Menswear brands
- Fashion retail
- Modular brand systems

## Vocabulary
- **Edge mosaic** — Small blocks arranged along borders.
- **White wash** — A translucent white overlay fading an image.
