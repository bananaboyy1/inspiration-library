## What it is
A desk scene of coworkers reviewing charts, overlaid with a translucent hexagonal icon network and the word REMARKETING glowing in cyan.

## What it means
Layering abstract data icons over a real photo grounds an intangible concept (digital marketing) in a relatable human moment, a common technique for B2B blog headers.

## Techniques
- overlay a glowing icon network atop a photo to visualize an abstract digital concept
- connect icons with thin lines radiating from a central glowing keyword
- desaturate the underlying photo slightly so the overlay graphics pop forward

## Best for
- marketing/adtech blog headers
- B2B service explainer graphics
- concept-driven stock photography compositions

## Vocabulary
- **overlay graphic** — a semi-transparent graphic layer placed on top of a photograph to add conceptual or data information
