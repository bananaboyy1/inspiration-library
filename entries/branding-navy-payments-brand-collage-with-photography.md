# Navy Payments Brand Collage

![](../images/branding-navy-payments-brand-collage-with-photography.jpg)

**Discipline:** branding · **Design type:** Brand application mockups
**Tags:** navy, payments, photography, logo, card, phone, rounded-tiles, monoline

## What it is
A rounded-tile brand board: a woman with a bank card over her eye, a card on navy cloth beside pasta, the flowing white 'payno' logo on navy, a browser tab, a smiling man using a phone with a balance card, and a phone banner 'Payno Goes Wherever You Go'.

## What it means
Everyday moments (dinner, commuting) make finance human, while navy and white stay disciplined. Rounded tiles echo the friendly script logo.

## Techniques
- Mixed grid of photo, logo, UI and slogan tiles
- Props and clothing dyed to the brand navy
- Balance card overlaid on photography as a UI-in-photo device
- Playful gesture photography (card over the eye)
- Consistent corner radius across all tiles

## Best for
- Fintech brand presentations
- Payment and card brands
- Identity systems that mix photography with UI

## Vocabulary
- **UI-in-photo** — A UI card overlaid directly on a photograph.
- **Tile board** — A grid of rounded panels each showing one brand asset.
- **Brand wardrobe** — Styling photo subjects in brand colors.
