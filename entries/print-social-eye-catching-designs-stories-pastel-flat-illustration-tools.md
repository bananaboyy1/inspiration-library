# 'Transforming Ideas into Eye-Catching Designs' story set in pastel with flat-illustrated design tools

![](../images/print-social-eye-catching-designs-stories-pastel-flat-illustration-tools.avif)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Story set · **Design style:** Playful & Illustrated
**Tags:** design-agency, pastel, flat-illustration, story, tools

## What it is
Four vertical story cards alternating cream, purple and pink pastels, each with a flat illustration of oversized design tools (a giant pencil, a laptop with a portrait, colour swatches, a camera and ruler), and headlines ('Need graphic design services?', 'Need Creative solutions for your company?', 'Unlock Your Brand's Visual Potential').

## What it means
Friendly and reassuring; oversized familiar tools make abstract creative services feel concrete and approachable.

## Techniques
- Scale a design tool larger than life as the hero prop.
- Alternate pastel background colours across a story set.
- Colour one key phrase within each headline for emphasis.

## Best for
- Design agency stories
- Creative service marketing
- Pastel illustrated branding

## Vocabulary
- **Oversized prop** — An object illustrated larger than realistic scale for visual impact.
- **Pastel palette** — Soft, desaturated colours used together.
