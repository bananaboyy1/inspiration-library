# BEDE design agency posts with black-and-white photo cutouts, duotone triangle panels in pink and cyan

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Social template pack · **Design style:** Neo-Brutalist
**Tags:** agency, duotone, pink, cyan, triangle, dark

## What it is
A dark navy square cover and four smaller posts for 'BEDE': a headline 'We Have the Best Designers in the World' in cyan and pink, greyscale photos of people in coats cropped against pink and cyan triangular colour-block panels, small headlines and vertical rotated taglines, closing with a pink-to-cyan gradient footer bar.

## What it means
Bold and confident; sharp triangle colour blocks against greyscale photos feel like a design agency showing off its own craft.

## Techniques
- Crop photos against angular duotone colour-block panels.
- Rotate a short tagline vertically along a panel edge.
- Close a set with a gradient footer bar tying the palette together.

## Best for
- Design agency branding
- Creative studio social content
- Bold portfolio promotion

## Vocabulary
- **Duotone panel** — A flat colour-block shape used to frame or crop a photo.
- **Gradient footer** — A colour-fading bar closing a layout.
