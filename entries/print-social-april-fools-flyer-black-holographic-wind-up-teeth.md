# 'April Fool's Is Just Around The Corner' flyer with holographic wind-up chattering teeth on black

![](../images/print-social-april-fools-flyer-black-holographic-wind-up-teeth.avif)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Event poster · **Design style:** Retro & Pixel
**Tags:** april-fools, holographic, black, novelty, typography, flyer

## What it is
A black A5 flyer with a holographic gradient ribbon wave, a red wind-up chattering-teeth toy rendered in holographic texture, a blue wind-up key, holographic star and ring shapes, and bold white condensed 'APRIL FOOL'S IS JUST AROUND THE CORNER'.

## What it means
Playful and eye-catching; the holographic foil effect on a novelty toy makes a joke holiday feel like a premium event.

## Techniques
- Render a novelty object in holographic gradient texture.
- Set bold condensed type against pure black for contrast.
- Use a wavy ribbon line to add movement across the layout.

## Best for
- Novelty and prank events
- April Fools' campaigns
- Playful flyer design

## Vocabulary
- **Holographic texture** — A shifting rainbow gradient mimicking foil or prism material.
- **Novelty object** — A playful prop chosen for its comic association.
