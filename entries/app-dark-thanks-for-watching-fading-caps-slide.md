# Thanks for Watching in Fading Caps

![](../images/app-dark-thanks-for-watching-fading-caps-slide.png)

**Discipline:** app · **Design type:** Case study closing slide
**Tags:** closing, dark, caps, fade, follow, contact, columns

## What it is
A near-black slide with giant caps 'THANKS FOR WATCHING!' fading from bright silver to nearly invisible, a blue tilted Follow pill, four category labels, a short paragraph and two contact fields boxed in blue.

## What it means
The fading letters create drama and depth, and the blue pill and contact boxes give clear next steps.

## Techniques
- Gradient fade across giant uppercase letters
- Tilted blue Follow pill overlapping the type
- Vertical hairline column guides
- Contact info in blue-boxed fields with corner marks
- Slash-prefixed service labels across the top

## Best for
- Portfolio sign-offs
- Designer and studio closing slides
- Contact calls to action

## Vocabulary
- **Fade-out type** — Lettering that loses opacity across its width.
- **Corner brackets** — Small marks at the corners of a boxed field.
- **Service label** — A short tag naming an offered service.
