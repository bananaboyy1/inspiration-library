# Frosted Onboarding Cards with 3D Coins

![](../images/app-frosted-glass-onboarding-cards-with-3d-coins-and-arrows.jpg)

**Discipline:** app · **Design type:** Onboarding screens
**Tags:** onboarding, frosted, 3d, coins, pastel, carousel, glass-cards

## What it is
A pastel iridescent field with tall frosted glass cards in a horizontal carousel, two centre cards showing a paper plane with silver coins under 'No loan offer is available right now.' and circular arrows around a bitcoin under 'Smart money insights', each with a white Let's go pill.

## What it means
Glass cards over a soft holographic gradient feel modern and gentle, and small 3D objects illustrate ideas without clutter.

## Techniques
- Tall frosted glass cards on an iridescent backdrop
- 3D objects (coins, arrows, paper plane) as illustrations
- White pill buttons at each card base
- Cropped side cards implying a carousel
- Centred sentence-case headlines

## Best for
- Fintech onboarding and feature cards
- Glassmorphism card carousels
- 3D icon illustrations

## Vocabulary
- **Glass card** — A translucent blurred card over a colourful backdrop.
- **Card carousel** — A horizontal row of cards with the neighbours cropped.
- **3D icon** — A small rendered object used as an illustration.
