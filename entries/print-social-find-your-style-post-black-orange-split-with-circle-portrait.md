# 'Find Your Style & Your Self.' post split black and orange with a circle portrait and arc segments

![](../images/print-social-find-your-style-post-black-orange-split-with-circle-portrait.avif)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Social promo post · **Design style:** Photographic & Cinematic
**Tags:** fashion, orange, black, split, circle, portrait

## What it is
A square post split vertically into black and orange, with a circle-cropped portrait of a woman straddling the split (greyscale on the black side, warm on the orange), thin white arc segments around the circle, three small squares, and a white and orange headline.

## What it means
Stylish and dual; the split portrait suggests two sides of a personality.

## Techniques
- Split the background and the portrait along the same line.
- Desaturate the half of the photo on the dark side.
- Orbit thin arc segments around a circle crop.

## Best for
- Fashion brands
- Personal styling
- Bold promo posts

## Vocabulary
- **Split composition** — A layout divided into two contrasting halves.
- **Arc segment** — A partial circular line.
