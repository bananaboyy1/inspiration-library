# Charcoal and lime 'We Are Hiring!' story with megaphones and a frosted role list

![](../images/print-social-charcoal-and-lime-hiring-story-with-glass-role-list.avif)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Recruitment post · **Design style:** Dark & Neon
**Tags:** hiring, recruitment, charcoal, lime, story, megaphone

## What it is
A vertical charcoal story with a lime and white headline (lime 'HIRING!' and 'CREATIVE', 'Professional' in a lime box), two black-and-lime megaphones, a frosted glass panel with 'Open Positions' and role bars, a lime 'Apply now' button, WhatsApp number and a solid lime footer band.

## What it means
Bold and modern; lime on charcoal feels electric and tech-savvy.

## Techniques
- Box one word of the headline in the accent colour.
- End the story with a solid accent footer band.
- Frame roles in a frosted panel between props.

## Best for
- Instagram stories
- Tech hiring
- Bold recruitment

## Vocabulary
- **Story format** — A 9:16 vertical social post.
- **Footer band** — A solid strip closing the layout.
