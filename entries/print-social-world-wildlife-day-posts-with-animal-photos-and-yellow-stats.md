# World Wildlife Day pack with close animal photos, teal angled panels and yellow population stats

![](../images/print-social-world-wildlife-day-posts-with-animal-photos-and-yellow-stats.avif)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Social template pack · **Design style:** Photographic & Cinematic
**Tags:** wildlife, conservation, teal, yellow, animals, stats

## What it is
Five square posts with striking animal photos (leopard, stag, lizard, duck, tiger), dark teal angled panels and green-to-yellow gradient bars, bold white headlines ('Save Wild Life', 'Do Good to Wildlife', 'Cage the animals who cage the animals'), and yellow stats ('3,900 Tigers', '6,000 Species').

## What it means
Urgent and inspiring; real animals plus hard numbers make conservation personal.

## Techniques
- Pair a powerful animal photo with a single stat.
- Use angled panels and gradient bars for motion.
- Put key numbers in bold yellow.

## Best for
- Conservation campaigns
- NGO social posts
- Awareness days

## Vocabulary
- **Awareness stat** — A number that makes an issue tangible.
- **Angled panel** — A slanted shape holding text.
