# Indigo Engagement Hub Long Sheet

![](../images/app-indigo-engagement-hub-long-sheet-with-floating-cards.jpg)

**Discipline:** app · **Design type:** Case study slide
**Tags:** engagement, indigo, floating-cards, hands, dashed-path, tote-bag, long-scroll

## What it is
A tall lavender sheet with a Solving UX pill, tilted Challenge and Solution cards on a winding dashed path, hand-held indigo wellness tracker phones, floating reminder cards (pills, walk the dog, mood) and a tote bag with a serif message.

## What it means
Floating cards along a dashed path show gentle reminders in motion, and a merch bag extends the brand into the real world.

## Techniques
- Winding dashed path linking elements
- Floating reminder cards at varied sizes
- Blurred hands holding phones
- Tilted Challenge and Solution cards
- Merch item as a brand extension

## Best for
- Wellness app case studies
- Engagement feature explanations
- Long-scroll storytelling

## Vocabulary
- **Floating reminder** — A small card representing a notification or task.
- **Dashed path** — A dashed line guiding the eye through a page.
- **Brand extension** — Applying a brand to an extra product like a bag.
