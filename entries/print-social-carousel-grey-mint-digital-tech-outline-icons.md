# Grey and mint digital-technology carousel with outline icons

![](../images/print-social-carousel-grey-mint-digital-tech-outline-icons.avif)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Social template pack · **Design style:** Corporate & Clean-Tech
**Tags:** carousel, tech, mint grey, outline icons, corporate, instagram

## What it is
An eight-post-plus-cover Instagram grid for a 'Digital Technology' brand, using grey and mint-green tiles with chunky outline icons (chip, cable, gear) and a mix of photo and flat text tiles.

## What it means
A cool grey-and-mint corporate palette paired with friendly rounded outline icons balances a 'serious tech' feel with approachability, avoiding an overly clinical look.

## Techniques
- mix photographic tiles with flat icon-led tiles within one grid for content variety
- use thick rounded-stroke outline icons rather than thin technical line icons for a friendlier tone
- reserve one bold accent color (mint) to highlight key words within otherwise neutral tiles

## Best for
- B2B tech company social content
- conference/summit promotional grids
- corporate SaaS Instagram kits

## Vocabulary
- **stroke weight** — the thickness of an icon's or line's outline, affecting how bold or delicate it reads
