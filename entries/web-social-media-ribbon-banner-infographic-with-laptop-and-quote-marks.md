# 'Social Media' concept illustration with a teal ribbon banner, giant quote marks and a laptop hub icon

![](../images/web-social-media-ribbon-banner-infographic-with-laptop-and-quote-marks.avif)

**Discipline:** web · **Design type:** Feature illustration · **Design style:** Playful & Illustrated
**Tags:** social-media, infographic, teal, ribbon-banner, laptop, icons

## What it is
A white illustration with a teal ribbon banner reading 'SOCIAL MEDIA', a central laptop icon with browser chrome, giant black quote marks flanking it, and three numbered labels (Share, Like, Chat) each with a small icon, connected to the laptop by dotted lines.

## What it means
Clear and iconic; a central device hub with radiating labelled actions makes an abstract concept like 'social media' easy to diagram.

## Techniques
- Use a ribbon banner shape for a classic infographic title.
- Radiate labelled actions from a central device icon.
- Use oversized quote marks as a nod to conversation and sharing.

## Best for
- Social media strategy explainers
- Marketing concept illustrations
- Presentation and blog graphics

## Vocabulary
- **Ribbon banner** — A title shape with folded, flag-like ends.
- **Hub-and-spoke diagram** — A layout radiating related items from one central element.
