# Crowdfunding App on Violet Terrazzo

![](../images/app-violet-terrazzo-podium-crowdfunding-app-phone.png)

**Discipline:** app · **Design type:** Device mockup scene
**Tags:** violet, terrazzo, podium, 3d, dark-ui, lime, crowdfunding

## What it is
A glassy-framed phone with a dark Explore screen, watch product card and lime plus button, standing among lilac terrazzo cylinders in a violet room.

## What it means
Speckled terrazzo and lilac give a playful, gallery-like stage, while lime adds a fun jolt.

## Techniques
- Terrazzo-textured podiums as scene props
- Translucent glass phone frame
- Purple range from dark UI to pale plinths
- Lime accent for the central action
- Soft window light shadows on the floor

## Best for
- Marketplace and crowdfunding apps
- Product renders with a set
- Playful premium branding

## Vocabulary
- **Terrazzo** — A speckled surface of chips in a flat matrix, used as a texture.
- **Plinth** — A block or cylinder used as a display stand.
- **Stage set** — A staged 3D environment for a product shot.
