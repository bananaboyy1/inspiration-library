# 'Graphic Design House' flyer in bold yellow and black with a zigzag pattern and pen-tool anchor icon

![](../images/print-social-graphic-design-house-flyer-yellow-black-zigzag-pen-tool.avif)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Social ad · **Design style:** Neo-Brutalist
**Tags:** design-agency, yellow, black, zigzag, pen-tool, poster

## What it is
A bright yellow A4 flyer with a black corner block holding a zigzag pattern and vertical stripes, a bold black-and-yellow split 'GRAPHIC DESIGN HOUSE' wordmark, 'Tips for home office' subhead, two-column body copy, and outline icons (pen tool, target/crosshair) in yellow.

## What it means
Loud and confident; heavy black-yellow contrast and a pen-tool icon make a design studio's own marketing double as a craft demonstration.

## Techniques
- Use one bright colour with black for maximum shelf-stopping contrast.
- Split a wordmark's colour across two words for rhythm.
- Add zigzag and stripe patterns as bold corner texture.

## Best for
- Design studio flyers
- Bold brand identities
- High-contrast print marketing

## Vocabulary
- **Zigzag pattern** — A repeating angular line texture.
- **Anchor icon** — A pen-tool control-point symbol representing vector design.
