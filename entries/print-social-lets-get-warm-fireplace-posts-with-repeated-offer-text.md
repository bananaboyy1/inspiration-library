# 'Let's Get Warm' fireplace pack with repeated 'Special Offer' lines and slate-blush blocks

![](../images/print-social-lets-get-warm-fireplace-posts-with-repeated-offer-text.jpg)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Social template pack · **Design style:** Warm & Organic
**Tags:** home, fireplace, winter, slate, blush, instagram

## What it is
Nine square posts pairing slate blue and blush blocks with fireside photos, a lead post with 'Let's Get Warm' in serif and italic and 'SPECIAL OFFER' repeated four times, 'Winter is coming' banners, a dog by the fire and circular discount badges.

## What it means
Cosy and seasonal; the repeated offer line adds rhythm and urgency to a calm palette.

## Techniques
- Repeat a phrase in a stacked list for rhythm.
- Split squares into colour blocks and photo halves.
- Add a thin border inset to frame each post.

## Best for
- Winter home promos
- Interior retailers
- Seasonal offers

## Vocabulary
- **Repetition** — Repeating a word or phrase for emphasis.
- **Inset border** — A thin frame drawn inside the edge.
