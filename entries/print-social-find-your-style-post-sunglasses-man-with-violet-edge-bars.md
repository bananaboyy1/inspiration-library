# 'Find Your Style & Your Self.' post with a sunglasses portrait framed by violet edge bars and dot grids

![](../images/print-social-find-your-style-post-sunglasses-man-with-violet-edge-bars.avif)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Social promo post · **Design style:** Photographic & Cinematic
**Tags:** fashion, violet, black, portrait, dot-grid, frame

## What it is
A square black post with an inset photo of a young man in round sunglasses, thin violet and white vertical bars on the left and right edges, a violet-white bar along the bottom, white dot grids and a white and violet bold headline.

## What it means
Clean and stylish; thin coloured bars frame the photo like a light strip.

## Techniques
- Frame an inset photo with thin coloured edge bars.
- Split bars into two colours for rhythm.
- Scatter dot grids at corners.

## Best for
- Fashion posts
- Personal branding
- Dark themed feeds

## Vocabulary
- **Inset photo** — A photo placed inside a margin rather than full-bleed.
- **Edge bar** — A thin coloured line along a side.
