# 'Golden Co' advertising agency story set with flat illustrations of megaphones, lightbulbs and charts

![](../images/print-social-golden-co-advertising-agency-story-set-flat-illustration.avif)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Story set · **Design style:** Playful & Illustrated
**Tags:** agency, stories, flat-illustration, cream, navy, marketing

## What it is
Four vertical story cards alternating navy and cream, each with a bold headline ('From Strategy to Spotlight', 'Creative Sparks Lasting Impact', 'Turning Visions into Brands', 'Innovate Create Captivate') and a flat illustration combining a megaphone, lightbulb, target icon, bar chart and speech bubbles in coral, teal, mustard and navy.

## What it means
Confident and cohesive; recurring icon motifs (megaphone, lightbulb, target) across four cards build instant brand recognition.

## Techniques
- Reuse a set of icon motifs across multiple cards for cohesion.
- Alternate background colour while keeping the illustration style fixed.
- Pair a punchy two-line headline with a one-line subhead.

## Best for
- Marketing agency social content
- Instagram story series
- Flat illustration branding

## Vocabulary
- **Icon motif** — A recurring symbol used across a set of designs.
- **Flat illustration** — Illustration using solid colours without gradients or shading.
