A hand holds a tilted phone with a light-grey monthly calendar, day/week/month toggle, coloured dashes under dates, a black today circle and a tab bar with a blue selected pill, over a tan quilted sofa and navy trousers.

Everyday and calm; the sofa scene sets the app in a relaxed evening moment.

- Use a segmented control for views.
