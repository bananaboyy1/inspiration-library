# Project Introduction with Jumping Travellers

![](../images/app-lavender-project-introduction-with-metadata-and-jumping-travellers-photo.jpg)

**Discipline:** app · **Design type:** Case study intro slide
**Tags:** introduction, lavender, photo, metadata, topic-pills, progress-bar, travel

## What it is
A lavender slide titled Project Introduction with a plane progress bar, a large paragraph, a metadata row (project, year, industry, topic pills) and a wide rounded photo of five backpackers jumping by the sea with an overlaid caption.

## What it means
An energetic photo makes an introduction feel joyful, and the metadata row keeps the facts tidy.

## Techniques
- Four-column metadata row with hairline dividers
- Topic pills as tags
- Large rounded photo with white caption overlay
- Blue gradient in the second title word
- Plane on the progress bar

## Best for
- Project introduction slides
- Travel case studies
- Metadata plus photo layouts

## Vocabulary
- **Metadata row** — A row of labelled facts divided by hairlines.
- **Topic pill** — A rounded tag naming a subject.
- **Caption overlay** — Text placed over the lower edge of a photo.
