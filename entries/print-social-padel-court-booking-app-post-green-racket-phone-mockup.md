## What it is
A dark green square post with bold white 'Stop Calling. Start Playing.' headline, a phone mockup showing a padel court booking calendar with duration and date selectors, real yellow padel rackets angled behind it, a green checkmark calendar icon, and a supporting line 'Instant Padel court booking in your pocket.'

## What it means
Direct and credible; pairing real sports equipment with the actual booking UI proves the app solves a real, specific problem.

## Techniques
- Contrast an old behaviour ('calling') with the new solution in the headline.
- Prop real sporting equipment around the app mockup.
- Show the actual booking interface, not just a generic screen.

## Best for
- Sports booking apps
- Niche sport marketing
- Problem-solution app promotion
