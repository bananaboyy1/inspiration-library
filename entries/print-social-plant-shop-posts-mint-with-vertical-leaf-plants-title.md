## What it is
Nine square posts on a crinkled white paper texture with pale mint panels, lush green leaf photos, dark green headlines ('How to take care of your indoor plants?'), a large vertical 'Leaf Plants' title, Roman numeral I/II tips and plant names (Monstera, Scindapsus, Parlour Palm).

## What it means
Fresh and gentle; paper texture and mint feel natural and hand-tended.

## Techniques
- Use a crinkled paper texture as the base.
- Run a large title vertically along a panel.
- Number tips with Roman numerals.
- Let leaf photos bleed off edges.

## Best for
- Plant shops
- Gardening tips
- Botanical brands
