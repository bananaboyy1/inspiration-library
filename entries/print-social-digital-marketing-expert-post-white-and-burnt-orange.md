# 'Digital Marketing Expert' post with a smiling woman and burnt-orange corner shapes

![](../images/print-social-digital-marketing-expert-post-white-and-burnt-orange.avif)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Social ad · **Design style:** Corporate & Clean-Tech
**Tags:** agency, orange, white, portrait, plaster-texture, ad

## What it is
A square post on a white plaster-textured background with a large black 'Digital Marketing Expert' headline, a dark 'Join us' pill, a smiling woman in a patterned headwrap and red top, and burnt-orange angled blocks at the top-right and bottom-left holding contact info.

## What it means
Warm and friendly; orange blocks echo her headwrap for a coordinated look.

## Techniques
- Pick the accent colour from the subject's clothing.
- Use angled blocks in opposite corners for balance.
- Add a subtle wall texture behind white areas.

## Best for
- Marketing coaches
- Personal-brand ads
- Warm service promos

## Vocabulary
- **Colour pick** — Taking an accent colour from within the photo.
- **Diagonal balance** — Weights placed in opposite corners.
