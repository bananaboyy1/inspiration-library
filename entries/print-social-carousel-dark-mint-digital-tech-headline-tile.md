**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Social template pack · **Design style:** Corporate & Clean-Tech
**Tags:** carousel, tech, mint dark, outline icons, corporate, instagram

## What it is
A companion grid from the same Digital Technology brand, reordering the lead tile to a bold 'DIGITAL TECHNOLOGY' dark headline card with a large outline gear icon and glow-dot accent.

## What it means
Swapping the grid's lead tile for a headline-forward cover shows an alternate first impression a brand can test, while every downstream tile stays visually identical to the sibling set.

## Techniques
- use a giant single icon as the dominant graphic on a cover/title tile
- add a small glowing dot accent to a flat icon to hint at 'active/on' status
- keep a consistent grid density (mix of photo and icon tiles) across cover variants

## Best for
- B2B tech company social content
- conference/summit promotional grids
- cover-tile A/B testing for template packs

## Vocabulary
- **A/B cover testing** — producing multiple lead/cover tile variants of the same content set to compare performance
