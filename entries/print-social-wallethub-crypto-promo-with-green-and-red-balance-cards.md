# 'All Your Assets in One Powerful WalletHub' promo with glowing green and red balance cards

![](../images/print-social-wallethub-crypto-promo-with-green-and-red-balance-cards.avif)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Product UI promo card · **Design style:** Dark & Neon
**Tags:** crypto, wallet, fintech, dark, neon-green, charts

## What it is
A portrait black post on a faint grid with a big uppercase headline half white, half mint green, a time-range segmented control (24h, Week, Month) with 'Week' active, and two balance cards: a green-outlined card with a rising area chart and +2.47% tag, and a red-outlined card with a falling chart and -3.47% tag.

## What it means
Clear and thrilling; gain and loss colours make portfolio moves instantly readable.

## Techniques
- Pair a green gain card with a red loss card.
- Outline cards in neon matching their chart.
- Include a segmented control to hint at interactivity.
- Split the headline colour at the product name.

## Best for
- Crypto wallet marketing
- Fintech app promos
- Trading dashboards

## Vocabulary
- **Segmented control** — A row of toggle buttons choosing one option.
- **Area chart** — A line chart with the area below filled.
