# Merchant Transaction Card over Cafe Photo

![](../images/app-merchant-transaction-card-over-street-cafe-photo.jpg)

**Discipline:** app ·  · **Design style:** Photographic & Cinematic
**Tags:** transaction, card, photo, cafe, payment, overlay, realistic

## What it is
A wide street photo of a barista handing a paper bag to a customer, with a white rounded transaction card over the bottom showing a merchant logo, category, price and reference number.

## What it means
Placing a UI card onto real-life imagery shows exactly what the data represents, linking the digital record to the physical moment.

## Techniques
- UI card overlaid on documentary-style photography
- Card anchored across the lower third without hiding faces
- Merchant logo and category as the main hierarchy
- Price and ID right-aligned in a contrasting weight
- Soft stacked shadow beneath the card

## Best for
- Fintech feature marketing
- Spending and transaction features
- Storytelling that ties numbers to life

## Vocabulary
- **UI overlay** — An interface element placed over a photo.
- **Transaction card** — A component showing one payment's details.
- **Documentary photo** — Candid, real-life photography used for authenticity.
