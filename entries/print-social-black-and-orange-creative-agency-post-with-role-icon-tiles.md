# Black and orange 'Creative Marketing Agency' post with role icon tiles and halftone orange bar

![](../images/print-social-black-and-orange-creative-agency-post-with-role-icon-tiles.avif)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Social ad · **Design style:** Corporate & Clean-Tech
**Tags:** agency, hiring, black, orange, icon-tiles, halftone

## What it is
A square black post with a big white and orange headline overlapping a photo of a smiling woman in an orange sweater holding a tablet, set on an orange halftone bar, plus four dark tiles with orange icons (Graphic Designer, General Manager, Account Executive, Research Staff) and an orange 'Register now' button.

## What it means
Energetic and clear; icon tiles make roles scannable while the orange ties to the model's sweater.

## Techniques
- Let the headline overlap onto the photo.
- Present roles as icon tiles in a 2x2 grid.
- Back the photo with a halftone-dotted colour bar.
- Match the accent to the subject's clothing.

## Best for
- Agency hiring posts
- Team introductions
- Service menus

## Vocabulary
- **Icon tile** — A square with an icon and short label.
- **Overlap** — Text or shapes crossing into another element.
