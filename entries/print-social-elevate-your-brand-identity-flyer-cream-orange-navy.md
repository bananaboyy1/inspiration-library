# 'Elevate Your Brand Identity' freelance designer flyer with a cream header band and outlined chain-link doodles

![](../images/print-social-elevate-your-brand-identity-flyer-cream-orange-navy.avif)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Social ad · **Design style:** Retro & Pixel
**Tags:** freelance, designer, cream, orange, navy, flyer

## What it is
A mustard-bordered A5 flyer with a cream header band, a pink half-circle and blue starburst accent, bold headline 'Elevate Your Brand Identity', the designer's name and title, a bulleted services list, a pink 'Ready to Transform Your Brand?' bar, social handles, and orange outlined interlocking chain-link doodles beside a line-drawn pencil.

## What it means
Warm and personal; hand-drawn doodles and a named designer make a freelance pitch feel human rather than templated.

## Techniques
- Name the freelancer directly in the headline area for personal branding.
- Use interlocking outline doodles as a playful signature motif.
- Highlight the call-to-action in a solid colour bar.

## Best for
- Freelance designer marketing
- Personal brand flyers
- Warm service pitches

## Vocabulary
- **Personal branding** — Marketing built around an individual's name and identity.
- **Outline doodle** — A decorative shape drawn as strokes only.
