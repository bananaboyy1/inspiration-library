# Three-slide 'Market Value' carousel in violet, lime and white with circle-cropped photos

![](../images/print-social-market-value-carousel-violet-lime-with-circle-photos.avif)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Social carousel · **Design style:** Corporate & Clean-Tech
**Tags:** carousel, business, violet, lime, circle-photos, marketing, educational

## What it is
Three portrait slides: 'What Does "Market Value" Really Mean?' with the term in a lime pill, 'Why Market Value Actually Matters to You', and a white 'That Shape Your Market Value' slide; team photos in circles ringed in white or lime, violet ring shapes and arrows.

## What it means
Punchy and educational; the pill highlight turns a dry business term into a scroll-friendly hook.

## Techniques
- Put the key term in a highlighter pill inside the headline.
- Crop photos into rings that bleed across slide edges.
- Flip one slide to white to break carousel rhythm.
- Use big arrows to push the swipe.

## Best for
- Educational business carousels
- Marketing agency content
- Finance explainers

## Vocabulary
- **Carousel** — A multi-slide social post swiped horizontally.
- **Highlight pill** — A coloured capsule behind a word for emphasis.
