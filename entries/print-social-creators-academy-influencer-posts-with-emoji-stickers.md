**Tags:** influencer, creator, black, violet, orange, stickers

## What it is
Nine square posts on black, violet, orange and lilac with bold white headlines ('You can become an influencer!'), cut-out photos of young creators, yellow smileys, white lightning bolts, blue hearts, orange starbursts and repeating 'CREATORS' text patterns.

## What it means
Loud and Gen-Z; sticker-like emoji graphics speak the language of social media.

## Techniques
- Scatter emoji-style stickers around subjects.
- Repeat the brand word as a background pattern.
- Use a big colour circle behind cut-outs.
- Italicise the key word in caps.

## Best for
- Creator courses
- Youth brands
- Social media academies

- **Sticker graphics** — Emoji-like icons placed as if stuck on.
