## What it is
Nine dark navy-to-blue square posts with neon orange and green arrow and squiggle shapes, wavy line textures, black-and-white gym photos, bold headlines ('Dedication, Determination', 'Transform Your Body, Transform Your Life', 'Hustle for that Muscles'), and a small 'Modern Gym' pixel-cross logo.

## What it means
Energetic and driven; neon directional arrows give a gym pack forward momentum that matches its motivational copy.

## Techniques
- Use neon arrow shapes to suggest direction and progress.
- Add wavy line textures behind greyscale photos for movement.
- Keep a consistent small pixel-style logo across every post.

## Best for
- Gym and fitness centres
- Motivational sports content
- Dark neon-accented branding
