# Pearl banking app in hand beside a hanging truss poster with a stepped bar chart

![](../images/app-pearl-mobile-app-in-hand-beside-stepped-bar-chart-board.jpg)

**Discipline:** app ·  · **Design style:** Glassmorphism
**Tags:** mockup, fintech, pearl, hand, poster, truss, bar-chart, grey

## What it is
A split image: at left a hand holds a pale pearl banking app with serif balance and quick-action avatars in hard sunlight; at right a poster hung in a steel truss with red eyebrow, headline about smarter banking and an ascending hatched bar chart.

## What it means
Precise and airy; the poster extends the UI's calm chart language into print.

## Techniques
- Show app and print piece together in one grey palette.
- Hang a poster in an industrial truss.
- Draw hatched bars ascending like steps.
- Use one red eyebrow as accent.

## Best for
- Fintech brand presentations
- App-plus-poster showcases
- Monochrome premium systems

## Vocabulary
- **Truss frame** — A steel lattice structure holding a poster.
- **Hatched bar** — A bar filled with fine diagonal lines.
- **Eyebrow accent** — A small coloured label above a headline.
- **Hard sunlight** — Direct light casting sharp shadows.
