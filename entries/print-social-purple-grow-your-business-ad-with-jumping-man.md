# Purple 'Great Ideas to Grow Your Business!' ad with a jumping man holding a laptop

![](../images/print-social-purple-grow-your-business-ad-with-jumping-man.avif)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Social ad · **Design style:** Playful & Illustrated
**Tags:** agency, purple, yellow, cutout, services-list, ad

## What it is
A square violet ad with a cut-out man mid-leap holding a laptop in front of a yellow circle, a chunky white and yellow headline, a numbered services list with a vertical 'What we do' label, curved hand-drawn arrows and a yellow 'Contact us' pill.

## What it means
Energetic and fun; the jump sells momentum and optimism.

## Techniques
- Use a mid-air pose for energy.
- Put a bright circle behind the cut-out.
- Rotate a section label vertically beside its list.
- Draw curvy arrows to guide the eye to CTAs.

## Best for
- Agency promos
- Youthful service ads
- Growth marketing

## Vocabulary
- **Action pose** — A dynamic photo pose like jumping.
- **Vertical label** — A rotated tag beside content.
