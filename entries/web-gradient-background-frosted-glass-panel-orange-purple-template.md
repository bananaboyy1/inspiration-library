# 'Gradient Background' template with a frosted glass panel over a vivid orange-purple blurred gradient

![](../images/web-gradient-background-frosted-glass-panel-orange-purple-template.avif)

**Discipline:** web · **Design type:** Background pattern · **Design style:** Glassmorphism
**Tags:** glassmorphism, gradient, orange-purple, frosted-panel, template

## What it is
A vivid blurred orange-to-purple gradient background with a large frosted-glass rounded rectangle panel at centre holding a bold 'GRADIENT BACKGROUND' title, a subtitle and placeholder body text.

## What it means
Warm and premium; a frosted panel over a rich sunset-toned gradient gives any headline instant visual depth.

## Techniques
- Blur a vivid multi-colour gradient for a soft, glowing backdrop.
- Float a frosted glass panel at centre for content legibility.
- Keep panel content minimal so the gradient stays the star.

## Best for
- Glassmorphism UI templates
- Warm-toned background assets
- Presentation and banner backgrounds

## Vocabulary
- **Frosted glass panel** — A translucent blurred rectangle mimicking textured glass.
- **Blurred gradient** — A soft, out-of-focus colour transition.
