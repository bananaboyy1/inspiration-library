# '3 Days to Launch' countdown poster with a huge glowing green numeral and radial floor glow

![](../images/print-social-3-days-to-launch-countdown-poster-glowing-green-numeral.avif)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Social promo post · **Design style:** Dark & Neon
**Tags:** countdown, launch, dark-green, glow, numeral, minimal

## What it is
A dark green portrait poster with a small logo circle, double-chevron up arrows in the corners, a giant black numeral '3' outlined in a bright green glow, 'Days to Launch' beneath in white and green, 'Backed by Your Company' and a website line, all sitting above a glowing radial floor light.

## What it means
Suspenseful and minimal; one giant glowing number does all the work a busy countdown graphic usually needs many elements for.

## Techniques
- Let one huge glowing numeral carry the entire message.
- Ground the numeral with a radial floor glow for weight.
- Use tiny repeated chevron marks as a subtle branding motif.

## Best for
- Product launch countdowns
- App and SaaS teasers
- Minimal dark-mode promos

## Vocabulary
- **Radial floor glow** — A light that fades outward from a point at the base of a composition.
- **Hero numeral** — A number rendered extremely large as the focal graphic.
