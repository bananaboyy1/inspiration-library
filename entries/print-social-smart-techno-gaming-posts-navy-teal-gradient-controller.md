# 'Smart Techno' gaming pack in navy with teal-green gradient game controller illustrations and headphone character

![](../images/print-social-smart-techno-gaming-posts-navy-teal-gradient-controller.jpg)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Social template pack · **Design style:** Dark & Neon
**Tags:** gaming, tech, navy, teal-gradient, controller, instagram

## What it is
Five navy-and-black square posts with a teal-to-green gradient game controller line illustration, a flat character wearing headphones playing a controller, circular progress-ring accents, dashed lines, and headlines ('Smart Techno', 'Great Offer').

## What it means
Cool and energetic; the gradient controller motif and headphone character make a generic tech offer feel specifically gamer-focused.

## Techniques
- Render a product icon (controller) in a bold gradient outline.
- Repeat a headphone-wearing character as the campaign's face.
- Add dashed lines and rings as light sci-fi texture.

## Best for
- Gaming and esports promotions
- Tech gadget offers
- Gradient-accented dark templates

## Vocabulary
- **Gradient outline icon** — An icon drawn as a stroke filled with a colour fade.
- **Progress ring accent** — A circular arc used as decorative texture.
