# Conversational Planner with Quick Replies

![](../images/app-conversational-planner-chat-with-quick-reply-chips.gif)

**Discipline:** app ·  · **Design style:** Warm & Organic
**Tags:** chat, quick-replies, yellow, cream, voice, assistant, calendar

## What it is
A feature slide with a cropped phone showing a chat: yellow user bubbles, assistant text and white pill choices (Children, Parents, Work...), a voice input bar, beside a headline 'Organize your life, just by talking'.

## What it means
Warm yellow and cream feel personable, and quick-reply chips lower the effort of planning.

## Techniques
- Chip choices instead of open text prompts
- Yellow only for the user's messages
- Blue mic button as the sole cool accent
- Phone cropped off the left/top to enlarge the UI
- Small blue eyebrow label above a large light headline

## Best for
- Assistant and planner apps
- Feature explanation slides
- Conversational product marketing

## Vocabulary
- **Quick reply** — Tappable suggested answers below a chat message.
- **Cropped device** — A device partly out of frame to enlarge the screen.
- **Eyebrow** — A small label above a heading.
