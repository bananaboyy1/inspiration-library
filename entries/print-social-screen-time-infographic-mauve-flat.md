# Mauve flat-illustration screen-time infographic

![](../images/print-social-screen-time-infographic-mauve-flat.avif)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Product infographic set · **Design style:** Playful & Illustrated
**Tags:** infographic, screen time, flat illustration, mauve, charts, icons

## What it is
A vertical infographic on a dusty mauve background breaking down phone screen time by category (health, messengers, social, work, entertaining) with small illustrated panels, a pie chart and bar chart.

## What it means
Grouping stats into labeled illustrated 'windows' rather than one dense chart lets a reader skim by topic instead of parsing everything at once.

## Techniques
- frame each data category in its own small illustrated panel/device screen
- mix chart types (pie, bar) within one infographic to match each metric's shape
- use one muted background color to let the varied panel colors stay legible without clashing

## Best for
- digital wellbeing content
- app usage reports
- lifestyle blog infographics

## Vocabulary
- **infographic** — a visual representation of data or information designed for quick comprehension
