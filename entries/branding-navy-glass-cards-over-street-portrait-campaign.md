# Navy Card and Glass Card over Street Portrait

![](../images/branding-navy-glass-cards-over-street-portrait-campaign.jpg)

**Discipline:** branding · **Design type:** Campaign photography
**Tags:** campaign, photography, navy, glass, script-italic, fintech, street

## What it is
A low-angle photo of a smiling woman in a blue jacket holding a phone in a transit station, with a navy rounded card reading 'Spend with more control.' and a frosted glass card with a glossy coin and 'One wallet. More control.'

## What it means
Colour-matched wardrobe and glass cards tie the photo to the brand, and the low angle feels aspirational.

## Techniques
- Wardrobe in the brand blue
- Two card styles (solid navy, frosted glass) over one photo
- Italic serif word inside a sans headline
- Low camera angle for confidence
- Glossy 3D coin as a brand object

## Best for
- Fintech campaign imagery
- Brand systems combining photo and cards
- Social and web hero visuals

## Vocabulary
- **Overlay card** — A brand card placed over a photograph.
- **Low-angle shot** — A photo taken from below the subject.
- **Glass card** — A frosted translucent card.
