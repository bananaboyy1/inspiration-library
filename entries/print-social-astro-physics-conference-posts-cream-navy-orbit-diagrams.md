# 'Astro Physics' conference pack with cream, navy and orange orbit-line diagrams and rotated serif type

![](../images/print-social-astro-physics-conference-posts-cream-navy-orbit-diagrams.avif)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Social template pack · **Design style:** Editorial
**Tags:** conference, astrophysics, cream, navy, orbit-diagram, serif

## What it is
Five square posts in cream, orange, navy and pink, with thin orbit-line diagrams and circles as recurring background motifs, bold rotated serif headlines ('ANNUAL CONFERENCE', 'PHYSICS'), speaker photo cards with quote marks, a schedule grid and pill labels for topics (Maths, Physics, Matter).

## What it means
Sophisticated and thematic; orbit-line diagrams double as both scientific illustration and elegant background decoration.

## Techniques
- Use orbit-line diagrams as a recurring thematic background motif.
- Rotate large serif words vertically for editorial flair.
- Label topics as small rounded pills for quick scanning.

## Best for
- Academic and science conferences
- Editorial event branding
- Elegant typographic social packs

## Vocabulary
- **Orbit diagram** — Concentric elliptical lines suggesting astronomical motion.
- **Rotated type** — Text set vertically rather than horizontally.
