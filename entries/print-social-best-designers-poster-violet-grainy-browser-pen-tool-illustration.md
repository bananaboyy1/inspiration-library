# 'The Best Designers In The World Are Here!' poster with a grainy browser window and giant pen-tool illustration

![](../images/print-social-best-designers-poster-violet-grainy-browser-pen-tool-illustration.avif)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Poster · **Design style:** Editorial
**Tags:** agency, violet, grainy-texture, browser-window, pen-tool, poster

## What it is
A violet-bordered cream A4 poster with a bold two-weight headline, a browser-window frame containing a grainy purple mountain-like abstract shape with vector anchor points, and a giant yellow-pink pen tool icon overlapping the frame, closing with a black contact bar and a rotated 'Graphic Design' side label.

## What it means
Meta and credible; illustrating the tools of design (the pen tool, anchor points) is a clever way for a design agency to show its craft.

## Techniques
- Illustrate the literal tools of the trade as the hero graphic.
- Add grain texture to a flat illustration for tactile depth.
- Rotate a category label along the poster's edge.

## Best for
- Design agency posters
- Vector and illustration studios
- Meta/self-referential branding

## Vocabulary
- **Anchor point** — A control point used to draw vector paths, shown here as a design motif.
- **Grain texture** — A fine noise texture added for tactile depth.
