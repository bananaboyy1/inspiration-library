# Portfolio cover with a frosted glass folder holding flyer thumbnails over a green glow

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Social cover post
**Tags:** portfolio, flyer, black, lime-green, glass-folder, thumbnails, glow, stickers

## What it is
A small dark portrait cover with a bold 'Social Media Flyer Designs' title with tilted pill labels, a frosted translucent folder from which several green-black flyers fan out, a lime glow behind, a name pill and a row of four photo thumbnails with tool credits at the bottom.

## What it means
Techy and glossy; the glass folder makes a portfolio feel like an object.

## Techniques
- Fan work out of a glass folder.
- Use a lime glow behind.
- Add tilted pill labels.
- Credit tools at the base.

## Best for
- Designer portfolio covers
- Dark green-lime brands
- Glass folder motifs

## Vocabulary
- **Glass folder** — A translucent folder graphic.
- **Fanned thumbnails** — Works spread out like cards.
- **Tool credit** — Small text naming software used.
- **Green glow** — A luminous green backdrop.
