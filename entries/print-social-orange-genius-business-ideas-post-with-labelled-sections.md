# Orange 'Genius Business Ideas' post with boxed headline, labelled text sections and brush-edge footer

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Social ad · **Design style:** Corporate & Clean-Tech
**Tags:** business, orange, white, webinar, labels, ad

## What it is
A square white-and-orange post with a smiling man in an orange denim jacket on a phone, fading into white, a headline where 'BUSINESS' sits in an orange box, orange labels 'About us' and 'Online webinar' over paragraphs, and an orange footer with a rough brush edge.

## What it means
Cheerful and informative; labels structure the copy like a mini brochure.

## Techniques
- Box the middle word of a three-line headline.
- Head short paragraphs with small colour labels.
- Give the footer a brushed torn edge.

## Best for
- Business webinars
- Coaching promos
- Information-heavy ads

## Vocabulary
- **Section label** — A small coloured tag heading a block of text.
- **Brush edge** — A rough painted border.
