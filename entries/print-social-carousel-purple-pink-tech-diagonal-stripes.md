# Purple/pink tech carousel with diagonal stripe accents

![](../images/print-social-carousel-purple-pink-tech-diagonal-stripes.jpg)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Social carousel · **Design style:** Dark & Neon
**Tags:** carousel, tech, purple pink, diagonal stripes, circuit pattern, flat illustration

## What it is
A five-tile Instagram carousel for a 'Minimal Tech' brand, using deep purple-to-pink gradients, thin circuit-line patterns, and diagonal magenta stripe blocks around a flat illustrated tech worker.

## What it means
Diagonal stripe blocks cutting across otherwise flat tiles inject energy and a tech/circuit feel without needing literal wiring diagrams on every panel.

## Techniques
- cut diagonal accent stripes across one corner of a tile as a recurring structural motif
- overlay thin circuit-board line patterns for subtle tech texture behind flat illustration
- keep one consistent flat character illustration style across all tiles for brand continuity

## Best for
- tech startup social carousels
- minimal dark-mode brand kits
- developer/SaaS marketing content

## Vocabulary
- **circuit pattern** — a decorative background texture resembling printed circuit board traces, evoking technology
