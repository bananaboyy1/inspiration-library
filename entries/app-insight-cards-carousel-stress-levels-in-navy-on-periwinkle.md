# Wellbeing insight cards: a tall navy stress card between cream cards on periwinkle

![](../images/app-insight-cards-carousel-stress-levels-in-navy-on-periwinkle.gif)

**Discipline:** app · **Design type:** Health app screens
**Tags:** insights, cards, navy, periwinkle, cream, large-word, chart, cropped-carousel

## What it is
A rounded periwinkle panel showing three vertical cards, the middle one solid navy with STRESS LEVELS text and a giant white 'High' at the bottom, flanked by cropped cream cards with a blue gradient orb and an orange line chart.

## What it means
Clear and calm; one huge word delivers the result immediately.

## Techniques
- Give each insight one huge verdict word.
- Distinguish cards by colour: navy for the focused one, cream for neighbours.
- Crop side cards to imply swiping.
- Use a single orange line for data.

## Best for
- Wellbeing and health insight apps
- Card carousels in mobile UI
- Summarising metrics in plain words

## Vocabulary
- **Verdict word** — A single large word summarising a result.
- **Cropped carousel** — Side cards cut by the frame to show more exist.
- **Focus card** — The active card given the strongest colour.
- **Insight card** — A card giving guidance based on data.
