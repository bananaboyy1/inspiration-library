# Core Banking Flow Sheet with Italic Serif Headline

![](../images/app-core-banking-dashboard-flow-sheet-with-italic-serif-headline.jpg)

**Discipline:** app ·  · **Design style:** Editorial
**Tags:** core-banking, flow, screens, serif-italic, blue-grey, keypad, payment

## What it is
A tall pale sheet with a Core Banking pill, a headline 'One Dashboard, Every Financial Move.' with italic serif, four tag pills and phones laid out in a flow: home, transactions, send money, confirm transfer, add money, add money keypad, pay bills, provider list, payment confirmation and bill paid.

## What it means
Laying out related screens in a staggered flow shows the whole journey, and a shared blue-grey gradient unifies them.

## Techniques
- Staggered rows of phones showing a flow
- Bottom sheets rising over blurred screens
- Keypad with translucent keys
- Serif italic accent in the headline
- Tag pills for the four main actions

## Best for
- Fintech flow documentation
- Banking app screen sheets
- Payment journey presentations

## Vocabulary
- **Flow sheet** — A page of screens arranged to show a journey.
- **Bottom sheet** — A panel rising from the bottom over a dimmed screen.
- **Translucent keypad** — A numeric pad with semi-transparent keys.
