# A glossy 3D gift box bursting open inside a phone screen outline, with confetti and scattered shapes

**Discipline:** branding · **Design type:** Graphic assets · **Design style:** 3D & Surreal
**Tags:** 3d-render, gift-box, confetti, blue, reward, app-promo

## What it is
A blue-background 3D render of an orange gift box with a blue ribbon bow opening inside the silhouette of a phone, red and blue ribbon curls, confetti dots and small floating geometric shapes (stars, triangles, squares) around it.

## What it means
Celebratory and app-native; nesting the gift inside a phone shape ties a reward directly to a mobile experience.

## Techniques
- Nest a 3D object inside a device silhouette.
- Scatter confetti and simple shapes for celebratory energy.
- Use a bold two-tone gift box against a flat colour backdrop.

## Best for
- App reward and referral promos
- Loyalty programme graphics
- Gamification illustrations

## Vocabulary
- **Device silhouette** — An outline shape suggesting a phone or screen.
- **Confetti motif** — Scattered small shapes suggesting celebration.
