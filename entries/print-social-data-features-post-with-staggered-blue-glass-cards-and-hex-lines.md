# 'Powerful Data Features for Modern Products' post with staggered blue glass cards and neon hexagon lines

![](../images/print-social-data-features-post-with-staggered-blue-glass-cards-and-hex-lines.avif)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Social educational post · **Design style:** Glassmorphism
**Tags:** data, saas, blue, glass, hexagon, features

## What it is
A portrait blue gradient post with a white headline, three staggered frosted-blue cards (Secure storage, Analytics with a 'Feature' tag, Data integration) each with a light-blue icon, over glowing cyan hexagon outlines.

## What it means
Techy and polished; staggered glass cards feel like floating UI panels.

## Techniques
- Stagger feature cards vertically for movement.
- Tag the lead card with a small label.
- Draw neon hexagon outlines behind.
- Keep one blue family throughout.

## Best for
- Data platforms
- SaaS features
- Tech agency posts

## Vocabulary
- **Staggered layout** — Items placed at different heights.
- **Hexagon grid** — Honeycomb shapes suggesting tech or data.
