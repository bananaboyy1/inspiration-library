# Orange 'We Provide Marketing Services' post with script accent, 3D like bubbles and swirl texture

![](../images/print-social-orange-marketing-services-post-with-3d-like-bubbles.avif)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Social ad · **Design style:** Gradient & Aurora
**Tags:** marketing, orange, yellow, 3d-icons, script, ad

## What it is
A square orange-to-yellow post with a swirling ripple texture, a red script 'we' over rounded white 'Provide Marketing Services', a smiling man in a beanie and pink jacket, glossy 3D heart and like bubbles, dashed curved arrows and a giant faded vertical word on the right.

## What it means
Warm, youthful and social; glossy reactions make it feel like engagement is guaranteed.

## Techniques
- Add a subtle ripple texture to a gradient.
- Float 3D reaction icons around the subject.
- Run a giant ghost word vertically as backdrop.
- Use dashed arrows for a playful path.

## Best for
- Social media agencies
- Youth-focused marketing
- Influencer services

## Vocabulary
- **Ghost type** — Oversized, low-contrast text used as background.
- **Reaction icon** — A like, heart or emoji symbol.
