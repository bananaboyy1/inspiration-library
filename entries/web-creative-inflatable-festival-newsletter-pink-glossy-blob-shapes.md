# 'Creative Inflatable Festival' newsletter with glossy pink-orange-blue balloon-like 3D blob shapes

**Discipline:** web · **Design type:** Feature illustration · **Design style:** 3D & Surreal
**Tags:** newsletter, inflatable, 3d-blob, pink, orange, email

## What it is
A pink email newsletter template with a white card holding glossy inflatable-style 3D blob shapes in orange, magenta and blue at the corners, a bold 'CREATIVE INFLATABLE FESTIVAL' headline, a 'BUSINESS WEBINAR' pill, toggle-switch list items, a date line and a 'Read more' button.

## What it means
Playful and tactile; balloon-like glossy blobs give a business webinar newsletter an unexpectedly fun, inflatable-art feel.

## Techniques
- Use balloon-like glossy 3D shapes as pure decorative accents.
- Frame content in a clean white card against a bright pink field.
- Style list items as toggle switches for a modern feel.

## Best for
- Email newsletters
- Webinar and event announcements
- Playful 3D-accented branding

## Vocabulary
- **Inflatable 3D shape** — A glossy, balloon-like rendered object.
- **Toggle-switch list** — List items styled to look like on/off UI switches.
