# Cropped phone corner with lime humidity card and photographic room tiles

![](../images/app-lime-smart-home-tiles-cropped-phone.jpg)

**Discipline:** app · **Design type:** Device mockup scene
**Tags:** smart-home, lime, mockup, crop, dark-olive, photo-tile, bold-shapes

## What it is
A close, cropped view of a phone's lower corner on a deep olive background. The screen shows a big lime rounded card '70% Humidity' with a white chip and a room photo tile labelled 'Office' with round icon buttons.

## What it means
Bold, tactile and modern; the lime device edge echoes the UI and the crop makes it feel like a detail shot.

## Techniques
- Crop a device tightly so UI shapes become abstract graphics.
- Match the phone's frame colour to the UI accent.
- Use photographic room tiles with overlaid round controls.
- Contrast a saturated lime card with a dark olive stage.

## Best for
- Smart-home and IoT apps
- Case-study close-ups
- Brands with a neon accent

## Vocabulary
- **Detail crop** — A tightly cropped view of a device or screen.
- **Accent-matched hardware** — A device coloured to match the UI's accent.
- **Photo tile** — A card whose background is a photograph.
- **Round control** — A circular icon button.
