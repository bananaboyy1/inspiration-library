# Black 'Event Schedule April — May' poster with orange headline and ruled rows of dates

![](../images/print-social-black-event-schedule-poster-with-orange-headline-and-ruled-rows.avif)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Event poster · **Design style:** Editorial
**Tags:** event, schedule, black, orange, typography, corporate

## What it is
A portrait black poster with a huge orange uppercase 'EVENT SCHEDULE', a white 'April — May' subhead and six ruled rows each pairing a bold uppercase session title, a large date and time (25.04 14:00) and a small orange category with a description, plus a contact footer.

## What it means
Clear and confident; ruled rows turn a schedule into a typographic poster.

## Techniques
- Set a schedule as ruled table rows.
- Give dates big numerals as a column.
- Colour only the headline and category labels.
- Keep everything on one strong grid.

## Best for
- Corporate event agendas
- Conference programmes
- Monthly schedules

## Vocabulary
- **Ruled rows** — Rows separated by horizontal lines.
- **Tabular layout** — Content aligned in columns like a table.
