# Newhouse pack in crimson and forest green with notched white frames and window shadows

![](../images/print-social-newhouse-posts-red-and-green-with-notched-white-frames.avif)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Social template pack · **Design style:** Minimalist
**Tags:** real-estate, home, crimson, green, frames, shadows

## What it is
Five square posts in crimson or forest green with irregular notched white frames holding photos of people moving into homes, 'Hello New Home!' and 'Enjoy your first Home' headlines, bullet pills and soft window-light shadows falling across the design.

## What it means
Homely and calm; the soft shadows make graphics feel like they sit in a sunlit room.

## Techniques
- Cut frames with stepped notches for interest.
- Overlay soft window shadows for realism.
- Alternate two deep colours across a set.

## Best for
- Real estate
- Moving services
- Home brands

## Vocabulary
- **Notched frame** — A frame with stepped cut corners.
- **Shadow overlay** — A soft shadow texture laid over a design.
