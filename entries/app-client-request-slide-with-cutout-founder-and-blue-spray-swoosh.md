# Client Request with Founder Cut-Out and Blue Spray

![](../images/app-client-request-slide-with-cutout-founder-and-blue-spray-swoosh.png)

**Discipline:** app ·  · **Design style:** Photographic & Cinematic
**Tags:** client-request, founder, portrait, blue-spray, quote, tone-shift, pale

## What it is
A pale grey slide with a numbered Client Request pill, a large headline fading as it descends, a small avatar overlapping the first line, a quote with bold key words beside a grainy blue spray swoosh, a name and role beside a blue line and a large cut-out portrait fading out at the bottom.

## What it means
A portrait fading into the page makes the client feel present, and the spray swoosh adds a burst of energy.

## Techniques
- Portrait fading into the background
- Grainy blue spray swoosh crossing the quote
- Headline fading line by line
- Bold key words within a grey quote
- Blue rule beside the name

## Best for
- Client request and testimonial slides
- Health-tech case studies
- Founder introductions

## Vocabulary
- **Fade-out portrait** — A photo whose lower part dissolves into the page colour.
- **Spray swoosh** — A soft grainy stroke of colour.
- **Avatar overlap** — A small portrait placed over the end of a headline line.
