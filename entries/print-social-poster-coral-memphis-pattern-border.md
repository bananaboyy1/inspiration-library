# Coral Memphis-pattern bordered convention poster

![](../images/print-social-poster-coral-memphis-pattern-border.avif)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Event poster · **Design style:** Retro & Pixel
**Tags:** poster, memphis pattern, coral, border frame, event, convention

## What it is
A vertical A4 poster framed top and bottom by a thick coral Memphis-style squiggle pattern border, with a centered cream panel holding bold headline text, star ratings and a small phone/heart illustration.

## What it means
A patterned border acting as a picture frame lets the interior content stay simple and legible while the poster still feels decorated and event-worthy at a glance.

## Techniques
- frame content top and bottom with a busy decorative pattern strip while keeping the center calm
- use a thin vertical divider line to separate a visual anchor from repeated detail rows
- repeat star icon ratings as a lightweight trust signal within a text-heavy layout

## Best for
- conference/convention event posters
- retro pattern-forward marketing
- two-tone poster template packs

## Vocabulary
- **Memphis pattern** — a bold, playful abstract pattern style associated with 1980s Memphis Group design, using squiggles and geometric shapes
