# 'Moment of Evolution, Explore the Future' tech pack in teal-purple with glitchy dot-grid VR photos

![](../images/print-social-moment-of-evolution-tech-posts-teal-purple-glitch-vr.avif)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Social template pack · **Design style:** Dark & Neon
**Tags:** tech, vr, teal, purple, glitch, instagram

## What it is
Nine square posts on teal-to-purple gradients with photos of a person in a VR headset and jacket, a fine dot-grid glitch texture, split-colour headlines ('moment of evolution', 'Explore the future') mixing purple and white, and small corner logo details.

## What it means
Futuristic and immersive; the glitch dot texture over moody VR photography sells 'the future' as something already arriving.

## Techniques
- Overlay a fine dot-grid glitch texture on photos.
- Split headline words across two colours for emphasis.
- Vary crop and framing of the same photo subject across posts.

## Best for
- VR and tech product launches
- Futuristic brand campaigns
- Dark gradient social packs

## Vocabulary
- **Glitch texture** — Visual noise or distortion mimicking digital error.
- **Split-colour headline** — A headline where different words use different colours.
