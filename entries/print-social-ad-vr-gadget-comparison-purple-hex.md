# Purple VR gadget comparison ad with hexagon pattern

![](../images/print-social-ad-vr-gadget-comparison-purple-hex.avif)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Social promo post · **Design style:** Dark & Neon
**Tags:** social ad, vr gadget, comparison, purple, hexagon pattern, product photo

## What it is
A square ad comparing two VR/gadget products side by side against a purple gradient with a faint hexagon-pattern texture, joined by a dotted radial guide line and an 'or' choice cue.

## What it means
Framing two products as an explicit either/or choice (rather than showing just one) helps a viewer quickly grasp there's a decision or comparison being marketed.

## Techniques
- position two competing products symmetrically with a clear 'or' choice cue between them
- add a subtle repeating hexagon texture for tech ambiance without overpowering the products
- use dotted radial guide lines radiating from each product like measurement callouts

## Best for
- product comparison ads
- gadget/tech e-commerce marketing
- VR/wearable device promotions

## Vocabulary
- **comparison layout** — a composition presenting two or more options side by side to aid a viewer's decision-making
