# Church service pack in dark green and beige with half-circles, sparkles and bold serif

![](../images/print-social-church-service-posts-dark-green-with-mid-century-shapes.avif)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Social template pack · **Design style:** Geometric & Bauhaus
**Tags:** church, faith, dark-green, beige, half-circles, serif, instagram

## What it is
Five posts in deep green and beige: 'Sundays Are For Serenity' in bold white serif, a photo of hands holding a Bible and rosary, service times '8hrs 12hrs 18hrs', mint and brown half-circles, four-point sparkles and dot grids.

## What it means
Calm, modern and welcoming; mid-century shapes make a church feel fresh without losing warmth.

## Techniques
- Use earthy half-circles as quiet decoration.
- List times in a large stacked serif column.
- Place a rounded photo inside a beige band.
- Add small sparkles for a hint of the sacred.

## Best for
- Church and ministry social
- Community events
- Faith-based organisations

## Vocabulary
- **Mid-century shapes** — Simple arcs and circles in muted retro tones.
- **Stacked list** — Items set one per line as a column.
