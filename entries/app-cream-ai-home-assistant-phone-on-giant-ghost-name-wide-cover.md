# Cream Assistant Phone on Giant Ghost Name

![](../images/app-cream-ai-home-assistant-phone-on-giant-ghost-name-wide-cover.png)

**Discipline:** app · **Design type:** Device mockup scene
**Tags:** assistant, cream, ghost-type, gradient-arc, voice, wide, cover

## What it is
A wide cream cover with a giant faint 'Niva' behind a phone showing 'How can I help you today?' with chips, a blue-to-orange gradient arc and an Ask Niva field with a blue mic button, with upcoming activity cards peeking below.

## What it means
The huge faint name fills the frame quietly and the warm gradient arc is a friendly focus.

## Techniques
- Ghost name spanning the width
- Gradient arc as the assistant's signature
- Suggestion chips above the field
- Blue voice button as a cool accent
- Cream fading to white at the base

## Best for
- AI assistant covers
- Soft cream mockups
- Family and home organisation apps

## Vocabulary
- **Ghost name** — Oversized faint brand text behind a subject.
- **Voice input** — A microphone button for speaking a request.
- **Peek cards** — Cards partly visible below the fold.
