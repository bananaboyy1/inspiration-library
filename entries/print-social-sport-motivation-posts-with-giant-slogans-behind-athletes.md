# Sport pack with giant slogans ('Do It', 'Hit Hard', 'Let's Go') weaving behind athletes on vivid gradients

![](../images/print-social-sport-motivation-posts-with-giant-slogans-behind-athletes.avif)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Social template pack · **Design style:** Photographic & Cinematic
**Tags:** sport, motivation, gradient, bold-type, athletes, qr-code

## What it is
Nine square posts on saturated gradients (orange, blue, red, green, purple) with huge white condensed slogans set behind and in front of cut-out athletes (basketball player, boxer, runner, footballer), red label bars ('Don't overthink it'), QR codes and small vertical text.

## What it means
Explosive and motivating; athletes breaking through type feel powerful and in motion.

## Techniques
- Layer the athlete between letters so they break through the type.
- Use a different saturated gradient per post.
- Add a red label bar with a sub-slogan.
- Include a QR code for engagement.

## Best for
- Sports brands
- Gyms and clubs
- Motivational campaigns

## Vocabulary
- **Type interlock** — A subject overlapping some letters and behind others.
- **Saturated gradient** — A vivid colour blend.
