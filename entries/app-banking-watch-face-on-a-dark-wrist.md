# Banking smartwatch balance screen with orange buttons on a silhouetted wrist

![](../images/app-banking-watch-face-on-a-dark-wrist.jpg)

**Discipline:** app · **Design type:** Wearable UI
**Tags:** smartwatch, wearable, banking, orange, silhouette, grey-backdrop, glass-card, minimal

## What it is
A pale grey background crossed by a dark, backlit forearm with a black smartwatch and green band. The watch shows an account card with balance and card digits on a pearly glass card with two rounded orange Send and Receive buttons.

## What it means
Calm and dramatic; the near-black arm makes the small bright screen glow.

## Techniques
- Silhouette the arm against a light backdrop so the screen is the only lit area.
- Limit the watch UI to one card and two orange buttons.
- Use a pearly gradient card as a payment visual.
- Keep the palette to grey, black and orange.

## Best for
- Wearable and watch app showcases
- Payment and banking micro-interfaces
- High-contrast product photography

## Vocabulary
- **Silhouette lighting** — Lighting an object so it appears as a dark shape.
- **Glance UI** — An interface for very quick reading on a small screen.
- **Pearl card** — A card with an iridescent white gradient.
- **Dual action** — Two side-by-side primary buttons.
