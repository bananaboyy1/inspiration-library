# 'Enjoy Your Music Anywhere' streaming story set on dark charcoal with orange-blue split headlines and asterisk icons

![](../images/print-social-enjoy-your-music-anywhere-stories-dark-orange-blue-asterisk.avif)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Story set · **Design style:** Minimalist
**Tags:** music-streaming, dark, orange, blue, asterisk, story

## What it is
Five dark charcoal vertical story cards with a pill 'YOURLOGO' tag, split-colour headlines ('Enjoy your music anywhere', 'Enjoy your sound and your essence') alternating orange and blue, asterisk icon rows, and pill buttons ('Test 1 month', 'Free', 'Download our app').

## What it means
Clean and confident; alternating orange-blue headline colours across cards create rhythm without needing any imagery.

## Techniques
- Alternate two accent colours across headline words per card.
- Use a repeated asterisk icon row as a simple brand texture.
- Keep every card free of photography for a pure typographic feel.

## Best for
- Music streaming apps
- Subscription service marketing
- Typographic dark-mode branding

## Vocabulary
- **Split-colour headline** — A headline where different words use different colours.
- **Typographic design** — A layout that relies on text and type rather than imagery.
