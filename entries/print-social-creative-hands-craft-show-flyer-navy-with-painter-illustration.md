# 'Creative Hands Craft Show' flyer in hot pink and navy with a flat illustration of a woman painting

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Event poster · **Design style:** Playful & Illustrated
**Tags:** craft-show, event, pink, navy, flat-illustration, flyer

## What it is
A navy A5 flyer bordered in hot pink, with mint 'Date' and address details, a bold white 'CREATIVE HANDS CRAFT SHOW' headline, small star sparkles, and a flat illustration of a woman in overalls painting at an easel beside a vase, ruler and paint palette.

## What it means
Friendly and crafty; a warm illustrated scene makes a community event feel personal rather than corporate.

## Techniques
- Frame a poster in a single bold contrasting border colour.
- Illustrate the event's activity directly (a person crafting).
- Scatter small sparkle stars for a light celebratory touch.

## Best for
- Craft and maker fairs
- Community workshops
- Illustrated event flyers

## Vocabulary
- **Bordered flyer** — A poster framed by a contrasting solid colour edge.
- **Sparkle accent** — A small star shape used for light decorative emphasis.
