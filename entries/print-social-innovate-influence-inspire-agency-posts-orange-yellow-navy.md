# 'Innovate Influence Inspire' advertising agency pack with flat character illustrations in orange, yellow and navy

![](../images/print-social-innovate-influence-inspire-agency-posts-orange-yellow-navy.avif)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Social template pack · **Design style:** Playful & Illustrated
**Tags:** agency, advertising, orange, flat-illustration, navy, instagram

## What it is
Five square posts alternating orange, navy and white, each with a flat-illustrated figure interacting with icon props (megaphone, magnet, target, envelope, play button, chart), bold headlines ('Innovate Influence Inspire', 'Beyond Ads Creating Experiences', 'Ads Agency') and a wavy organic edge shape.

## What it means
Energetic and consistent; recurring icon props across every post build a memorable visual vocabulary for the agency.

## Techniques
- Reuse the same icon-prop set across every post in a series.
- Alternate background colour while keeping illustration style fixed.
- Add a wavy organic shape as a soft background accent.

## Best for
- Advertising agency branding
- Marketing service promotion
- Flat illustration social packs

## Vocabulary
- **Icon prop** — A small symbolic object a character interacts with.
- **Organic accent shape** — A soft, irregular background shape.
