# Black Friday sale illustration: a giant tilted phone rendered as a pink-and-black shop window with an awning

![](../images/print-social-black-friday-phone-shop-window-awning-illustration.jpg)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Social ad · **Design style:** Playful & Illustrated
**Tags:** black-friday, sale, pink, illustration, shop-window, 3d

## What it is
A tilted 3D phone whose screen is illustrated as a boutique shop window with a striped pink awning, a display of a shoe, lipstick and gift box, a 'Black Friday Sale' circle badge, and a pink 'Discount up to 85%' rounded banner across the base.

## What it means
Charming and clear; turning a phone into a literal shop window makes 'shopping from your phone' visually explicit.

## Techniques
- Turn a device screen into a literal illustrated scene.
- Use a shop awning to signal retail instantly.
- Angle the whole object for a dynamic hero composition.

## Best for
- E-commerce sale banners
- Retail app promotion
- Black Friday campaigns

## Vocabulary
- **Screen-as-scene** — Treating a device screen as an illustrated environment rather than a UI.
- **Shop awning** — A striped fabric canopy, a classic retail storefront cue.
