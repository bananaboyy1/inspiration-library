# Portrait post with translucent grey vertical and diagonal panels and a yellow-and-white headline

![](../images/print-social-your-headline-here-post-with-grey-glass-panels-and-yellow-type.avif)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Social promo post · **Design style:** Photographic & Cinematic
**Tags:** portrait, yellow, translucent, diagonal, template, dark

## What it is
A square post with a moody photo of a curly-haired woman with hands at her neck in an underpass, overlaid by translucent grey vertical strips and a diagonal cut, a white 'LOGO', a right-aligned headline 'Your' in yellow and 'Headline Here' in white, body copy and yellow social icon circles.

## What it means
Stylish and editorial; glass panels add architecture without hiding the subject.

## Techniques
- Overlay translucent vertical strips and a diagonal slice.
- Right-align a stacked headline with one yellow word.
- Use yellow circles for social icons.

## Best for
- Fashion promos
- Personal branding
- Editorial ads

## Vocabulary
- **Translucent strip** — A see-through band over an image.
- **Right alignment** — Text lined up to the right edge.
