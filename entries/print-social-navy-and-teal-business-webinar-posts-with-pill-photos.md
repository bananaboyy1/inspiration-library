# Navy business webinar pack with pill-shaped photo crops, teal text and outline arrows

![](../images/print-social-navy-and-teal-business-webinar-posts-with-pill-photos.avif)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Social template pack · **Design style:** Corporate & Clean-Tech
**Tags:** business, webinar, navy, teal, pill-crops, arrows, instagram

## What it is
Nine navy square posts with 'We bet on the companies of the future' where 'companies' is teal, people photos cropped into wide pills and arches, periwinkle circles, thin outlined circle arrows and small event details.

## What it means
Calm and modern; soft pill crops make a corporate event feel approachable.

## Techniques
- Crop portraits into pills and arches.
- Colour one word of the headline in a fresh accent.
- Use outlined circles with arrows as directional marks.
- Repeat layout with swapped photo shapes across the set.

## Best for
- Online webinars
- Business events
- Corporate social series

## Vocabulary
- **Pill crop** — A photo masked into a stadium shape.
- **Arch crop** — A photo masked with a rounded top.
