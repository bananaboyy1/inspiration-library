# Instagram recruitment posts with stepped violet blocks, coral and yellow squares and cutout portraits

![](../images/print-social-purple-block-shapes-recruitment-posts-with-cutout-portraits.webp)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Social template pack
**Tags:** recruitment, violet, coral, yellow, blocks, cutout, instagram, carousel

## What it is
A lavender board showing three Instagram posts side by side: a violet abstract slide, a central post where a smiling woman with arms crossed overlaps a stepped violet block holding 'Simplify talent acquisition' with small coral and yellow squares, and a coral post with a yellow zig shape behind a cropped person.

## What it means
Bright and modern; stepped blocks and cutouts feel like a friendly recruiter.

## Techniques
- Build stepped blocks that a cutout overlaps.
- Add small coral and yellow squares.
- Use a violet, coral and yellow palette.
- Show posts in Instagram frames.

## Best for
- Recruitment and HR platform posts
- Block-shape systems
- Cutout portrait layouts

## Vocabulary
- **Stepped block** — A rectangle with a stair-like edge.
- **Overlapping cutout** — A person placed in front of a shape.
- **Accent squares** — Small coloured squares as decoration.
- **Post frame** — A card shaped like an Instagram post.
