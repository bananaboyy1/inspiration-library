# Roobert Specimen and Four-Colour Palette

**Discipline:** branding ·  · **Design style:** Glassmorphism
**Tags:** typography, specimen, palette, grey, green, red, gradient-swatches

## What it is
A pale grey slide titled Typography & Colors with a giant black 'Roobert' specimen partly covered by a frosted weight card, heading and text alphabets, a type scale card and four tall gradient swatches (charcoal, white, green, red) with hex codes and a solid bar at the base.

## What it means
A frosted card overlapping the giant name adds depth to a spec sheet, and glowing swatches make a simple palette feel rich.

## Techniques
- Frosted weight card overlapping the specimen
- Swatches as blurred glows with a solid bar
- Left-aligned labels beside alphabet samples
- Type scale card with sizes in px
- Very large specimen filling the width

## Best for
- Type and colour documentation
- Minimal brand guidelines
- Fintech identity slides

## Vocabulary
- **Frosted card** — A translucent blurred card placed over content.
- **Glow swatch** — A colour sample shown as a soft glow above a solid bar.
- **Type scale card** — A card listing text roles and their sizes.
