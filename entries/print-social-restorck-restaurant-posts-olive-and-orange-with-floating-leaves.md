# Restorck restaurant pack with olive headline bars, orange accents and floating green leaves

![](../images/print-social-restorck-restaurant-posts-olive-and-orange-with-floating-leaves.avif)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Social template pack · **Design style:** Warm & Organic
**Tags:** restaurant, olive, orange, leaves, food, discount

## What it is
Five light posts with olive green headline bars in condensed type ('Lets you Eat Better', 'Heart of Perfect Food Hangout'), orange hashtag tags and 'Open daily' buttons, diners in arch-cropped photos, big opening hours and blurred green leaves floating over the frame.

## What it means
Fresh and appetising; floating leaves add depth and a farm-fresh feel.

## Techniques
- Scatter blurred leaves in the foreground for depth.
- Set headlines on solid olive bars.
- Tag with a hashtag label above the title.
- Show opening hours large.

## Best for
- Healthy restaurants
- Cafés
- Fresh food brands

## Vocabulary
- **Depth of field** — Blurred foreground elements suggesting 3D space.
- **Headline bar** — A solid shape holding the title.
