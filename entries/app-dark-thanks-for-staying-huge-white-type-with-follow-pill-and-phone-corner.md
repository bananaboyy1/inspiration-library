# Thanks for Staying Till the End

![](../images/app-dark-thanks-for-staying-huge-white-type-with-follow-pill-and-phone-corner.jpg)

**Discipline:** app ·  · **Design style:** Playful & Illustrated
**Tags:** closing, dark, navy, huge-type, emoji, follow, phone-corner

## What it is
A near-black navy slide with faint grid lines, a Design by studio pill and an email pill at the top, a giant white headline 'Thanks for staying till the end.' with a cocktail emoji, a blue Follow Us pill, a smaller line 'Don't miss a new project' with a thumbs-up and a phone corner rising at the bottom right.

## What it means
Very big friendly type with playful emoji ends a case study warmly, and blue pills are clear calls to action.

## Techniques
- Giant white headline with an emoji inside
- Blue pills for follow and like
- Frosted pills for studio and email
- Phone cropped at a corner with a 3D pedestal
- Faint grid on near-black

## Best for
- Case-study closing slides
- Studio sign-offs
- Follow and contact calls to action

## Vocabulary
- **Emoji accent** — An emoji placed inline for personality.
- **Follow pill** — A rounded button inviting followers.
- **Corner device** — A phone cropped into the corner of a slide.
