# Colour-blocked agency pack in green, red and yellow with greyscale photos and ultra-bold caps

![](../images/print-social-colorful-agency-posts-with-color-block-grids-and-ultra-bold-type.avif)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Social template pack · **Design style:** Neo-Brutalist
**Tags:** agency, colour-block, green, red, yellow, bold-type, instagram

## What it is
Nine square posts split into flat blocks of green, red, yellow and white, greyscale team photos in circles and rectangles, ultra-bold wide uppercase headlines ('Unlocking Potential, Building Success!', 'Ideas That Work') and yellow ovals with 'Join with us' and arrows.

## What it means
Loud, confident and retro-pop; the block grid feels like a bold poster wall.

## Techniques
- Divide the square into flat colour blocks.
- Run type vertically in a narrow block.
- Keep photos greyscale inside colour blocks.
- Use huge ovals as CTA shapes.

## Best for
- Creative agencies
- Bold marketing campaigns
- Youthful brands

## Vocabulary
- **Colour blocking** — Solid areas of contrasting colour side by side.
- **Extended type** — Very wide letterforms.
