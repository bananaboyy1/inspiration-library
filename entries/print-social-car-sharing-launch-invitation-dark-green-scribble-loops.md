# 'Launch event of our new APP' car-sharing invitation with mint scribble loops and a floating route-list card

![](../images/print-social-car-sharing-launch-invitation-dark-green-scribble-loops.avif)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Event flyer · **Design style:** Playful & Illustrated
**Tags:** invitation, app-launch, dark-green, scribble, car-sharing, phone-mockup

## What it is
A dark green portrait invitation with mint green scribbled loop lines in the corners, 'INVITATION — Car Sharing' header, 'Share A Car' tagline, a bold headline 'Launch event of our new APP' with 'new' in script, a date/time line, and a floating phone-screen card showing a route search list with avatars and green add buttons.

## What it means
Playful yet informative; the loose scribble loops soften a launch-event invite while the floating app card previews the product itself.

## Techniques
- Frame a dark invitation with loose hand-drawn scribble loops.
- Preview the actual product UI as a floating card.
- Highlight one word of the headline in a casual script.

## Best for
- App launch events
- Mobility and rideshare invitations
- Product reveal flyers

## Vocabulary
- **Scribble loop** — A loose, hand-drawn looping line used as decoration.
- **Floating UI card** — A screen preview shown as if hovering above the layout.
