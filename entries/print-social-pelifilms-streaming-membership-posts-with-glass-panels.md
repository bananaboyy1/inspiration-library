# Pelifilms streaming membership pack with teal frosted-glass panels over movie-night photos

![](../images/print-social-pelifilms-streaming-membership-posts-with-glass-panels.avif)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Social template pack · **Design style:** Glassmorphism
**Tags:** streaming, membership, teal, glass, dark, free-trial

## What it is
Five posts showing friends watching TV in a dark room, with dark teal frosted-glass panels carrying 'Enjoy our free trial month' and 'Discover our new rates' in white, a cyan 'Subscribe now' pill and social icons.

## What it means
Cosy and cinematic; frosted glass lets the living-room scene show through like a screen overlay.

## Techniques
- Overlay a frosted-glass panel on moody photos.
- Keep the palette dark with one cyan CTA.
- Show the product in use, not the product itself.

## Best for
- Streaming services
- Subscription offers
- Entertainment apps

## Vocabulary
- **Frosted glass** — A translucent blurred panel.
- **Lifestyle photo** — A photo showing people using a product naturally.
