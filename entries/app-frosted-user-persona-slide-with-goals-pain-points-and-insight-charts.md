# Persona slide on blurred blue-grey with a portrait card, frosted goals cards and green bar insights

![](../images/app-frosted-user-persona-slide-with-goals-pain-points-and-insight-charts.jpg)

**Discipline:** app · **Design type:** UX documentation
**Tags:** persona, research, frosted-glass, blue-grey, green, insight-charts, portrait, fintech

## What it is
A misty blue-grey slide '06 User Persona' with a large rounded portrait of a businessman with name overlay, a quote, three frosted cards (Goals, Pain Points, Needs) and three insight cards with percentage pills over thin green bar charts.

## What it means
Calm and credible; frosted panels make dense research feel soft.

## Techniques
- Frost every content card on a blurred backdrop.
- Fade the portrait into the card bottom.
- Draw insight charts as thin green bars.
- Use small icons before card titles.

## Best for
- Persona slides
- Research summaries
- Glass-style presentations

## Vocabulary
- **Persona card** — A portrait with name and role.
- **Frosted card** — A translucent blurred card.
- **Insight pill** — A small percentage badge.
- **Goals/pains/needs** — A common persona structure.
