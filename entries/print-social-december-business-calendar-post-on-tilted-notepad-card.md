# 'December Business & Marketing Calendar' post with a tilted notepad card, flag ribbon and paperclip

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Social educational post · **Design style:** Minimalist
**Tags:** calendar, marketing, blue, notepad, hashtags, personal-brand

## What it is
A square pale-blue post with logo and author avatar, a bold 'December' title and italic blue subtitle, and a slightly tilted white notepad card with punch holes, a blue ribbon tag, and ruled lines listing dates, events and hashtags (#AnnualPlanning), finished with a paperclip.

## What it means
Handy and personable; the notepad metaphor makes a list feel like a saved planner.

## Techniques
- Present a list on a tilted paper card.
- Add a ribbon tag for the theme.
- Use a paperclip as a realistic touch.
- Pair each item with a hashtag.

## Best for
- Content calendars
- Marketing tips
- Personal brand posts

## Vocabulary
- **Skeuomorphic** — Imitating real objects like paper or clips.
- **Ribbon tag** — A flag-shaped label.
