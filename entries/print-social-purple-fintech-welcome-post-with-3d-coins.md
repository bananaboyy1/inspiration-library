# Purple welcome post with a smiling person, 3D coins and notification card

![](../images/print-social-purple-fintech-welcome-post-with-3d-coins.jpg)

**Discipline:** print-social ·  · **Design style:** 3D & Surreal
**Tags:** fintech, purple, 3d-coins, cutout, notification, app-badges, startup-launch

## What it is
A violet square post with a bold white welcome headline, a smiling man in a patterned sweater on an orange armchair holding a phone and waving, glossy gold coins flanking him, sparkle stars, a white transfer-success notification card and a URL pill with store icons.

## What it means
Friendly, joyful and easy to trust; the notification card proves the product works.

## Techniques
- Place a cut-out photo on a violet field with radiating light.
- Overlay a fake notification as proof of the feature.
- Float glossy 3D coins around the person.
- Use a URL pill and store icons as a footer.

## Best for
- Payment and money-transfer app launches
- Welcome and onboarding posts
- Startup promos

## Vocabulary
- **Notification proof** — A UI notification card used as evidence of function.
- **3D coin** — A glossy rendered coin used as a money symbol.
- **Radial glow** — A soft burst of light behind a subject.
- **URL pill** — A rounded capsule containing a web address.
