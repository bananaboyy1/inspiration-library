# Mind Matters podcast pack with blue-violet-pink gradients, rounded photo panels and sparkle stars

![](../images/print-social-mental-health-podcast-posts-blue-violet-pink-gradients.avif)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Social template pack · **Design style:** Gradient & Aurora
**Tags:** podcast, mental-health, gradient, violet, pink, sparkles

## What it is
Five posts on deep blue to violet to pink gradients with rounded photo panels of podcast hosts at microphones, bold white headlines ('Embark on a Journey Within', 'Real Stories, Real Impact'), large sparkle stars, half-circle shapes and a hashtag footer.

## What it means
Soothing yet contemporary; dreamy gradients match the introspective topic.

## Techniques
- Use a cool-to-warm gradient to suggest calm and hope.
- Put hosts in rounded photo panels.
- Use a big sparkle as a spark-of-insight motif.

## Best for
- Podcasts
- Mental health campaigns
- Wellness content

## Vocabulary
- **Aurora gradient** — A soft multi-hue gradient like northern lights.
- **Sparkle star** — A four-point star shape.
