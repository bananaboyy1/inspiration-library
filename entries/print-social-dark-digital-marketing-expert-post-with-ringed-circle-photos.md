# Charcoal 'We Are Digital Marketing Expert' post with two circle photos linked by orbit lines

![](../images/print-social-dark-digital-marketing-expert-post-with-ringed-circle-photos.avif)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Social ad · **Design style:** Corporate & Clean-Tech
**Tags:** agency, charcoal, yellow, circle-photos, orbit, ad

## What it is
A square charcoal post with two overlapping circle photos (a woman at a laptop, a team of three) wrapped in white arcs dotted with small circles, a white headline with yellow 'Marketing Expert', a yellow 'Join now' button and contact details.

## What it means
Clean and connected; orbit lines link individual and team.

## Techniques
- Overlap two circle photos to show individual and team.
- Wrap photos in orbit arcs with dot nodes.
- Use yellow only for key words and the button.

## Best for
- Agency recruitment
- Team promotions
- Service ads

## Vocabulary
- **Orbit arc** — A curved line circling an element with dot nodes.
- **Overlap** — Layering one element partly over another.
