# Scope of Work Four-Sprint Staircase

![](../images/app-scope-of-work-four-sprint-staircase-slide.jpg)

**Discipline:** app · **Design type:** Process slide
**Tags:** scope, sprints, staircase, grey, icons, lists, minimal

## What it is
A light grey slide titled Scope of work with a paragraph on the right and four staggered stages stepping down: sprint pills with icons (1 to 4), each above a heading (Research, Strategy, UI Design, Delivery) and a short list of activities with a thin vertical rule.

## What it means
A downward staircase shows progress through time, and lists under each pill make scope clear.

## Techniques
- Stages descending as a staircase
- Sprint pills with round icon buttons and faded end lines
- Thin vertical rules beside each list
- Small grey text for lists and larger for headings
- Paragraph placed high right to balance the stair

## Best for
- Scope and process slides
- Sprint-based project plans
- Minimal case-study documentation

## Vocabulary
- **Staircase layout** — Items stepping down diagonally to show progression.
- **Sprint pill** — A capsule labelling a work sprint with an icon.
- **Scope list** — A list of activities included in a phase.
