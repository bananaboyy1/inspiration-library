# 'Why Choose Us?' post with a looping dotted path from logo to success over green light bars

![](../images/print-social-why-choose-us-post-with-dotted-path-journey-and-green-glow.avif)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Social educational post · **Design style:** Dark & Neon
**Tags:** agency, journey, green, dotted-path, dark, growth

## What it is
A portrait black post with a pill logo and email, a large green uppercase 'WHY CHOOSE US?', and a looping dotted green path rising from 'Yourlogo' through 'Our Strategy', 'More Reach' and 'More growth' to 'Success', each with a green icon, over vertical light bars with a green glow at the bottom.

## What it means
Motivating and clear; the winding path turns a pitch into a journey.

## Techniques
- Draw a looping dotted path as a journey.
- Label milestones with small icons.
- Glow vertical light bars from the bottom.
- Start the path at your logo.

## Best for
- Agency pitches
- Process explainers
- Growth stories

## Vocabulary
- **Journey map** — A path showing stages to a goal.
- **Milestone** — A marked point along a path.
