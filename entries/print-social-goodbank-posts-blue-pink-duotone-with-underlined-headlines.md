# GoodBank pack with blue-to-magenta duotone portraits, stripe shards and underlined headlines

![](../images/print-social-goodbank-posts-blue-pink-duotone-with-underlined-headlines.avif)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Social template pack · **Design style:** Gradient & Aurora
**Tags:** banking, fintech, blue, magenta, duotone, gradient

## What it is
Nine square posts with a man in a pink suit rendered in blue-magenta duotone, diagonal stripe shards, white and blue panels with bold black headlines ('We Make Banking Easy For You') where a word is underlined, small icon feature rows and pink 'Know more' pills.

## What it means
Bold and friendly; the duotone and hot pink make a bank feel youthful and approachable.

## Techniques
- Render photos as a two-colour duotone.
- Underline one key word in the headline.
- Slice diagonal stripe shards across the background.
- Use tiny icon rows for features.

## Best for
- Neobanks
- Fintech apps
- Youthful financial services

## Vocabulary
- **Duotone** — An image in two colours, usually dark and light.
- **Stripe shard** — An angled shape filled with diagonal stripes.
