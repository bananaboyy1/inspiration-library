# Purple 'Social Media Designs' cover with a fan of tilted post cards showing thorn crown, portraits and stickers

![](../images/print-social-purple-ticketing-brand-post-set-with-crown-of-thorns-and-portraits.jpg)

**Discipline:** print-social ·  · **Design style:** Playful & Illustrated
**Tags:** portfolio-cover, purple, tilted-cards, yellow-pill, stickers, portraits, gradient, event

## What it is
A deep purple gradient cover with a small logo, a large white and yellow 'Social Media Designs' title, a yellow 'Ticketsdeck' pill, a semicircle fan of tilted white and purple post cards with photos of smiling people and a crown of thorns, sparkles and a designer credit pill at the bottom.

## What it means
Energetic and celebratory; a fanned card arc makes a body of work feel abundant.

## Techniques
- Fan tilted post cards in an arc.
- Highlight the client in a yellow pill.
- Mix white and purple cards.
- Add sparkle glyphs.

## Best for
- Social media portfolio covers
- Event and faith brands
- Purple gradient sets

## Vocabulary
- **Card arc** — Cards tilted and fanned in a curve.
- **Client pill** — A capsule naming the client.
- **Sparkle glyph** — A small star accent.
- **Credit pill** — A capsule with the designer's name.
