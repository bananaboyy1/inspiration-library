# 'Join Our Party Tonight' story set in navy with yellow ring photo frames and dot-grid accents

![](../images/print-social-join-our-party-stories-navy-yellow-ring-photo-frames.avif)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Story set · **Design style:** Retro & Pixel
**Tags:** party, nightlife, navy, yellow, ring-frame, story

## What it is
Ten navy vertical story cards with a recurring thick yellow ring shape framing party photos (clinking glasses, dancing, DJ lights), a small yellow dot grid, and bold yellow headlines ('Join Our Party Tonight', 'Let's Come Party', 'Feel the Music Now', 'Party Tonight Feel the Music').

## What it means
Energetic and consistent; the recurring ring frame gives varied nightlife photos one unified, recognisable brand shape.

## Techniques
- Frame every photo with the same thick ring shape for consistency.
- Use a dot grid as a light textural accent.
- Keep a single bold accent colour against dark navy throughout.

## Best for
- Nightlife and party events
- Club and bar promotion
- Dark neon-accented story sets

## Vocabulary
- **Ring frame** — A thick circular outline used to crop and highlight a photo.
- **Dot grid accent** — A pattern of small dots used as light background texture.
