# Violet, grey and black packing tape printed with a repeating logo on grey concrete

![](../images/branding-logo-printed-tape-in-violet-black-and-grey-on-concrete.jpg)

**Discipline:** branding ·  · **Design style:** Dark & Neon
**Tags:** tape, pattern, violet, black, concrete, perspective, packaging, repeat-logo

## What it is
An angled photo of three strips of adhesive tape on rough concrete: a grey one, a bright violet one folding upward into shadow, and a black one, each printed with a repeating symbol and wordmark.

## What it means
Confident and industrial; the repeat pattern turns a logo into ownable texture.

## Techniques
- Print the logo as a repeating pattern on tape.
- Fold the tape to catch light and shadow.
- Show three colourways on a raw surface.
- Use hard directional light.

## Best for
- Packaging and shipping identities
- Pattern-based brand systems
- Tech and logistics brands

## Vocabulary
- **Logo repeat** — A logo tiled as a pattern.
- **Colourway set** — Multiple colour versions shown together.
- **Raking light** — Low-angle light that emphasises texture.
- **Tape mockup** — Adhesive tape used as a brand surface.
