# 'Design Is A Solution To A Problem' poster with navy background, organic cut-out shapes and a greyscale designer photo

![](../images/print-social-design-is-a-solution-poster-navy-with-organic-cutouts.avif)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Poster · **Design style:** Neo-Brutalist
**Tags:** design-agency, navy, cutout-shapes, greyscale, poster, typography

## What it is
A navy A4 poster with bold white headline 'Design is A Solution to A Problem', scattered flat organic shapes in pink, purple and orange around the edges, a greyscale photo of a man reviewing print samples at his desk, a pink 'We Think & Design' tagline, and social icons.

## What it means
Confident and philosophical; stating a design principle as the headline positions the studio as thoughtful, not just decorative.

## Techniques
- Lead with a stated design philosophy as the headline.
- Frame a greyscale work photo with colourful cut-out shapes.
- Keep shape placement asymmetric for energy.

## Best for
- Design studio posters
- Agency philosophy statements
- Bold portfolio marketing

## Vocabulary
- **Design philosophy** — A stated belief guiding a studio's creative approach.
- **Asymmetric placement** — Elements arranged unevenly rather than in a mirrored balance.
