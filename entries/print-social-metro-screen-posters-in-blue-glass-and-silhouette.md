# Three digital-screen posters on brushed steel: blue ribbons, profile silhouette, glass steps

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Out-of-home poster set
**Tags:** ooh, posters, fintech, blue, silhouette, 3d-ribbon, steel-wall, slogans

## What it is
Three vertical screens in a steel-panelled wall. Left: pale blue folded ribbon shapes with 'Your money Your control'. Centre: a faded silhouette of a man's profile with 'Move money. No friction.' Right: deep blue radial light and glass steps with 'Spend smarter Live easier'.

## What it means
Calm and premium; abstract blues and short slogans suit commuters glancing.

## Techniques
- Use abstract 3D forms in brand blue as hero art.
- Fade a silhouette into the background.
- Write two-line parallel slogans.
- Show posters in an urban environment.

## Best for
- Fintech out-of-home campaigns
- Metro and digital-signage creative
- Abstract brand posters

## Vocabulary
- **Ribbon render** — A folded 3D ribbon shape.
- **Profile silhouette** — A side-view head shape used as image.
- **Parallel slogan** — Two short lines with matching structure.
- **Digital signage** — Screens displaying advertising.
