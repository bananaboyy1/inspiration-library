# 'Creativity Never Ends' BeCrea story set with collage-style photo cutouts, halftone dots and orbit lines

![](../images/print-social-becrea-creativity-stories-collage-orange-teal-halftone.avif)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Story set · **Design style:** Retro & Pixel
**Tags:** creativity, collage, orange, teal, halftone, story

## What it is
Nine story cards on cream-to-orange gradients with collaged black-and-white photo cutouts of people, glasses and cameras, spray-paint texture, thin orbit-line circles with small planets, star confetti, and headlines ('Creativity never ends', 'Explore Your Creativity', 'Unleash your Creativity').

## What it means
Eclectic and artistic; mixing collage cutouts, spray texture and orbit lines makes a creativity school feel genuinely experimental.

## Techniques
- Collage cropped photo cutouts at varied angles and sizes.
- Add spray-paint texture for a raw, artistic feel.
- Scatter small orbiting planet motifs alongside collage elements.

## Best for
- Art and design schools
- Creative workshop marketing
- Collage-style branding

## Vocabulary
- **Collage cutout** — A cropped photo element layered irregularly with others.
- **Spray texture** — A speckled paint-like effect used for grit.
