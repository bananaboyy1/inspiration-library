# Campaign poster with smiling candidate over sunset landmarks and yellow condensed type

![](../images/print-social-political-campaign-portrait-with-landmarks.png)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Campaign poster
**Tags:** campaign, political, portrait, sunset-gradient, condensed-type, yellow, red-label, map-outline

## What it is
A warm orange-to-red sunset backdrop with silhouettes of historic buildings and cacti. A smiling woman with long hair fills the left, a red name tag with a heart sits behind her shoulder, the slogan 'JUNTOS por Pernambuco e pelo Brasil' is set in tall yellow and white condensed caps, and a yellow outline map and green-yellow flag shape run along the bottom.

## What it means
Warm, local and optimistic; regional landmarks and national colours anchor identity.

## Techniques
- Place a large friendly portrait against a warm sunset gradient.
- Use tall condensed caps in yellow and white for the slogan.
- Bring in local landmarks as tinted silhouettes.
- Add a red name tag with a heart as a brand device.
- Outline the region's map as a graphic.

## Best for
- Political and civic campaigns
- Regional identity posters
- Personality-led promotions

## Vocabulary
- **Name tag** — A small coloured label carrying a person's name.
- **Landmark silhouette** — A tinted outline of a local building used as backdrop.
- **Condensed slogan** — A slogan in tall narrow letters.
- **Map outline** — A thin line drawing of a region.
