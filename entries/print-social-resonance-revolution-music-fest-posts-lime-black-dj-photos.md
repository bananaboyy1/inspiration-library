## What it is
Nine lime-and-black square posts with bold black condensed headlines ('Resonance Revolution', 'Euphonic Escape', 'Electrify the Night'), photos of DJs performing with light trails, small asterisk and starburst icons, dashed circle graphics and 'Festival Fusion Where Genres Collide' taglines.

## What it means
Loud and electric; a high-contrast lime-black palette with light-trail photos captures the sensory intensity of a music festival.

## Techniques
- Use a single vivid accent colour with black for maximum contrast.
- Capture performers with motion light trails for energy.
- Vary headline phrasing across posts while keeping the layout fixed.

## Best for
- Music festival marketing
- Nightlife and DJ event promotion
- High-contrast bold branding

## Vocabulary
- **Light trail photo** — A photo capturing streaks of light from movement.
- **High-contrast palette** — A colour scheme using strongly opposing values for impact.
