# Yellow/black World Graphics Day workshop poster

![](../images/print-social-poster-yellow-black-workshop-photo-split.avif)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Event poster · **Design style:** Corporate & Clean-Tech
**Tags:** poster, workshop, yellow black, photo duotone, event, corporate

## What it is
A vertical A4 poster split into a black headline zone, a yellow-duotone photo of a designer at a desk, and a blue footer zone with registration details and concentric arc decoration.

## What it means
Splitting the poster into three horizontal color zones (black/yellow/blue) creates clear reading order — hook, proof, action — without needing any borders or dividers.

## Techniques
- stack three flat-colored horizontal zones to create implicit hook/proof/action reading order
- duotone a photo into the poster's accent color so it doesn't need its own separate palette
- add a corner grid texture over the photo zone for a subtle 'design tool' reference

## Best for
- design workshop/course event posters
- corporate training marketing
- three-zone informational poster templates

## Vocabulary
- **reading order** — the implied sequence in which a viewer's eye moves through a composition's zones or elements
