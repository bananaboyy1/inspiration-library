# Cafe Tap Payment Photo in a White Swoosh

![](../images/print-social-white-swoosh-crop-cafe-payment-tap-photo.jpg)

**Discipline:** print-social ·  · **Design style:** Photographic & Cinematic
**Tags:** campaign, photo, white-swoosh, payment, cafe, card, navy-logo

## What it is
A photograph of a smiling barista offering a phone showing a payment screen while a hand taps a blue card, cropped inside a large white curved swoosh with a logo pill at top right.

## What it means
The curved white crop frames the tap moment like a lens, and the warm cafe scene humanises payment.

## Techniques
- Photo cropped by a large curved white shape
- Two devices meeting in the centre of the frame
- Warm bokeh background
- Logo pill in the corner
- Brand blue in the card and the barista's clothing

## Best for
- Payment and POS campaigns
- Photo crops with brand shapes
- Everyday commerce visuals

## Vocabulary
- **Swoosh crop** — A photo masked by a large curved shape.
- **Tap moment** — The instant a card meets a payment device.
- **Bokeh background** — Soft out-of-focus lights behind a subject.
