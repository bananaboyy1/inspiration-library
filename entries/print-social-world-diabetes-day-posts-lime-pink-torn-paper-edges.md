# World Diabetes Day pack in lime green and pink with torn-paper edges and numbered info cards

![](../images/print-social-world-diabetes-day-posts-lime-pink-torn-paper-edges.avif)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Social template pack · **Design style:** Retro & Pixel
**Tags:** diabetes, health, lime, pink, torn-paper, instagram

## What it is
Five lime-green square posts with jagged torn-paper pink accents, bold black headlines ('Protect Your Family', 'World Diabetes Awareness'), an illustrated insulin bottle, numbered info cards (01, 02), a small earth icon and date details for a November campaign.

## What it means
Urgent yet approachable; torn-paper edges give a health-awareness campaign a hand-made, grassroots energy.

## Techniques
- Use jagged torn-paper shapes as accent decoration.
- Number key facts for easy scanning.
- Illustrate the relevant medical object (insulin bottle) directly.

## Best for
- Health awareness campaigns
- NGO and nonprofit content
- Bold two-colour social packs

## Vocabulary
- **Torn-paper edge** — A jagged shape mimicking ripped paper.
- **Info card** — A card presenting one fact or step in a list.
