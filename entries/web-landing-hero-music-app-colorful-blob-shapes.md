# Colorful music app landing hero with organic blob shapes and phone mockup

![](../images/web-landing-hero-music-app-colorful-blob-shapes.avif)

**Discipline:** web · **Design type:** Landing page hero · **Design style:** Playful & Illustrated
**Tags:** landing page, hero section, music app, blob shapes, colorful, phone mockup

## What it is
A web hero section with a blue border frame, colorful overlapping blob shapes on the left surrounding a music-app phone mockup, and a bold 'More Music. More Fun.' headline with CTA on the right.

## What it means
Overlapping organic blobs in varied bright colors behind a phone mockup create energy and playfulness appropriate for a consumer entertainment product, without needing a busy photo.

## Techniques
- overlap several organic blob shapes in varied colors to create a playful, energetic backdrop
- frame the entire hero section in a bold solid-color border for a distinct 'card' feel
- balance a graphic-heavy left side against a clean typographic right side

## Best for
- music/entertainment app landing pages
- playful consumer product websites
- colorful SaaS hero sections

## Vocabulary
- **hero section** — the large, prominent top section of a landing page, usually containing the main headline and call to action
