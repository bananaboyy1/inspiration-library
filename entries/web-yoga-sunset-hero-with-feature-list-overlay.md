# Sunset Yoga Hero with Feature List

![](../images/web-yoga-sunset-hero-with-feature-list-overlay.png)

**Discipline:** web ·  · **Design style:** Minimalist
**Tags:** hero, sunset, photo, white-type, feature-list, rounded, wellness

## What it is
A rounded hero panel with a dimmed sunset photo of a person in a lunge on a beach, a white headline 'Simplifying Your Everyday Health Journey' and a scattered list of features (Health Score, Analytics, AI Assistant, Wellness Tracking, Insights, Progress, Alerts) with plus signs.

## What it means
A calm sunset and a scatter of light labels feel serene, and the person's pose echoes the promise of ease.

## Techniques
- Darkened photo to keep white type legible
- Feature words scattered in a loose grid with plus icons
- Headline left of the subject
- Rounded panel on pale page
- Silhouetted body forming part of the composition

## Best for
- Wellness website heroes
- Photo-led feature overviews
- Calm emotional hero sections

## Vocabulary
- **Scattered list** — Labels spread in an irregular layout rather than a column.
- **Dimmed photo** — A photo darkened so text on top reads clearly.
- **Silhouette pose** — A dark body shape used as a composition element.
