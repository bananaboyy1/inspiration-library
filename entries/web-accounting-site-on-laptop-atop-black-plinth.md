# Accounting firm site on a laptop placed on a black plinth in a dark studio

![](../images/web-accounting-site-on-laptop-atop-black-plinth.jpg)

**Discipline:** web · **Design type:** Device mockup scene
**Tags:** laptop-mockup, dark-background, plinth, blue, accounting, team-photo, product-shot, project-cover

## What it is
A laptop showing a white website with royal-blue accents and a team photo sits on a black cube in a black void, lit softly from one side.

## What it means
Formal and focused; the void makes the bright screen the only subject.

## Techniques
- Stage the laptop on a plinth in a fully black scene.
- Let one side light reveal the edges.
- Keep the website white to glow.
- Leave large empty areas for a premium feel.

## Best for
- Professional services and corporate site covers
- Dark portfolio thumbnails
- Minimal case-study heroes

## Vocabulary
- **Plinth staging** — Placing a device on a block like a museum object.
- **Black void** — An empty black background.
- **Edge light** — Light that only reveals the edges of an object.
- **Glow contrast** — A bright screen against black.
