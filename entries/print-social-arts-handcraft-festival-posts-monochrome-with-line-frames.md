# Arts & handcraft festival pack in black and white with thin line frames and sunburst icons

![](../images/print-social-arts-handcraft-festival-posts-monochrome-with-line-frames.avif)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Social template pack · **Design style:** Editorial
**Tags:** art, handcraft, pottery, monochrome, line-frame, serif

## What it is
Nine white square posts with thin black ruled frames and grids, greyscale photos of hands shaping pottery, serif headlines ('Piece of Art', 'Feel Something Wonderful'), small sunburst line icons, sparkle stars and a small square logo.

## What it means
Quiet and crafted; monochrome and fine rules feel like a gallery catalogue.

## Techniques
- Divide the post with thin rules like a catalogue page.
- Keep photos greyscale for a timeless look.
- Use tiny line sunbursts as ornaments.
- Set headlines in a modest serif.

## Best for
- Art festivals
- Craft studios
- Gallery events

## Vocabulary
- **Ruled frame** — A layout divided by thin lines.
- **Monochrome** — Using only black, white and greys.
