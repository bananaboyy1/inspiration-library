## What it is
A black landing page hero with a yellow outlined 'Log In' button, a white headline, a yellow 'Sign Up now' pill, a 3D scene of a black safe with a Bitcoin coin, stacked gold bars, floating coins, a 'BUY' sign and chess-pawn user figures in gold, silver and black, soft golden glows and partner logos (Binance, Coinbase).

## What it means
Premium and secure; gold 3D props tie crypto to the trust of physical wealth.

## Techniques
- Build a 3D hero scene from symbolic props.
- Use gold glows on black for luxury.
- List partner logos as trust signals.
- Add a hatched circle accent near the headline.
