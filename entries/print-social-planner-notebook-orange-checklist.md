# Spiral notebook weekly content planner checklist

![](../images/print-social-planner-notebook-orange-checklist.avif)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Social educational post · **Design style:** Warm & Organic
**Tags:** checklist, notebook, spiral binding, orange, planner, social post

## What it is
A square post styled as a photographed spiral-bound notebook page on an orange background, listing six weekly content-planning tasks each with a checkbox circle.

## What it means
Framing a checklist as a 'real notebook page' rather than a flat digital list makes routine productivity content feel more personal and tactile, encouraging a viewer to treat it like a to-do list.

## Techniques
- render a checklist as a photographed physical notebook page rather than flat digital text
- use open circle checkboxes to invite a viewer to mentally (or literally) tick items off
- let torn paper/color shapes bleed off-canvas at the edges for a scrapbook, non-static feel

## Best for
- social media strategy/education content
- productivity and planning brand posts
- content creator resource templates

## Vocabulary
- **checkbox list** — a list format pairing each item with an empty box or circle, implying actionable, completable steps
