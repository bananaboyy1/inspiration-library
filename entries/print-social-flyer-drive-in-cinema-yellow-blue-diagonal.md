# Drive-in cinema flyer with diagonal yellow/blue split

![](../images/print-social-flyer-drive-in-cinema-yellow-blue-diagonal.avif)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Event flyer · **Design style:** Retro & Pixel
**Tags:** flyer, cinema, diagonal split, yellow blue, retro, popcorn

## What it is
A square flyer for a drive-in cinema, split diagonally into dark, yellow and blue triangular fields, with a line-art car illustration, popcorn photo cutouts and a rounded logo lockup.

## What it means
A diagonal color split breaks the usual rectangular grid, giving a simple flyer a sense of motion appropriate to a car/drive-in theme.

## Techniques
- split a square canvas diagonally into contrasting color fields instead of stacking horizontal bands
- overlay a cut-out real photo prop (popcorn) atop flat vector shapes for tactile contrast
- contain the brand name in a rounded pill/blob logo lockup to soften a geometric layout

## Best for
- cinema/entertainment event flyers
- retro-themed local business marketing
- diagonal-composition poster designs

## Vocabulary
- **diagonal split** — a layout technique dividing a canvas along an angled line rather than horizontally or vertically
