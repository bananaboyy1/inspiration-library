## What it is
A near-black page with a big white heading and grey paragraph above four bordered dark cards in a bento arrangement: a wide Creators Special card with a 'Most Recommended' tag, a light Subscribe button, $19/month in monospace and four checked features; Starters $0, Teams $49 and Enterprise $99 each with a small tag, a dark Subscribe button and check lists.

## What it means
Technical and calm; monospace prices and outlined cards feel like a developer product.

## Techniques
- Set prices in monospace.
- Highlight the recommended plan with a light button.
- Use a wide card for the featured tier.
- Keep hairline borders on black.

## Best for
- Dark SaaS pricing
- Developer tool sites
- Monospace price displays

## Vocabulary
- **Monospace price** — A price set in a fixed-width typeface.
- **Featured tier** — The recommended plan given more space.
- **Check list** — Feature lines marked with tick circles.
- **Plan tag** — A small pill naming a tier.
