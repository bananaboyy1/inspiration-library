# Glowing particle-built envelope icons connected by thin data-network lines on deep blue

![](../images/web-glowing-particle-envelope-icons-network-dark-blue.jpg)

**Discipline:** web · **Design type:** Feature illustration · **Design style:** Dark & Neon
**Tags:** email-marketing, particles, network, dark-blue, glow, abstract

## What it is
A dark blue abstract composition of several envelope icons rendered as glowing particle grids and connected by thin white lines and dot nodes, evoking a digital network or data visualisation.

## What it means
Abstract and technical; particle-built icons feel more like data science than a literal photo, fitting automation and analytics themes.

## Techniques
- Build an icon from a grid of glowing particles instead of a solid shape.
- Connect multiple icons with thin network lines for a systemic feel.
- Keep the palette to one glowing colour on near-black for focus.

## Best for
- Email automation and MarTech
- Data science visuals
- Abstract tech backgrounds

## Vocabulary
- **Particle grid** — An icon or shape formed from many small glowing points.
- **Network line** — A thin connecting line suggesting data flow between nodes.
