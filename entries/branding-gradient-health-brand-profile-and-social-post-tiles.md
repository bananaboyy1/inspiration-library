# Gradient Health Brand Profile and Post

![](../images/branding-gradient-health-brand-profile-and-social-post-tiles.png)

**Discipline:** branding · **Design type:** Brand application mockups
**Tags:** profile, gradient, lavender, black, social, verified, wellness

## What it is
Two rounded tiles: a black tile holding a social profile card with a lilac cover and a gradient petal-person avatar, and a white-to-lilac tile with a large statement headline and the gradient wordmark.

## What it means
A soft gradient brand feels modern and caring, and showing both profile and post proves it works in the feed.

## Techniques
- Black tile to make a light card glow
- Gradient avatar as a brand stamp
- Large left-aligned statement with a wordmark sign-off
- Blur blobs of blue and violet in the post
- Consistent large corner radius

## Best for
- Health-tech identities
- Social profile presentations
- Gradient brand systems

## Vocabulary
- **Profile mockup** — A social account page showing brand assets.
- **Gradient wordmark** — Lettering filled with a colour gradient.
- **Contrast tile** — A dark tile that makes a light element stand out.
