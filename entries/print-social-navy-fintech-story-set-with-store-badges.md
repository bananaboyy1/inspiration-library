# Navy Fintech Story Set with Store Badges

![](../images/print-social-navy-fintech-story-set-with-store-badges.jpg)

**Discipline:** print-social ·  · **Design style:** Glassmorphism
**Tags:** stories, fintech, sky, card, glass-coin, store-badges, blue

## What it is
Three vertical stories: a woman with headphones under a cloudy sky, a translucent blue card, and a glass dollar orb, each with a centred navy headline and the App Store and Google Play badges.

## What it means
Airy pastel blues make finance feel light, and repeated headline and badge positions make the set cohesive.

## Techniques
- Consistent story chrome and headline position
- One hero object per story (person, card, orb)
- Store badges anchored at the bottom
- Blur and pearlescent glass rendering for objects
- Blue gradient washes from the sky to white

## Best for
- App install campaigns
- Fintech social stories
- Product launch story sets

## Vocabulary
- **Store badge** — Official App Store or Google Play download buttons.
- **Glass orb** — A translucent sphere rendered with refraction.
- **Story set** — A sequence of vertical stories designed together.
