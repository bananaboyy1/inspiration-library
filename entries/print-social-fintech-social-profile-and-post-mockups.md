# Fintech Social Profile Cover and Posts

![](../images/print-social-fintech-social-profile-and-post-mockups.jpg)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Feed mockup
**Tags:** fintech, facebook, instagram, glass, orange, profile-cover, mockup

## What it is
A presentation board titled 'Social Media' showing a bank's Facebook profile with a wide cover of layered translucent glass sheets, an orange logo avatar, and two Instagram-style posts with 3D silver coins.

## What it means
Soft glass and pearlescent gradients feel modern and trustworthy while a single orange accent gives the brand a memorable voice. Showing real platform chrome proves the design works in context.

## Techniques
- Fan of translucent glass panes as a banner illustration
- One orange word in a black headline as the brand accent
- Silver 3D objects on pale iridescent grounds for the feed posts
- Real platform UI shown around the artwork for context
- Generous grey margins so the board itself feels calm and premium

## Best for
- Fintech and banking brands
- Social media brand-system presentations
- Launch kits that need consistent profile and post visuals

## Vocabulary
- **Profile cover** — The wide banner image at the top of a social profile.
- **Glassmorphism** — Translucent, blurred panels that suggest frosted glass.
- **Accent word** — A single word colored differently to carry emphasis.
