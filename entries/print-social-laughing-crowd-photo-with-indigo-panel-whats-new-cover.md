# Laughing Photo with Indigo What's New Panel

![](../images/print-social-laughing-crowd-photo-with-indigo-panel-whats-new-cover.png)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Social cover post
**Tags:** product-update, indigo, photo, panel, swipe, carousel, events-app

## What it is
An Instagram carousel cover that splits a close-up of a laughing man in cream sunglasses at an outdoor event above a solid indigo panel with a faint arc pattern, the heading 'What's New on Tix?', a short line, the site name and an outlined SWIPE pill.

## What it means
A joyful face on top and a clear panel below make an update feel human, and the notch in the panel corner adds a designed detail.

## Techniques
- Photo on top and colour panel below
- Large light headline in white on indigo
- Cut corner where photo meets panel
- Outlined swipe pill as the cue
- Faint concentric arc pattern on the panel

## Best for
- App update announcements
- Event platform carousels
- Photo plus colour block covers

## Vocabulary
- **Split cover** — A cover divided into a photo area and a text panel.
- **Swipe pill** — An outlined capsule prompting the next slide.
- **Arc pattern** — Concentric curves used as a subtle texture.
