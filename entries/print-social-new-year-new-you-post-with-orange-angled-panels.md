# 'New Year New You' fashion post with orange translucent angled panels and dark fade

![](../images/print-social-new-year-new-you-post-with-orange-angled-panels.avif)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Social promo post · **Design style:** Photographic & Cinematic
**Tags:** fashion, new-year, orange, angled, portrait, shop-now

## What it is
A square post with a low-angle photo of a curly-haired woman in an orange hoodie against a mural, translucent orange angled panels, a dark fade at the bottom holding a bold white 'NEW YEAR NEW YOU' headline, two text columns and a white 'Shop now' button with an orange edge.

## What it means
Fresh and confident; orange panels pick up the hoodie and push energy across the frame.

## Techniques
- Pick the accent from the outfit.
- Layer angled translucent panels over the photo.
- Fade to dark at the bottom for text.

## Best for
- Fashion sales
- New Year campaigns
- Streetwear promos

## Vocabulary
- **Low angle** — A photo shot from below the subject.
- **Angled panel** — A slanted translucent shape.
