# PyxonLab logo on a giant blue stage screen with 'Premium & Trust Feel' and a lone silhouette

**Discipline:** branding · **Design type:** Brand application mockups · **Design style:** Gradient & Aurora
**Tags:** logo, stage, blue, gradient, keynote, monogram

## What it is
A dark auditorium with rows of chairs facing a huge wide screen filled with a blue gradient, a white angular 'P' monogram, 'Premium & Trust Feel' and 'Design That Turns Views Into Customers', with a man silhouetted walking in front.

## What it means
Grand and aspirational; the keynote stage makes a small brand feel big.

## Techniques
- Mock the logo on a keynote stage screen.
- Include a silhouette for scale.
- Echo the monogram shape as large faint forms on screen.

## Best for

- Agency identity decks
- Keynote-style mockups
