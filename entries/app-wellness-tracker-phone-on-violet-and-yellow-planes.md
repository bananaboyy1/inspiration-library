# Medication adherence app on periwinkle with yellow accent, standing on a textured slope

![](../images/app-wellness-tracker-phone-on-violet-and-yellow-planes.jpg)

**Discipline:** app · **Design type:** Health app screens
**Tags:** health, periwinkle, yellow, donut-chart, 3d-scene, tilted-phone, planes, wellness

## What it is
A periwinkle phone tilts on a lavender textured slope against vertical colour planes in navy, indigo, yellow and white. The screen shows a wellness tracker with yellow segmented tabs, a white card with a donut chart (54%) and three status pills, and a yellow plus button in the nav.

## What it means
Friendly, clear and optimistic; blue with a sunny yellow accent softens a medical topic.

## Techniques
- Colour the whole UI in one hue with one yellow accent.
- Stage the device against flat colour planes that match the UI.
- Use a soft donut chart with status pills.
- Place a shadow-casting phone on a textured surface.

## Best for
- Health-tracking and medication apps
- Two-colour brand systems
- Mockups built from flat planes

## Vocabulary
- **Colour planes** — Flat vertical blocks of colour used as a backdrop.
- **Donut chart** — A ring chart showing a proportion.
- **Accent pop** — A single contrasting colour used sparingly.
- **Textured slope** — A tilted surface with grainy texture.
