# Frosted pearl finance dashboard with fanned cards and hairline bar charts on a monitor

![](../images/app-pearl-glass-finance-dashboard-on-monitor.jpg)

**Discipline:** app ·  · **Design style:** Glassmorphism
**Tags:** dashboard, fintech, pearl, glass, monochrome, serif-numerals, fanned-cards, monitor-mockup

## What it is
A monitor on a steel stand against grey, showing a translucent light dashboard: a tile sidebar with a black active tile, a fan of frosted payment cards, four stat strips with tiny sparklines, hairline bar and wave charts and a transaction list. Numbers are set in a light serif.

## What it means
Refined and quiet; pearl glass and serif figures feel like private banking.

## Techniques
- Fan translucent cards at the top as a hero.
- Draw charts with hairline bars and single waves.
- Set numerals in a light serif.
- Keep a nearly monochrome pearl palette with a black active state.

## Best for
- Premium banking and wallet dashboards
- Glass-style web apps
- Neutral, elegant data UIs

## Vocabulary
- **Card fan** — Cards overlapped to show edges.
- **Hairline chart** — A chart drawn in very thin lines.
- **Tile sidebar** — A navigation of square icon tiles.
- **Pearl glass** — A pale iridescent translucent surface.
