# 'Your Digital Marketing Partner' poster with a red-duotone portrait and a wireframe mesh accent

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Poster · **Design style:** Editorial
**Tags:** agency, blue, red-duotone, wireframe-mesh, serif, poster

## What it is
A blue-bordered white poster with a bold high-contrast serif headline 'Your Digital Marketing Partner', a dotted pattern and solid orange circle accent, a red-duotone photo of a smiling person, a wireframe mesh grid shape, and a small connected-node logo mark with the brand name 'TARUKA'.

## What it means
Editorial and confident; pairing serif type with a duotone photo and wireframe mesh feels design-forward rather than templated.

## Techniques
- Pair a high-contrast serif headline with a duotone photo.
- Add a wireframe mesh shape as an abstract tech accent.
- Use a connected-node icon as a distinctive small logo mark.

## Best for
- Marketing agency posters
- Editorial-style corporate branding
- Duotone photo campaigns

## Vocabulary
- **Wireframe mesh** — A grid of lines suggesting a 3D or digital surface.
- **Node logo** — A mark built from connected dots, suggesting a network.
