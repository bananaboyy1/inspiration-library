# 'Super Cool Title' hero with flowing grey contour-line waves and a minimal top nav

![](../images/web-super-cool-title-hero-grey-flowing-line-waves.avif)

**Discipline:** web · **Design type:** Landing page hero · **Design style:** Minimalist
**Tags:** hero, grey, line-waves, minimal, abstract, landing-page

## What it is
A grey gradient hero with a simple top nav (Home, News, Events, Blog, About, Contact us), a bold left-aligned white headline 'Super Cool Title', body copy, a 'Discover more' pill button, and dense flowing contour-line waves filling the right two-thirds of the background.

## What it means
Calm and abstract; dense flowing line waves give a minimal grey hero visual richness without using any colour or photography.

## Techniques
- Fill empty hero space with dense flowing contour lines.
- Keep the headline and nav minimal so the line pattern can lead.
- Use a subtle pill button that doesn't compete with the background.

## Best for
- Abstract minimal landing pages
- Agency and portfolio heroes
- Monochrome hero backgrounds

## Vocabulary
- **Contour line wave** — A flowing pattern of parallel curved lines.
- **Left-aligned hero** — A hero layout with text anchored to one side rather than centred.
