# 'Digital Breakthrough' tech event poster with a huge gradient headline over blurred frosted-glass device panels

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Event poster · **Design style:** Glassmorphism
**Tags:** tech-event, webinar, maroon, glassmorphism, gradient-type, poster

## What it is
A blurred maroon-toned poster with a massive gradient-shaded 'Digital Break Through' headline stacked across three lines, speaker names, a date, session schedule, and two overlapping frosted-glass rounded rectangles suggesting blurred devices, closing with a dark maroon footer bar listing site, category and event name.

## What it means
Sleek and futuristic; the huge gradient type and frosted glass shapes suggest a high-production tech conference.

## Techniques
- Scale a headline across the full poster height in a gradient fill.
- Use blurred frosted rectangles as abstract device suggestions.
- List a session schedule in a clean two-column format.

## Best for
- Tech conference posters
- Webinar promotion
- Gradient-type-led branding

## Vocabulary
- **Gradient type** — Lettering filled with a colour fade instead of a flat colour.
- **Frosted glass shape** — A blurred, translucent rounded rectangle suggesting blurred glass.
