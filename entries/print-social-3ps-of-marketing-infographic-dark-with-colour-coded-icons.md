# '3P's of Marketing' dark infographic slide with a torn-corner header card and colour-coded icon columns

![](../images/print-social-3ps-of-marketing-infographic-dark-with-colour-coded-icons.avif)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Presentation slides · **Design style:** Corporate & Clean-Tech
**Tags:** infographic, marketing, dark, icons, presentation, three-point-list

## What it is
A dark navy slide with a white header card with a folded top-right corner, a three-column band below in orange, red and green each labelled 'P', connected by vertical lines down to outline icons (box, dollar sign, megaphone) and text blocks for Product, Price and Promotion.

## What it means
Structured and presentation-ready; the folded-corner card and colour-coded columns make a classic marketing framework easy to teach.

## Techniques
- Give a header card a folded-corner paper effect.
- Connect a coloured band to its icon with a straight vertical line.
- Colour-code each of three parallel points differently.

## Best for
- Marketing training slides
- Business presentation templates
- Framework explainer graphics

## Vocabulary
- **Folded corner** — A card design detail suggesting a turned paper corner.
- **Three-point list** — A framework or argument built from exactly three parallel items.
