# Illustrated Farm Landing on Monitor with Phone

![](../images/web-illustrated-farm-landing-hero-on-monitor-with-phone.jpg)

**Discipline:** web · **Design type:** Device mockup scene
**Tags:** illustration, farm, landing, blue, monitor, phone, drone

## What it is
A cover with a flat illustrated landscape of hills, clouds, wind turbines and a drone continuing seamlessly from a monitor showing a blue farming landing page with a centred headline, and a phone in front showing a matching app home.

## What it means
The illustration spilling out of the screen makes the product world feel real and cheerful, and the sky blue is friendly for agtech.

## Techniques
- Illustration bleeding out of the monitor frame
- Same illustration style in web hero and app screen
- Sky-blue hero with a floating pill nav
- Phone overlapping the monitor's base
- Flat vector landscape with layered hills

## Best for
- Agtech and sustainability sites
- Illustrated hero mockups
- Web and app pairings

## Vocabulary
- **Bleed-out illustration** — Artwork that continues beyond the device frame.
- **Layered hills** — Overlapping flat landscape bands giving depth.
- **Pill nav** — A rounded floating navigation bar.
