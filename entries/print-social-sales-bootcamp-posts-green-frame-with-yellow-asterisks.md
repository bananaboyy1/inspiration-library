# Sales Bootcamp pack with green borders, big yellow asterisks, coral loops and speech-bubble CTAs

![](../images/print-social-sales-bootcamp-posts-green-frame-with-yellow-asterisks.avif)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Social template pack · **Design style:** Playful & Illustrated
**Tags:** sales, bootcamp, training, green, yellow, coral

## What it is
Five cream posts with thick green frames, oversized yellow asterisks, coral ring and loop shapes, circle photos of trainers presenting, a shopping-bag chat logo 'Sales Bootcamp', navy headlines and coral speech-bubble dates.

## What it means
Upbeat and energetic; big playful shapes make corporate training feel fun.

## Techniques
- Frame posts with a thick border.
- Use giant asterisks as bold decoration.
- Put dates inside speech bubbles.
- Let coral loops weave behind photos.

## Best for
- Training bootcamps
- Workshops
- Event series

## Vocabulary
- **Asterisk motif** — A star-like symbol used as decoration.
- **Speech bubble** — A callout shape like dialogue.
