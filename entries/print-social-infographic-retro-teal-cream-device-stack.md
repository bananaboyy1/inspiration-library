# Retro teal/cream infographic with stacked device illustrations

![](../images/print-social-infographic-retro-teal-cream-device-stack.avif)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Product infographic set · **Design style:** Retro & Pixel
**Tags:** infographic, retro, teal, cream, device stack, donut chart

## What it is
A vertically stacked infographic in muted teal and cream, using flat retro-style monitor/laptop/phone illustrations, a donut chart, gender icon comparison bars and a bottom row of small social icons.

## What it means
A muted retro palette and rounded flat device illustrations soften a data-heavy layout, making device-usage statistics feel more editorial than a typical dashboard export.

## Techniques
- stack devices by size (monitor, laptop, phone) to visually represent a usage hierarchy
- use dashed leader lines from a donut chart to labeled percentages
- bookend the layout with a banner header and icon-row footer for clear start/end points

## Best for
- device usage/market share reports
- retro-branded tech content
- editorial-style data infographics

## Vocabulary
- **donut chart** — a pie chart variant with a hollow center, often used to display proportional data
