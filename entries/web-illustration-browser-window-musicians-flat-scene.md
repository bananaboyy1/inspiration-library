# Flat illustration of musicians framed inside a browser window

![](../images/web-illustration-browser-window-musicians-flat-scene.jpg)

**Discipline:** web · **Design type:** Feature illustration · **Design style:** Playful & Illustrated
**Tags:** flat illustration, browser window, musicians, playful, website graphic

## What it is
A flat vector illustration of two people playing music at a small table, staged inside a browser-window frame with decorative clouds, lanterns, target and flag icons floating around the outside.

## What it means
Embedding an illustrated scene inside a browser-chrome frame signals 'this is what the website/product looks like' even though the content is purely illustrative, bridging marketing art and product screenshot.

## Techniques
- contain an illustrated scene within a literal browser-window frame to imply 'this is the site'
- surround the framed scene with smaller decorative icon props to fill negative space
- use warm terracotta and purple tones to give a flat illustration a cozy, inviting feel

## Best for
- creative/community platform landing pages
- hobby or event website hero illustrations
- playful SaaS marketing illustration sets

## Vocabulary
- **browser frame device** — using a browser window's visual chrome as a container to signal web/digital context
