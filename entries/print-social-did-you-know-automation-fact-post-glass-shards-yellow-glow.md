# 'Did You Know?' automation fact post with a frosted glass card and glass shard corners over a green glow

![](../images/print-social-did-you-know-automation-fact-post-glass-shards-yellow-glow.avif)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Social educational post · **Design style:** Glassmorphism
**Tags:** fact-post, automation, black, glassmorphism, glass-shards, yellow

## What it is
A black square post with a wavy yellow glow-streaked 'Did You Know?' headline, a circled question-mark badge, a frosted glass rounded card stating an automation statistic ('Automation is expected to impact 50%+ of jobs worldwide by 2030!') with '2030' highlighted yellow, over a soft green glow and glass shard fragments in two corners.

## What it means
Intriguing and premium; glass shard fragments and a glowing headline make a single statistic feel like a striking reveal.

## Techniques
- Frame a statistic inside a frosted glass card for emphasis.
- Scatter glass shard fragments as corner accents.
- Highlight the most important number in a contrasting colour.

## Best for
- Fact and statistic series
- Tech and AI content
- Glassmorphism dark branding

## Vocabulary
- **Glass shard** — A fragment-shaped translucent decorative element.
- **Fact post** — A social post format built around one statistic or trivia fact.
