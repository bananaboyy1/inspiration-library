# 'st construct' pack in lime and white with angled cut shapes, blueprint photos and 35% offers

![](../images/print-social-st-construct-posts-lime-and-white-with-angled-cuts.avif)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Social template pack · **Design style:** Geometric & Bauhaus
**Tags:** construction, lime, white, geometric, blueprint, offer

## What it is
Nine square posts in lime green and white with angled polygon cuts, faded greyscale blueprint and drafting photos, a boxed 'st construct' logo, service lists ('Construction, Calculations, Draft & Designs, Projects') and '35% off' offers in lime type.

## What it means
Precise and fresh; sharp angles echo architecture while lime keeps it modern.

## Techniques
- Cut blocks at sharp angles to evoke building forms.
- Fade blueprint photos as texture.
- Run the logo vertically along an edge.
- List services in the accent colour.

## Best for
- Construction firms
- Architecture studios
- Engineering services

## Vocabulary
- **Polygon cut** — A shape with straight angled edges.
- **Blueprint** — A technical drawing of a building plan.
