# Home decor pack in black and white with huge high-contrast italic serif and a wavy line

![](../images/print-social-home-decor-posts-black-and-white-with-high-contrast-italic-serif.avif)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Social template pack · **Design style:** Editorial
**Tags:** home, decor, black-and-white, serif, editorial, interior

## What it is
Ten square posts alternating black and white with oversized high-contrast italic serif phrases ('Making comfortable spaces', 'Home sweet home'), a small '.h.' monogram, circle outlines, a squiggle line and small interior photos of plants and sofas.

## What it means
Elegant and editorial; giant italics feel like a design magazine cover.

## Techniques
- Let a huge italic serif phrase fill the post.
- Alternate black and white posts.
- Use a tiny monogram as the only branding.
- Add a single wavy line as decoration.

## Best for
- Interior design studios
- Home decor brands
- Editorial feeds

## Vocabulary
- **High-contrast serif** — A serif with very thick and thin strokes, like Didone.
- **Monogram** — A logo made of initials.
