# Dark green circuit-line tech business card

![](../images/branding-business-card-circuit-dark-green.avif)

**Discipline:** branding · **Design type:** Brand application mockups · **Design style:** Dark & Neon
**Tags:** business card, circuit lines, dark green, tech branding, mockup

## What it is
A two-sided business card mockup on a wood surface, using thin green circuit-trace lines over a black background with white/green typography for name and contact details.

## What it means
Circuit-line traces read instantly as 'tech company' without needing a literal chip or gadget icon, letting the typography stay the clear focal point.

## Techniques
- draw thin branching circuit-trace lines as a background texture standing in for a tech logo mark
- limit the palette to black, one accent green and white for high contrast at small card size
- carry the same corner graphic motif from front to back for a matched two-sided card

## Best for
- tech/electronics company stationery
- dark-mode brand identity systems
- IT or engineering firm branding

## Vocabulary
- **circuit trace** — a thin line motif resembling the conductive paths on a printed circuit board
