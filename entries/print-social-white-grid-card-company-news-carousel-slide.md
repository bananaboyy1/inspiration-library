# Company News Grid-Card Slide

![](../images/print-social-white-grid-card-company-news-carousel-slide.jpg)

**Discipline:** print-social ·  · **Design style:** Playful & Illustrated
**Tags:** carousel, white, blue, grid-paper, news, portrait, icons

## What it is
A portrait carousel slide with a stacked white card on faint grid paper: a bold sans 'Company news', three icon-led news lines, and a book cover, badge and flag sticker cluster.

## What it means
A clear structure with airy white makes updates friendly and skimmable. The sticker cluster adds personality.

## Techniques
- Stacked paper cards under the main card for depth
- Blue rounded icon tiles as bullet markers
- Bold key phrases in otherwise light text
- Pill footers with page count and navigation arrow
- Overlapping stickers and mockups at the card edge

## Best for
- Company updates and newsletters on LinkedIn
- Monthly highlight carousels
- B2B content marketing

## Vocabulary
- **Stacked card** — Layers of offset sheets peeking from under the main card.
- **Icon bullet** — An icon in a small tile that replaces a bullet dot.
- **Slide counter** — A '02/06' style pill showing position in the carousel.
