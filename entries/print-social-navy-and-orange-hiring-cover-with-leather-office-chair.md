# 'We Are Hiring! Join Our Creative Team' cover banner with an empty leather office chair and navy diagonal

![](../images/print-social-navy-and-orange-hiring-cover-with-leather-office-chair.avif)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Recruitment post · **Design style:** Corporate & Clean-Tech
**Tags:** hiring, recruitment, navy, orange, office-chair, facebook-cover

## What it is
A wide white-to-navy Facebook cover split by a diagonal, with a navy and orange headline ('We Are Hiring! Join Our CREATIVE & Professional Team!'), a navy box of open positions with arrow bullets, a black leather executive chair in the centre, and WhatsApp, address and tagline on the navy side.

## What it means
Aspirational and direct; the empty chair invites you to fill the seat.

## Techniques
- Use an empty chair as a metaphor for a vacancy.
- Split the banner with a diagonal between light and dark.
- Highlight one word in an orange tag.

## Best for
- Hiring banners
- Corporate recruitment
- Facebook covers

## Vocabulary
- **Visual metaphor** — An object standing for an idea, like an empty seat for a vacancy.
- **Diagonal split** — A slanted division between two areas.
