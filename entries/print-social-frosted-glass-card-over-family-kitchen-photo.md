# Playful family photo with a frosted glass frame and a white blob-shaped message card

![](../images/print-social-frosted-glass-card-over-family-kitchen-photo.jpg)

**Discipline:** print-social ·  · **Design style:** Glassmorphism
**Tags:** campaign, fintech, glass-frame, blob-card, family, navy, photography, italic-serif

## What it is
A candid kitchen photo of a father, child and two blurred family members. A glass rounded frame highlights the centre, with a hashtag and logo pill, and a white gradient blob card with headline 'Less managing. More living.', an advisor avatar and a navy CTA.

## What it means
Warm and human; the glass frame focuses the moment and the blob card feels friendly.

## Techniques
- Frame the focus area with a glass border and blur the outside.
- Use a blob-shaped card with a notched edge.
- Pair a two-line bold headline with a short line.
- Add an avatar and name for trust.

## Best for
- Fintech lifestyle ads
- Photo-led brand campaigns
- Testimonial-style social posts

## Vocabulary
- **Glass frame** — A translucent border isolating part of a photo.
- **Blob card** — A card with soft irregular edges.
- **Selective blur** — Blurring everything outside the focus area.
- **Advisor avatar** — A small portrait giving credibility.
