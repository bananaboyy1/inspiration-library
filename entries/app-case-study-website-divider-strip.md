# Black divider strip with outlined pill and one-line description

![](../images/app-case-study-website-divider-strip.png)

**Discipline:** app ·  · **Design style:** Minimalist
**Tags:** divider, minimal, black, pill-tag, case-study, two-tone-text

## What it is
A very thin, wide black strip with an outlined pill labelled 'WEBSITE' at the left and a tiny paragraph at the right where part of the text is white and part grey.

## What it means
Quiet structure; it acts as a chapter break in a long case study.

## Techniques
- Use an outlined pill as a chapter label.
- Split a sentence into bright and dim tones to show emphasis.
- Keep everything tiny on pure black to make a subtle divider.

## Best for
- Long case studies with several deliverables
- Chapter headers
- Minimal dark presentations

## Vocabulary
- **Chapter strip** — A slim band that labels a new section.
- **Outlined pill** — A capsule with a thin border and no fill.
- **Dim emphasis** — Lowering the brightness of secondary words.
- **Divider** — An element that separates sections.
