# Onboarding Divider with Blue Eyebrow

![](../images/app-onboarding-heading-slide-blue-eyebrow-and-large-black-statement.png)

**Discipline:** app · **Design type:** Case study divider
**Tags:** divider, white, black, blue-eyebrow, minimal, typography, section-title

## What it is
A nearly empty white slide with a small blue uppercase eyebrow 'ONBOARDING', a large black two-line statement 'Personalization begins with a conversation.' and a one-line description.

## What it means
Only type and space make a calm section opener, and the blue label marks the section quietly.

## Techniques
- Small blue eyebrow above a large statement
- Left-aligned block placed low on the page
- Only three text sizes
- Huge white margins
- No imagery

## Best for
- Case-study section dividers
- Type-only slides
- Minimal presentations

## Vocabulary
- **Eyebrow** — A small label above a heading naming the section.
- **Section divider** — A slide that introduces a new part of a document.
- **Type-only slide** — A slide made only of text.
