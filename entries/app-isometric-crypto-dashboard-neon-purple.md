# Isometric crypto dashboard scene in neon purple and pink

![](../images/app-isometric-crypto-dashboard-neon-purple.avif)

**Discipline:** app · **Design type:** Fintech app screens · **Design style:** Dark & Neon
**Tags:** isometric, crypto, fintech, dark ui, neon, bitcoin, dashboard

## What it is
A tilted isometric collage of phone and tablet crypto-trading screens connected by glowing pink circuit lines over a checkerboard dark background, with bitcoin coin icons scattered around.

## What it means
The isometric 'floating circuit board' framing turns a set of flat UI mockups into a single dramatic hero image that reads as high-tech and valuable.

## Techniques
- arrange multiple UI screens in isometric perspective to form one connected scene
- link screens with glowing neon circuit-line paths to imply data flow
- scatter oversized brand icon tokens (coins) around the scene for thematic reinforcement

## Best for
- crypto/fintech app landing pages
- dark-mode trading dashboard case studies
- tech conference or pitch-deck hero art

## Vocabulary
- **isometric illustration** — a style depicting 3D objects with parallel projection lines and no perspective convergence, common in tech marketing
