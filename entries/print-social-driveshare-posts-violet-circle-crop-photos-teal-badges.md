Five violet square posts with a simple car-outline logo, circle-cropped photos of people driving, teal circular '50% off' badges, small phone-and-pin app icons, dot grids, and headlines ('Get Around with Ease', 'Your Ride Solution', 'Share the Ride, Save Money').

Friendly and modern; circle photo crops and a bright teal badge make a mobility service feel approachable and rewarding.

- Crop driver photos into circles for a warm, personal feel.
- Use a bright teal badge for discounts against a violet field.
- Repeat a small app-icon device to reinforce the digital product.
