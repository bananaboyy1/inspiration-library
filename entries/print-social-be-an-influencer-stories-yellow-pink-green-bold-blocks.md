# 'Be an Influencer' story set in yellow, pink, purple and green blocks with sticker icons and a phone selfie photo

![](../images/print-social-be-an-influencer-stories-yellow-pink-green-bold-blocks.avif)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Story set · **Design style:** Playful & Illustrated
**Tags:** influencer, social-media, yellow, pink, sticker-icons, story

## What it is
Ten vertical story cards in alternating bold yellow, pink, purple and green blocks, with a woman taking a phone selfie, sticker-style icons (target, planet, lightbulb, eye, computer), repeated 'Influencer' text patterns, and headlines ('Be an Influencer', 'How to create new content', 'Digital Influencer', 'Plan your content').

## What it means
Loud and youthful; clashing bold colour blocks and sticker icons speak directly to a social-media-native audience.

## Techniques
- Alternate several bold colour blocks across a large story set.
- Scatter sticker-style icons for a playful social-media feel.
- Repeat the campaign word as background texture.

## Best for
- Influencer and creator courses
- Social media education
- Youth-focused bold branding

## Vocabulary
- **Sticker icon** — A small illustration with a bold outline, like a digital sticker.
- **Colour block alternation** — Switching between several solid background colours across a set.
