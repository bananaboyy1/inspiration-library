**Discipline:** web · **Design type:** Feature illustration · **Design style:** Photographic & Cinematic
**Tags:** cdp, concept-photo, wooden-blocks, icons, marketing-tool

## What it is
A photo of wooden cube blocks spelling 'CDP' on a light wood table, with two blocks above showing a people-and-chart icon and a monitor-with-chart icon, and a hand placing a third block with a connected-cube network icon into position.

## What it means
Tangible and approachable; physical wooden blocks make an abstract software acronym feel graspable and concrete.

## Techniques
- Spell an acronym with physical letter blocks for a tactile concept photo.
- Pair each letter with an icon block explaining its meaning.
- Show a hand mid-action to suggest the concept is still being assembled.

## Best for
- Marketing technology concept photos
- Blog and article headers
- B2B software explainers

## Vocabulary
- **Concept photography** — A staged photo representing an abstract business or tech idea.
- **Letter block** — A physical cube used to spell out words or acronyms.
