# Cream AI Home Assistant over Ghost Name

![](../images/app-cream-ai-home-assistant-phone-over-giant-ghost-name.png)

**Discipline:** app · **Design type:** Device mockup scene
**Tags:** ai-assistant, cream, ghost-type, gradient-arc, voice, chips, cover

## What it is
A cream cover with a giant faint 'Niva' behind a phone showing 'How can I help you today?' with quick chip actions, a colourful blue-to-orange gradient arc and an ask field with a blue voice button, with upcoming activity cards below.

## What it means
A warm gradient arc gives the assistant a friendly personality, and the cream palette stays calm.

## Techniques
- Ghost brand name behind the device
- Blue-to-orange gradient arc as an assistant graphic
- Suggestion chips above the input
- Blue mic button as the sole cool accent
- Cream page and UI for warmth

## Best for
- AI assistant and family apps
- Soft cream covers
- Conversational UI presentations

## Vocabulary
- **Suggestion chip** — A tappable pill offering a prompt.
- **Gradient arc** — A curved band of blended colour used as decoration.
- **Ghost name** — Oversized faint brand text behind a subject.
