# 'Mystery Deal Surprise' banner with gadgets exploding out of a glowing red gift box

![](../images/print-social-mystery-deal-surprise-gadgets-exploding-gift-box-red.avif)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Social ad · **Design style:** 3D & Surreal
**Tags:** mystery-deal, gadgets, red, gift-box, 3d-render, offer

## What it is
A red radial-glow banner with a bold gradient 'Mystery Deal Surprise' headline, 'Get Up To 50% Off On Gadgets' subline with the discount highlighted yellow, and a red gift box with its lid popping off as headphones, a smartwatch, earbuds and a power bank burst out amid gold confetti ribbons.

## What it means
Exciting and urgent; an exploding gift box makes a generic discount offer feel like a genuine surprise reveal.

## Techniques
- Explode products out of a gift box for a surprise reveal effect.
- Glow the background radially behind the burst for drama.
- Highlight the discount number in a contrasting colour.

## Best for
- E-commerce mystery deals
- Gadget and electronics sales
- High-energy product reveal ads

## Vocabulary
- **Product burst** — Items exploding outward from a container for dramatic effect.
- **Radial glow** — Light fading outward from a central point.
