# 'Best Agency for Your Business' post with an angled orange tint panel over a team photo

![](../images/print-social-orange-best-agency-post-with-angled-tint-panel.avif)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Social ad · **Design style:** Corporate & Clean-Tech
**Tags:** agency, orange, team, angled-panel, ad, corporate

## What it is
A square post with a photo of two smiling colleagues, a translucent orange angled panel on the left carrying a thin-and-bold uppercase headline, a paper-plane logo, an orange quote tab top-right and a white footer with 'Join us' and phone.

## What it means
Professional and upbeat; translucent colour keeps the photo visible while branding it.

## Techniques
- Lay a semi-transparent angled panel over the photo.
- Alternate light and bold weights line by line.
- Balance a top-right tab against a bottom footer.

## Best for
- Marketing agencies
- B2B services
- Corporate recruiting

## Vocabulary
- **Translucent panel** — A see-through colour shape over an image.
- **Weight contrast** — Mixing thin and bold type for emphasis.
