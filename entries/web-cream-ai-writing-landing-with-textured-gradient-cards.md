# Cream AI writing tool with serif headline, hand-drawn underline and three painterly gradient feature cards

![](../images/web-cream-ai-writing-landing-with-textured-gradient-cards.webp)

**Discipline:** web · **Design type:** SaaS landing page
**Tags:** saas, cream, serif, gradient-cards, texture, orange, blue, purple, pill-nav

## What it is
A warm off-white page with a floating pill nav, a chip, a large light serif headline with a lavender hand-drawn underline, black and white buttons, and three large cards with textured orange, blue and purple backgrounds holding UI snippets (suggestions, rephrase, tone options).

## What it means
Warm and craft-like; painterly textures make an AI tool feel human.

## Techniques
- Use serif for the hero headline on cream.
- Add a hand-drawn underline stroke.
- Give feature cards richly textured single-hue backgrounds.
- Float small UI cards over them.

## Best for
- AI writing and productivity tools
- Warm SaaS pages
- Feature card sections

## Vocabulary
- **Painterly texture** — A brush-like surface on a background.
- **Hand-drawn underline** — A loose stroke under a headline.
- **Feature card** — A card demoing a capability.
- **Floating UI** — Interface snippets over imagery.
