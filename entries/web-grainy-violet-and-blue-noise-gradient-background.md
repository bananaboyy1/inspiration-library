# Grainy navy gradient with violet and grey blooms

![](../images/web-grainy-violet-and-blue-noise-gradient-background.webp)

**Discipline:** web · **Design type:** Background pattern
**Tags:** gradient, grain, navy, violet, noise, background, texture, dark

## What it is
A wide navy field with soft violet and grey-white blooms and a coarse film-like grain over everything.

## What it means
Moody and tactile; grain makes a digital gradient feel printed.

## Techniques
- Overlay strong noise on a gradient.
- Place blooms off-centre.
- Keep tones within navy and violet.
- Use as hero or cover backdrop.

## Best for
- Hero backgrounds
- Album and poster art
- Dark gradient textures

## Vocabulary
- **Grain gradient** — A gradient with visible noise.
- **Bloom** — A soft area of lighter colour.
- **Film noise** — Random speckle like film grain.
- **Off-centre glow** — A light area away from the middle.
