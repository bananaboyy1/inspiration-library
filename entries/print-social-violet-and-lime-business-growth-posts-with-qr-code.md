# Violet business posts with lime arc framing a team photo and a QR 'Unlock your market value'

![](../images/print-social-violet-and-lime-business-growth-posts-with-qr-code.avif)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Social promo post · **Design style:** Corporate & Clean-Tech
**Tags:** business, violet, lime, qr-code, arcs, registration, corporate

## What it is
Two portrait posts on violet: one with 'Register Now & Grow' in lime above a team photo cropped by a thick lime arc and violet circles; one with 'Let's Unlock Your True Market Value', a lime 'Ready to grow?' pill and a large QR code in a rounded frame.

## What it means
The electric violet and lime contrast reads bold and youthful for a business message; arcs soften the corporate photo.

## Techniques
- Frame a group photo with a thick contrasting arc.
- Highlight the key phrase in the accent colour.
- Give the QR code a big rounded frame as the focal CTA.
- Echo partial rings in corners to link a two-post set.

## Best for
- Business event registration
- Consulting promotions
- QR-driven campaigns

## Vocabulary
- **Arc crop** — A curved band that masks or frames a photo.
- **Pill label** — A rounded capsule holding short text.
