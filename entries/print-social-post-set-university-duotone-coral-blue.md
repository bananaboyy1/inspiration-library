# Coral/blue duotone university social post set

![](../images/print-social-post-set-university-duotone-coral-blue.avif)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Social template pack · **Design style:** Editorial
**Tags:** duotone, university, social pack, coral, blue, education

## What it is
A five-post Instagram set for a sign-language university course, mixing duotone-treated photos in coral and blue with flat color panels and consistent geometric triangle accents.

## What it means
Alternating duotone photo tiles with flat color tiles in the same two-color palette keeps a photo-heavy set feeling unified rather than like disconnected stock images.

## Techniques
- duotone-treat all photography into just two brand colors for instant cohesion
- alternate photo tiles with flat-color text tiles across a grid for visual rhythm
- repeat a small geometric accent shape (triangle) across every tile as a brand signature

## Best for
- education/university marketing
- inclusive/accessibility-focused campaigns
- photo-heavy social content packs

## Vocabulary
- **duotone** — an image treatment mapping shadows and highlights to two chosen colors instead of full color or greyscale
