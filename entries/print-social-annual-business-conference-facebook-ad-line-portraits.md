# '2024 Annual Business Conference' Facebook ad with a ruled info table and four line-art portrait tiles

![](../images/print-social-annual-business-conference-facebook-ad-line-portraits.avif)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Social ad · **Design style:** Editorial
**Tags:** conference, facebook-ad, teal, line-portraits, ruled-layout, corporate

## What it is
A cream portrait ad on teal with a small square logo and company name, a ruled table row for the date ('MAY 04') and 'LIVE EVENT' tag beside the bold title, contact details in a second ruled row, and a 2x2 grid of red-and-teal duotone line-art portrait illustrations with a black 'connecting PEOPLE' tag overlapping the centre.

## What it means
Professional yet warm; hand-drawn line portraits soften a ruled, almost legalistic layout into something inviting.

## Techniques
- Structure logistics as ruled table rows like a formal document.
- Illustrate attendees as simple duotone line portraits.
- Overlap a tag label across the seam of a photo/illustration grid.

## Best for
- Business conference ads
- Corporate event marketing
- Facebook and Instagram ad templates

## Vocabulary
- **Ruled table row** — Content organised in a line-divided row like a form.
- **Duotone line portrait** — A face illustrated in outline using two colours.
