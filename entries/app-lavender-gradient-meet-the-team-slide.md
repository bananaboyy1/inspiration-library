# Lavender Meet the Team Slide

![](../images/app-lavender-gradient-meet-the-team-slide.jpg)

**Discipline:** app · **Design type:** Case study slide
**Tags:** team, portraits, lavender, dashed-path, duotone, minimal

## What it is
A lavender gradient slide with a two-tone 'Meet the Team' heading and three circular duotone portraits connected by a dashed curving path, each with name and role.

## What it means
A path linking the portraits adds a sense of journey and collaboration, and the duotone photos keep everyone in a consistent style.

## Techniques
- Portraits placed on a diagonal dashed path
- Blue duotone treatment for photos
- Soft ringed halos around avatars
- Two-tone heading with faded first word
- Gradient darker at the top fading to white

## Best for
- Team credits in case studies
- About pages
- Agency portfolios

## Vocabulary
- **Dashed path** — A dashed line linking items in a route-like way.
- **Duotone portrait** — A photo tinted with a single colour range.
- **Halo ring** — A soft circular border around an avatar.
