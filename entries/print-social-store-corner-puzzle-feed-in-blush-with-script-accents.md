# 'Store Corner' blush puzzle feed with interior photos, serif quotes and script accents

![](../images/print-social-store-corner-puzzle-feed-in-blush-with-script-accents.avif)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Instagram feed grid · **Design style:** Minimalist
**Tags:** feed, blush, interior, script, quote, puzzle-grid

## What it is
A 3x3 Instagram grid in blush pink and white for a home store: interior photos of plants, desks and lamps, framed quote posts with serif text, stacked 'LO REM IP SUM' type, vertical script titles and thin rule lines, plus a single post shown in Instagram UI.

## What it means
Soft and curated; alternating photo and quote tiles make the feed feel like a calm lookbook.

## Techniques
- Alternate photo tiles with typographic quote tiles.
- Run script titles vertically along edges.
- Keep one blush tone across the whole grid.
- Split words across lines as a type pattern.

## Best for
- Interior and decor shops
- Lifestyle bloggers
- Minimal feeds

## Vocabulary
- **Puzzle feed** — A grid planned so posts look cohesive together.
- **Quote tile** — A post showing only a framed quote.
