# 'The Youth Fashion Revolution' poster with glossy orange 3D blob letters on black and a pink flower badge

![](../images/print-social-youth-fashion-revolution-poster-black-glossy-orange-blobs.avif)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Poster · **Design style:** 3D & Surreal
**Tags:** fashion, youth, black, orange, 3d-blob, poster

## What it is
A pink-bordered black square poster with two glossy orange 3D blob shapes (one shaped like a plus sign) flanking bold cream text 'The Youth Fashion Revolution', a circular pink-and-orange flower badge above, and a cream 'Visit Our Website' button.

## What it means
Bold and youthful; glossy squishy 3D shapes give a fashion statement real physical presence and playfulness.

## Techniques
- Use glossy 3D blob shapes as pure abstract decoration.
- Frame a black poster in one bright contrasting border colour.
- Place a circular badge above the headline as a stamp-like anchor.

## Best for
- Youth fashion campaigns
- Bold 3D-accented posters
- Streetwear branding

## Vocabulary
- **3D blob** — A glossy, softly rendered organic shape.
- **Flower badge** — A circular emblem with petal-like radiating shapes.
