# 'Creativity Workshop' poster with a red duotone photo of hand-painting a T-shirt and a scrolling ticker header

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Event poster · **Design style:** Retro & Pixel
**Tags:** workshop, red, duotone, ticker, poster, typography

## What it is
A red-bordered poster with a black 'creativity' scrolling ticker band across the top, a wireframe globe icon, bold black 'Creativity Workshop' title, an orange tagline 'Find Your Type Of Creativity', social handles in a black bar, and a red-duotone photo of hands painting a zigzag pattern on a T-shirt.

## What it means
Bold and tactile; the duotone painting photo and scrolling ticker give a workshop poster real editorial energy.

## Techniques
- Run a repeating word as a scrolling ticker band.
- Duotone a process photo to match the brand's accent colour.
- Pair a wireframe icon with bold flat type for contrast.

## Best for
- Workshop and class posters
- Craft and maker events
- Editorial-style event marketing

## Vocabulary
- **Ticker band** — A horizontal strip of repeating scrolling text.
- **Process photo** — An image showing an activity being performed, not just its result.
