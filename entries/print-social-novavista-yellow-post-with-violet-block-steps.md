# Novavista yellow post with violet headline, plus-sign grid and stepped violet blocks

![](../images/print-social-novavista-yellow-post-with-violet-block-steps.jpg)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Social promo post · **Design style:** Geometric & Bauhaus
**Tags:** agency, yellow, violet, geometric, grid, minimal

## What it is
A square yellow post with a faint grid of plus signs, violet headline 'Where Creativity Meets Results, We Are Your Marketing Partner.', and a bottom-right stack of violet and lilac rectangles like steps.

## What it means
Cheerful and structured; the plus grid and blocks suggest building results piece by piece.

## Techniques
- Use a plus-sign grid as subtle texture.
- Stack rectangles in two tints as a corner motif.
- Set a single-colour headline on a bright field.

## Best for
- Agency brand series
- Partnership messaging
- Bold minimal posts

## Vocabulary
- **Plus grid** — A repeated pattern of small plus marks.
- **Tint** — A lighter version of a colour.
