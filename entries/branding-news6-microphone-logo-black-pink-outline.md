# 'news6' logo: a pink-and-white outline microphone forming the number 6 on black

![](../images/branding-news6-microphone-logo-black-pink-outline.avif)

**Discipline:** branding · **Design type:** Wordmarks · **Design style:** Minimalist
**Tags:** logo, news, microphone, pink, black, wordmark

## What it is
A black square logo tile with a thin white microphone outline whose cord and head curl to form the shape of the number '6' in hot pink, above a lowercase 'news6' wordmark where the '6' matches the icon's pink accent.

## What it means
Clever and memorable; hiding the brand number inside the product icon (a mic) creates an instantly ownable mark.

## Techniques
- Shape an icon so it doubles as a number or letter.
- Match one accent colour between the icon and the wordmark.
- Use thin outline strokes for a modern, light logo feel.

## Best for
- News and media brand identities
- Broadcast and podcast logos
- Number-integrated logo design

## Vocabulary
- **Icon-letter fusion** — Designing an icon to double as a letter or number shape.
- **Outline logo** — A logo drawn using only line strokes, no fills.
