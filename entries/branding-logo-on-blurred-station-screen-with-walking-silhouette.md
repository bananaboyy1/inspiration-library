# White violet-screen billboard logo in a station with a motion-blurred walker

![](../images/branding-logo-on-blurred-station-screen-with-walking-silhouette.jpg)

**Discipline:** branding · **Design type:** Brand application mockups
**Tags:** billboard, ooh, violet, white, motion-blur, silhouette, tunnel, logo

## What it is
A wide violet LED billboard in a dark tunnel-like station with a white Sovrix logo and a blurred silhouette walking with a backpack, on a reflective floor.

## What it means
Immersive and modern; the walker gives scale and motion.

## Techniques
- Show the logo on a huge violet screen.
- Blur a passer-by for motion.
- Use a reflective floor.
- Keep the space dark.

## Best for
- Out-of-home logo presentations
- Transit advertising
- Tech identities

## Vocabulary
- **LED billboard** — A large screen used for ads.
- **Motion blur silhouette** — A blurred walking figure.
- **Transit scene** — A station or tunnel setting.
- **Reflective floor** — A glossy floor mirroring light.
