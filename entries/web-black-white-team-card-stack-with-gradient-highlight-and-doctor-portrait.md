# Team Card Stack with Gradient Highlight

![](../images/web-black-white-team-card-stack-with-gradient-highlight-and-doctor-portrait.png)

**Discipline:** web ·  · **Design style:** Gradient & Aurora
**Tags:** team, card-stack, gradient, doctor, portrait, black, white, list

## What it is
A split image: a black panel with a stack of white cards for a team list where the front card is a lilac and blue gradient with a circular portrait of a doctor inside a dotted ring, name, role and number, and a white panel with a cut-out doctor and a name card over the lower body.

## What it means
A stack with one highlighted card shows a selected item, and the gradient plus ring makes the profile the focus.

## Techniques
- Stack of cards with one lifted gradient card
- Portrait in a circle with a dotted ring
- Names and numbers in the lower corners
- Cut-out portrait with a frosted name card
- Black panel contrasted with white panel

## Best for
- Team and doctor list components
- Health platform profiles
- Card stack interactions

## Vocabulary
- **Card stack** — Overlapping cards showing a list in layers.
- **Dotted ring** — A circle of small ticks around an avatar.
- **Name card** — A frosted card with a person's name and role.
