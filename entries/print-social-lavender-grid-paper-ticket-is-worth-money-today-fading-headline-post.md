# Your Ticket Is Worth Money Today Fading Headline

![](../images/print-social-lavender-grid-paper-ticket-is-worth-money-today-fading-headline-post.png)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Social ad
**Tags:** fading-headline, lavender, grid-paper, cutout-collage, stressed-man, blue-footer, urgency

## What it is
A lavender graph-paper post with a small logo and a mono NIGERIA · RESALE label, a large headline that fades line by line: 'Your ticket is worth money TODAY.' in dark with blue emphasis, 'Tomorrow it's worth less.' in grey and 'After the event worth nothing.' almost invisible, three pill chips with the same timeline (the last struck through), a cut-out collage of a stressed man with a phone, clock, clipboard and hands, and a blue footer bar with the site and a LIST TODAY button.

## What it means
The headline literally loses value as it fades, making the message physical, and the collage adds humour to the stress.

## Techniques
- Headline fading line by line to illustrate loss
- Pill chips repeating the timeline, last one struck through
- Cut-out collage of hands, phone, clock and clipboard
- Faint graph-paper background
- Solid blue footer bar with CTA

## Best for
- Urgency and resale campaigns
- Concept-led type treatments
- Cut-out collage posts

## Vocabulary
- **Fading headline** — Lines that lose opacity to express decline.
- **Cut-out collage** — Masked photo pieces arranged around a subject.
- **Footer CTA bar** — A coloured strip at the bottom with a link and a button.
