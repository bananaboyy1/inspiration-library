# Cream Education Landing Page with Yellow Cards

![](../images/web-cream-education-platform-landing-page-yellow-accents.jpg)

**Discipline:** web · **Design type:** Landing page
**Tags:** education, cream, yellow, cards, outlined, courses, testimonials, long-page

## What it is
A long cream landing page with a black-outlined nav, hero with a portrait in an arch shape and yellow rating cards, stats, outlined course cards, pastel category tiles, mentor cards, yellow testimonials, blog cards and a footer.

## What it means
Black outlines with pastel and yellow fills give a playful neo-brutalist feel that is friendly for learning.

## Techniques
- Thick black outlines on all cards
- Portrait in an arch with a yellow outline
- Sparkle doodles beside cards
- Pastel tiles per category with icon badges
- Yellow highlight cards for testimonials

## Best for
- Education and course marketplaces
- Friendly consumer platforms
- Playful outlined UI systems

## Vocabulary
- **Outlined card** — A card defined by a bold border rather than a shadow.
- **Arch crop** — A photo masked into a rounded-top arch.
- **Category tile** — A coloured card representing a course category.
