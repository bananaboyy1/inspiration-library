# Beauty clinic cover: face half hidden behind a leaf inside a thin white rounded frame with flared serif type

![](../images/print-social-cosmetology-clinic-cover-with-leaf-and-line-frame.png)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Brand campaign visual
**Tags:** beauty, clinic, leaf, green, white-frame, flared-serif, photography, cyrillic

## What it is
A dark green photo of a woman with one side of her face hidden behind a large tropical leaf, her nails visible. A thin white rounded rectangle frames the image, with a flared serif 'ABC MED' at top left, a small city label at top right and huge 'КОСМЕТОЛОГИЯ' along the bottom edge.

## What it means
Elegant and natural; the leaf half-mask is a classic beauty gesture and the frame gives a magazine feel.

## Techniques
- Half-hide the face with a natural prop.
- Draw a thin white keyline frame inside the photo.
- Set big flared serif type along the frame.
- Keep the palette to green and skin tones.

## Best for
- Cosmetology and skincare clinics
- Photo-led beauty covers
- Frame-and-serif layouts

## Vocabulary
- **Keyline frame** — A thin outline drawn inside an image.
- **Flared serif** — A serif with slightly widening stroke ends.
- **Prop mask** — An object covering part of a face.
- **Edge-hugging type** — Text running along the frame edge.
