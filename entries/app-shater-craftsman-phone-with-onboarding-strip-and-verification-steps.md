## What it is
A near-identical companion slide to the craftsman onboarding spread: a large glowing phone held in a hand above a strip of five sequential app screens, and below, four verification steps ending in a green-checked 'account activated' state with an orange divider line and Arabic caption.

## What it means
Reinforcing; repeating the same verification story with a bigger hero phone emphasises the flow's importance.

## Techniques
- Scale up the hero device for emphasis.
- Keep the verification steps identical across slides for consistency.
- Divide sections with a thin orange rule.
