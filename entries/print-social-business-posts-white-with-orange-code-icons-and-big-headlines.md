# White business pack with heavy black headlines, orange code-tag icons and cursor arrows

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Social template pack · **Design style:** Minimalist
**Tags:** business, digital-marketing, white, orange, typography, icons

## What it is
Ten square white posts with big bold black headlines ('Connect with Your Audience', 'Skyrocket Your Sales'), small photos of a team, orange circle icons with code brackets and arrows, cursor pointers, dot columns, orange stars and pill 'Join us' buttons.

## What it means
Straightforward and digital; web-style icons signal online marketing expertise.

## Techniques
- Let a heavy headline fill half the post.
- Use cursor and code icons as a digital signal.
- Keep one small orange accent on white.

## Best for
- Digital marketing agencies
- Web studios
- Clean business feeds

## Vocabulary
- **Cursor icon** — A mouse-pointer symbol used as decoration.
- **Code brackets** — The < > symbols signalling development.
