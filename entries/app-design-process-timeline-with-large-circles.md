# Four-week design process shown with big overlapping hairline circles

![](../images/app-design-process-timeline-with-large-circles.jpg)

**Discipline:** app ·  · **Design style:** Minimalist
**Tags:** process, timeline, white, hairline, circles, italic-serif, minimal, case-study

## What it is
A near-white slide with a headline mixing regular sans and bold italic serif. Four huge thin-outlined circles overlap across the page, each holding a small week tag, a phase title (Discover, Define, Structure, Design) and a two-line description, with small square nodes on the outlines.

## What it means
Elegant and airy; oversized circles suggest an ongoing cycle rather than a rigid list.

## Techniques
- Use giant hairline circles as containers for phases.
- Mark connection points with small square nodes.
- Tag each phase with a tiny week pill.
- Mix regular and italic serif in the headline.

## Best for
- Process and methodology slides
- Case-study timelines
- Elegant fintech presentations

## Vocabulary
- **Hairline circle** — A very thin circular outline used as a container.
- **Node marker** — A small square or dot on a line.
- **Phase tag** — A small label naming a stage or week.
- **Overlapping cycle** — Circles that intersect to imply flow.
