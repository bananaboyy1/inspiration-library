# Four-step roadmap on lilac with indigo icon nodes and floating calendar, mood and map cards

![](../images/app-solution-roadmap-slide-with-dashed-path-and-floating-ui.jpg)

**Discipline:** app ·  · **Design style:** Minimalist
**Tags:** roadmap, process, lilac, indigo, dashed-path, floating-cards, numerals, accessibility

## What it is
A pale lilac board titled 'The Solution Roadmap' with an S-shaped dashed line linking four round indigo icon nodes, each with a faint giant numeral and label, beside floating UI cards: calendar, meal and hydration reminders, medication donut and mood chart, and a safe-zone map.

## What it means
Gentle and guided; the winding path turns features into a journey.

## Techniques
- Link nodes with a winding dashed path.
- Fade giant numerals behind labels.
- Alternate UI cards left and right.
- Keep to lilac and indigo.

## Best for
- Solution and roadmap case-study slides
- Health and care apps
- Feature prioritisation stories

## Vocabulary
- **Path roadmap** — Steps joined by a curved line.
- **Icon node** — A round icon marking a step.
- **Faded numeral** — A large low-contrast number.
- **Floating card** — A UI card with a soft shadow.
