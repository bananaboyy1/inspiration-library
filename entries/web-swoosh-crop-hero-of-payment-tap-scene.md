# Smiling barista holding a tap-to-pay phone and a card, cropped by a giant white swoosh

**Discipline:** web ·  · **Design style:** Photographic & Cinematic
**Tags:** fintech, photography, swoosh, white, payment, logo-pill, lifestyle, blue-card

## What it is
A café photo of a woman with curly hair presenting a phone showing $25.80 and a hand offering a pale blue card, masked by a huge white swoosh curve that sweeps from top left to bottom right, with a white logo pill at the top right.

## What it means
Warm and effortless; the swoosh carves the photo into a soft window.

## Techniques
- Mask a photo with a big white swoosh.
- Hold product and UI together in the moment.
- Place a pill logo in a corner.
- Keep the colour palette to blue and warm café tones.

## Best for
- Fintech and payments campaigns
- Photo heroes with a graphic mask
- Lifestyle scenes

## Vocabulary
- **Swoosh mask** — A large curve cropping a photo.
- **Tap-to-pay scene** — A photo showing contactless payment.
- **Logo pill** — A capsule with the logo.
- **Café bokeh** — Blurred lights in a cafe background.
