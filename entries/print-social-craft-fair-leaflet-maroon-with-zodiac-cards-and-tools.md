# 'The Great American Craft Fair' leaflet on maroon with gold display type and two zodiac fact cards

![](../images/print-social-craft-fair-leaflet-maroon-with-zodiac-cards-and-tools.avif)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Event poster · **Design style:** Playful & Illustrated
**Tags:** craft-fair, event, maroon, gold, pink, illustration

## What it is
A maroon-bordered leaflet with a bold gold-and-pink condensed 'THE GREAT AMERICAN CRAFT FAIR' title, a date and venue line, two colour-block fact cards ('Andromeda', 'Aquarius') explaining constellations, and a flat illustration of craft tools (glue, pins, a cutting mat, stamps) in the bottom corner.

## What it means
Warm and characterful; mixing astronomy trivia with craft tools suggests the fair itself has quirky, curious stallholders.

## Techniques
- Pair two colour-block fact cards for quick trivia.
- Use condensed display type in gold for a fairground feel.
- Illustrate the event's actual tools and materials in one corner.

## Best for
- Craft fairs and markets
- Community event leaflets
- Hobbyist event marketing

## Vocabulary
- **Fact card** — A small block presenting a short piece of trivia or information.
- **Display type** — Bold, decorative lettering meant for large headline use.
