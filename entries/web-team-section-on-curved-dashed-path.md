# Meet-the-team section with duotone portraits on a diagonal dashed path

![](../images/web-team-section-on-curved-dashed-path.jpg)

**Discipline:** web · **Design type:** Page section
**Tags:** team-section, duotone, periwinkle, gradient, dashed-line, portraits, minimal

## What it is
A periwinkle-to-white vertical gradient with a small title 'Meet the Team' at the top. Three circular duotone portraits with names and roles step diagonally down to the left along a dotted curved path.

## What it means
Airy, friendly and guided; the path invites the eye through the team like a journey.

## Techniques
- Place portraits along a dashed curved line instead of a grid.
- Tint portraits in the brand blue for cohesion.
- Give each portrait a soft circular halo and shadow.
- Keep type minimal in two tones (dark and grey).

## Best for
- Team and about pages
- Startup and agency sites
- Any section wanting an organic flow

## Vocabulary
- **Dashed path** — A dotted line connecting elements.
- **Duotone portrait** — A photo tinted with brand colours.
- **Halo** — A soft circle around an avatar.
- **Diagonal stagger** — Arranging items in a stepped diagonal.
