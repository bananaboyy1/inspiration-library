# 'Start Today' gym pack on cobalt with orange photo panels, italic caps and sticker badges

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Social template pack · **Design style:** Retro & Pixel
**Tags:** gym, fitness, cobalt, orange, italic, stickers

## What it is
Nine cobalt posts with heavy white italic 'START TODAY' headers, photos of athletes on orange-gradient panels, round sticker badges ('Don't give up!'), triangle play marks, dot grids and orange URL bars.

## What it means
High-energy and retro-sporty; the saturated blue-orange clash shouts motivation.

## Techniques
- Clash complementary cobalt and orange.
- Put mottos on round sticker badges.
- Use italic caps for speed.
- Add dot grids and triangles as sporty ornaments.

## Best for
- Gyms
- Fitness challenges
- Sportswear promos

## Vocabulary
- **Sticker badge** — A round label like a sticker.
- **Complementary clash** — Opposite colours placed together for vibrancy.
