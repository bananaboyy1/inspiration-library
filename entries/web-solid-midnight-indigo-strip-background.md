# Solid Midnight Indigo Strip

![](../images/web-solid-midnight-indigo-strip-background.jpg)

**Discipline:** web ·  · **Design style:** Dark & Neon
**Tags:** indigo, dark, solid, strip, background, flat

## What it is
A very wide, very short flat strip in a single deep midnight indigo, with no visible content or gradient.

## What it means
A dark saturated flat tone serves as a quiet base color: professional, night-like and versatile behind light text.

## Techniques
- Use one deep, slightly purple-tinted navy instead of pure black
- Let a flat band act as a section divider or footer base
- Keep it uncluttered so overlaid white or neon text pops
- Treat colour alone as the design

## Best for
- Footer or divider bands
- Dark-mode section backgrounds
- Colour reference for tech and finance palettes

## Vocabulary
- **Flat colour** — A uniform fill without gradient or texture.
- **Divider band** — A thin coloured strip separating page sections.
- **Tinted black** — A very dark colour with a hint of hue, warmer or cooler than pure black.
