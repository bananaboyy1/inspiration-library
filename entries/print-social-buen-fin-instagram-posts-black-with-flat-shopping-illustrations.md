# 'El Buen Fin' Instagram pack on black with colourful flat illustrations of people shopping

![](../images/print-social-buen-fin-instagram-posts-black-with-flat-shopping-illustrations.avif)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Social template pack · **Design style:** Playful & Illustrated
**Tags:** buen-fin, sale, black, flat-illustration, spanish, instagram

## What it is
Five black square posts for the Mexican 'Buen Fin' sale event, with a red shopping-bag logo, bold white and teal headlines in Spanish ('El Buen Fin: ¡Más por menos', 'Descuentos que conquistan'), flat illustrations of people in bright clothing carrying oversized shapes and bags, small clouds, dot grids and flower doodles.

## What it means
Festive and friendly; flat character illustrations on black make a retail sale event feel like a celebration.

## Techniques
- Illustrate people interacting with oversized abstract shapes.
- Keep a black base with bright, varied character colours.
- Use small doodles (clouds, flowers, dots) as filler texture.

## Best for
- Retail sale campaigns
- Spanish-language marketing
- Seasonal shopping events

## Vocabulary
- **Flat character illustration** — People drawn with solid colours and minimal detail.
- **Filler texture** — Small decorative marks used to fill empty space.
