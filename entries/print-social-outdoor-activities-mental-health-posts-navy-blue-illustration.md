## What it is
Five navy-to-blue gradient square posts with white-bordered cards, a flat illustration of a woman in a sun hat sitting cross-legged surrounded by giant leaf shapes and gold coins, bold headlines ('New Outdoor Event', 'Outdoor Activities for Mental Health!') and event date details.

## What it means
Calming and inviting; giant stylised leaves around a relaxed figure make an outdoor wellness event feel restorative.

## Techniques
- Surround a relaxed figure with oversized nature motifs.
- Frame supporting posts in a white card border.
- Use a deep gradient background to set a calm mood.

## Best for
- Mental health and wellness events
- Outdoor activity marketing
- Nature-themed social content

## Vocabulary
- **Oversized motif** — A decorative element drawn larger than realistic scale for effect.
- **Gradient mood background** — A colour fade used to set an emotional tone.
