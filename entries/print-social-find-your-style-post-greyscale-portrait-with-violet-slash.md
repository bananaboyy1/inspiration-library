# 'Find Your Style & Your Self.' post with a greyscale portrait and translucent violet diagonal slash

![](../images/print-social-find-your-style-post-greyscale-portrait-with-violet-slash.avif)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Social promo post · **Design style:** Photographic & Cinematic
**Tags:** fashion, violet, greyscale, diagonal, portrait, dark

## What it is
A square dark post with a close greyscale portrait of a young man, a bold white and violet headline, translucent violet diagonal bands slicing across, halftone dot grids, diagonal hatch marks and a thin rule with URL.

## What it means
Bold and urban; the violet slash adds energy to a moody portrait.

## Techniques
- Slice a translucent colour band diagonally over a greyscale portrait.
- Colour the second half of the headline.
- Add dot grids and hatch marks as tech texture.

## Best for
- Fashion and streetwear
- Personal branding
- Bold promo posts

## Vocabulary
- **Diagonal band** — A slanted stripe crossing the composition.
- **Hatch marks** — Short parallel diagonal lines.
