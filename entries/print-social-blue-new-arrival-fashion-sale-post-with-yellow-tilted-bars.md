**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Social ad · **Design style:** Corporate & Clean-Tech
**Tags:** fashion, sale, blue, yellow, cutout, ad

## What it is
A square royal-blue post with faint rings, a cut-out man in sunglasses and camel coat before a glowing circle, a blue '50% off' tab, tilted cyan 'New Arrival' and yellow 'FASHION SALE' bars in condensed type, vertical side taglines and a URL pill.

## What it means
Confident and retail-ready; tilted bars inject energy into a monochrome blue scene.

## Techniques
- Stack tilted colour bars as headline blocks.
- Run small taglines vertically down both edges.
- Back the cut-out with a soft glowing circle.

## Best for
- Fashion retail sales
- Menswear promos
- New arrival posts

## Vocabulary
- **Condensed type** — Narrow letterforms.
- **Tilted bar** — A rotated rectangle behind text.
