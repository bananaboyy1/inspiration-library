# Drive-in cinema pack in teal, pink and yellow with retro car illustrations and popcorn boxes

![](../images/print-social-drive-in-cinema-posts-retro-teal-pink-yellow-car-illustration.avif)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Social template pack · **Design style:** Retro & Pixel
**Tags:** cinema, drive-in, retro, teal, pink, yellow

## What it is
Nine dark teal square posts with retro flat illustrations of a pink vintage car facing a giant cinema screen shape, black boxed headlines ('Enjoy the Best Premieres in Our Drive-In Cinema', 'Coming Soon in Our Cinema'), a movie ticket icon, popcorn box and drink illustrations, and a small 'THE CINEMA' logo.

## What it means
Nostalgic and fun; the vintage car and screen silhouette instantly evoke classic American drive-in culture.

## Techniques
- Illustrate a vintage car facing an implied cinema screen shape.
- Box headlines in black for a retro marquee feel.
- Add themed props (popcorn, tickets) as supporting icons.

## Best for
- Cinema and entertainment venues
- Retro-themed events
- Nostalgic brand campaigns

## Vocabulary
- **Marquee box** — A boxed headline evoking a cinema sign.
- **Retro illustration** — Flat art styled after mid-century advertising.
