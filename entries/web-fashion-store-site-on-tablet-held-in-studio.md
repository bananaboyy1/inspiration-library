# Fashion Store on a Tablet in a Studio

![](../images/web-fashion-store-site-on-tablet-held-in-studio.png)

**Discipline:** web ·  · **Design style:** Corporate & Clean-Tech
**Tags:** tablet, fashion, hands, studio, minimal, e-commerce, photo-hero

## What it is
Hands hold a tablet showing a white fashion store with a hero photo of coats on a rail and the heading 'CloudeyStyles.', in a softly blurred photo studio with a tripod.

## What it means
A relaxed studio setting fits a fashion brand, and the photo hero inside the screen ties the site to the scene.

## Techniques
- Tablet held at reading angle
- Blurred studio props behind
- Big overlaid title on a photo hero
- Centred heading section below the hero
- Neutral clothing to keep focus on the screen

## Best for
- Fashion e-commerce sites
- Contextual tablet mockups
- Lifestyle site presentations

## Vocabulary
- **Held mockup** — A device shown in hands rather than on a surface.
- **Photo hero** — A hero section built around a large photograph.
- **Studio blur** — A softly defocused workspace as a backdrop.
