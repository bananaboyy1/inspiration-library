**Discipline:** app · **Design type:** UX documentation · **Design style:** Corporate & Clean-Tech
**Tags:** user-roles, arabic, handshake, tablet, orange, ecosystem

## What it is
A light page with 'فهم وبناء التجربة' (Understanding and Building the Experience), an intro line, and a triangular diagram: client (01) and craftsman (02) phones flank a tablet control panel (03), with two curved orange arrows connecting to a handshake photo between them.

## What it means
Warm and structural; the handshake makes an abstract three-role system feel like a real partnership the platform enables.

## Techniques
- Arrange roles in a triangle around a connecting photo.
- Use curved arrows to show relationships, not just hierarchy.
- Number each role for easy reference later.

## Best for
- Multi-sided marketplace UX
- Ecosystem diagrams
- Stakeholder presentations

## Vocabulary
- **Multi-sided platform** — A product serving two or more distinct user groups.
- **Relationship diagram** — A diagram showing how entities connect, not just rank.
