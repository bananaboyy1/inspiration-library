# 'Business & Data' navy pack with greyscale meeting photos inside blue rings and arcs

![](../images/print-social-business-and-data-posts-with-blue-ring-photo-frames.avif)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Social template pack · **Design style:** Corporate & Clean-Tech
**Tags:** business, data, navy, blue, rings, greyscale, instagram

## What it is
Nine navy square posts with greyscale office photos masked into circles and ringed with thick cyan-to-blue arcs, a blue slanted-stripe logo, bold 'BUSINESS & DATA' headlines, triangle patterns and small hashtags.

## What it means
Techy and serious; bright rings energise sober photos and tie the series together.

## Techniques
- Desaturate photos so the brand blue carries the colour.
- Wrap photos with thick partial rings.
- Repeat a bold title lockup on every post.
- Add tiny triangle patterns as tech texture.

## Best for
- Data and analytics companies
- Corporate events
- Business consulting

## Vocabulary
- **Partial ring** — An incomplete circle stroke around a shape.
- **Desaturation** — Removing colour from a photo.
