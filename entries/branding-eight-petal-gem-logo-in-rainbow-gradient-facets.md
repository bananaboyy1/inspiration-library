# An eight-facet gem-petal logo in blue, pink, orange and green gradients above a bold 'Lorem Ipsum' wordmark

![](../images/branding-eight-petal-gem-logo-in-rainbow-gradient-facets.avif)

**Discipline:** branding · **Design type:** Logo with symbol · **Design style:** Geometric & Bauhaus
**Tags:** logo, gem, gradient, geometric, rainbow, wordmark

## What it is
A symmetrical mark made of eight faceted diamond 'petals' arranged in a ring around empty centre space, each facet gradient-shaded in a different hue (blue, pink, orange, green), sitting above a bold black 'LOREM IPSUM' wordmark and a thin 'SLOGAN HERE' tagline.

## What it means
Vibrant and premium; faceted gradients make a simple geometric ring feel jewel-like and dynamic.

## Techniques
- Arrange faceted shapes symmetrically around a negative-space centre.
- Gradient-shade each facet a different hue for vibrancy.
- Pair a colourful mark with a plain black wordmark for balance.

## Best for
- Creative agency logos
- Multi-colour brand marks
- Jewel or gem-themed identities

## Vocabulary
- **Facet** — A flat angled surface, as if cut like a gemstone.
- **Negative space** — The empty area within or around a shape that is part of the design.
