# Real Estate Case Study with Overlapping Circles

![](../images/app-real-estate-app-long-case-study-with-problem-circles-and-handshake.png)

**Discipline:** app · **Design type:** Case study slide
**Tags:** real-estate, long-scroll, circles, problem-solution, handshake, for-sale, white

## What it is
A very tall white page with a Problem & Solution heading, two overlapping circles (black for problem, white for solution) with red and green dots, a full-width handshake and house model photo, a hand-held app, social media posts and a red For Sale sign photo.

## What it means
The dark and light circles show the contrast between problem and solution at a glance, and full-width photos add human context.

## Techniques
- Overlapping black and white circles as a diagram
- Coloured dot markers linked by thin lines
- Full-width photographic bands
- Phone in hand on grey ground
- Social post mockups as a touchpoint

## Best for
- Real estate case studies
- Problem and solution storytelling
- Long-scroll portfolio pages

## Vocabulary
- **Overlapping circles** — Circles intersecting to show contrast or relation.
- **Photo band** — A full-width photograph separating text sections.
- **Marker dot** — A small coloured dot signalling a label.
