# Fati's donut site: animated overview grid of pink screens (hero, how it works, bundles, categories)

![](../images/web-fatis-donut-site-screens-grid-in-pink.gif)

**Discipline:** web · **Design type:** Landing page sections · **Design style:** Playful & Illustrated
**Tags:** food, donuts, pink, screens-grid, ecommerce, animation

## What it is
An animated grid of rounded browser frames showing Fati's pages: the 'Sweet Moments for Every Craving' hero, a 'How it works' three-step section, 'Build Your Bundle', categories, product grids and a brand story with a pink donut illustration.

## What it means
Complete and lively; tiling every screen proves the brand feel holds site-wide.

## Techniques
- Tile all key screens in a neat grid.
- Keep hot pink as the constant across pages.
- Use handwritten yellow sub-headings under bold titles.

## Best for
- Website case studies
- E-commerce overviews
- Brand-led redesigns

## Vocabulary
- **Screen grid** — A tiled overview of many pages.
- **Sub-heading script** — A handwritten line under a bold title.
