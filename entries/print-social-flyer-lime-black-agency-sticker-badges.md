# Lime-green agency flyer with sticker-style service badges

![](../images/print-social-flyer-lime-black-agency-sticker-badges.avif)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Event flyer · **Design style:** Playful & Illustrated
**Tags:** flyer, agency, lime green, sticker badge, scalloped shape, vibrant

## What it is
A lime-green A5 flyer for a creative agency, featuring a black content card with a scalloped/cloud-shaped corner cutout, and six numbered service items in blue/orange sticker-badge shapes.

## What it means
Rendering each list item as a distinct hand-drawn 'sticker' badge rather than plain text rows makes a services list feel collectible and playful, matching a youthful agency brand voice.

## Techniques
- render list items as irregular sticker/badge shapes instead of a plain bulleted list
- cut a scalloped cloud-shaped notch into a card corner for a hand-crafted, non-rigid feel
- use one bright unifying background (lime) so black-and-badge content pops with maximum contrast

## Best for
- creative agency self-promotion
- youth-brand service flyers
- playful vibrant marketing kits

## Vocabulary
- **sticker badge** — a small irregularly-outlined shape styled to resemble a physical sticker, used to highlight short text
