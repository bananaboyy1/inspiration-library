# 'Let's Increase Knowledge About Healthy Lifestyles' pack with purple-orange gradients and flat wellness illustrations

![](../images/print-social-healthy-lifestyles-posts-purple-gradient-flat-illustration.avif)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Social template pack · **Design style:** Gradient & Aurora
**Tags:** health-education, purple, gradient, flat-illustration, wellness, instagram

## What it is
Nine posts on purple-to-orange gradients with a white content card, flat illustrations of people meditating, cycling and walking dogs, circular icon badges, headlines ('Commit to a Fit Healthy Lifestyle', 'The Benefits of Cycling Burn Cardio') and a consistent logo and colour dot pagination.

## What it means
Uplifting and consistent; a repeated card-on-gradient format with varied wellness illustrations keeps a health series visually cohesive.

## Techniques
- Float a white content card over a full-bleed gradient background.
- Illustrate a different healthy activity per post within one style.
- Use dot pagination indicators to imply a longer content series.

## Best for
- Health and wellness education
- Gym and lifestyle brands
- Gradient card-based social packs

## Vocabulary
- **Content card** — A distinct panel holding the main message, floated over a background.
- **Dot pagination** — Small dots indicating position within a series of items.
