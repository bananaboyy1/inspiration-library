# Ultra-Long Case Study Overview

![](../images/app-ultra-long-plus-jakarta-sans-case-study-thumbnail-overview.jpg)

**Discipline:** app ·  · **Design style:** Minimalist
**Tags:** ultra-long, case-study, typography, monochrome, orange, screens, overview

## What it is
A very narrow tall thumbnail of an extremely long case-study page for an AI support product: a Plus Jakarta Sans specimen, colour swatches of black, grey and orange, and dozens of sections of phones, laptop and tablet shots, flows and hand-held photos on a white and grey base.

## What it means
Even at thumbnail size the rhythm of alternating text and device sections shows a very complete story.

## Techniques
- Consistent rhythm of text block then device image
- Monochrome palette with orange accent
- Dark sections interleaved with white
- Flow diagrams between screen sections
- Specimen and palette at the top

## Best for
- Case-study page structure reference
- AI and enterprise product portfolios
- Long-form scroll layouts

## Vocabulary
- **Page thumbnail** — A miniature overview of an entire long page.
- **Section rhythm** — A repeating pattern of text and image blocks down a page.
- **Dark interlude** — A dark section placed between light ones.
