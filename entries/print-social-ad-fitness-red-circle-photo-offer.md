# White/red fitness ad with circular photo frame and offer badge

![](../images/print-social-ad-fitness-red-circle-photo-offer.jpg)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Social ad · **Design style:** Corporate & Clean-Tech
**Tags:** social ad, fitness, red white, circular photo frame, offer badge

## What it is
A clean white square ad for a gym, featuring a circular dotted-border photo of a woman training, a bold 'Find Your Strength' headline, a red circular '50% off' pedestal badge, and a bulleted feature list.

## What it means
A dotted circular photo frame plus a matching circular offer badge creates two visual anchors of the same shape, giving an otherwise text-heavy layout a sense of intentional geometry.

## Techniques
- frame a portrait photo in a dotted-outline circle rather than a plain rectangle
- repeat the circle motif in a separate offer badge to tie two focal points together visually
- use a bold accent color (red) sparingly for only the offer and key headline word for hierarchy

## Best for
- gym/fitness studio advertising
- clean corporate promotional posts
- offer-driven service marketing

## Vocabulary
- **dotted border** — a frame made of small repeated dots rather than a solid line, giving a lighter, more textured edge
