# Dark 'Digital Marketing Experts' webinar post with a grid of yellow and white date pills

![](../images/print-social-dark-marketing-webinar-post-with-yellow-date-pills.avif)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Social ad · **Design style:** Corporate & Clean-Tech
**Tags:** webinar, schedule, dark, yellow, date-pills, ad

## What it is
A square post with a man in a white shirt checking his phone, darkened left side with a white and yellow headline, four pill-shaped schedule items pairing grey date tabs (SAT 9th JUN) with yellow or white topic blocks, a 'Register now' outlined button and contacts.

## What it means
Informative and busy-but-tidy; date pills turn a schedule into a compact visual list.

## Techniques
- Split each session into a date tab and topic block.
- Alternate block colours to separate sessions.
- Darken one side of the photo for legible type.

## Best for
- Webinar series
- Workshop schedules
- Event promos

## Vocabulary
- **Date pill** — A capsule combining a date and a title.
- **Vignette** — Darkened area of a photo to hold text.
