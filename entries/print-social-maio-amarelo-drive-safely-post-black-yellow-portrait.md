# 'Maio Amarelo' road safety post with a black-yellow diagonal band and a serious male portrait

![](../images/print-social-maio-amarelo-drive-safely-post-black-yellow-portrait.avif)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Social ad · **Design style:** Corporate & Clean-Tech
**Tags:** road-safety, maio-amarelo, black, yellow, portrait, campaign

## What it is
A black square post with a thin white wireframe square overlay, a yellow diagonal geometric band cutting across the top-right, a bold Portuguese headline 'Dirigir com Responsabilidade e Consciência salva vidas!', a yellow chevron-arrow tag with a checkmark shield icon, and a serious close-up portrait of a man lit from the side.

## What it means
Serious and credible; stark black-yellow contrast and direct portrait lighting suit a road-safety awareness message.

## Techniques
- Cut a bold colour band diagonally across a dark photo.
- Light a portrait dramatically from one side for gravity.
- Use a shield-and-check icon to signal safety and trust.

## Best for
- Road safety campaigns
- Public awareness advertising
- High-contrast social ads

## Vocabulary
- **Diagonal band** — An angled colour or shape cutting across a composition.
- **Directional lighting** — Light falling from one side to add drama to a portrait.
