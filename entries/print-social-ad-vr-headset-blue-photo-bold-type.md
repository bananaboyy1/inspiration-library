# Blue Facebook ad for VR headset with bold red type

![](../images/print-social-ad-vr-headset-blue-photo-bold-type.avif)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Social ad · **Design style:** Dark & Neon
**Tags:** facebook ad, vr, technology, blue, bold type, photo

## What it is
A wide-format ad on a flat blue background with a dramatic red-lit photo of a person wearing a VR headset, paired with a bold red slanted headline and dotted texture accents.

## What it means
Contrasting a cool blue backdrop against warm red lighting in the photo and headline makes the ad's focal elements pop forward with strong depth.

## Techniques
- light the hero photo in a contrasting color to the flat background for depth
- slant/rotate bold headline text slightly for energy rather than a static horizontal line
- scatter a subtle dot-pattern texture in negative space to avoid a flat empty background

## Best for
- tech/gadget Facebook or display ads
- VR/AR product launches
- bold color-blocked ad templates

## Vocabulary
- **color grading** — adjusting the color tones of an image, often to push shadows/highlights toward a stylistic hue
