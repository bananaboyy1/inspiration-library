# Purple World Music Day carousel with swirl line accents

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Social carousel · **Design style:** Gradient & Aurora
**Tags:** carousel, music, purple gradient, swirl lines, instagram, photo

## What it is
A five-tile carousel on a purple gradient for World Music Day, mixing an action photo of a singer with thin white swirl/ribbon line accents, a striped arc motif, and flat icon tiles (camera, headphones).

## What it means
Thin swirling ribbon lines around the singer photo visually suggest sound waves or music notes without needing literal musical iconography on every tile.

## Techniques
- draw thin swirling ribbon lines around a photo subject to abstractly suggest sound/motion
- repeat a striped arc motif at the bottom of tiles as a grounding structural element
- mix photographic and icon-led tiles within one carousel to vary pacing

## Best for
- music event/campaign social content
- World [X] Day awareness campaigns
- gradient-and-photo carousel templates

## Vocabulary
- **sound wave motif** — abstract line or wave shapes used to visually evoke audio, music or sound without literal notation
