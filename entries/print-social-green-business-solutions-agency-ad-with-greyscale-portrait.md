# Grey and green 'Business Solutions Agency' ad with a greyscale man on a laptop and circle shapes

![](../images/print-social-green-business-solutions-agency-ad-with-greyscale-portrait.avif)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Social ad · **Design style:** Corporate & Clean-Tech
**Tags:** agency, green, greyscale, circles, services-list, ad

## What it is
A square ad with a greyscale young man typing on a laptop, large green and black circles behind, a headline 'Business Solutions' with the second word in bright green, letter-spaced 'Agency', a dark circle 'Have an idea or project?' and a green phone pill.

## What it means
Modern and focused; greyscale plus one green makes the brand pop.

## Techniques
- Let a single vivid colour carry a greyscale design.
- Letter-space small lines around a bold headline.
- Put a question in a dark circle as a secondary hook.

## Best for
- Consulting agencies
- Business services
- Mono-accent campaigns

## Vocabulary
- **Letter-spacing** — Extra space added between letters.
- **Mono-accent** — A design with one accent colour.
