# 'Free Daily Insight' tarot flyer with fanned illustrated tarot cards and dotted rays from a sparkle

![](../images/print-social-tarot-free-daily-insight-flyer-fanned-cards-dotted-rays.avif)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Social ad · **Design style:** Playful & Illustrated
**Tags:** tarot, astrology, fanned-cards, dotted-rays, orange, flyer

## What it is
A square flyer with a sparkle star logo 'tarot.com', a bold black 'FREE Daily Insight' headline, dotted rays radiating from small sparkle and diamond icons toward a fan of illustrated tarot cards (numbered 18, 21, 22) showing sun and figure motifs, blue and purple circle accents, and an orange footer with a phone number.

## What it means
Mystical yet approachable; dotted rays connecting icons to the cards suggest insight radiating outward toward the reader.

## Techniques
- Fan illustrated cards to suggest a real tarot spread.
- Connect small icons to the main visual with dotted rays.
- Use simple sparkle and diamond shapes as mystical accents.

## Best for
- Astrology and tarot businesses
- Spiritual and wellness marketing
- Card-based product promotion

## Vocabulary
- **Card fan** — Cards spread in an overlapping arc.
- **Dotted ray** — A broken line suggesting light or energy radiating outward.
