# Black field with a soft indigo-violet glow rising from the bottom edge in a shallow arch

![](../images/web-deep-violet-glow-rising-from-black-gradient-background.webp)

**Discipline:** web ·  · **Design style:** Gradient & Aurora
**Tags:** background, gradient, violet, black, glow, dark, hero, minimal

## What it is
A plain black frame whose lower third glows deep indigo to violet, brightest at the bottom corners and fading upward along a shallow arch.

## What it means
Quiet and atmospheric; a horizon glow suggests dawn behind a dark page.

## Techniques
- Fade a single hue up from the bottom edge.
- Curve the glow edge slightly.
- Keep the top pure black.
- Use as a hero base.

## Best for
- Dark hero backdrops
- AI and tech landing pages
- Violet gradient brands

## Vocabulary
- **Horizon glow** — A glow rising from the bottom edge.
- **Radial fade** — A gradient fading outward from a point.
- **Deep violet** — A dark saturated purple.
- **Hero base** — A background layer under hero content.
