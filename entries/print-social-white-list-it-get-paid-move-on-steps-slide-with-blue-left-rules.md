# List It, Get Paid, Move On Steps Slide

## What it is
A white slide with a blue tag 'HERE'S HOW IT WORKS', a huge black heavy 'LIST IT. GET PAID. MOVE ON.' and seven pale rounded rows each with a blue left rule, holding one short step in regular text with bold key words and a final italic grey 'Nobody loses.'

## What it means
Three verbs as a headline and a stack of one-line steps turn a process into something you can read in seconds.

## Techniques
- Three-verb headline as a summary
- Rows with a blue left border for steps
- Bold key words in each step
- Italic grey last line as a soft close
- Blue tag above the headline

## Best for
- How-it-works slides
- Process explanations in carousels
- Step lists

## Vocabulary
- **Left-rule row** — A row marked by a coloured line on its left edge.
- **Verb headline** — A headline made of short action phrases.
- **Soft close** — A quiet final line that wraps up a list.
