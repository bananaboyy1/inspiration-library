**Discipline:** web ·  · **Design style:** Glassmorphism
**Tags:** fintech, pearl, serif, landing, pricing, testimonials, long-page, overview

## What it is
A presentation of a long pearl-toned landing page for a finance app shown as two offset columns: a hero with a serif headline and a phone, trusted-by logos, about and stats, a feature grid, and at right a global services row, testimonial portraits, three-tier pricing, a phone download section with a QR code and a footer with a giant brand name.

## What it means
Serif headlines and pearl gradients feel refined and calm for finance, and the overview shows every section at a glance.

## Techniques
- Serif headlines on a pearl gradient
- Feature grid of soft glass cards
- Portrait testimonial cards in a row
- Three-tier pricing with a highlighted middle plan
- Footer with a giant brand name over a photo

## Best for
- Fintech landing page presentations
- Premium finance marketing sites
- Full-page overview boards

## Vocabulary
- **Offset columns** — Two page screenshots staggered in one presentation.
- **Feature grid** — A grid of cards each describing a feature.
- **Giant footer wordmark** — A brand name set enormous across a site footer.
