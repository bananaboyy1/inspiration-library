# Blue 'We Are Hiring For Our Business' story with a white card, leather chair and megaphone

![](../images/print-social-blue-hiring-for-our-business-story-with-chair-and-megaphone.avif)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Recruitment post · **Design style:** Corporate & Clean-Tech
**Tags:** hiring, recruitment, blue, chair, megaphone, story

## What it is
A vertical blue story with a white card holding 'We Are Hiring For Our Business' in black and blue mixed weights, a black 'Open Positions' label and chevron role rows, a black leather chair with a blue megaphone overlapping the card edge, contact icons and a navy footer.

## What it means
Professional and bold; the product-style chair photo makes the vacancy feel premium.

## Techniques
- Overlap the prop onto the white card edge.
- Use chevron-square bullets for roles.
- Vary weights line by line in the headline.

## Best for
- Corporate hiring stories
- Business recruitment
- Instagram stories

## Vocabulary
- **Chevron bullet** — A small arrow marker for list items.
- **Weight variation** — Changing font weights for hierarchy.
