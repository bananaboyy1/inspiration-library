# Chibi artist mascot in a brown beret holding a brush and palette

![](../images/branding-cartoon-artist-mascot-with-beret-palette-and-brush.jpg)

**Discipline:** branding · **Design type:** Mascot illustration · **Design style:** Playful & Illustrated
**Tags:** mascot, character, artist, chibi, flat-illustration, icon

## What it is
A small square icon on pale blue of a big-headed cartoon kid in a brown beret, scarf and blue sweater, holding a paintbrush up and a wooden palette with paint dots, drawn with thick dark outlines.

## What it means
Friendly and approachable; the chibi proportions make a creative service feel fun and accessible.

## Techniques
- Exaggerate head size for instant cuteness.
- Use a thick uniform outline to keep it legible at icon size.
- Give the character one prop that states its role.
- Set it on a flat pale background for clean reuse.

## Best for
- Art classes and kids' workshops
- Creative app avatars
- Friendly brand mascots

## Vocabulary
- **Chibi** — A cute style with a big head and small body.
- **Mascot** — A character that personifies a brand.
