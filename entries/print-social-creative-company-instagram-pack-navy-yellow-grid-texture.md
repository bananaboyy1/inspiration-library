# 'Creative Company' Instagram pack on navy with a grainy grid texture, yellow arrows and colour-block logo squares

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Social template pack · **Design style:** Retro & Pixel
**Tags:** design-studio, navy, yellow, grid-texture, retro, instagram

## What it is
Nine navy square posts with a subtle grain and grid texture, yellow corner arrows, a small colour-block logo mark (red, yellow, cream squares), bold headlines ('Creative Company', 'Join Us!', 'Bold Design for Bold Ideas'), and orange pill tags listing services (Graphic Design, Layout & Advertising, Social Media, Photography).

## What it means
Confident and textured; grain and grid patterns give a flat navy-yellow palette real depth and studio credibility.

## Techniques
- Add fine grain and grid texture to a flat colour background.
- Use a small colour-block grid as a compact logo mark.
- Mark corners with directional arrow icons for energy.

## Best for
- Design studio branding
- Creative agency social content
- Textured retro palettes

## Vocabulary
- **Grain texture** — A fine noise pattern added for depth and warmth.
- **Colour-block mark** — A logo built from small solid-colour squares.
