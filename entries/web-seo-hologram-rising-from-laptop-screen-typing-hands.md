# Glowing blue 'SEO' hologram with data icons rising from a laptop screen as hands type

![](../images/web-seo-hologram-rising-from-laptop-screen-typing-hands.jpg)

**Discipline:** web · **Design type:** Feature illustration · **Design style:** Photographic & Cinematic
**Tags:** seo, hologram, blue, laptop, concept-photo, data-icons

## What it is
A photo of hands typing on a laptop with a glowing blue holographic 'SEO' word and surrounding data icons (charts, gears, a magnifying glass, a location pin) projecting up from the screen, set against a softly blurred office background.

## What it means
Believable and futuristic; grounding the hologram in a real photographed hand gesture makes an abstract concept feel tangible.

## Techniques
- Project a glowing holographic concept from a real device screen.
- Surround the main word with small related data icons.
- Keep the background softly blurred to focus on the hologram.

## Best for
- SEO and digital marketing blogs
- Tech concept photography
- Website hero images

## Vocabulary
- **Holographic projection** — A glowing digital overlay suggesting light projected from a device.
- **Concept photography** — A staged photo representing an abstract business or tech idea.
