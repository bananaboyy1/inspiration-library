# 'Find Your Style & Your Self.' post with a suited man and yellow glassy diagonal shards

![](../images/print-social-find-your-style-post-suited-man-with-yellow-glass-shards.avif)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Social promo post · **Design style:** Photographic & Cinematic
**Tags:** fashion, menswear, yellow, diagonal, dark, portrait

## What it is
A square dark post with a photo of a bearded man in a suit looking down, overlaid with dark and yellow translucent diagonal shards that fragment the image, a white and yellow bold headline and two small text columns.

## What it means
Sharp and dynamic; fractured glass shards give a classic portrait a modern edge.

## Techniques
- Fragment the photo with translucent angled shards.
- Use yellow as the accent against dark tones.
- Keep text in a clear dark area on the left.

## Best for
- Menswear brands
- Business events
- Bold ads

## Vocabulary
- **Shard** — A sharp angled translucent shape.
- **Fragmented overlay** — Layered shapes that break up an image.
