# The Clean UI Formula Cover

![](../images/print-social-slate-clean-ui-formula-carousel-cover.jpg)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Social carousel cover
**Tags:** carousel, cover, slate, cream, column-lines, weight-mix, swipe

## What it is
A slate-blue cover with vertical hairline column guides, a headline mixing light and bold weights ('The CLEAN UI Formula'), a right-aligned condensed subhead, page marks and a swipe cue with two dots.

## What it means
Weight contrast inside one headline gives rhythm, and the visible columns show the theme of structure.

## Techniques
- Alternate light and bold words in a headline
- Visible column lines as decoration
- Right-aligned condensed caps subhead
- Filled and hollow dots as pagination
- Part numbers in the corners

## Best for
- Carousel covers
- Design education
- Type-led posts

## Vocabulary
- **Weight mix** — Combining light and bold weights in one line.
- **Column guides** — Visible vertical lines showing a grid.
- **Pagination dots** — Filled and hollow circles showing slide position.
