# VR flyer with glitch photo effect and outline typography

![](../images/print-social-flyer-vr-glitch-cyan-purple-outline-type.avif)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Social ad · **Design style:** Dark & Neon
**Tags:** flyer, vr, glitch effect, cyan, outline type, tech

## What it is
A square tech flyer on a purple/cyan split background, featuring a VR-headset photo with a red/cyan RGB glitch offset effect and a large outline-only 'TECH' wordmark.

## What it means
A glitch/chromatic-aberration effect on the photo signals 'digital/VR' more viscerally than a clean photo would, while the outline-only headline keeps the layout from feeling cluttered.

## Techniques
- offset red and cyan color channels on a photo for a glitch/VR distortion effect
- render a large headline in outline-only type so it reads as bold without adding visual weight
- split the background into two flat color fields to separate photo and text zones

## Best for
- VR/AR product marketing
- tech conference/event flyers
- glitch-aesthetic digital brand campaigns

## Vocabulary
- **chromatic aberration / glitch effect** — an intentional offsetting of color channels to simulate digital distortion or a glitch
