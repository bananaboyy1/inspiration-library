# Shater offers, wallet and support screens grid ending in a '+60 Screen' orange banner

![](../images/app-shater-offers-and-orders-screens-grid-with-60-screen-banner.webp)

**Discipline:** app · **Design type:** UX documentation · **Design style:** Corporate & Clean-Tech
**Tags:** screens-grid, arabic, offers, wallet, support, orange

## What it is
A 4x5 grid of Arabic phone screens covering submitting and editing offers, order details, a wallet with balance and withdrawal, a craftsman profile with ratings, invoice details and support tickets with chat, closing in a bold orange '+60 Screen' banner.

## What it means
Exhaustive and credible; wallet and support screens show the platform handles money and problems, not just bookings.

## Techniques
- Include financial and support screens, not just the happy path.
- Keep a consistent 4-column screen grid.
- End every grid with a scale-proving banner.

## Best for
- Marketplace platforms
- Wallet and payments UX
- Support flow documentation

## Vocabulary
- **Wallet screen** — A UI showing balance and withdrawal options.
- **Support ticket** — A tracked customer service request.
