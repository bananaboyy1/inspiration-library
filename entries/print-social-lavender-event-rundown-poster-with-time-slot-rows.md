# Lavender 'Event Rundown' poster with outlined time-slot rows and a circled back arrow

![](../images/print-social-lavender-event-rundown-poster-with-time-slot-rows.avif)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Event poster · **Design style:** Minimalist
**Tags:** event, agenda, lavender, outline, schedule, conference

## What it is
A portrait pale lavender poster with a 'TehHub' logo, a thin 'EVENT' over heavy 'RUNDOWN' between rules with a circled arrow, date and venue boxes, and eight outlined rows pairing a lavender time cell (09:00 AM) with a session name, plus a contact footer in a rounded band.

## What it means
Tidy and calm; the thin outlines feel like a friendly form.

## Techniques
- Pair a light and heavy weight for a two-word title.
- Use outlined rows with a filled time cell.
- Put date and venue in twin info boxes.

## Best for
- Conference agendas
- Corporate events
- Workshop timetables

## Vocabulary
- **Rundown** — A running order of an event.
- **Time cell** — A small filled box holding a time.
