# 'Launch Your Business' pack in purple-pink gradient with a flat rocket illustration and speech bubbles

![](../images/print-social-launch-your-business-posts-purple-pink-flat-rocket-illustration.avif)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Social template pack · **Design style:** Playful & Illustrated
**Tags:** startup, business, purple, pink, rocket, flat-illustration

## What it is
Nine purple-to-pink gradient square posts with a flat illustration of a yellow-and-white rocket launching through clouds, gear icons and speech bubbles, flat-illustrated people exchanging lightbulbs and coins, and headlines ('Launch Your Business', 'Expand Your Ideas', 'We Teach You How').

## What it means
Optimistic and energetic; a launching rocket makes 'starting a business' feel like an exciting journey rather than a risk.

## Techniques
- Use a rocket launch as a literal metaphor for starting a business.
- Illustrate abstract exchanges (ideas, help) as literal handoffs.
- Keep a consistent gradient across a full post set.

## Best for
- Startup and business launch campaigns
- Entrepreneurship education
- Flat illustration branding

## Vocabulary
- **Launch metaphor** — Using a rocket or liftoff to represent starting something new.
- **Gradient consistency** — Using the same colour fade across every asset in a set.
