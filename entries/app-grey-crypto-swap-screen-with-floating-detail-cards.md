# Swap Screen with Floating Detail Cards

![](../images/app-grey-crypto-swap-screen-with-floating-detail-cards.jpg)

**Discipline:** app · **Design type:** Fintech app screens
**Tags:** swap, grey, floating-cards, hand, crypto, connectors, light

## What it is
A pale grey slide with headline 'Seamless money transfer for a better banking Experience.', a hand-held swap screen and enlarged You Pay and You Receive cards linked by thin lines, with small captions.

## What it means
Pulling cards out at large scale explains the flow while the neutral palette stays calm.

## Techniques
- Enlarged cards connected by hairlines
- Headline with the last words dimmed
- Hand fading into the background at the bottom
- Small icon and paragraph captions
- Monochrome UI with coin colours as accents

## Best for
- Fintech feature explainers
- Crypto swap flows
- Callout-based screen presentations

## Vocabulary
- **Callout card** — An enlarged UI piece linked to its place on the screen.
- **Hairline connector** — A thin line linking two related elements.
- **Fade-out hand** — A hand that fades into the background.
