# 'Cinecar Outdoors' drive-in cinema pack in black with pink-yellow-blue triangle blocks and popcorn illustration

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Social template pack · **Design style:** Retro & Pixel
**Tags:** cinema, drive-in, black, triangle-blocks, popcorn, instagram

## What it is
Nine black square posts with angular pink, yellow and blue triangle colour blocks, a yellow bean-shaped 'Cinecar Outdoors' logo, illustrated popcorn and a car silhouette, headlines ('2x1 Movies Every Monday', 'Book your ticket now', 'Live the movie experience in your car') and film-reel icons.

## What it means
Bold and playful; sharp triangle colour blocks give a retro drive-in concept a modern graphic edge.

## Techniques
- Cut backgrounds with angular triangle colour blocks.
- Use a bean-shaped logo badge for a friendly, rounded brand mark.
- Repeat popcorn and car icons as recurring thematic props.

## Best for
- Drive-in cinema marketing
- Retro entertainment branding
- Bold triangle-block layouts

## Vocabulary
- **Triangle colour block** — An angular flat shape used to divide a background.
- **Bean-shaped badge** — A rounded, organic label shape for a logo.
