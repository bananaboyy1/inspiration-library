# 'Creative Agency' poster with a Bauhaus shape header band and three circular service icons

![](../images/print-social-creative-agency-poster-bauhaus-shapes-icon-services.avif)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Poster · **Design style:** Geometric & Bauhaus
**Tags:** agency, bauhaus, coral, navy, icons, poster

## What it is
A cream A4 poster with a coral-and-navy Bauhaus shape band (circles, a starburst, a squiggle, a jagged triangle) across the top, a bold two-tone 'CREATIVE AGENCY' title, a highlighted tagline bar, three circular navy icon badges (rocket, lightbulb, connection) labelled Production, Big Ideas and Connections, and a puzzle-piece logo.

## What it means
Playful and structured; a Bauhaus shape band signals creativity before a single word is read.

## Techniques
- Open a poster with a horizontal band of abstract shapes.
- Circle each service icon in a matching dark badge.
- Highlight the tagline in a solid colour bar.

## Best for
- Creative agency posters
- Bauhaus-inspired branding
- Service overview flyers

## Vocabulary
- **Shape band** — A horizontal strip of decorative geometric forms.
- **Icon badge** — A circular container holding a service icon.
