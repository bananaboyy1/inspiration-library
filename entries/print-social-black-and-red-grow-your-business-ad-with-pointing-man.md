# Black and red 'Grow Your Business' ad with a pointing man, crumpled texture and script quote

![](../images/print-social-black-and-red-grow-your-business-ad-with-pointing-man.avif)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Social ad · **Design style:** Corporate & Clean-Tech
**Tags:** agency, black, red, script, crumpled-paper, ad

## What it is
A square black ad with a crumpled-paper texture, a smiling man in a tweed blazer pointing at the viewer, red script 'Great ideas to' over bold white and red 'GROW YOUR Business', a red services list, a handwritten quote with an arrow and red circle outlines.

## What it means
Direct and persuasive; the pointing gesture makes the offer personal.

## Techniques
- Pose the subject pointing at the viewer.
- Mix handwritten script with heavy sans type.
- Add a crumpled paper texture for grit.
- Echo red outline circles off the edges.

## Best for
- Business coaching ads
- Agency promos
- Bold red-black campaigns

## Vocabulary
- **Direct address** — A pose or copy speaking straight to the viewer.
- **Paper texture** — A scanned paper surface overlaid for tactility.
