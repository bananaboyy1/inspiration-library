# Six wallet, cards, profile, QR scan and payment success screens with slate-blue gradients

![](../images/app-six-phone-wallet-flow-on-slate-blue-gradient-screens.jpg)

**Discipline:** app · **Design type:** Fintech app screens
**Tags:** fintech, slate-blue, gradient, italic-serif, wallet, qr-scan, success-modal, phones

## What it is
An off-white board with 'More Control, Built Into Every Detail.' (italic serif on the last words), pill tabs and six phones: wallet with a hatched limit bar and weekly bars, cards with a big card and round actions, a card details sheet, a profile list, a QR scan with a payment sheet and a payment success sheet.

## What it means
Calm and precise; slate-blue gradients and hatched bars feel like private banking.

## Techniques
- Use a white-to-slate gradient on every screen.
- Draw limits as hatched bars.
- Use bottom sheets for details.
- Present phones in a stepped grid.

## Best for
- Fintech app case studies
- Wallet and payment flows
- Slate-blue palettes

## Vocabulary
- **Hatched limit bar** — A progress bar with diagonal fill.
- **Bottom sheet** — A panel sliding up with details.
- **QR scan screen** — A camera view with a code frame.
- **Success sheet** — A confirmation panel after payment.
