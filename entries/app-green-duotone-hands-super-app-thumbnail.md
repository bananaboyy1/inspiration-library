# Green Duotone Super-App Thumbnail

![](../images/app-green-duotone-hands-super-app-thumbnail.jpg)

**Discipline:** app · **Design type:** Device mockup scene
**Tags:** green, duotone, hands, thumbnail, super-app, dark, cover

## What it is
A small square cover version of the green duotone scene: two glowing green hands holding a phone with a colorful service-grid home screen against a dark green gradient.

## What it means
Kept simple at thumbnail size, the single colour and the bright screen still read instantly in a gallery grid.

## Techniques
- Strong single-hue scene that survives shrinking
- Screen as the brightest, most colorful zone
- Centred device with symmetrical hands
- Dark vignette edges
- No text, image alone carries the pitch

## Best for
- Portfolio grid thumbnails
- Project covers
- Quick-glance product teasers

## Vocabulary
- **Thumbnail cover** — A small image representing a project in a gallery.
- **Glow screen** — A bright display that lights the surrounding scene.
- **Vignette** — Darkened edges focusing on the centre.
