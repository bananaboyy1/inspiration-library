# 'Technology Simplified' webinar flyer with an orange-purple grid of flat illustration and abstract tiles

![](../images/print-social-technology-simplified-webinar-flyer-orange-purple-grid.avif)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Social ad · **Design style:** Playful & Illustrated
**Tags:** webinar, grid-layout, orange, purple, flat-illustration, flyer

## What it is
A purple-bordered black flyer with a pill-shaped 'TECHNOLOGY SIMPLIFIED' header, a grid of tiles: a flat illustration of a man checking his phone on orange, two abstract geometric pattern tiles, pink 'Join the Webinar' and yellow date pills, and a bold purple 'Innovate the Future' headline with speaker credit and registration link.

## What it means
Organised and modern; treating logistics as grid tiles alongside illustration makes a webinar flyer feel like a tidy dashboard.

## Techniques
- Arrange mixed content types (illustration, pattern, logistics) as grid tiles.
- Use pill-shaped tiles for date and action details.
- Frame the whole flyer in one bold border colour.

## Best for
- Webinar and course promotion
- Grid-based flyer layouts
- Tech education marketing

## Vocabulary
- **Tile grid** — A layout of equal-sized cells each holding different content.
- **Pill tile** — A rounded rectangle used to hold a short piece of information.
