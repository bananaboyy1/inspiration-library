# Red gadget mega-deal story with gift bag and confetti

![](../images/print-social-story-gadget-deal-red-gift-confetti.avif)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Story set · **Design style:** Gradient & Aurora
**Tags:** instagram story, gadget deal, red gold, confetti, promo, gift bag

## What it is
A vertical story-format promo on a red radial-gradient background, showing product renders spilling from a red gift bag with gold confetti and a bold gold-gradient headline.

## What it means
Using a radial red gradient centers attention directly on the products while the falling confetti motif ties this story to the matching square-post version of the same campaign.

## Techniques
- use a radial gradient centered on the hero product to naturally draw the eye inward
- show products 'spilling out' of a bag/container for a gift-reveal narrative
- match confetti and gold-gradient treatments across format variants (story vs. square) for campaign cohesion

## Best for
- gadget/tech flash-sale campaigns
- Instagram story ad sets
- holiday gift promo templates

## Vocabulary
- **radial gradient** — a gradient that transitions outward from a central point in concentric rings, rather than in a straight line
