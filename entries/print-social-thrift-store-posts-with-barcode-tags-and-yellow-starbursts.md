# Thrift store pack with serif headlines, barcode 'Buy now' tags and yellow starbursts

![](../images/print-social-thrift-store-posts-with-barcode-tags-and-yellow-starbursts.avif)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Social template pack · **Design style:** Retro & Pixel
**Tags:** thrift, secondhand, fashion, barcode, starburst, serif

## What it is
Nine off-white posts with photos of clothing racks and shoppers, black serif headlines ('Find Your Perfect Fit', 'Secondhand to Chic & Luxurious'), price tag labels with barcodes, yellow starbursts, orange sparkles and small wavy lines.

## What it means
Vintage and friendly; barcodes and starbursts feel like real shop tags.

## Techniques
- Style CTAs as price tags with barcodes.
- Add starbursts for a bargain feel.
- Pair big serif headlines with candid shop photos.

## Best for
- Thrift and vintage stores
- Secondhand marketplaces
- Retail promos

## Vocabulary
- **Price tag** — A label shape used as a CTA.
- **Starburst** — A spiky star shape for attention.
