# Circular Text Badge Monogram on Navy

![](../images/branding-navy-circular-text-badge-monogram-strip.jpg)

**Discipline:** branding ·  · **Design style:** Retro & Pixel
**Tags:** monogram, badge, circular-text, navy, lilac, stamp, studio

## What it is
A wide navy strip with a small lilac pixelated monogram at the centre surrounded by a ring of white uppercase text reading a studio name, city and year.

## What it means
The circular text works like a seal, giving a small mark authority and a crafted feel.

## Techniques
- Text set on a circle around a mark
- Blocky monogram in lilac on deep navy
- Separators as asterisks in the ring
- Huge empty space to make it a stamp
- Two-colour palette

## Best for
- Studio sign-offs
- Seals and stamps
- Footers and dividers

## Vocabulary
- **Circular text** — Lettering set along a circular path.
- **Seal** — A round mark that signals authorship or authenticity.
- **Pixel monogram** — A letter mark built from square blocks.
