# '5 Tips to Reduce Your Carbon Footprint' story shown in a phone mockup with a flat green-planet illustration

![](../images/print-social-carbon-footprint-tips-story-phone-mockup-cyan-pink.avif)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Story set · **Design style:** Playful & Illustrated
**Tags:** environment, eco, cyan, pink, flat-illustration, story

## What it is
A vertical Instagram story shown in a black phone frame: a flat illustration of a green half-planet with wind turbines and buildings on a pink-to-navy sky, above a cyan panel listing five underlined eco tips (eat more plant foods, switch to low-carbon energy, reduce/reuse/recycle, energy-efficient appliances, second-hand clothes) with small icon accents.

## What it means
Optimistic and actionable; a friendly illustrated planet makes a climate message feel hopeful rather than alarming.

## Techniques
- Illustrate the earth as a simplified, friendly half-globe.
- Underline each tip individually for scannability.
- Pair each list item with a small relevant icon.

## Best for
- Environmental and sustainability campaigns
- Educational Instagram stories
- NGO awareness content

## Vocabulary
- **Tip list** — A short, scannable set of actionable suggestions.
- **Flat planet illustration** — A simplified, stylised depiction of the earth.
