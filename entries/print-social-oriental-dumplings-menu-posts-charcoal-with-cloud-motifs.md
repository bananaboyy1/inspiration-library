# Oriental food menu pack on charcoal with orange and white titles, tilted photo cards and cloud motifs

![](../images/print-social-oriental-dumplings-menu-posts-charcoal-with-cloud-motifs.avif)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Social template pack · **Design style:** Photographic & Cinematic
**Tags:** asian-food, menu, charcoal, orange, polaroid, clouds

## What it is
Nine charcoal square posts, each naming a dish in orange and white condensed caps ('Oriental Dumplings', 'Ramen Seafood', 'Sushi Original'), with a tilted white-bordered food photo, small orange cloud and brushstroke motifs, and chopsticks line art.

## What it means
Appetising and consistent; tilted photo cards feel like snapshots pinned to a board.

## Techniques
- Tilt food photos in white borders like prints.
- Split dish names into two colours.
- Add small cultural motifs like clouds as accents.
- Keep one template and swap the dish.

## Best for
- Asian restaurants
- Menu series
- Food delivery promos

## Vocabulary
- **Photo card** — A photo with a white border like a print.
- **Motif** — A repeated decorative element.
