# 'Creatives Meet up' flyer in red and mustard with a yellow-block portrait and doodle sunburst corners

![](../images/print-social-creatives-meet-up-flyer-red-yellow-photo-block.avif)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Event flyer · **Design style:** Retro & Pixel
**Tags:** event, meetup, red, mustard, photo-block, doodle

## What it is
A red A5 flyer with a mustard-yellow photo block of two posed models in pink and denim, a diamond logo, social icons, two overlapping colour-tag headlines ('Creatives' orange, 'Meet up' tan), a subtitle 'With artist and designer professional', a wavy line motif, and hand-drawn sunburst doodles in two corners.

## What it means
Warm and social; overlapping colour tags and sunburst doodles make a professional meetup feel relaxed and inviting.

## Techniques
- Frame a photo in a solid contrasting colour block.
- Overlap two tag-style headline words at a slight offset.
- Add hand-drawn sunburst doodles as informal corner accents.

## Best for
- Creative meetups and networking events
- Community flyers
- Warm two-tone branding

## Vocabulary
- **Tag headline** — A short word set on its own solid colour background like a label.
- **Sunburst doodle** — A hand-drawn radiating line cluster used for emphasis.
