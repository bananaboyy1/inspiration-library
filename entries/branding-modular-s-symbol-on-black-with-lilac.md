# Violet modular S symbol with white wordmark on near-black

![](../images/branding-modular-s-symbol-on-black-with-lilac.jpg)

**Discipline:** branding ·  · **Design style:** Dark & Neon
**Tags:** logo, symbol, violet, white, dark, modular, tech, wordmark

## What it is
The same rounded modular symbol in violet beside a white wide sans 'Sovrix' on a near-black background.

## What it means
Premium and crisp; the violet glows on black.

## Techniques
- Colour the symbol violet and the wordmark white.
- Present on near-black.
- Keep spacing generous.
- Use as the dark version of the logo.

## Best for
- Dark-mode logo versions
- Tech identity presentations
- Two-colour lockups

## Vocabulary
- **Dark version** — The logo variant for dark backgrounds.
- **Two-colour lockup** — A logo where symbol and name differ in colour.
- **Generous spacing** — Ample room around a logo.
- **Glow on black** — A colour that seems luminous against black.
