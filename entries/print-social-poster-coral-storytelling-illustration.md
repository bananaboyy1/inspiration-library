# Coral storytelling poster with illustrated desk scene

![](../images/print-social-poster-coral-storytelling-illustration.avif)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Poster · **Design style:** Playful & Illustrated
**Tags:** poster, storytelling, coral, illustration, desk scene, lightbulb

## What it is
An A4 poster on a coral-pink background pairing a bold two-tone headline with a flat illustration of a seated figure sketching at a desk and a lightbulb-and-star motif representing ideas.

## What it means
Placing an idea/lightbulb icon opposite a working illustrated figure visually narrates the creative process itself (idea → execution) within a single static poster.

## Techniques
- split a headline across two font weights/colors for emphasis without extra elements
- use a lightbulb-with-rays motif as visual shorthand for 'ideas'
- balance an icon zone and an illustration zone side-by-side rather than stacking them

## Best for
- creative workshop/course posters
- writing or storytelling brand marketing
- playful editorial event posters

## Vocabulary
- **visual shorthand** — a simplified, widely recognized symbol used to instantly communicate a concept (e.g. lightbulb = idea)
