# Purple marketing agency speaker post with script 'We Are Creative' and orange headline

![](../images/print-social-purple-marketing-agency-speaker-post-with-script-accent.avif)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Social ad · **Design style:** Corporate & Clean-Tech
**Tags:** agency, speaker, purple, orange, script, ad

## What it is
A square post with a smiling man at a laptop, a purple gradient overlay rising from the left, a script 'We Are Creative' above bold orange 'MARKETING' and white 'AGENCY', guest speaker name and role, social icons and a phone number.

## What it means
Personable and professional; the script adds warmth to a corporate announcement.

## Techniques
- Fade a brand colour over a photo to hold text.
- Stack script, coloured caps and white caps as a headline trio.
- Label the speaker with a coloured name and small role.

## Best for
- Agency webinars
- Speaker announcements
- Marketing promos

## Vocabulary
- **Gradient overlay** — A colour fade laid over a photo.
- **Script accent** — A handwritten-style line above the main headline.
