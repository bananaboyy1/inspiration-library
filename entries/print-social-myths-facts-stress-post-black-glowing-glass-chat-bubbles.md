# 'Myths & Facts On Stress' post with glowing frosted-glass chat bubbles marked by a red X and green tick

![](../images/print-social-myths-facts-stress-post-black-glowing-glass-chat-bubbles.avif)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Social educational post · **Design style:** Glassmorphism
**Tags:** educational, myths-facts, black, glassmorphism, glow, chat-bubble

## What it is
A black square post with a ghosted 'Myths & Facts' word repeated behind, two frosted-glass chat-bubble panels glowing green at the edges: one marked with a red X labelled 'Myth' stating a misconception, one marked with a green tick labelled 'Fact' correcting it, with social icons in the footer.

## What it means
Trustworthy and clear; the myth/fact contrast with colour-coded icons makes correcting misinformation feel authoritative yet simple.

## Techniques
- Pair a myth and its correcting fact in matching glass bubble shapes.
- Mark right and wrong with a consistent tick/cross icon system.
- Ghost the topic word behind the content for texture.

## Best for
- Health and wellness education
- Myth-busting content series
- Dark glassmorphism branding

## Vocabulary
- **Myth/fact pairing** — A content format contrasting a misconception with the correct information.
- **Glass bubble** — A frosted, glowing speech-bubble-shaped panel.
