# Banking research insights as floating stat cards

![](../images/app-banking-research-insight-cards.jpg)

**Discipline:** app ·  · **Design style:** Editorial
**Tags:** research, case-study, navy, stat-cards, italic-serif, progress-bar, hatching

## What it is
A pale slide with a two-tone headline where 'Banking Experience.' is set in bold italic serif. Three white rounded cards at staggered positions carry a question, a big thin percentage and a hatched progress bar or weekday bar.

## What it means
Clear, analytical and refined; stagger and whitespace keep the data light.

## Techniques
- Set one phrase of a headline in italic serif for emphasis within a sans title.
- Show stats as huge thin numerals with tiny labels.
- Fill progress bars with fine diagonal hatching instead of solid colour.
- Stagger cards diagonally down the page to create a reading path.

## Best for
- UX research summaries
- Fintech case studies
- Report or deck stat slides

## Vocabulary
- **Hatched fill** — A pattern of thin diagonal lines used as a fill.
- **Stat card** — A card with a question and one key figure.
- **Staggered layout** — Elements offset diagonally instead of aligned.
- **Italic serif accent** — An italic serif phrase inside sans text.
