# 'Eyesight Agency' flyer on yellow with a black card and colour-coded pill service tags

![](../images/print-social-eyesight-agency-flyer-black-yellow-pill-service-tags.avif)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Social ad · **Design style:** Minimalist
**Tags:** agency, yellow, black-card, pill-tags, minimal, flyer

## What it is
A yellow A5 flyer with a black rounded card holding a diamond-eye logo, bold cream headline 'Innovative Marketing for Bold Brands.', a subtitle, an outlined info box, and four colour-coded pill tags (Social Media Management, SEO & Google Ads, Email Campaigns, Brand Development) in pink, blue, red and yellow.

## What it means
Clean and confident; colour-coded service pills make a dense agency offering easy to scan at a glance.

## Techniques
- Colour-code each service as its own pill tag.
- Nest a black content card inside a bright colour field.
- Keep the layout to a single card for focus.

## Best for
- Marketing agency flyers
- Service menu design
- Minimal high-contrast branding

## Vocabulary
- **Service pill** — A small rounded tag naming one offered service.
- **Content card** — A distinct panel holding the main message, set against a background field.
