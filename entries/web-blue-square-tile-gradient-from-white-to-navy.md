# Grid of flat blue squares shading from white at top left to deep navy at bottom right

![](../images/web-blue-square-tile-gradient-from-white-to-navy.webp)

**Discipline:** web ·  · **Design style:** Retro & Pixel
**Tags:** pattern, pixel-grid, gradient, blue, squares, background, diagonal, flat

## What it is
A rectangle of uniform square tiles whose blue tones shift diagonally from near white to deep navy, with slight tile-to-tile variation.

## What it means
Cool and digital; the tile mosaic reads as a pixelated sky-to-ocean gradient.

## Techniques
- Build a gradient from equal squares.
- Add small random tile variation.
- Run the ramp diagonally.
- Use as a background or divider.

## Best for
- Hero and section backgrounds
- Blue tech and finance decks
- Pixel-style textures

## Vocabulary
- **Tile mosaic** — A pattern of equal squares.
- **Tonal variation** — Slight differences in tone between tiles.
- **Diagonal ramp** — A gradient running corner to corner.
- **Pixel sky** — A gradient rendered with blocky pixels.
