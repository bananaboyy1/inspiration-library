# Cream 'Events Schedule September' poster with lime tilted label and pastel colour-coded bars

![](../images/print-social-cream-events-schedule-poster-with-pastel-colour-bars.avif)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Event poster · **Design style:** Playful & Illustrated
**Tags:** event, schedule, cream, pastel, lime, calendar

## What it is
A portrait cream poster with a heavy black 'EVENTS SCHEDULE SEPTEMBER' headline, a tilted lime '2026' sticker, arrow buttons and a month switcher, and five rounded rows pairing a white date cell (01 SUN) with a pastel mint, pink, lime or blue title bar.

## What it means
Friendly and organised; pastel coding makes each event distinct at a glance.

## Techniques
- Colour-code rows with pastel bars.
- Tilt a small sticker label across the headline.
- Split each row into a date cell and title bar.
- Mimic UI controls like arrows and month tabs.

## Best for
- Team calendars
- Community schedules
- Monthly event posts

## Vocabulary
- **Colour coding** — Using colour to categorise items.
- **Sticker label** — A small tilted tag layered on type.
