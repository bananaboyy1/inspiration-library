# 'AIDA' marketing funnel poster with isometric 3D block letters, a striped funnel and tiny business figures

![](../images/print-social-aida-funnel-poster-isometric-magenta-with-3d-type.avif)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Presentation slides · **Design style:** 3D & Surreal
**Tags:** aida, marketing-funnel, magenta, isometric, 3d-type, presentation

## What it is
A magenta poster with 'BUSINESS — Attention, Interest, Desire & Action' as the title, a large isometric 3D 'AIDA' wordmark behind a striped funnel shape (teal Attention, orange Interest, yellow Desire, navy Action), surrounded by small isometric business figures, a target, coins, cash and a magnifier, with concentric ring shadows beneath.

## What it means
Authoritative and dimensional; rendering both the acronym and the funnel in 3D makes an abstract marketing model feel like a physical machine.

## Techniques
- Render an acronym as extruded 3D block letters.
- Colour-code each funnel stage and repeat the colour in its label.
- Populate a diagram with small isometric figures for scale and life.

## Best for
- Marketing framework explainers
- Business training presentations
- Isometric infographic posters

## Vocabulary
- **Isometric illustration** — 3D-style art drawn without perspective distortion, at a fixed angle.
- **Marketing funnel** — A model showing stages from awareness to purchase.
