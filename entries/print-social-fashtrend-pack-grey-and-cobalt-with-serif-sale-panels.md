# Fashtrend nine-post pack in pale grey and cobalt with serif titles and offset photo panels

![](../images/print-social-fashtrend-pack-grey-and-cobalt-with-serif-sale-panels.avif)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Social template pack · **Design style:** Minimalist
**Tags:** fashion, sale, cobalt, grey, serif, grid

## What it is
A 3x3 grid of pale grey square posts with cobalt blue blocks, black serif headlines ('New Arrivals', 'Casual Pants', 'Big Discount 60% Off'), greyscale fashion photos offset over blue panels, small promo-code boxes and square 'Shop now' buttons.

## What it means
Crisp and stylish; one cobalt accent on grey feels like a boutique catalogue.

## Techniques
- Offset photos over solid colour blocks.
- Use one saturated accent against neutral grey.
- Pair a serif title with small sans details.
- Vary layouts while keeping palette fixed across a grid.

## Best for
- Fashion boutiques
- Clothing sales
- Minimal retail feeds

## Vocabulary
- **Offset panel** — A colour block shifted behind a photo.
- **Feed grid** — Posts designed to look cohesive as a 3x3 profile grid.
