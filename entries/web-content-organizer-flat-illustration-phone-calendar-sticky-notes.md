# Flat illustration of a man organizing content with a giant phone media player and a calendar full of sticky notes

![](../images/web-content-organizer-flat-illustration-phone-calendar-sticky-notes.avif)

**Discipline:** web · **Design type:** Feature illustration · **Design style:** Playful & Illustrated
**Tags:** content-calendar, flat-illustration, phone, sticky-notes, organizer, white

## What it is
A white-background flat illustration of a man placing a photo card into folders, beside a giant phone showing a video player and list rows, and a large calendar grid covered in colourful sticky-note reminders with ticks and crosses.

## What it means
Friendly and clear; the giant phone and calendar make abstract 'content planning' feel like a tangible, physical task.

## Techniques
- Scale key tools (phone, calendar) larger than the human figure.
- Cover a calendar with varied sticky-note shapes for texture.
- Use a simple flat palette against white for universal appeal.

## Best for
- Content calendar and scheduling tools
- Social media management apps
- Productivity marketing

## Vocabulary
- **Sticky-note texture** — Small varied note shapes used to fill a surface with detail.
- **Scale emphasis** — Enlarging key objects relative to a figure to highlight their importance.
