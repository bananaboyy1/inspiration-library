# Calendar app with floating category tags and pastel event cards around the phone

![](../images/app-pastel-tag-calendar-with-floating-event-cards.png)

**Discipline:** app ·  · **Design style:** Geometric & Bauhaus
**Tags:** calendar, planner, pastel, tags, event-cards, exploded-ui, white-background, blue-accent

## What it is
A white board with a centred phone showing a month view with coloured dashes. Around it float removable category tags (Home, Plants, Friends, School, Work, Sport, Grandma) in bright pills, and event cards in warm grey with a category chip and a coloured letter tile. A headline reads 'Where your time finally makes sense'.

## What it means
Cheerful and organised; each colour is a life category, so the interface feels personal.

## Techniques
- Explode UI components around the device to explain a colour-tagging system.
- Give each category a solid colour used for dashes, chips and tiles.
- Use a large letter tile as an avatar on event cards.
- Place a light two-line headline beside the phone.

## Best for
- Planner and calendar apps
- Explaining tag or category systems
- Family and lifestyle organisers

## Vocabulary
- **Exploded UI** — Interface parts scattered around a device to explain them.
- **Category pill** — A coloured capsule naming a category.
- **Letter tile** — A coloured block with a single letter as an icon.
- **Colour coding** — Assigning colours to categories.
