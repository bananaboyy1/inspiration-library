# Blue 'We Are Hiring! Graphic Designer' post with a man on a yellow armchair between glass responsibilities and requirements panels

![](../images/print-social-blue-hiring-graphic-designer-post-with-responsibilities-panels.avif)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Recruitment post · **Design style:** Glassmorphism
**Tags:** hiring, recruitment, blue, yellow, glass, instagram

## What it is
A square bright blue post with a white and yellow headline, a smiling man with headphones working on a laptop in a yellow armchair, flanked by two glass panels with orange-yellow headers: 'Responsibilities' (Social media posts, Branding material…) and 'Requirements' (Strong creativity, Time management…), plus WhatsApp and email.

## What it means
Friendly and informative; flanking panels explain the role while the relaxed pose sells the culture.

## Techniques
- Flank the subject with two symmetrical info panels.
- Match the chair colour to the accent.
- Use glass panels with coloured header tabs.

## Best for
- Role-specific job ads
- Design team hiring
- Instagram recruitment

## Vocabulary
- **Symmetrical flank** — Equal panels on both sides of a centre subject.
- **Header tab** — A coloured strip titling a panel.
