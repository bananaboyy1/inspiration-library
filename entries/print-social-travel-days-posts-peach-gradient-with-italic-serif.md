# 'Travel Days' pack with peach gradients, circle photos and large italic serif phrases

![](../images/print-social-travel-days-posts-peach-gradient-with-italic-serif.avif)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Social template pack · **Design style:** Warm & Organic
**Tags:** travel, peach, orange, serif, circle-photos, lifestyle

## What it is
Nine square posts on peach-to-cream gradients with orange circles behind circle-cropped travel photos, big orange italic serif phrases ('Let the journey heal your soul', 'Explore, dream, and find your escape') and a plane logo.

## What it means
Dreamy and soothing; soft gradients and italic serif make travel feel restorative.

## Techniques
- Use soft warm gradients as the whole palette.
- Let a large italic serif carry the message.
- Crop photos in circles with a colour circle behind.

## Best for
- Travel agencies
- Wellness retreats
- Lifestyle brands

## Vocabulary
- **Italic serif** — A slanted serif typeface with an elegant feel.
- **Soft gradient** — A gentle colour blend with low contrast.
