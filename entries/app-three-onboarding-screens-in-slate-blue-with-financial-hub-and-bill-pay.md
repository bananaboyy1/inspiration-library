# Three light-to-slate-blue onboarding screens: banking simplified, growth hub and bill pay, with an italic serif headline

![](../images/app-three-onboarding-screens-in-slate-blue-with-financial-hub-and-bill-pay.jpg)

**Discipline:** app · **Design type:** Onboarding screens
**Tags:** onboarding, fintech, slate-blue, italic-serif, progress, skip-next, white-cards, phones

## What it is
An off-white board with a pill label, a headline mixing light sans and bold italic serif 'Banking Experience.' and three phones on a stepped layout: a transfer card with amount, a growth hub with a weekly bar and stats, and a bill-pay arc of icons with a receipt, each with a progress line and Skip and Next buttons.

## What it means
Calm and reassuring; white cards over a slate gradient guide the user step by step.

## Techniques
- Fade screens from white to slate at the bottom.
- Use two bottom buttons: Skip and a navy Next.
- Show a preview card in each step.
- Stagger phones diagonally.

## Best for
- Fintech onboarding flows
- Slate-blue app systems
- Feature-tour screens

## Vocabulary
- **Feature tour** — Onboarding screens each showing a benefit.
- **Skip/Next pair** — A secondary and a primary button together.
- **Progress line** — A thin line showing steps.
- **Preview card** — A small card showing the feature.
