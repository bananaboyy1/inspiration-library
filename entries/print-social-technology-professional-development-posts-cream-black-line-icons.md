# 'Technology For Your Professional Development' pack alternating cream and black with simple line icons

![](../images/print-social-technology-professional-development-posts-cream-black-line-icons.avif)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Social template pack · **Design style:** Minimalist
**Tags:** technology, professional-development, cream, black, line-icons, minimal

## What it is
Nine square posts alternating cream and black, each with a simple black-outline icon (envelope, stacked cards, folder, desktop computer), the recurring headline 'Technology For Your Professional Development', a small browser-window frame device on the cover, and a cursor icon accent.

## What it means
Calm and professional; alternating light and dark posts with one consistent icon style keeps a repetitive message visually interesting.

## Techniques
- Alternate background colour while repeating the same message.
- Use a single consistent icon style across an entire set.
- Frame the lead post in a small browser-window device.

## Best for
- EdTech and training platforms
- Professional development content
- Minimalist B2B branding

## Vocabulary
- **Line icon** — An icon drawn using only outline strokes.
- **Browser-window frame** — A small UI device showing content as if inside a browser tab.
