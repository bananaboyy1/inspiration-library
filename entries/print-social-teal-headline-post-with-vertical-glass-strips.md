# Teal post with a smiling man in profile seen through vertical translucent glass strips

![](../images/print-social-teal-headline-post-with-vertical-glass-strips.avif)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Social promo post · **Design style:** Photographic & Cinematic
**Tags:** teal, blue, glass-strips, portrait, minimal, template

## What it is
A square post in deep teal with a photo of a man looking up and smiling, overlaid by vertical translucent teal strips of varying opacity like fluted glass, a bold white 'HEADLINE HERE' and short text blocks on the left.

## What it means
Calm and optimistic; the strips give depth and a sense of looking through glass.

## Techniques
- Overlay vertical strips of varying opacity.
- Tint the whole image in one hue.
- Leave the face clear between strips.

## Best for
- Wellness brands
- Corporate campaigns
- Minimal promo posts

## Vocabulary
- **Fluted glass** — Ribbed glass that distorts in vertical bands.
- **Monochrome tint** — A single-colour wash over a photo.
