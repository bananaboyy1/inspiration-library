# Podcast banner set in white with orange and violet headlines, episode numbers and sound-wave strips

![](../images/print-social-podcast-live-banner-set-white-with-orange-and-violet.avif)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Social template pack · **Design style:** Corporate & Clean-Tech
**Tags:** podcast, live-show, orange, violet, sound-wave, instagram

## What it is
Six white square posts for a podcast: photos of hosts with headphones and mics in outlined notched frames, orange and black headlines ('Sunday Special Podcast', 'QnA Podcast Live Show Today!'), vertical 'Episode #255' labels, violet 'Start listening' buttons, name tags, squiggles and faint sound-wave strips.

## What it means
Lively and organised; the sound waves and episode tags make each post read as an audio show.

## Techniques
- Add faint sound-wave strips as a texture.
- Rotate the episode number vertically beside the photo.
- Use name tags on guest photos.
- Pair orange headlines with violet buttons.

## Best for
- Podcast promotions
- Live show announcements
- Audio content brands

## Vocabulary
- **Sound wave** — A graphic of audio amplitude bars.
- **Name tag** — A label with a person's name and role.
