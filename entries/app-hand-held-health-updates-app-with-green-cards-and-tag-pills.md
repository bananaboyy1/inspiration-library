# Health Updates App in Dark Hands

![](../images/app-hand-held-health-updates-app-with-green-cards-and-tag-pills.png)

**Discipline:** app ·  · **Design style:** Glassmorphism
**Tags:** health, green, hands, glass, pills, cover, white-ui

## What it is
A pale green-yellow gradient cover with dark hands holding a phone showing a Health Updates screen with a glass header, a green specialty card, a consultation card and a floating green button, with brand and discipline pills in the bottom corners.

## What it means
Soft green gradients feel fresh and healing, and dark hands make a strong contrast to the pale UI.

## Techniques
- Dark hands against pale gradient
- Green accent cards inside a white UI
- Glass header with blurred imagery
- Rounded pill labels in the corners
- Finger about to tap the screen

## Best for
- Health and therapy apps
- Soft gradient covers
- Hand-held hero shots

## Vocabulary
- **Glass header** — A translucent header showing a blurred image.
- **Corner pills** — Rounded labels in the corners of a cover.
- **Specialty card** — A card representing a category of care.
