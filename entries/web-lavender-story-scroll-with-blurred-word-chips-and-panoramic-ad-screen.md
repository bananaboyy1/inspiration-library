# Lavender Story Scroll with Blurred Word Chips

![](../images/web-lavender-story-scroll-with-blurred-word-chips-and-panoramic-ad-screen.jpg)

**Discipline:** web ·  · **Design style:** Glassmorphism
**Tags:** storytelling, lavender, italic-sans, dashed-path, blurred-chips, photos, billboard

## What it is
A tall pale lavender page with a winding dashed path connecting italic headline statements ('Helping Seniors Living Independently', 'With or Without Memory Challenges'), blurred word chips at the sides (Overwhelming, Anxious), tilted photo cards, a panoramic display showing a joyful grandparent and child beside a phone ad, and a Location Tracker node at the base.

## What it means
A dotted path and blurred chips create a soft narrative journey, and warm photography brings human emotion to a pale page.

## Techniques
- Dashed path threading statements together
- Blurred out-of-focus word chips for depth
- Tilted photo cards floating in white space
- Italic light headline statements
- Panoramic screen as a mid-page feature

## Best for
- Storytelling scroll pages
- Care and wellness brands
- Emotional narrative landing pages

## Vocabulary
- **Blurred chip** — A defocused pill of text used as background texture.
- **Narrative path** — A line that leads the eye through a story.
- **Panoramic screen** — A wide display showing multiple pieces of content.
