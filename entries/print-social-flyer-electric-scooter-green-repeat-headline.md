# Green electric transport flyer with repeated stacked headline

![](../images/print-social-flyer-electric-scooter-green-repeat-headline.avif)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Social promo post · **Design style:** Neo-Brutalist
**Tags:** flyer, transport, electric, green, repeated headline, photo

## What it is
A square flyer on a lime-green background with a black photo panel, a repeated 'THE BEST TRANSPORT' headline stacked three times in shifting gradient colors, and a rotated 'ELECTRIC' text running vertically.

## What it means
Repeating the same headline phrase directly on top of itself in slightly different colors creates a strobing, glitch-like emphasis effect without needing motion or video.

## Techniques
- stack the identical headline phrase multiple times in different colors for a strobe/echo effect
- run supporting text vertically along the frame edge to use otherwise empty margin space
- convert the hero photo to black-and-white so only text and small accent shapes carry color

## Best for
- e-mobility/EV brand marketing
- bold youth-oriented product flyers
- high-contrast, energetic ad templates

## Vocabulary
- **echo/strobe text effect** — repeating the same text in a stack with slight offsets or color shifts to create visual vibration
