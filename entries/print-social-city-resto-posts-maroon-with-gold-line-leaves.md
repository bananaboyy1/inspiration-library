# City Resto pack in deep maroon with gold condensed type, line leaves and zigzag bands

![](../images/print-social-city-resto-posts-maroon-with-gold-line-leaves.avif)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Social template pack · **Design style:** Warm & Organic
**Tags:** restaurant, maroon, gold, line-art, food, instagram

## What it is
Five maroon posts with a boxed script 'City RESTO' logo, gold condensed uppercase headlines ('Living Cuisine', 'The best food in town!'), photos of diners and noodle bowls, thin gold line-drawn leaves and zigzag and diagonal stripe bands at corners.

## What it means
Rich and warm; maroon and gold feel like an evening dining room.

## Techniques
- Use a two-tone maroon and gold palette.
- Add fine line botanicals behind photos.
- Put stripe bands in corners as frame accents.
- Box a script logo for a signage feel.

## Best for
- Restaurants
- Food delivery promos
- Evening dining brands

## Vocabulary
- **Line art** — Illustration made of thin strokes.
- **Zigzag band** — A strip of repeating zigzag lines.
