# Frosted App Store Screenshots for a Fintech App

![](../images/app-store-screenshot-set-with-frosted-glass-headlines-and-cropped-phones.jpg)

**Discipline:** app ·  · **Design style:** Glassmorphism
**Tags:** app-store, screenshots, frosted, blue-grey, hands, headlines, carousel, rating

## What it is
A row of six tall rounded store screenshots on white, each in blue-grey frost with a light headline (Everything At Your Fingertips, Every Transfer Made Easy, Stay Financially Ahead, Utility Payments Simplified), cropped phones and hands, a rating laurel and a card being held.

## What it means
Cropped devices bleeding across neighbouring cards make the row read as one continuous scene, and the frosted palette is calm.

## Techniques
- Phones cropped across card boundaries
- Frosted gradient cards with light headlines
- Hands holding devices and a card
- Floating stat cards over screens
- Laurel rating on the first card

## Best for
- App Store and Play Store screenshot sets
- Fintech app marketing
- Continuous carousel panels

## Vocabulary
- **Store screenshot** — A marketing image shown on an app listing.
- **Continuous panel** — An image that continues across adjacent cards.
- **Rating laurel** — A wreath around a ranking and score.
