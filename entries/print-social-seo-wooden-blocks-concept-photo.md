**Tags:** seo, concept photo, wooden blocks, minimal, marketing, tabletop

## What it is
A tabletop photo of a hand placing a wooden cube printed with a magnifying-glass and monitor icon onto a stack forming a simple SEO pictogram, against a plain blue-grey backdrop.

## What it means
Building an icon out of physical wooden blocks makes an abstract acronym feel tactile and constructive, a visual metaphor for 'building' SEO strategy piece by piece.

## Techniques
- assemble an icon or word from physical building-block props instead of drawing it flat
- shoot with shallow depth of field and generous negative space for a calm, premium feel
- use a neutral, low-saturation backdrop so the wood tone and icon stay the focal point

## Best for
- marketing agency blog headers
- SEO/strategy service pages
- concept photography for abstract business topics

## Vocabulary
- **concept photography** — photography staged to visually represent an abstract idea or metaphor rather than document a literal scene
