# Problem and solution slide with tick ruler, two problem cards and a central solution card in a circle

![](../images/app-problem-and-solution-map-with-tick-ruler-and-central-circle.jpg)

**Discipline:** app · **Design type:** Process slide
**Tags:** problem-solution, diagram, white, italic-serif, ruler, dashed-connectors, quote, fintech

## What it is
A white slide with an outlined pill label, a headline with an italic serif phrase, a horizontal ruler of ticks, dashed connectors dropping to two grey Problem cards, and a central Solution card inside a huge thin circle that ends with a quote and CEO avatar.

## What it means
Structured and elegant; dashed lines and circle suggest a system converging on one answer.

## Techniques
- Drop dashed connectors from a tick ruler to cards.
- Place the solution card inside a giant thin circle.
- Mark problems with red squares and solution with green.
- End with a quote and small avatar.

## Best for
- Problem and solution case-study slides
- Fintech strategy presentations
- Minimal light decks

## Vocabulary
- **Tick ruler** — A line of small vertical ticks used as a decorative scale.
- **Status square** — A small coloured square marking a category.
- **Converging layout** — Elements arranged to lead to one central answer.
- **Avatar quote** — A quote with a small portrait and name.
