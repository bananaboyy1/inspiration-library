# Bright blue 'Happy New Month' post with a voucher card sliding from a folded flap

![](../images/print-social-blue-voucher-new-month-post-with-torn-fold.jpg)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Social promo post
**Tags:** voucher, new-month, blue, white, paper-fold, gift-icon, grain, promo

## What it is
A textured royal-blue post with a brand name at the top, 'Happy New Month' and a welcome line, a cream voucher with '01 08' numerals and a gift icon peeking from a folded blue flap, and a white footer bar with URL and handles.

## What it means
Warm and celebratory; the flap suggests a gift being opened.

## Techniques
- Slide a voucher out of a folded flap.
- Use big numerals for the date.
- Keep colours to blue, white and cream.
- Add a white footer bar.

## Best for
- Monthly greetings and offers
- Voucher and gift posts
- Simple two-colour posts

## Vocabulary
- **Voucher card** — A card offering a gift or discount.
- **Paper flap** — A folded shape hiding part of an object.
- **Date numerals** — Big numbers showing a date.
- **Footer bar** — A white strip with contact info.
