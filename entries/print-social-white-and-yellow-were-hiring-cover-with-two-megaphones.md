# White and yellow 'We're Hiring!' cover banner with two 3D megaphones and a vertical 'Open Positions' tab

![](../images/print-social-white-and-yellow-were-hiring-cover-with-two-megaphones.avif)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Recruitment post · **Design style:** Corporate & Clean-Tech
**Tags:** hiring, recruitment, yellow, white, megaphone, facebook-cover

## What it is
A wide white Facebook cover with two black-and-yellow 3D megaphones, a headline 'We're HIRING! Join Our Creative & Professional Team!' mixing yellow and black weights, an outlined tagline box, and a yellow rounded panel with a vertical 'Open Positions' label beside dark role bars, plus WhatsApp and address.

## What it means
Bright and welcoming; yellow feels optimistic and draws attention to the roles.

## Techniques
- Place two megaphones facing inward toward the headline.
- Rotate a section label vertically beside its list.
- Put roles on dark bars inside a yellow panel.

## Best for
- Hiring banners
- Creative agency careers
- Facebook covers

## Vocabulary
- **Vertical tab** — A rotated label on a panel edge.
- **Role bar** — A bar naming one job opening.
