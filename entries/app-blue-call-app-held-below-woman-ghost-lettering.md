# Blue Calls App with Low-Angle Portrait

![](../images/app-blue-call-app-held-below-woman-ghost-lettering.png)

**Discipline:** app ·  · **Design style:** Photographic & Cinematic
**Tags:** blue, low-angle, portrait, ghost-type, gauge, calls, cover

## What it is
A cover with a phone in a hand extended toward the camera showing a blue call-statistics gauge, a woman in a dark blazer behind, and giant translucent 'SOFTPHONE' lettering above.

## What it means
The low angle and outstretched hand make the phone feel immediate, and the blue gradient keeps it corporate yet energetic.

## Techniques
- Phone pushed toward the viewer for depth
- Portrait cropped behind the device
- Ghost brand name across the top
- Blue semicircle gauge as key graphic
- Small pill labels in the corners

## Best for
- Communication and telephony apps
- Case-study covers
- Business tools wanting energy

## Vocabulary
- **Forced perspective** — Making a near object look large by proximity to the camera.
- **Ghost lettering** — Oversized faint brand text behind a subject.
- **Semicircle gauge** — A half-ring showing a value.
