# Month in Review Calendar with Polaroids

![](../images/print-social-white-grid-card-month-in-review-calendar-polaroid-slide.jpg)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Social carousel cover
**Tags:** carousel, cover, calendar, polaroids, pins, blue, pink, white

## What it is
A portrait carousel cover with a blue and black headline 'Month in review: Linkedist style' with a rocket emoji, a calendar grid with pinned polaroid photos in blue and pink frames, sticky notes, handwritten notes and series chrome with a slide counter.

## What it means
Photos pinned to a calendar make a recap feel tactile and personal, and blue with pink adds cheer.

## Techniques
- Polaroid-style photos pinned on a calendar
- Sticky notes and red pins as details
- Two-colour headline
- Handwritten note with arrow
- Series chrome with counter and tags

## Best for
- Monthly recap carousels
- Company culture content
- Calendar-themed posts

## Vocabulary
- **Polaroid frame** — A photo with a thick bottom border like an instant print.
- **Push pin** — A red pin graphic holding items to a board.
- **Recap cover** — A first slide summarising a period.
