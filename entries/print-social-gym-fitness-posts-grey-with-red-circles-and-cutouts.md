# 'Shape Your Body' gym pack on grey with red circles and athletic cut-outs

![](../images/print-social-gym-fitness-posts-grey-with-red-circles-and-cutouts.avif)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Social template pack · **Design style:** Photographic & Cinematic
**Tags:** fitness, gym, red, grey, cutout, discount

## What it is
Five square posts on light grey with cut-out photos of a woman in black and red activewear with dumbbells and headphones, large red circles behind, bold white 'SHAPE YOUR BODY TODAY' type, black '20% off' circle badges and a phone number.

## What it means
Strong and motivational; the red circle feels like a spotlight on effort.

## Techniques
- Place a cut-out athlete over a big colour circle.
- Match the circle to the outfit colour.
- Stamp discounts on a dark circle badge.
- Keep the background neutral grey.

## Best for
- Gyms and trainers
- Fitness memberships
- Activewear promos

## Vocabulary
- **Spotlight circle** — A large circle behind a subject to focus attention.
- **Badge** — A small shape containing an offer.
