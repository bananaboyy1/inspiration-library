# 'Where Creative Ideas Grow' poster with a yellow sun burst, an eye-in-frame icon and a zigzag grid strip

![](../images/print-social-where-creative-ideas-grow-poster-yellow-sun-geometric.avif)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Poster · **Design style:** Geometric & Bauhaus
**Tags:** agency, geometric, yellow, navy, eye-icon, poster

## What it is
A navy-bordered white poster with a yellow starburst sun, bold navy 'Where Creative Ideas Grow' headline, an italic 'Abstract.' label, a stacked yellow-framed eye illustration, a red zigzag line, a row of red triangles, and a navy grid strip with an arch, dot and sun-face shapes.

## What it means
Playful and confident; Bauhaus-style primary shapes and a whimsical eye icon make abstract creativity feel concrete and fun.

## Techniques
- Stack simple geometric icons (eye, sun, arch) as content blocks.
- Use a starburst shape as a bold focal accent.
- Keep the palette to a few bold primary and secondary colours.

## Best for
- Creative agency posters
- Geometric brand systems
- Bauhaus-inspired marketing

## Vocabulary
- **Starburst** — A radiating spiky shape, often used to suggest energy or a sun.
- **Bauhaus shapes** — Simple geometric forms like circles, triangles and squares.
