# Futuristic blue/purple story with triangle-mountain motif

![](../images/print-social-story-futuristic-blue-purple-triangle-mountain.avif)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Story set · **Design style:** Gradient & Aurora
**Tags:** instagram story, gradient, futuristic, triangle, geometric, glow

## What it is
A vertical story template on a deep blue-to-purple gradient, centered on a glowing triangular mountain/arrow shape built from overlapping translucent planes, framed by a rounded outline border and 'Trendy Futuristic' headline.

## What it means
An upward-pointing faceted triangle formed from glowing translucent planes reads as both 'mountain' and 'arrow/growth,' giving an abstract geometric story template a sense of ascent and progress.

## Techniques
- build a central icon shape from several overlapping translucent triangular planes
- frame the whole story in a soft rounded outline border to separate it from the feed
- add a scattering of small glowing dots as ambient sparkle in negative space

## Best for
- futuristic/tech brand story templates
- event countdown or launch stories
- gradient-heavy social template packs

## Vocabulary
- **faceted plane shape** — a 3D-suggestive shape built from multiple flat overlapping surfaces, common in geometric brand marks
