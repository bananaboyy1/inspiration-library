# Email marketing portrait: a man in an orange jacket checking his phone amid glowing envelope icons and bokeh

![](../images/web-email-marketing-portrait-with-glowing-envelope-icons-bokeh.jpg)

**Discipline:** web · **Design type:** Feature illustration · **Design style:** Photographic & Cinematic
**Tags:** email-marketing, bokeh, orange, glowing-icons, portrait, night

## What it is
A moody night photo of a bearded man in glasses and an orange hooded jacket looking at his phone, surrounded by glowing blue and orange envelope and bowtie-ribbon icons and soft circular bokeh lights.

## What it means
Immersive and futuristic; glowing icons floating around a real person make an abstract marketing channel feel like it surrounds daily life.

## Techniques
- Float glowing icons around a real photographic subject.
- Use bokeh lights to add depth and a tech-night mood.
- Colour-code icon types (envelope vs ribbon) for variety.

## Best for
- Email marketing campaigns
- Digital marketing blog headers
- Tech lifestyle photography

## Vocabulary
- **Bokeh** — Soft, blurred circular light spots in a photo's background.
- **Glowing overlay icon** — A digitally added icon rendered with a light-emitting effect.
