## What it is
A white rounded dialog on black with a dark charcoal image at the top showing a soft grey donut with faint vertical lines and a close x, a bold title 'Welcome to Origin UI', a grey description, four page dots at the left and a bordered Skip and a black Next with an arrow at the right.

## What it means
Polished and friendly; a hero image and clear navigation make a tour feel light.

## Techniques
- Put a hero image above the copy.
- Add page dots for progress.
- Pair outlined Skip with black Next.
- Round the dialog generously.
