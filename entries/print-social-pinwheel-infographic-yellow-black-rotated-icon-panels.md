# Yellow-and-black pinwheel infographic with four rotated rounded panels around a central globe icon

![](../images/print-social-pinwheel-infographic-yellow-black-rotated-icon-panels.avif)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Presentation slides · **Design style:** Geometric & Bauhaus
**Tags:** infographic, pinwheel, yellow, black, icons, rotated-panels

## What it is
A mustard-yellow square with four black rounded-end panels arranged in a pinwheel around a central white square holding a globe icon, each panel rotated to a different angle and paired with an icon (bar chart, suitcase, dollar sign, running businessman) and sideways body text.

## What it means
Dynamic and unconventional; rotating panels around a central hub breaks the usual grid and makes a data slide feel kinetic.

## Techniques
- Arrange panels in a rotational pinwheel instead of a grid.
- Rotate body text to match each panel's angle.
- Anchor the composition with one central icon.

## Best for
- Global business presentations
- Infographic slide decks
- Unconventional layout systems

## Vocabulary
- **Pinwheel layout** — Elements arranged rotating around a shared centre point.
- **Rotated text** — Text angled to follow its container's orientation.
