# Green Energy App with Glass Notifications

![](../images/app-green-glass-notifications-energy-app-in-hand-over-giant-name.jpg)

**Discipline:** app · **Design type:** Device mockup scene
**Tags:** green, energy, glass, notifications, hand, giant-type, cover

## What it is
A green gradient cover with a giant white 'cubee' wordmark behind a hand holding a dark green phone showing a savings figure and an app cube, with two frosted glass notification pills floating outside the phone.

## What it means
Glass notifications escaping the device show the product talking to the user, and green signals energy savings.

## Techniques
- Giant wordmark cropped behind the phone
- Frosted glass notification cards floating outside the screen
- Dark green UI with mint highlights
- Hand holding the phone from the side
- Green radial gradient background

## Best for
- Energy and eco apps
- Notification-led feature covers
- Hero covers with giant type

## Vocabulary
- **Glass notification** — A frosted translucent pill imitating a push notification.
- **Giant wordmark** — A brand name set at enormous scale behind a subject.
- **Radial green** — A green gradient glowing outward from the centre.
