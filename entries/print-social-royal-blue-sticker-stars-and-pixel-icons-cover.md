# Cobalt cover with four sticker portraits, glitter starbursts and pixel icons

![](../images/print-social-royal-blue-sticker-stars-and-pixel-icons-cover.png)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Social cover post
**Tags:** cover, cobalt, sticker-portraits, starburst, pixel-icons, bubble-type, yellow-pill, cyrillic

## What it is
A cobalt paper-textured 4:5 post with four cut-out selfies with cream borders in the corners, two glittery lilac starbursts with yellow outlines, a pixel monitor, heart and gamepad, central chunky white rounded title, a mustard pill with green handwritten text and a pixel cursor pointer.

## What it means
Youthful, sociable and playful; a team of faces makes the brand feel like friends.

## Techniques
- Place cut-out portraits in the corners so the title sits in the centre.
- Mix glitter starbursts with pixel-art icons.
- Use a mustard pill for a secondary line with a cursor pointer.
- Keep one saturated background.

## Best for
- Team introductions and community posts
- Education and youth brands
- Carousel covers

## Vocabulary
- **Corner portraits** — People cutouts placed in the four corners.
- **Glitter starburst** — A sparkling spiky shape.
- **Pixel cursor** — A blocky arrow pointer as decoration.
- **Bubble title** — Fat rounded headline letters.
