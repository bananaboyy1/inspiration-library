# 'Novacore Studio' banner with a black-to-green-blue gradient glow and outlined service badges

![](../images/print-social-novacore-studio-banner-black-green-blue-gradient-glow.avif)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Social banner · **Design style:** Dark & Neon
**Tags:** studio, tech, black, green-blue-gradient, glow, banner

## What it is
A square banner with a black-to-green-to-blue diagonal gradient glow, a interlocking-square logo mark, 'NOVACORE STUDIO' wordmark, 'We specialize in:' with three outlined pill badges (3D, WEB, UX), a rounded URL bar and a blue 'VISIT US' button.

## What it means
Sleek and confident; the diagonal gradient glow alone gives a plain service list a premium, high-tech backdrop.

## Techniques
- Use a diagonal gradient glow as the entire background.
- Present specialties as simple outlined pill badges.
- Combine a URL bar and CTA button into one rounded element.

## Best for
- Creative studio banners
- Tech service advertising
- Dark gradient branding

## Vocabulary
- **Gradient glow** — A diagonal colour fade used to light a dark background.
- **Pill badge** — A small rounded outline label naming one item.
