# Blog article page with sage gradient, large sans title, meta pills, rounded hero photo and tag sidebar

![](../images/web-clean-blog-article-page-with-blurred-green-gradient-and-tag-sidebar.webp)

**Discipline:** web ·  · **Design style:** Gradient & Aurora
**Tags:** blog, article, green, white, meta-pills, sidebar, tags, related-posts

## What it is
A white page over a blurred green gradient with a green logo and nav (active underlined with a dot), breadcrumb, large title, meta pills (author, category, read time, date, heart), a rounded hero photo of a man with a laptop through plants, body text and a right sidebar with share icons, tags, related posts and more tags.

## What it means
Clear and modern; soft pills and a plant photo make business content feel calm.

## Techniques
- Show article meta in soft pills.
- Round the hero image.
- Add a sidebar of tags and related posts.
- Use a blurred gradient behind the page.

## Best for
- Blog and article layouts
- Business content sites
- Green minimal pages

## Vocabulary
- **Meta pill** — A capsule showing author, time or date.
- **Tag cloud** — A group of tag pills.
- **Related posts** — A list of thumbnails with titles.
- **Active nav dot** — A dot under the current menu item.
