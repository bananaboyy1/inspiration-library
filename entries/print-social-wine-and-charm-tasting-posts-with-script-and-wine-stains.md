# 'Wine & Charm' tasting pack with coral duotone photos, teal script words and wine-glass ring stains

![](../images/print-social-wine-and-charm-tasting-posts-with-script-and-wine-stains.avif)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Social template pack · **Design style:** Editorial
**Tags:** wine, tasting, coral, teal, script, duotone

## What it is
Nine cream posts with coral duotone photos of wine glasses and tasters, large teal script words ('flavors', 'aromas', 'senses', 'best wines') overlapping images, coral ring stains like wine glass marks, thin uppercase teal copy and a small 'Wine & Charm' wordmark.

## What it means
Elegant and sensory; stains and flowing script evoke a real tasting table.

## Techniques
- Add ring stains as authentic texture.
- Let a big script word overlap photo and background.
- Render photos as a single-colour duotone.
- Pair script with small uppercase sans.

## Best for
- Wine bars and tastings
- Vineyards
- Upscale events

## Vocabulary
- **Ring stain** — A circular mark like a glass base.
- **Duotone** — An image rendered in two colours.
