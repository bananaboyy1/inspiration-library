# Dark 'Market Calendar' post with flag-tagged glass cards and a 3D glass calendar tile

![](../images/print-social-dark-market-calendar-cards-with-flags-and-3d-calendar-render.avif)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Social educational post · **Design style:** Dark & Neon
**Tags:** finance, forex, calendar, dark, flags, 3d

## What it is
A square black post with ribbed teal-edged background, an 'Events' pill and 'Market Calendar' title, five dark rounded cards each with a date, weekday tag, country flag, bold event name and blue country label, and a sixth card showing a glowing 3D glass calendar render with a blue 'Forex' tag.

## What it means
Sleek and data-savvy; the 3D render adds a premium, high-tech touch to a list of events.

## Techniques
- Give each event a card with flag and country label.
- Replace one card with a hero render.
- Use ribbed vertical texture with edge glows as a backdrop.

## Best for
- Forex brokers
- Trading education
- Weekly market posts

## Vocabulary
- **Hero render** — A standout 3D image in a grid.
- **Ribbed texture** — Repeating vertical grooves.
