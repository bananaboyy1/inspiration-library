# Navy Finance Brand Guide Slide Collage

**Discipline:** branding ·  · **Design style:** Editorial
**Tags:** brand-guide, navy, slides, italic-serif, logo, palette, typography, pitch-deck

## What it is
A pale board of many slides for a finance app: a welcome slide with a script wordmark, contents, problem and principle pages, a growth chart, colour and type slides (Navy, Gray, Helvetica Neue), logo and browser-tab mockups, alternating white and navy backgrounds.

## What it means
Alternating pale and deep navy slides create rhythm, and italic serif emphasis words add elegance to a plain sans system.

## Techniques
- Italic serif accent word in each headline
- Alternating light and navy slides
- Consistent small header and footer chrome
- Ruled footer line on every slide
- Palette and type slides as compact swatch and specimen cards

## Best for
- Brand guideline and pitch decks
- Fintech identity documentation
- Presenting a whole deck at a glance

## Vocabulary
- **Slide collage** — Many slides arranged together as an overview.
- **Accent word** — A word set in a contrasting style within a headline.
- **Slide chrome** — Repeated header and footer details across slides.
