# Dark hero 'Production Ready Digital Marketing blocks' with a new-badge pill, a bordered email field, bullet list and blurred photo strip

![](../images/web-dark-marketing-blocks-hero-with-photo-strip-and-email-capture.webp)

**Discipline:** web · **Design type:** Landing page hero
**Tags:** hero, dark, black, white, email-capture, photo-strip, badge, minimal

## What it is
A near-black page with a logo and nav plus outlined and white Login buttons, a pill badge 'New Introduction Tailark Html', a large bold white headline, a grey paragraph, an outlined email input with a white Get Started button, a three-item bullet list, and a row of dark photo cards (a woman, a saxophonist, a busker) fading at the edges.

## What it means
Sleek and professional; a faded photo strip adds depth to a text-driven hero.

## Techniques
- Use a badge pill with an arrow above the headline.
- Embed an email field with a button in one bordered bar.
- Fade a photo strip at the sides.
- Keep everything black and white.

## Best for
- Dark SaaS and template heroes
- Email-capture heroes
- Minimal monochrome pages

## Vocabulary
- **New badge** — A pill announcing a feature with an arrow.
- **Email capture bar** — An input and button in one bordered container.
- **Faded photo strip** — A row of photos dimmed at the edges.
- **Monochrome hero** — A hero in black and white only.
