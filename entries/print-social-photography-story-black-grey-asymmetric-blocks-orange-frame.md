# 'Photography' story with asymmetric black-grey blocks and a rotated orange outline frame

![](../images/print-social-photography-story-black-grey-asymmetric-blocks-orange-frame.avif)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Story set · **Design style:** Minimalist
**Tags:** photography, black, grey, asymmetric-blocks, orange-frame, story

## What it is
A vertical story split into four asymmetric black and grey rectangular blocks, with a rotated orange outline square overlapping the centre holding a vertical 'PHOTOGRAPHY' label, and small dot-grid and menu icons in the bottom corner.

## What it means
Minimal and editorial; asymmetric grey blocks and one rotated frame feel like a gallery composition rather than a typical template.

## Techniques
- Divide a canvas into uneven asymmetric colour blocks.
- Overlay a rotated outline frame as the focal label.
- Keep type vertical and minimal for an editorial feel.

## Best for
- Photography portfolios
- Minimal editorial branding
- Asymmetric layout templates

## Vocabulary
- **Asymmetric block layout** — A composition of unevenly sized rectangular sections.
- **Rotated outline frame** — An angled unfilled rectangle used as a label container.
