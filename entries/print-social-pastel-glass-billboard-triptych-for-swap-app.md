# Three pearl-gradient billboards with glass cards, encrypted-orb and blurred app UI

![](../images/print-social-pastel-glass-billboard-triptych-for-swap-app.jpg)

**Discipline:** print-social ·  · **Design style:** Glassmorphism
**Tags:** ooh, posters, pastel, pearl, glass, fintech, 3d-orb, light

## What it is
Three light boxes on grey tiles. One shows translucent bank cards on a pastel gradient with a small paragraph; one an iridescent concentric orb with a padlock and a pixel wordmark under 'Your data protected by advanced encryption'; one a frosted app UI over a soft gradient under 'All your finances simplified in one place'.

## What it means
Soft and futuristic; pearl gradients feel secure and gentle.

## Techniques
- Use pearlescent pastel gradients across all three posters.
- Show one glass object per poster.
- Set short centred headlines in light type.
- Present in illuminated signage.

## Best for
- Crypto and fintech OOH
- Feature-per-poster campaigns
- Pearl-gradient brands

## Vocabulary
- **Pearl gradient** — A soft iridescent pastel blend.
- **Glass card** — A translucent 3D card.
- **Light box** — An illuminated advertising panel.
- **One-idea poster** — A poster carrying one message.
