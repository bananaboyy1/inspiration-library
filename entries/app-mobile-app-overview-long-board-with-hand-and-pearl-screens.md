## What it is
A tall off-white board '12 Mobile App' opening with a large hand holding a phone with a pearl onboarding, then screens with side captions: home dashboard, analytics with mirrored bars, manage cards with a fluted pearl card, money transfer keypad, a hand-held transaction details chart, choose a card and asset swap.

## What it means
Refined and airy; pearl glass and serif figures make a banking app feel private.

## Techniques
- Open with a hand-held hero photo.
- Alternate phone left and right with short captions.
- Use pearl glass throughout.
- Show a hand mid-flow for detail.

## Best for
- Fintech app showcases
- Long-scroll case study boards
- Pearl glass UI

## Vocabulary
- **Staggered captions** — Titles and descriptions placed beside alternating phones.
- **Pearl glass** — A pale iridescent translucent surface.
- **Hand-held hero** — A large photo of a hand holding the device.
- **Mirrored bars** — Bars above and below a centre line.
