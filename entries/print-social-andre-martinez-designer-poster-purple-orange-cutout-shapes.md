## What it is
A purple A4 poster with a large bold condensed name 'ANDRE MARTINEZ' and pink 'GRAPHIC DESIGNER' pill, a lime 'HIRE ME' tag with a pointing hand, a photo of the designer reviewing a print in a mustard blazer bordered in white, a services list in a lime rounded box, and cut-out pink and lime hand and leaf shapes.

## What it means
Bold and personal; cut-out hands and shapes turn a personal portfolio poster into a piece of graphic art itself.

## Techniques
- Use cut-out hand shapes as playful pointing devices.
- Border a portrait photo in white for a sticker-like pop.
- Name the person as the headline for direct personal branding.

## Best for
- Freelance portfolio posters
- Personal brand marketing
- Bold self-promotion pieces

## Vocabulary
- **Sticker border** — A thick white outline making a photo look cut out and applied.
- **Pointing device** — A graphic element like a hand or arrow directing attention.
