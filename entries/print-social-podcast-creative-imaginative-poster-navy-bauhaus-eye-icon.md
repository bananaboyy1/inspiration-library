# 'Podcast — Creative and Imaginative' poster with a corner shape strip and a Bauhaus eye illustration

![](../images/print-social-podcast-creative-imaginative-poster-navy-bauhaus-eye-icon.avif)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Poster · **Design style:** Geometric & Bauhaus
**Tags:** podcast, navy, bauhaus, eye-icon, geometric, poster

## What it is
A navy-bordered white poster with a diagonal strip of alternating triangle shapes in the top-left, a serif 'Podcast' title with a quoted tagline, speaker name and date, a yellow-framed illustration of an eye with a starburst pupil, small confetti dots and stars, and a colour-block bar at the bottom.

## What it means
Playful yet credible; the illustrated eye adds intrigue to an otherwise straightforward event announcement.

## Techniques
- Use a diagonal strip of alternating triangles as a header accent.
- Illustrate a symbolic eye as the poster's visual anchor.
- Close with a small multi-colour bar summarising the palette.

## Best for
- Podcast episode announcements
- Geometric event posters
- Bauhaus-inspired branding

## Vocabulary
- **Triangle strip** — A repeating row of alternating triangle shapes.
- **Palette bar** — A small strip of colour swatches used as a design flourish.
