# Novavista purple post with yellow 'Magic.' highlight and a triangle–circle corner block

![](../images/print-social-novavista-purple-post-with-yellow-geometric-corner.jpg)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Social promo post · **Design style:** Geometric & Bauhaus
**Tags:** agency, purple, yellow, geometric, typography, minimal

## What it is
A square violet post with a small N logo, a large white headline 'Unlock Your Brand's Potential with Our Marketing Magic.' where 'Magic.' is yellow, faint curved lines, and a bottom-right block of yellow and dark violet triangles and circles.

## What it means
Bold and simple; the geometric corner acts like a brand stamp.

## Techniques
- Park a geometric composition in one corner.
- Highlight the last word in a second colour.
- Keep type left-aligned with a generous margin.
- Add faint contour lines for texture.

## Best for
- Agency brand posts
- Minimal marketing series
- Geometric identities

## Vocabulary
- **Corner graphic** — A shape cluster anchored to one corner.
- **Contour lines** — Thin curved lines like a topographic map.
