# Multicolor recolored carousel with bracket-framed phone photos

![](../images/print-social-carousel-photo-bracket-job-opportunities-multicolor.avif)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Social carousel · **Design style:** Corporate & Clean-Tech
**Tags:** carousel, recolor variants, bracket frame, multicolor, job ad, phone photo

## What it is
The same twelve-tile carousel (phone-in-hand photos plus text tiles framed by corner brackets and a small logo) repeated across many different background color combinations from teal to orange to purple.

## What it means
Showing one layout re-skinned in a dozen color combinations demonstrates a template's flexibility and lets a brand pick a palette without redesigning the structure.

## Techniques
- frame a photo with four corner brackets instead of a full border for a 'viewfinder' feel
- design one fixed layout, then swap only background/accent color to generate a whole palette family
- repeat a small dot-grid icon consistently as a structural motif across every color variant

## Best for
- recruitment/job posting campaigns
- template packs sold with multiple color options
- education or career services marketing

## Vocabulary
- **corner bracket frame** — four L-shaped marks at an image's corners suggesting a frame without fully enclosing it, evoking a camera viewfinder
