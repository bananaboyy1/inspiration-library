# Food Ordering App in Dark Hands

![](../images/app-food-ordering-curved-category-arc-held-in-dark-hands.jpg)

**Discipline:** app ·  · **Design style:** Dark & Neon
**Tags:** food, hands, close-up, arc, peach, black, touch

## What it is
A close-up of two dark-skinned hands holding a phone with a food ordering screen: 'Hungry? Order & Eat.', a search bar, circular category thumbnails along an arc and product cards for noodles, pizza and burgers, with a dark floating nav bar.

## What it means
Big close-ups and a finger about to tap make the app feel immediate and appetising, while cutout food photos give it energy.

## Techniques
- Arc of round category thumbnails
- Cut-out food images with a soft shadow
- Two-tone headline (bold question, light command)
- Dark floating nav with an orange home button
- Peach gradient background

## Best for
- Food delivery apps
- Close-up app hero shots
- Playful e-commerce UI

## Vocabulary
- **Category arc** — Round category icons arranged along a curve.
- **Cut-out product** — A product photo isolated from its background.
- **Touch close-up** — A tight crop of hands interacting with a screen.
