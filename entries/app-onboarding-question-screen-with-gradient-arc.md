# Onboarding Question with Warm Gradient Arc

![](../images/app-onboarding-question-screen-with-gradient-arc.gif)

**Discipline:** app · **Design type:** Onboarding screens
**Tags:** onboarding, question, periwinkle, gradient, orange, write, voice

## What it is
A phone with a periwinkle onboarding screen asking about the user's closest family, a step indicator, a large blurred blue-to-orange arc at the bottom and two pill buttons, Write and a blue voice button.

## What it means
The soft colour arc gives a warm human feel to a personal question, and two clear input choices reduce friction.

## Techniques
- Blurred blue-to-orange arc as a visual anchor
- Step dots with a long active bar
- Conversational text in two sizes
- White and blue pill pair for input modes
- Skip link under the buttons

## Best for
- Conversational onboarding
- Care and wellness apps
- Question-based flows

## Vocabulary
- **Step indicator** — Dots or bars showing progress through a flow.
- **Input mode pair** — Two buttons offering typing or speaking.
- **Colour arc** — A blurred curved band of colours used as decoration.
