# Black Monogram in Profile, Icons and Store

![](../images/branding-black-monogram-profile-icons-and-store-listing.jpg)

**Discipline:** branding ·  · **Design style:** Warm & Organic
**Tags:** monogram, black, profile, app-icon, store-listing, cream, metallic

## What it is
A three-part board: a social profile card with a metallic card cover and a black circular avatar holding the white mark, a row of three app icons including the brand's black tile beside Safari and Slack, and an app store listing.

## What it means
A single high-contrast mark works as avatar, icon and store icon, proving flexibility.

## Techniques
- Same bold mark in circle and rounded-square formats
- Neighbouring familiar icons for scale
- Metallic embossed card imagery as a profile cover
- Cream and black palette
- Store card with Get button

## Best for
- Fintech brand applications
- App icon and avatar presentations
- Minimal monochrome identities

## Vocabulary
- **Avatar crop** — The logo adapted to a circular profile picture.
- **Icon neighbours** — Familiar app icons placed beside a new one for comparison.
- **Embossed texture** — A raised, pressed surface look in imagery.
