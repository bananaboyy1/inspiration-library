# 'Creativity, The Power of Art' story set in magenta and navy with a tinted classical marble bust

![](../images/print-social-creativity-power-of-art-stories-purple-marble-bust.avif)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Story set · **Design style:** Geometric & Bauhaus
**Tags:** creativity, art, magenta, navy, marble-bust, story

## What it is
Five vertical story cards in magenta and navy geometric blocks, each featuring a purple-tinted photo of a classical marble bust sculpture (sometimes with flowers on its head), bold condensed headlines ('Make Art', 'Be Creative', 'Artistic') and a pink 'Make Art' pill button.

## What it means
Artistic and bold; a tinted classical sculpture against flat geometric blocks bridges fine art history with modern graphic design.

## Techniques
- Tint a classical sculpture photo in the brand's accent colour.
- Split the frame into large flat geometric colour blocks.
- Repeat one CTA pill across every card in a set.

## Best for
- Art and creativity campaigns
- Museum and gallery promotion
- Bold geometric branding

## Vocabulary
- **Marble bust** — A classical sculpture depicting a head and shoulders.
- **Geometric block** — A large flat shape used to structure a layout.
