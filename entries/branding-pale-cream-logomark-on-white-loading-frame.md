# Pale Orbit-Heart Mark on White

![](../images/branding-pale-cream-logomark-on-white-loading-frame.gif)

**Discipline:** branding ·  · **Design style:** Minimalist
**Tags:** logo, monoline, cream, white, minimal, orbit, heart, animation

## What it is
A wide white frame with a small outline logomark in pale cream at the centre, two crossing loops forming a heart shape, like the first frame of a logo reveal.

## What it means
A nearly invisible mark on white sets up an animation reveal and feels calm and delicate.

## Techniques
- Very low-contrast mark on white as a reveal start
- Two crossing loops forming a heart
- Monoline outline with rounded ends
- Centred with huge margin
- Single tint of cream

## Best for
- Logo reveal animations
- Delicate care brands
- Minimal mark presentations

## Vocabulary
- **Reveal frame** — An opening frame of an animation before the mark appears fully.
- **Monoline mark** — A logo drawn with one uniform stroke.
- **Crossing loops** — Two ellipses overlapping to create a symbol.
