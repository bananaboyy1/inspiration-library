# Yellow and navy 'We Are Digital Marketing Expert' post with rounded blocks and a greyscale coffee photo

![](../images/print-social-yellow-and-navy-marketing-expert-post-with-rounded-blocks.avif)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Social ad · **Design style:** Corporate & Clean-Tech
**Tags:** agency, yellow, navy, greyscale, rounded-blocks, ad

## What it is
A square post built from rounded-corner blocks: a navy logo tab, a large yellow panel with a black headline and 'Join us' pill, a greyscale photo of a smiling man with coffee at a laptop, a pale grey contact block and a quote line.

## What it means
Friendly and orderly; soft rounded blocks make a corporate ad feel approachable.

## Techniques
- Build the layout from interlocking rounded blocks.
- Keep the photo greyscale and let yellow carry the brand.
- Give the logo its own dark tab in a corner.

## Best for
- Marketing consultants
- Agency promos
- Clean corporate ads

## Vocabulary
- **Block layout** — A composition of solid shape panels.
- **Tab** — A small shape attached to an edge holding a label.
