# 'Mega Sale 50% Off' banner with jagged orange zigzag corners and a glowing tilted price tag

![](../images/print-social-mega-sale-50-off-banner-black-orange-zigzag-glow.avif)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Social ad · **Design style:** Neo-Brutalist
**Tags:** sale, discount, black, orange, zigzag, glow

## What it is
A black square banner with jagged orange zigzag shapes cutting the top-left and bottom-right corners, bold white 'MEGA SALE' with a glowing orange outline, a tilted orange '50% OFF' price-tag ribbon, and small dashed line accents.

## What it means
Loud and urgent; jagged zigzag edges and a glow effect give a simple discount banner maximum shelf-stopping energy.

## Techniques
- Cut banner corners with jagged zigzag shapes for energy.
- Glow the headline's outline for a hot, urgent feel.
- Tilt the price callout for dynamic emphasis.

## Best for
- Flash sale promotions
- E-commerce discount banners
- High-energy retail ads

## Vocabulary
- **Zigzag corner cut** — A jagged angular shape used to break a banner's edge.
- **Outline glow** — A soft light effect applied to a shape's edge or stroke.
