# 'Bob the Bag' sales pack with lime splats, blue headlines and squiggle doodles

![](../images/print-social-bob-the-bag-sales-posts-with-lime-splats-and-blue-type.avif)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Social template pack · **Design style:** Playful & Illustrated
**Tags:** sale, discount, lime, blue, doodles, retail

## What it is
Nine white posts with lime green splat shapes, bold royal-blue headlines ('Discounts On Everything!', 'Don't Miss Out On These Amazing Deals!'), photos of an excited red-haired woman in yellow framed in blue, lime highlight bars and black squiggles and concentric arcs.

## What it means
Excited and youthful; neon splats and doodles mimic the model's surprise.

## Techniques
- Use jagged lime splats as energy bursts.
- Frame photos with a thick blue border.
- Highlight offer lines in lime bars.
- Add black doodles for motion.

## Best for
- Retail sales
- Youth fashion
- Promo campaigns

## Vocabulary
- **Splat** — An irregular burst shape.
- **Highlight bar** — A colour strip behind text.
