# 'Shape Ideas Virtually' poster in black with holographic blob shapes and a thin vertical rule

![](../images/print-social-shape-ideas-virtually-poster-black-holographic-blobs.avif)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Poster · **Design style:** Gradient & Aurora
**Tags:** holographic, black, abstract-blob, virtual, poster, minimal

## What it is
A black A4 poster on an indigo backdrop with a bold white three-line headline 'Shape Ideas Virtually', a thin vertical rule with a downward arrow, small sparkle stars, and two holographic gradient blob shapes glowing pink-blue-purple in opposite corners.

## What it means
Sleek and futuristic; two small holographic blobs are enough accent to make a mostly typographic poster feel high-tech.

## Techniques
- Use just two small holographic accents to punctuate a minimal layout.
- Add a thin vertical rule with an arrow as a subtle directional device.
- Keep the headline in plain bold sans for contrast against the shiny accents.

## Best for
- Virtual and 3D design tools
- Tech and software posters
- Minimal holographic branding

## Vocabulary
- **Holographic blob** — A glossy shape with a shifting rainbow gradient.
- **Directional rule** — A thin line with an arrow guiding the eye downward.
