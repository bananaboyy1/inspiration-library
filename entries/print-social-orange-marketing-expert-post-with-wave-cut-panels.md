# 'Digital Marketing Expert' post with an orange gradient wave panel and wave-cut corners

![](../images/print-social-orange-marketing-expert-post-with-wave-cut-panels.avif)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Social ad · **Design style:** Gradient & Aurora
**Tags:** agency, orange, gradient, wave, ad, corporate

## What it is
A square post with a photo of a smiling man sipping coffee, an orange gradient panel with a curved top edge holding a white headline and 'Join us' tag, a wave-cut white corner with an 'Agency' logo, and a white bottom-right wave carrying contact info.

## What it means
Smooth and friendly; flowing curves soften the corporate message.

## Techniques
- Cut panels with flowing wave edges.
- Outline curves with a thin coloured stroke.
- Carry the gradient from orange to red-orange for depth.

## Best for
- Marketing promos
- Coaching services
- Soft corporate ads

## Vocabulary
- **Wave cut** — A curved edge dividing areas.
- **Gradient panel** — A shape filled with a colour fade.
