# Mobile organizer illustration: a person taking a selfie beside a giant phone with checklist and calendar cards

![](../images/web-mobile-organizer-line-illustration-checklist-phone-scene.jpg)

**Discipline:** web · **Design type:** Feature illustration · **Design style:** Minimalist
**Tags:** organizer, checklist, line-illustration, flat-cartoon, phone, productivity

## What it is
A white-background line illustration of a woman taking a phone selfie, standing beside a giant phone outline displaying a checklist card (ticks, star, X marks) and a calendar/clock card, framed by orange paint-stroke accents and simple leaf plant illustrations.

## What it means
Clean and universally friendly; the loose line style and soft orange accents make a productivity app feel approachable rather than corporate.

## Techniques
- Combine line illustration with soft flat colour accents.
- Scale a checklist card to giant size as the scene's centrepiece.
- Frame the scene with simple plant illustrations for warmth.

## Best for
- Productivity and organizer apps
- To-do list marketing
- Clean line-illustration branding

## Vocabulary
- **Line illustration** — Artwork drawn primarily with outline strokes.
- **Paint-stroke accent** — A loose brush-like mark used as a decorative highlight.
