# User Testing Results with Tap Heatmaps

![](../images/app-user-testing-results-table-with-heatmap-screens.jpg)

**Discipline:** app ·  · **Design style:** Corporate & Clean-Tech
**Tags:** user-testing, table, heatmap, pills, grey, metrics, banking

## What it is
A pale grey slide titled User testing Result with a paragraph, a mission table with tasks, average time, success rate and completion in white capsule cells, and a row of pale banking screens overlaid with red-and-green tap heatmaps.

## What it means
Capsule cells make numbers stand out on a hairline table, and heatmaps make usability findings visual.

## Techniques
- Values in white capsules on hairlines
- Tap heatmaps drawn as red centres with green halos
- Screens shown desaturated so the heat stands out
- Large light title with a short paragraph
- Row of screens cropped at the right edge

## Best for
- Usability testing results
- Data table slides
- Heatmap presentations

## Vocabulary
- **Heatmap** — A colour overlay showing where users tap or look most.
- **Capsule value** — A number displayed inside a rounded white pill.
- **Task table** — A list of test tasks with time and success metrics.
