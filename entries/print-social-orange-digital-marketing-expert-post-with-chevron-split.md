# White and orange 'Digital Marketing Expert' post with a chevron split and orange-tinted photo

![](../images/print-social-orange-digital-marketing-expert-post-with-chevron-split.avif)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Social ad · **Design style:** Corporate & Clean-Tech
**Tags:** agency, orange, chevron, duotone, ad, corporate

## What it is
A square post with a white left side holding an orange and charcoal headline, quote and 'Learn more' pill, split from an orange-tinted photo of a smiling man at a laptop by a white chevron edge outlined in orange.

## What it means
Dynamic and confident; the chevron points readers from text to face.

## Techniques
- Split text and photo with a chevron edge.
- Tint the whole photo in the brand colour.
- Underline the headline with a short accent bar.

## Best for
- Agency ads
- Marketing expert promos
- Webinar posts

## Vocabulary
- **Chevron** — A V-shaped arrow form.
- **Duotone** — An image rendered in two colours.
