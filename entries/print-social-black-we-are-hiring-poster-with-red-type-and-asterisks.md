# Black 'We Are Hiring' poster with bold red type, outline italic 'New Member' and asterisk bullets

![](../images/print-social-black-we-are-hiring-poster-with-red-type-and-asterisks.jpg)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Recruitment post · **Design style:** Neo-Brutalist
**Tags:** hiring, recruitment, black, red, asterisk, typography

## What it is
A portrait black poster with huge red 'WE ARE *HIRING' with an orange outline ring motif and blue asterisk, outlined italic 'NEW MEMBER' with an arrow, two orange role pills with descriptions, asterisk-bulleted work terms (Full time, In-house, Hybrid) and a red 'APPLY NOW!' with a yellow deadline tape.

## What it means
Loud and urgent; big red type and tape make the call to apply unmissable.

## Techniques
- Mix solid and outline italic headline styles.
- Use coloured asterisks as bullets.
- Stick a tilted tape strip over the CTA for the deadline.
- Put roles in pill labels.

## Best for
- Job adverts
- Startup hiring
- Recruitment campaigns

## Vocabulary
- **Tape label** — A tilted strip like sticky tape carrying text.
- **Outline italic** — Slanted letters drawn only as strokes.
