# 'New Year Party' pack with big geometric numerals and Memphis-style shapes in blue, orange and black

![](../images/print-social-new-year-party-posts-geometric-memphis-blue-orange-black.avif)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Social template pack · **Design style:** Geometric & Bauhaus
**Tags:** new-year, party, geometric, memphis, blue-orange, instagram

## What it is
Five square posts with giant stacked '2021' numerals styled from geometric shapes, Memphis-pattern accents (stripes, dots, half-circles), bold 'PARTY' and 'NEW YEAR' headlines, and event details (Club Name, DJ Name, 8PM) in cream, blue, orange and black.

## What it means
Festive and graphic; treating numerals as geometric compositions makes a simple date the whole design's centrepiece.

## Techniques
- Build large numerals from simple geometric shapes.
- Use Memphis-style patterns (dots, stripes) as period-appropriate texture.
- Keep a consistent three-colour palette across a themed set.

## Best for
- New Year party promotion
- Nightclub event marketing
- Geometric numeral-led design

## Vocabulary
- **Memphis pattern** — Playful geometric shapes and patterns from 1980s design.
- **Numeral composition** — Treating a number as a primary graphic element.
