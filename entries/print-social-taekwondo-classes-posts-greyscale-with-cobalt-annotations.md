## What it is
Five pale grey posts with greyscale photos of taekwondo students, vertical condensed 'TAEKWONDO CLASSES' titles, cobalt blue rectangles, thin cobalt hand-drawn arrows and circled dates, a slider-like bar, and blue wavy line-mesh blobs at corners.

## What it means
Precise yet energetic; annotations feel like a coach's notes on technique.

## Techniques
- Annotate photos with hand-drawn arrows and circles.
- Keep photos greyscale with one cobalt accent.
- Set titles in tall condensed caps, vertically.
- Add line-mesh blobs at corners.

## Best for
- Martial arts schools
- Sports classes
- Editorial fitness posts
