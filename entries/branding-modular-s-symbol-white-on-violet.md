# White modular S made of two mirrored rounded L-shapes beside a wide sans wordmark on violet

![](../images/branding-modular-s-symbol-white-on-violet.jpg)

**Discipline:** branding · **Design type:** Logo with symbol
**Tags:** logo, symbol, violet, white, modular, rounded, tech, wordmark

## What it is
A flat violet field with a white symbol of two interlocking rounded L or S shapes with a cross-shaped gap and a wide round sans wordmark 'Sovrix'.

## What it means
Friendly and technical; rounded modular geometry feels dependable.

## Techniques
- Build an initial from mirrored modules.
- Leave a cross of negative space.
- Pair with a wide round sans.
- Present as white on flat colour.

## Best for
- Software and infrastructure brands
- Initial-based symbols
- Flat colour logo presentations

## Vocabulary
- **Mirrored module** — A shape repeated in reverse to form a symbol.
- **Negative-space cross** — A plus formed by gaps.
- **Flat colour presentation** — A logo on a single solid colour.
- **Wide sans** — A sans-serif with extended letter width.
