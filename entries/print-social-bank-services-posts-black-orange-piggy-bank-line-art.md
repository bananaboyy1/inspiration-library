# Bank services Instagram pack with black cards, orange spotlight glow and line-art piggy bank icon

![](../images/print-social-bank-services-posts-black-orange-piggy-bank-line-art.avif)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Social template pack · **Design style:** Dark & Neon
**Tags:** banking, fintech, black, orange, line-art, spotlight

## What it is
Nine black square posts with an orange radial spotlight glow, a recurring white line-art piggy bank with a hand dropping in a coin, bold white headlines ('Rely on us for reliable financial solutions.', 'Start Your Savings For The Future.') with orange highlighted words, and a blue 'Start Saving Online' pill.

## What it means
Trustworthy yet modern; the glowing spotlight on a simple line icon makes a bank feel approachable rather than corporate.

## Techniques
- Repeat one line-art icon across an entire post set for brand recall.
- Light the icon with a radial spotlight glow.
- Highlight the key action word in the accent colour.

## Best for
- Banking and fintech social content
- Savings product campaigns
- Dark-mode financial branding

## Vocabulary
- **Radial spotlight** — A glow that fades outward from a central point.
- **Line-art icon** — An icon drawn with thin outline strokes only.
