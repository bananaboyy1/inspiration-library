# Thrift shop pack with a chunky retro serif over clothing photos and pastel colour-block bars

![](../images/print-social-thrift-shop-posts-with-retro-serif-and-pastel-colour-bars.avif)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Social template pack · **Design style:** Retro & Pixel
**Tags:** thrift, vintage, serif, pastel, colour-blocks, fashion

## What it is
Five posts with photos of clothing racks and vintage shoppers, a chunky white retro serif 'thrift shop' with a mustard 'Vintage clothes' line, and bars of pastel colour blocks (terracotta, sage, mustard, peach) along edges, with thin frames and social icons.

## What it means
Nostalgic and warm; the retro serif and swatch bars feel like a 70s shop sign.

## Techniques
- Set a chunky retro serif over photos.
- Add colour-swatch bars along edges.
- Run the title vertically on some posts.
- Use a thin inner frame for structure.

## Best for
- Vintage and thrift shops
- Retro brands
- Secondhand fashion

## Vocabulary
- **Swatch bar** — A row of colour blocks like paint samples.
- **Retro serif** — A soft heavy serif evoking the 1970s.
