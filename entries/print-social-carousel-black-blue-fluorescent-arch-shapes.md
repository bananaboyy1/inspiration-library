# Black and fluorescent-blue carousel with concentric arch shapes

![](../images/print-social-carousel-black-blue-fluorescent-arch-shapes.avif)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Social template pack · **Design style:** Dark & Neon
**Tags:** carousel, fluorescent, neon blue, black, arch shapes, instagram

## What it is
A five-tile Instagram grid on black backgrounds, built from concentric electric-blue arch/rainbow shapes, bold sans headlines and a small diamond-brush texture accent.

## What it means
Concentric arch shapes glowing against pure black give a minimal two-color palette a strong sense of movement and depth, making 'neon' feel earned rather than just a color choice.

## Techniques
- stack several concentric arch shapes at different radii to build implied depth and motion
- keep the palette to exactly two colors (black, fluorescent blue) for maximum contrast punch
- add one small brushed-texture accent shape to break up otherwise hard-edged vector shapes

## Best for
- nightlife/electronic music brand kits
- bold two-color neon template packs
- tech/gaming social content

## Vocabulary
- **concentric shapes** — shapes sharing the same center point but differing in size, commonly used to imply radiating motion or depth
