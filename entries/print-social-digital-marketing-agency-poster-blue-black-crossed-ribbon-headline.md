# 'Digital Marketing Agency' poster with a blue-tinted portrait and a crossed black ribbon headline

![](../images/print-social-digital-marketing-agency-poster-blue-black-crossed-ribbon-headline.avif)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Poster · **Design style:** Neo-Brutalist
**Tags:** agency, poster, blue, black, ribbon-headline, photo

## What it is
A poster with a blue-tinted photo of a person using a phone and a play-button icon, a blue 'Digital Marketing Agency' bar, two crossed black ribbon bands both reading 'Creative Solutions' in different sizes, body copy, and a grey footer with service pills and contact icons.

## What it means
Bold and slightly chaotic on purpose; crossing two ribbons of the same message creates emphasis through visual noise.

## Techniques
- Cross two ribbon bands at an angle for graphic tension.
- Repeat one key phrase twice at different scales for emphasis.
- Tint a photo in the brand's primary colour.

## Best for
- Marketing agency posters
- Bold service promotion
- High-contrast brand systems

## Vocabulary
- **Crossed ribbon** — Two angled bands overlapping in an X shape.
- **Repetition emphasis** — Repeating a phrase to reinforce its importance.
