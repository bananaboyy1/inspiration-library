# 'A four-step path from blank page to launched site' with mono step numbers in circles on a vertical line and four bordered cards with icons

![](../images/web-how-it-works-four-step-list-with-numbered-timeline-and-icon-cards.webp)

**Discipline:** web ·  · **Design style:** Minimalist
**Tags:** how-it-works, steps, timeline, monospace, cards, white, icons, minimal

## What it is
A white page with a tiny caps label 'HOW IT WORKS', a bold centred headline and a vertical timeline where circles with monospace numbers (01 to 04) sit beside wide hairline cards each with a small icon tile, a bold title (Browse the catalog, Install via shadcn, Customize without fear, Ship) and grey description.

## What it means
Clear and confident; a numbered line and quiet cards make a process feel simple.

## Techniques
- Number steps in monospace circles on a vertical line.
- Use hairline cards.
- Add a small icon tile per step.
- Keep grey descriptions short.

## Best for
- How-it-works sections
- Developer product pages
- Minimal step lists

## Vocabulary
- **Numbered timeline** — Steps marked by numbers on a vertical line.
- **Hairline card** — A card with a very thin border.
- **Icon tile** — A small rounded square holding an icon.
- **Eyebrow label** — A tiny caps label above a heading.
