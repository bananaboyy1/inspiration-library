# Cloudy Sky Fintech Landing Page

![](../images/web-sky-clouds-fintech-landing-page-long.jpg)

**Discipline:** web · **Design type:** Landing page
**Tags:** clouds, sky, fintech, light, navy, long-page, pricing

## What it is
A long, airy landing page for a fintech app set on a cloud-filled sky: hero with a hand-held phone and floating cards, feature statements, transfer UI, pricing plans, testimonials and a closing download section.

## What it means
Sky and cloud imagery signal openness and calm, and light navy text on white cards stays trustworthy.

## Techniques
- Cloud photography as a section backdrop
- Light-weight headline with navy emphasis
- Floating stat cards around the phone
- Two-tier pricing cards with a highlighted plan
- Testimonial row cropped at the edges

## Best for
- Fintech and banking marketing sites
- Calm, trust-focused landing pages
- Long scrolling product pages

## Vocabulary
- **Atmospheric backdrop** — A sky or cloud image used as a mood-setting background.
- **Highlighted plan** — A pricing card elevated to steer choice.
- **Cropped carousel** — Cards cut off at the edge to imply more.
