# Stress Level Cards on Periwinkle

![](../images/app-cropped-stress-level-cards-on-periwinkle-carousel.gif)

**Discipline:** app ·  · **Design style:** Warm & Organic
**Tags:** cards, periwinkle, navy, carousel, cropped, insight, orange

## What it is
A periwinkle rounded panel showing three tall cards, a cropped grey left card, a navy centre card 'STRESS LEVELS' with a large 'High', and a cropped right card with an orange line chart.

## What it means
Cropped side cards imply a swipe carousel, and the navy card with one word makes the key message unmistakable.

## Techniques
- Cropped neighbours suggesting scrolling
- Single dark card among light ones as focal point
- Huge one-word result at the base
- Small caps card title with a short paragraph
- Orange line chart as the accent

## Best for
- Insight cards
- Wellness and household apps
- Swipeable summary cards

## Vocabulary
- **Cropped carousel** — Cards cut off at the sides to imply more.
- **Result word** — A single large word summarising an insight.
- **Focal card** — The one card styled differently to lead the eye.
