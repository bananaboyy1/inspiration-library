# Candid outdoor photo of two friends absorbed in their phones

![](../images/print-social-lifestyle-phone-scrolling-friends-photo.jpg)

**Discipline:** print-social · **Design type:** Brand campaign visual · **Design style:** Photographic & Cinematic
**Tags:** lifestyle photo, phone scrolling, candid, outdoor, social media, stock

## What it is
A naturally lit outdoor photo of two young women standing side by side, both looking down at their phones, shot with a shallow depth of field against a blurred street backdrop.

## What it means
Two people standing together but each absorbed in their own phone visually communicates 'digital disconnection,' a common metaphor in social-wellbeing and phone-habit content.

## Techniques
- position two subjects physically close but visually isolated (each looking at their own device) to imply disconnection
- use natural outdoor light and shallow depth of field for an authentic, unstaged feel
- let ambient background details blur softly so the subjects and their phones stay the clear focus

## Best for
- digital wellbeing/screen-time campaigns
- social media habit editorial content
- authentic lifestyle stock photography

## Vocabulary
- **shallow depth of field** — a photographic technique keeping the subject sharp while the background blurs, achieved with a wide aperture
